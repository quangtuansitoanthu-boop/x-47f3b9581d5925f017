Commentary: What I say thus is spoken by the Buddhas. This is how the Buddhas speak the Buddhadharma. What is not spoken thus is spoken by Papiyan. It is the demons' speech. "Papiyan" is Sanskrit, meaning "the evil one," referring to Mara, the demon king. All who come to hear sutra lectures surely must be people who can see. The blind cannot come hear the sutra; nor can the deaf and mute. The more people come hear sutra lectures, the cleverer they become. Everyone should open the true wisdom eye and truly turn the ear root back into their own nature. Do not hope to seek outside.

2. ONE MUST END KILLING

Sutra text: 阿難,又諸世界六道眾生,其心不殺,則不隨其生死相續。

Transliteration: A-nan, hựu chư thế giới lục đạo chúng sanh, kỳ tâm bất sát, tắc bất tuỳ kỳ sanh tử tương tục.

Translation: Further, Ananda, if beings of the six paths in all worlds had no killing in their minds, they would not follow the continuity of birth and death.

Commentary: If gods, humans, asuras, animals, hungry ghosts, beings in the hells, bore no heavy thoughts of killing, but instead stopped killing entirely and liberated all beings, then they could leave the wheel of birth and death. Here only thoughts of killing (mental karma) are mentioned, not yet acts of killing (bodily karma). If people stop the karma of killing, they will avoid drifting in birth and death through the six paths, and being lords of karma having to undergo birth-and-death accordingly.

Sutra text: 汝修三昧本出塵勞。殺心不除塵不可出。

Transliteration: Nhữ tu tam-muội, bổn xuất trần lao. Sát tâm bất trừ trần bất khả xuất.

Translation: In cultivating samadhi, your fundamental aim is to transcend worldly toil. If the killing mind is not removed, one cannot transcend the dust.

Commentary: Ananda, you wish to cultivate samadhi power. In cultivating samadhi, your fundamental aim is to transcend worldly toil. From the start, you hoped to escape worldly toil. If the killing mind is not removed, one cannot transcend worldly toil. How can we rid ourselves of killing thoughts? Stop killing entirely and liberate all beings. Above, the sutra text says, "If lustful thoughts are not removed, one cannot transcend worldly toil." So you must be strict with lustful thoughts to hope to escape worldly toil. That is the only way you can escape the wheel of birth and death. But if you still scatter the mind in lustful thoughts and still harbor a heavy killing mind, you can never leave the world. You can never transcend birth and death.

Sutra text: 縱有多智禪定現前,如不斷殺必落神道。上品之人為大力鬼。中品即為飛行夜叉諸鬼帥等。下品當 為地行羅剎。

Transliteration: Túng hữu đa trí thiền định hiện tiền, như bất đoạn sát, tất lạc thần đạo. Thượng phẩm chi nhơn vi đại lực quỷ. Trung phẩm tức vi phi hành dạ-xoa, chư quỷ soái đẳng. Hạ phẩm thượng vi địa hành la-sát.

Translation: Though one has much wisdom and samadhi manifests, if killing is not cut off, one will surely fall among spirits. The highest grade becomes powerful ghosts. The middle grade becomes flying yakshas, ghost commanders, and the like. The lowest grade becomes earth-traveling rakshasas.
