Commentary: If there are living beings who wish for a body at ease — they wish to be at ease, to go wherever they want. I appear before them in the body of an Easeful Heaven god, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to accomplish their wish. The gods of the Great Ease Heaven can go wherever they wish.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生欲身自在飛行虛空。我於彼前現大自在天身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh dục thân tự tại phi hành hư không, ngã ư bỉ tiền, hiện Đại Tự tại thiên thân. Nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who wish for a body at ease, flying through empty space, I appear before them in the body of a Great Easeful Heaven god, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who wish for a body at ease, flying through empty space. They wish to be at ease, their bodies transforming at will. They wish their bodies to fly through empty space. Because they have this wish, I shall rely upon the compassionate power of the Buddhas to enable them to manifest bodies as they wish. And I shall appear before them in the body of a Great Easeful Heaven god and speak Dharma for them, enabling them to accomplish their wish. The Great Easeful Heaven gods are most autonomous and full of blessings. Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva manifests as one of them to help beings accomplish their wishes.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生愛統鬼神救護國土。我於彼前現天大將軍身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh ái thống quỷ thần, cứu hộ quốc độ, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Thiên Đại tướng quân thân nhi vị thuyết pháp, linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who wish to command ghosts and spirits to rescue and protect their country, I appear before them in the body of a great heavenly general, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who wish to command ghosts and spirits to rescue and protect their country. There are some people who like to command ghosts and spirits. They do this work out of a patriotic spirit, to rescue and protect the homeland. I appear before them in the body of a great heavenly general, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生愛統世界保護眾生。我於彼前現四天王身,而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh ái thống thế giới, bảo hộ chúng sanh, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Tứ thiên vương thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp, linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who delight in ruling the world and protecting living beings, I appear before them in the body of one of the Four Heavenly Kings, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who delight in ruling the world and protecting living beings. They wish to govern the world in order to protect the beings in that world; I appear before them in the body of one of the Four Heavenly Kings, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish. The Four Heavenly Kings are:

1. Dhṛtarāṣṭra, Guardian of the Nation Heaven (s: Dhṛtarāṣṭra 持國天), who oversees the continent Purvavideha in the East;
2. Virūpākṣa, the Broad-Eyed Heaven (s: Virūpākṣa 廣目天), who oversees the continent Aparagodaniya in the West;
3. Virūḍhaka, the Growth Heaven (s: Virūḍhaka 増長天), who oversees the continent Jambudvipa in the South.
4. Vaiśravaṇa, the Much-Hearing Heaven, or Heavenly King Vaiśravaṇa (s: Vaiśravaṇa 多聞天), who oversees the continent Uttarakuru in the North.

For those who wish to rule the world and protect living beings, Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva will manifest the body of one of these Four Heavenly Kings.
