The old father reflected, seeing that all the gold and silver he had hoarded would bring joy to his first and second sons, while he himself had to die. He was flooded with profound remorse. He thought, "Would that I had built a temple or a Way-place in the time remaining — but it is too late. Now I am about to die."

The moral of this story is: do not be like that old man. If you have the means, do good works at once.

Sutra text: 十二者融形復聞,不動道場涉入世間,不壞世界能遍十方。供養微塵諸佛如來,各各佛邊為法王子。能令法界無子眾生。欲求男者,誕生福德智慧之男。

Transliteration: Thập nhị giả, dung hình phục văn, bất động đạo tràng thiệp nhập thế gian, bất hoại thế giới. Năng biến thập phương. Cúng dường vi trần chư Phật Như Lai, các các Phật biên vi pháp vương tử. Năng linh pháp giới vô tử chúng sanh. Dục cầu nam giả, đản sanh phước đức trí huệ chi nam.

Translation: Twelfth, form dissolved, I return to hearing; unmoving in the Way-place, I merge into the world without destroying the world. Able to pervade the ten directions, I make offerings to the Buddhas, the Tathagatas, as numerous as dust motes; beside each Buddha I am a Dharma Prince. I enable childless beings of the Dharma Realm who seek sons to give birth to sons of blessings, virtue, and wisdom.

Commentary: The twelfth fearlessness power is: form dissolved, I return to hearing — when the body has been transformed and returned to the hearing-nature — unmoving in the Way-place, I merge into the world without destroying the world.

"The unmoving Way-place" means constantly dwelling in one's original Way-place. For instance, Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva is now present in the Buddhist Lecture Hall, yet though he is here, he can still travel throughout the world. It is not that he goes from here; his transformation bodies appear everywhere. And the world is still not altered. With his Dharma body, he can make offerings to the Buddhas, the Tathagatas, as numerous as dust motes throughout the ten directions. He goes throughout the ten directions to do the Buddha's work. Beside each Buddha I am a Dharma Prince. I enable childless beings of the Dharma Realm who seek sons to give birth to sons of blessings, virtue, and wisdom.

Sutra text: 十三者六根圓通,明照無二含十方界。立大圓鏡空如來藏。承順十方微塵如來。祕密法門受領無失。能令法界無子眾生,欲求女者,誕生端正福德柔順,眾人愛敬有相之女。

Transliteration: Thập tam giả lục căn viên thông, minh chiếu vô nhị, hàm thập phương giới. Lập đại viên kính không Như Lai tạng. Thừa thuận thập phương vi trần Như Lai. Bí mật pháp môn thọ lĩnh vô thất. Năng linh pháp giới vô tử chúng sanh, dục cầu nữ giả, đản sanh đoan chánh phước đức nhu thuận, chúng nhân ái kính hữu tướng chi nữ.

Translation: Thirteenth, my six sense roots perfectly interfused, the illumination non-dual, containing the realms of the ten directions; establishing the great perfect mirror, the empty Tathagatagarbha, I receive and follow the secret Dharma-doors of the Tathagatas of the ten directions, as numerous as dust motes, without loss. I enable childless beings of the Dharma Realm who seek daughters to give birth to daughters of upright marks, blessings, virtue, and gentleness, loved and respected by all.

Commentary: The thirteenth fearlessness power is: my six sense roots perfectly interfused, illumination non-dual, containing the ten directions, establishing the great perfect mirror, the empty Tathagatagarbha.
