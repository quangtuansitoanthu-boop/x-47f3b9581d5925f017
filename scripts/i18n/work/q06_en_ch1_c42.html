Commentary: Therefore, Ananda, cultivating dhyana without cutting off killing. They must be strict with their seeds of compassion. Having lost the compassionate mind, it is like a person who stops his own ears, shouts loudly, and hopes no one hears. This is like the person who stops his ears to steal a bell, supposing that if he himself does not hear the bell, others will not hear either. Such ones are called those who, wishing to hide, reveal all the more. The more they wish to hide their deeds, the more they are revealed. Likewise, those who cultivate dhyana yet refuse to stop killing can hardly attain what they hope for.

Sutra text: 清淨比丘及諸菩薩,於岐路行不踏生草,況以手拔。云何大悲取諸眾生血肉充食?

Transliteration: Thanh tịnh tỷ-khưu cập chư Bồ-tát, ư kỳ lộ hành bất đạp sanh thảo, huống dĩ thủ bạt. Vân hà đại bi thủ chư chúng sanh huyết nhục sung thực?

Translation: Pure bhikshus and Bodhisattvas, walking along a forked road, do not tread on living grass, much less pull it up by hand. How can those practicing great compassion take beings' blood and flesh for food?

Commentary: Pure bhikshus and Bodhisattvas cultivating extremely pure conduct, walking along a road, do not tread on living grass. Where there are several paths, grass often grows beside the road. Pure bhikshus and Bodhisattvas will never step on living grass, fearing to kill the grass. Much less pull it up by hand. Nor do they uproot a single plant. How can those practicing great compassion take beings' blood and flesh for food? That cannot be.

Sutra text: 若諸比丘不服東方絲綿絹帛,及是此土靴覆裘毳乳酪醍醐。如是比丘於世真脫。酬還宿債不遊三界。

Transliteration: Nhược chư tỷ-khưu bất phục đông phương ty miên quyên bạch, cập thị thử độ ngoa phú cừu thuế, nhũ lạc đề hồ. Như thị tỷ-khưu ư thế chơn thóat. Thù hoàn túc trái, bất du tam giới.

Translation: If bhikshus do not wear silk, cotton-silk, or fine silk of the East, nor use this land's boots, furs, fleeces, milk, cream, or ghee, such bhikshus are truly liberated in the world. Having repaid old debts, they no longer roam the Triple Realm.

Commentary: If bhikshus do not wear silk, cotton-silk, or fine silk of the East, nor use this land's boots, furs, fleeces, milk, cream, or ghee, such bhikshus are truly liberated in the worldly dharma. Silk, leather, furs, down (bird feathers) all come from living beings. Those beings' lives must be taken to make these things. Ordinary cloth is not included among these kinds. Thus they do not use leather shoes or bags made of leather. Nor do they use foods made from milk. Having repaid old debts, they no longer roam the Triple Realm.
