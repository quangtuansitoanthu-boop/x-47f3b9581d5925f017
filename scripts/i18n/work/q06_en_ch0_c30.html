"Vajra" means strong and indestructible.

Translation: Or manifest two eyes, three eyes, four eyes, nine eyes, 108 eyes, then 1,000 eyes, 10,000 eyes, up to 84,000 pure precious eyes. Sometimes manifesting kindness, sometimes awesomeness, sometimes samadhi, sometimes wisdom, to rescue beings unto great ease.

Commentary: Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's Dharma body can manifest as two eyes, three eyes, four eyes, nine eyes, 108 eyes, then 1,000 eyes, 10,000 eyes, up to 84,000 pure precious eyes. How can Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva manifest so many transformation bodies? Because on the causal ground he cultivated the Great Compassion Mantra and the Forty-Two Hands and Eyes Dharma, so on the fruition ground, when cultivation effort was perfected, he obtained countless transformation bodies.

Or manifesting kindness, sometimes awesomeness, sometimes samadhi, sometimes wisdom, to rescue beings unto great ease.

His eyes sometimes express kindness, sometimes manifest awesomeness. Sometimes his eyes display the light of samadhi power or wisdom. He displays these marks to rescue beings unto perfect ease.
