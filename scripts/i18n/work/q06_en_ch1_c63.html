Sutra text: 是故阿難若不斷其大妄語者。如刻人糞為栴檀形,欲求香氣。 無有是處。

Transliteration: Thị cố A-nan, nhược bất đoạn kỳ đại vọng ngữ giả, như khắc nhơn phẩn vi chiên đàn hình,dục cầu hương khí, vô hữu thị xứ.

Translation: Therefore, Ananda, not cutting off great false speech is like carving human excrement into the shape of chandana, hoping to seek fragrance. That can never be.

Commentary: The Tathagata will give an analogy. Therefore, Ananda, not cutting off great false speech is like carving human excrement into the shape of chandana, hoping to seek fragrance.

Those wishing to become pure without cutting off great false speech are like people trying to carve chandana-scented wood from human excrement. That can never be. He will never make human excrement smell fragrant like chandana wood. This means if you speak great false speech, it is as if you smell most foul. If you cultivate dhyana, striving to become a Buddha, yet continue lying, then you are like a lump of excrement. For a liar hoping to become a Buddha is like trying to make a lump of excrement smell fragrant like a Buddha image. That is unreasonable.

Sutra text: 我教比丘直心道場。於四威儀一切行中尚無虛假。云何自稱得上人法。

Transliteration: Ngã giáo tỷ-khưu trực tâm đạo tràng. Ư tứ oai nghi nhất thiết hạnh trung, thượng vô hư giả. Vân hà tự xưng đắc thượng nhơn pháp?

Translation: I teach bhikshus to take the straightforward mind as the Way-place. In all conduct within the four deportments, there should be no falseness at all. How can anyone claim to have attained the dharmas of superior people?

Commentary: I teach bhikshus to take the straightforward mind as the Way-place. The bhikshus mentioned here include the four assemblies. Here, you cannot say, "I am a lay follower, so the Buddha is not speaking of me."

You must be straightforward in speech and thought. Do not be crooked; do not lie. Lacking a straightforward mind is like trying to make chandana fragrance from excrement. The Tathagata teaches them that in all conduct within the four deportments, there should be no falseness at all.

These deportments were presented in detail before. Each posture of walking, standing, lying, and sitting has 250 deportments. You should always practice most truly, truly cultivating within them. How can anyone claim to have attained the dharmas of superior people?

Why proclaim oneself certified to Bodhisattva or Arhat fruitions? Such speech is forbidden. Before you heard the sutra lectures, you might have unwittingly said so. But now, having heard the sutra lectures, you know you may no longer say you have certified to any fruition. Saying so is great false speech. Its retribution is falling into the tongue-pulling hells. In a later life, your tongue will be hooked by iron hooks and pulled out. After that, you will have no more chance to lie, for in the future, you will already be mute.
