THE REALM OF FORM

THE FIRST DHYANA

A-nan! Thế gian nhất thiết, sở tu tâm nhân. Bất giả Thiền-na, vô hữu trí tuệ.

Ananda! All those who cultivate the mind in the world, without Dhyana, have no wisdom.

Commentary: Ananda, now we shall speak of the Realm of Form, the heavens of the Four Dhyanas. All cultivators in the world cannot have wisdom without the aid of Dhyana. They are not yet accustomed to cultivating meditative concentration — they cannot yet restrain all their false thoughts, and therefore they have no wisdom yet.

  1. Đãn năng chấp thân, bất hành dâm dục. Nhược hành nhược tọa, tưởng niệm câu vô. Ái nhiễm bất sanh, vô lưu dục giới, thị nhân ưng niệm, thân vi phạm lữ. Như thị nhất loại, danh Phạm Chúng Thiên.

If one can restrain the body from sexual desire, whether walking, standing, sitting, or lying down, with no thought or recollection whatsoever; lust and defilement do not arise; without outflow from the desire realm — this person is called the Brahma Multitudes Heaven.

Commentary: One can control one's own body and mind and guard against sexual desire. "Whether walking, standing, sitting, or lying down, with no thought or recollection whatsoever" — at all times, whether walking, standing, sitting, or lying down, and even in dreams — "lustful defilement does not arise." Even when encountering a situation or a person, no thoughts of lust arise. Thus one "cultivates the pure precepts without outflow."

Such people can freely associate with Brahma heavens. They make friends with the Brahma heavens. And they no longer have any lustful desires. Such people are called the Brahma Multitudes Heaven. They are reborn as pure sages in those heavens. They become a celestial being among the great multitudes of Brahma heavens — one among the great multitude.

  1. Dức tập ký tự, ly dục tâm hiện. Ư chu luật nghi, ái lạc tự thuần. Thí nhân ứng thời, năng hành phạm độc. Như thị nhất loại, danh Phạm Phụ Thiên.

When virtue has long been accumulated, "the mind is exhausted of desire," following the precepts and rules, acting in accordance with the rules — this is called the Brahma Ministers Heaven.

Commentary: "Virtue has long been accumulated, the mind is exhausted of desire, following the precepts and rules." The basic nature of humans is to like food and sex. These are innate habits. But at this point, these beings have left behind the habits of lustful desire. When they no longer have lust or defilement, wisdom begins to arise. They uphold the precepts and follow the rules of discipline. They delight in the precepts and maintain purity in their cultivation. Such people can carry out the unique conduct in accordance with the rules — this is called the Brahma Ministers Heaven. At all times and in all circumstances, they cultivate according to the true Way. Their conduct is pure and clean. "Brahma Ministers" means purity. These beings are reborn in the Brahma Ministers Heaven. Upholding the Dharma, they assist the Great Brahma Minister Heaven.

  1. Thần tâm diệu viên, uy nghi bất khuyết. Thanh tịnh cấm giới, gia dĩ minh ngộ. Thí nhân ứng thời, năng thượng phạm chúng, vi Đại Phạm Vương. Như thị nhất loại, danh Đại Phạm Thiên.

Body and mind wondrously perfect, awesome deportment fully complete; precepts maintained purely, with spiritual power; leading the Brahma multitudes as the Great Brahma King — this is called the Great Brahma Heaven.

Commentary: Those who have "body and mind wondrously perfect, awesome deportment fully complete, precepts maintained purely, with spiritual power." These are beings who no longer have thoughts of lust, and who fully possess the 3,000 awesome deportments and 80,000 subtle manners. They perfect all conduct. They uphold the precepts purely and completely according to what the Buddha taught. Not only do they uphold the precepts perfectly, but they also have a comprehensive understanding of the precepts. They have penetrated through body, matter, and dharmas. They "lead the Brahma multitudes as the Great Brahma King — this is called the Great Brahma Heaven." When their lives end, they are reborn in the Brahma heavens and can rule over all in the Brahma realm.

A-nan! Thứ tam thắng lưu. Nhất thiết khổ não, sở bất năng bác. Tuy phi chính tu, chân tam-ma-đề. Thanh tịnh tâm trung, chử lậu bất động, danh vi sơ Thiền.

Ananda! These three are supreme and cannot be surpassed. Though not true cultivation, they are genuine Dhyana. Pure in mind, outflows cannot move them — this is called the First Dhyana.

Commentary: "Ananda! These three are supreme and cannot be surpassed." These three are the Brahma Multitudes Heaven, the Brahma Ministers Heaven, and the Great Brahma Heaven. These are supreme celestial ranks — the heavens of the First Dhyana — where defilement and affliction do not disturb the cultivator. "Though not true cultivation, they are genuine Dhyana." They have not yet cultivated true Dhyana, but their minds are pure, and afflictions have been suppressed. They have not cut off outflows, but within the First Dhyana, they are not moved by outflows. "Pure in mind, outflows cannot move them — this is called the First Dhyana."

THE SECOND DHYANA

  1. A-nan! Cử thrice Phạm thiên. Thường nhận Phạm nhân, viên mãn Phạm hành. Trưởng tâm bất động, tích trạm sinh quang. Như thị nhất loại, danh Thiếu Quang Thiên.

Ananda! The Brahma heavens above... (continues with all Second Dhyana heavens)

THE THIRD DHYANA

  1. A-nan! Như thí Thiên nhân. Viên quang thành âm, phi âm lự điều. Phát thành tinh hành, thông tích tích lạc. Như thị nhất loại, danh Thiếu Tịnh Thiên.

Ananda! Like those heavenly beings... (continues with all Third Dhyana heavens)

THE FOURTH DHYANA

  1. A-nan! Thứ Phúc thiên nhân. Bất bác thần tâm, khổ nhân dĩ tận. Lạc phi thường trí, cựu túc hội sinh. Khổ lạc nhị tâm, nhất thời cùng xả. Tưởng thư tích vi, sanh diệt vi nhân. Bất năng phát minh, bất sanh diệt tính. Sơ bán kiếp diệt, hậu bán kiếp sanh. Như thị nhất loại, danh Vô Tưởng Thiên.

Ananda! The Fourth Dhyana heavens... (continues with all Fourth Dhyana heavens including the Five Pure Abodes)
