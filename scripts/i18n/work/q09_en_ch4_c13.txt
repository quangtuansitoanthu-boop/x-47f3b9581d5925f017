<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Then the heavenly demon gets a convenient chance</strong> &#8211; the heavenly demon watching for when it sees a convenient chance &#8211; <strong>at once sending its essence flying to possess another person, using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma</strong>.</p>
<p>The demon lectures the Dharma through the mouth of the person it has possessed.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Kì nhân chung bất giác tri ma trước, diệc ngôn tự đắc vô thượng niết-bàn. Lai bỉ cầu không thiện nam tử xứ, phu tòa thuyết pháp. Ư đại chúng nội, kì hình hốt không, chúng vô sở kiến, hoàn tùng hư không, đột nhiên nhi xuất, tồn một tự tại. Hoặc hiện kì thân, đỗng như lưu li, hoặc thuỳ thủ túc, tác chiên đàn khí, hoặc đại tiểu tiện, như hậu thạch mật. Phỉ huỷ giới luật, khinh tiện xuất gia.     </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: That person, never aware of demonic possession, likewise claims to have attained unsurpassed nirvana. Coming to the place of the good man seeking emptiness, it spreads its seat and preaches the Dharma. Amid the great assembly, its form suddenly becomes empty &#8211; none see it &#8211; then from empty space suddenly emerging, existing and vanishing at will. Or manifesting its body transparent as crystal; or stretching its limbs, emitting sandalwood fragrance; or excrement and urine like rock candy. Slandering the moral code, slighting the left-home.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>That person, never aware of being demon-possessed, likewise claiming to have attained unsurpassed nirvana. Coming to the place of the one seeking empty stillness</strong> &#8211; coming to the good man craving spiritual powers &#8211; <strong>spreading its seat, preaching the Dharma.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Amid the great assembly, its form suddenly becomes empty &#8211; none see it</strong>.</p>
<p>Demonic power makes the possessed person's body suddenly vanish &#8211; seated there preaching the Dharma, suddenly no one sees it anymore. This is a kind of &#8220;<em>people empty,</em> <em>dharmas empty.</em>&#8221; People and dharmas both empty. <strong>Then from empty space suddenly emerging, existing and vanishing at will.</strong></p>
<p>Then minutes later, demonic power making the possessed person's body suddenly vanish &#8211; seated there preaching the Dharma, suddenly it reappears: appearing and vanishing at will &#8211; appearing if it wishes to appear, vanishing if it wishes to vanish, all controlled at its will.</p>
<p><strong>Or manifesting its body transparent as crystal</strong> &#8211; so crystalline you can see right through its body. <strong>Stretching its limbs, emitting sandalwood fragrance</strong>. <strong>Or excrement and urine like rock candy. It slanders the moral code</strong>, saying: &#8220;Do not bother keeping the precepts &#8211; that is Small Vehicle business; why should you keep them? The precepts matter nothing at all.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>Slighting the left-home</strong>: it says: &#8220;No need to leave home. If you wish to cultivate, just cultivate &#8211; why must you leave home to cultivate? What difference between laypeople and the left-home?&#8221; That is how it slights the left-home estate.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Khẩu trung thường thuyết vô nhân vô quả. Nhất tử vĩnh diệt, vô phục hậu thân, cập chư phàm thánh, tuy đắc không tịch, tiềm hành tham dục. Thọ kì dục giả, diệc đắc không tâm, bát vô nhân quả.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Its mouth constantly says there is no cause and no effect: at death eternally extinguished, no further body after, nor any ordinary or holy &#8211; though having attained empty stillness, secretly practicing greed and lust. Those receiving its lust likewise gain the empty mind, denying cause and effect.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>It constantly says there is no cause and effect</strong> &#8211; &#8220;Do not believe in the law of cause and effect,&#8221; it says: &#8220;That is utterly mistaken &#8211; never any such thing.&#8221; It says: <strong>at death eternally extinguished</strong> &#8211; &#8220;Once dead, one never returns &#8211; like a lamp utterly gone out.&#8221;</p>
<p>It says: <strong>no afterlife, no ordinary people or sages</strong> &#8211; all mere words. Al<strong>though having attained empty stillness, they secretly practice lustful acts</strong> &#8211; though having realized some principles of <em>emptiness</em>, they secretly commit lust. <strong>Those receiving its lust likewise gain the empty mind, denying cause and effect</strong>: feeling no need to believe in the law of cause and effect anymore.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thử danh nhật nguyệt, bạc thực tinh khí, kim ngọc chi thảo, lân phượng quy hạc. Kinh thiên vạn niên, bất tử vi linh, xuất sanh quốc độ, niên lão thành ma, não loạn thị nhân. Yếm túc tâm sanh, khứ bỉ nhân thể. Đệ tử dữ sư, đa hãm vương nạn.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is called the essences of sun and moon in eclipse gathered into gold and jade, healing herbs, unicorns, phoenixes, turtles, cranes &#8211; passing thousands upon myriads of years undying, become spirits, born in this land, aged into demons, harassing this person. Sated, they arise and leave that person's body. Disciple and teacher mostly fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>This is the essences gathered from solar and lunar eclipses into gold and jade, rare healing herbs, unicorns, phoenixes, turtles, cranes.</strong></p>
<p>What kind of ghosts are these? Ghosts formed from essences of solar and lunar eclipses: when moon and sun meet in conjunction &#8211; called solar and lunar eclipse phenomena &#8211; streams of essence spread across the earth, gathering into gold and jade, a few kinds of rare healing herbs, unicorns, phoenixes, turtles, cranes. <strong>These essences keep those beings alive thousands upon myriads of years undying, at last become spirits, born in this land, aged into ghosts</strong>. They <strong>harass cultivators</strong> cultivating right samadhi. But at last, <strong>sated, no longer possessing people &#8211; ceasing to possess people. Teacher and disciple both fall into legal peril</strong> &#8211; prosecuted by law.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đương tiên giác, bất nhập luân hồi. Mê hoặc bất tri, đọa vô gián ngục.     </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel. Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>You should know this matter beforehand, lest you enter</strong> the demon king's <strong>turning wheel</strong>. <strong>Deluded and unknowing.</strong> Failing to awaken to this principle, one <strong>falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong><em>10 &#8211; GREED FOR LONG LIFE</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu thiện nam tử, thọ ấm hư diệu, bất tao tà lự, viên định phát minh. Tam-ma-địa trung, tâm ái trường thọ, tân khổ nghiên cơ, tham cầu vĩnh diệt, khí phân đoạn sanh. Đốn hi biến dị, tế tướng thường trụ.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed. Within samadhi the mind fond of long life &#8211; bitterly studying the subtle, greedily seeking immortality, discarding sectional birth-and-death, abruptly hoping the subtle transforming marks ever abide.</strong></p>
