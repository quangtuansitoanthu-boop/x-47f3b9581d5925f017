Asuras are beings who put their contentious nature to useful service in the army and in defending their country. Asuras are those who channel their contentious nature in a bad direction, leading to such things as robbery and gangsterism. Asuras may dwell among the gods, among humans, among animals, or as ghosts. Sometimes asuras are counted among the three wholesome paths — gods, asuras, and humans. Sometimes they are ranked among the four evil destinies — hells, hungry ghosts, animals, and asuras. When asuras are grouped together with gods, humans, asuras, animals, hungry ghosts, and hells, that grouping follows the turning wheel of birth and death in the six paths.

The Buddhas' realm is above Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, so the Bodhisattva says, "Above, I unite with the compassionate minds of the Buddhas." The beings of the six paths are in a realm lower than Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, so the Bodhisattva says, "Below, I unite with the beings of the six paths." "Living beings" are defined as those born from many causes and conditions. Many factors are involved in the birth of a living being. The Bodhisattva continues: Together with living beings I share the same compassionate ngưỡng. Living beings all thirst for and look up to the pity of the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas.

Sutra text: 世尊由我供養觀音如來。蒙彼如來授我如幻聞薰聞修金剛三昧。與佛如來同慈力故。令我身成三十二應入諸國土。

Transliteration: Thế tôn, do ngã cúng dường Quán Âm Như Lai. Mông bỉ Như Lai thọ ngã Như huyễn văn huân văn tu kim cang tam-muội. Dữ Phật Như Lai đồng từ lực cố. Linh ngã thân thành tam thập nhị ứng, nhập chư quốc độ.

Translation: World Honored One, through my making offerings to the Tathagata Avalokiteshvara, I received from that Tathagata the Vajra samadhi of illusory hearing-perfumation and hearing-cultivation. Because I share the same compassionate power as the Buddhas, the Tathagatas, my body has perfected thirty-two response bodies, and I can enter all lands.

Commentary: World Honored One, through my making offerings to the Tathagata Avalokiteshvara, I received from that Tathagata the Vajra samadhi of illusory hearing-perfumation and hearing-cultivation. "Illusory" is spoken of because one cultivates yet does not cultivate, does not cultivate yet cultivates. It means the cultivator always knows what is happening at all times and never forgets. Yet, though never forgetting, the cultivator does not actually remember them either. He does not think of them, yet neither does he forget them.

"Hearing-perfumation" means that daily, the Bodhisattva constantly cultivated the dharma-door of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature until this effort thoroughly permeated him. This is the Vajra samadhi dharma. When the cultivator develops this samadhi power, the Vajra samadhi is perfected.

Because I share the same compassionate power as the Buddhas, the Tathagatas, my body has perfected thirty-two response bodies, and I can enter all lands. I have obtained compassion-power the same as the Buddhas, which enables me to have thirty-two response bodies. Thus I can go to all lands to teach living beings.

Sutra text: 世尊若諸菩薩入三摩地。進修無漏勝解現圓。我現佛身而為說法令其解脫。

Transliteration: Thế tôn, nhược chư Bồ-tát nhập tam-ma-địa, tấn tu vô lậu, thắng giải hiện viên, ngã hiện Phật thân nhi vị thuyết pháp, linh kỳ giải thoát.

Translation: World Honored One, if there are Bodhisattvas who enter samadhi, advance in cultivation of the unconditioned, have attained superior understanding and manifest perfection, I appear before them in a Buddha's body, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to attain liberation.
