When you meet this kind, they are most clever; but in fact, they are deviantly false. In the Chinese "Nature of Medicine," a kind of herb is mentioned having this quality. But in fact, that essence is not real. The middle grade becomes demons and sprites — a kind of strange person with spiritual powers, often harming people. The lowest grade becomes deviant people possessed by sprites.

Recall that kumbhanda ghosts are a kind of mei ghost-spirit, often causing stupor and paralysis in sleeping people. Such ghosts seize the bodies of waking people, then control those people's bodies, mouths, and minds to do private deeds for them. They speak through the person and take complete control of the person. People possessed so are also called mediums, or sometimes they become sorcerers or exorcists. In America, I met such a person — an American who said he was Jesus. A minute later, he said God had entered him telling him to speak. Then a while later he said God had come and wanted to talk with him. Some five years later, he came back to see me. I scolded him: "You do not even recognize yourself. You are entirely a demon, and you are no good." So he left. He had come to speak Dharma with me, but he never returned after I scolded him. And I thought to myself: "I do not understand how to speak to people. Why did I drive away that Jesus-God fellow?"

Anyway, that is an example of the possessed deviant kind. Why do they incur such retribution? Because in past lives, they stole. So they are compelled to fall into one of these grades.

Sometimes in China, such mediums are most fascinating. They can stick a knife into the crown of the head without dying. The mei ghost possessing them removes the blade using a mantra, so the person sheds not a drop of blood. Some can drive nails into their shoulders; from the nails they hang swords each weighing more than ten pounds. They can hang four such swords and whirl them around. It looks most terrifying. Everyone is horrified. Sometimes they truly have skill. I have met many such mei ghosts and outsiders' retinues. When you study the Shurangama Sutra, you will see that long ago the Buddha described every kind of being in the world most clearly. Thus, having heard the Shurangama Sutra explained, you will recognize whatever you must guard against.

This chapter is called the "Four Clear and Unalterable Expositions." It is a most important part of this sutra. So pay the utmost attention. If we do not stop stealing, we shall find it most hard to become Buddhas. Yet many people still hope to become Buddhas. Now that we understand this teaching, those who have committed stealing should change. Those without this fault should not let stealing thoughts arise. That is the best way to accord with the Way.

Sutra text: 彼等群邪亦有徒眾。各各自謂成無上道。

Transliteration: Bỉ đẳng quần tà diệc hữu đồ chúng. Các các tự vị thành vô thượng đạo.

Translation: Those groups of deviants also have retinues. They all claim to have perfected the unsurpassed Way.

Commentary: Those groups of deviants are demons, ghosts, goblins, and sprites harming people. They also have retinues.
