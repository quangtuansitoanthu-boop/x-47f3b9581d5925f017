Though some have used these expedients to abandon worldly toil, afflictions, and false thoughts, that is not the only method everyone can apply. When speaking Dharma, one must regard causes and conditions. One must regard each person's potentials. For the wise, one must speak the profound, sublime Dharma. For those lacking wisdom, one should explain simple principles. Dharma can never always be spoken in the same way. Only the ear root is the most perfectly-penetrating dharma-door for every root of cultivation. I consider this dharma-door the most perfectly penetrating.

Sutra text: 頂禮如來藏 無漏不思議 　願加被未來 於此門無惑 　方便易成就 堪以教阿難 　及末劫沈淪 但以此根修 　圓通超餘者 真實心如是。

Transliteration: Đảnh lễ Như Lai tạng Vô lậu bất tư nghị Nguyện gia bị vị lai Ư thử môn vô hoặc Phương tiện dị thành tựu Kham dĩ giáo A-nan Cập mạt kiếp trầm luân Đãn dĩ thử căn tu Viên thông siêu dư giả Chơn thật tâm như thị.

Translation: I bow to the Tathagatagarbha, The unconditioned, inconceivable; I vow to aid the future, That in this door there be no delusion. The expedient easy to perfect Is fit to teach Ananda And the submerged of the final kalpa: Just cultivate with this root; Perfect penetration surpasses all others. The true mind is just so.

Commentary: After Manjushri Bodhisattva finished presenting this verse, he reverently bowed to the Triple Jewel.
I bow to the Tathagatagarbha.
"Tathagata" is the Buddhas; "garbha" (treasury) is the Tripitaka — that is, the Dharma.
The unconditioned, inconceivable.
"And the unconditioned ones" is the Sangha. The unconditioned Bodhisattvas and Arhats have all certified to inconceivable states. Their spiritual powers and sublime functions are hard to conceive. Manjushri Bodhisattva bows to the sages of the Sangha.

I vow to aid the future,
That in this door there be no delusion.
I vow to ask them to aid all beings in future lives, that they have not a shred of doubt about the transliteration-door of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature.
The expedient easy to perfect
Is fit to teach Ananda.

This method is both easy to cultivate and easy to perfect. It is the best dharma-door for Ananda to cultivate. There is no better dharma-door.
And the submerged of the final kalpa:
Just cultivate with this root.
This dharma-door suits not only Ananda, but all potentials of beings in the Dharma-Ending Age. Just use the ear root as the means of cultivation.
Perfect penetration surpasses all others.
The true mind is just so.
This perfect-penetration cultivation surpasses the other twenty-five expedient dharma-doors. Easier, and bringing surpassing results. The true mind attained through this cultivation is as I have explained above.

Sutra text: 於是阿難及諸大眾,身心了然得大開示。觀佛菩提及大涅槃猶如有人因事遠遊未得歸還。明了其家所歸道路。

Transliteration: Ư thị A-nan cập chư đại chúng, thân tâm liễu nhiên, đắc đại khai thị. Quán Phật bồ-đề cập đại niết-bàn du như hữu nhân nhân sự viễn du vị đắc quy hoàn. Minh liễu kỳ gia sở quy đạo lộ.

Translation: Thereupon Ananda and the great assembly, body and mind all-clear, received the great instruction. They regarded the Buddhas' bodhi and great nirvana like a person who, traveling far on business, has not yet returned home, but clearly knows the road home.
