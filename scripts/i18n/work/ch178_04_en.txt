TITLE: 03. THE KARMIC RETRIBUTION OF THE TRAN BROTHERS
TOMTAT: I have a schoolmate named Tran, a soldier. Every time he came home on leave, he and his younger brother would go fishing, hunt animals in the hills, or shoot birds—all taken as mere pastimes. Later, calamities struck his family one after another...
-----
{align:justify|I have a schoolmate named Tran, a soldier. Every time he came home on leave, he and his younger brother would go fishing, hunt animals in the hills, or shoot birds—all taken as mere pastimes.}
{align:justify|Later, calamities struck his family one after another. First, his younger brother, while working on a construction site, carelessly fell from the second-floor scaffolding. Scattered steel bars lay strewn all over the site below; the young man was pierced through the belly, his intestines gravely wounded, and he died not long after. Then Tran himself was tormented by wretched sickness and racking pain. To top it all, over some trifle he quarreled fiercely with his wife until they divorced. A family once happy and complete was thus torn apart, leaving all who hear it sighing with regret.}
