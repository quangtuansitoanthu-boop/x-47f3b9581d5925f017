Commentary: Thereupon Ananda and the great assembly, body and mind all-clear. They attained thorough understanding. Receiving the profound instruction. Regarding the Buddhas' bodhi and great nirvana.
They fully recognized the Buddha's and Manjushri Bodhisattva's profound, sublime teaching. They regarded the teaching on bodhi-nirvana like a person who, traveling far on business, has not yet returned home, but clearly knows the road home.
These people have gone far on business and have not yet returned home. But they have gone far enough to still remember the road home. The meaning is that although Ananda and the assembly members have not yet certified to the first, second, or third Arhat fruitions, now at least they have recognized the principle.

Sutra text: 普會大眾天龍八部有學二乘,及諸一切新發心菩薩,其數凡有十恒河沙皆得本心,遠塵離垢獲法眼淨。

Transliteration: Phổ hội đại chúng thiên long bát bộ hữu học nhị thừa, cập chư nhất thiết tân phát tâm Bồ-tát, kỳ số phàm hữu thập hằng hà sa giai đắc bổn tâm, viễn trần ly cấu hoạch pháp nhãn tịnh.

Translation: The entire assembly — gods, dragons, and the eight divisions, learners of the Two Vehicles, and all newly-resolved Bodhisattvas, ten Ganges' sands in number — all attained their original minds, left dust and defilement far behind, and gained the pure Dharma eye.

Commentary: The entire assembly — gods, dragons, and the eight divisions.
Ghosts and spirits; the eight divisions comprise gandharvas, asuras, garudas, kinnaras, mahoragas, humans, non-humans, dragons.
Learners of the Two Vehicles — referring to Hearers awakened to the principle of Conditioned Origination. Together with all newly-resolved Bodhisattvas, ten Ganges' sands in number, they all attained their original minds. Unlike before, when they had not recognized their original minds. They left dust-marks far behind; thus they gained the pure Dharma eye.

Sutra text: 性比丘尼聞說偈已成阿羅漢。

Transliteration: Tánh tỷ-khưu-ni văn thuyết kệ dĩ thành A-la-hán.

Translation: The bhikshuni Xing, hearing the verse spoken, became an Arhat.

Commentary: The bhikshuni named Xing was Matanga's daughter. She certified to Arhatship right after hearing Manjushri speak this verse. By then, she had surpassed Ananda, for she had certified to the second Arhat fruition, while Ananda had only certified to the first fruition.

Sutra text: 無量眾生皆發無等等阿耨多羅三藐三菩提心

Transliteration: Vô lượng chúng sanh giai phát vô đẳng đẳng A-nậu-đa-la Tam-miệu tam-bồ-đề tâm.

Translation: Countless beings all brought forth the resolve for unsurpassed, equal-and-unequal anuttara-samyak-sambodhi.

Commentary: By this time, countless immeasurable beings in the Dharma assembly brought forth the resolve for unsurpassed, equal-and-unequal anuttara-samyak-sambodhi.
Nothing can compare with the degree of this resolve. It is utterly true. Anuttara-samyak-sambodhi means unsurpassed, right and equal, right enlightenment.
Anuttara means unsurpassed, with nothing comparable.
Samyak means right (proper) and equal. Sambodhi is right enlightenment, true enlightenment. Their vow is to become Buddhas.
