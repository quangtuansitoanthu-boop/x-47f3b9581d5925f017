{align:justify|At my workplace there was a colleague very fond of shooting birds with a slingshot. His aim was remarkably precise—hardly ever missing. Every shot struck a vital spot, wounding the bird in the head. Once he took aim, not a single one escaped.}
{align:justify|For years everyone admired this talent of his, never sparing words of amazement and praise.}
{align:justify|Last year, while our unit was repairing a construction project, the crane hook on a truck suddenly snapped and fell from high above, smashing straight onto the head of a worker below. The heavy iron hook crushed the victim's skull like a split watermelon, brains splattering and blood pooling on the ground. All who witnessed it were horrified and dared not look. The victim turned out to be none other than that marksman colleague.}
