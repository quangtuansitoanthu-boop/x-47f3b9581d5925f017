<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE FORM SKANDHA</strong></p>


<ol>
<li style="text-align:center"><strong>THE PURE MENTAL STATE BEFORE ENTERING THE REALM OF THE FORM SKANDHA</strong></li>

<ol>

<p><strong>Sutra text: A-nan đương tri, nhữ tọa đạo tràng, tiêu lạc chư niệm. Kì niệm nhược tận. Tắc chư li niệm nhất thiết tinh minh. Động tĩnh bất di, ức vong như nhất.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Ananda should know: sitting in the Way-place, you must dissolve all thoughts. When thoughts are exhausted, then apart from all thought there is utter purity and brightness. Still and moving unchanging, remembering and forgetting are as one.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary: Ananda should know</strong>, <strong>when you sit in the Way-place, you must dissolve all false thoughts</strong>. Ananda, you should know: you are one cultivating the Dharma of <em>returning the hearing to hear the self-nature</em> (turning the hearing inward to recognize your own nature).</p>
<p>With such effort you have dissolved all false thoughts. When false thoughts are entirely exhausted, no more discriminating thoughts remain in your mind. <strong>The pure, bright state</strong>, full of wisdom and samadhi power, <strong>will dwell within you</strong>. When <strong>still and moving unchanging, remembering and forgetting are as one</strong>, your mind does not change &#8211; whether you remember or forget that state, it is all the same, without difference.</p>


<p style="text-align:center">THE SCOPE OF THE FORM SKANDHA</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Đương trụ thử xứ, nhập tam-ma-đề, như minh mục nhân xử đại u ám. Tinh tính diệu tịnh, tâm vị phát quang. Thử tắc danh vi sắc ấm khu vũ.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Dwelling in this state and entering samadhi, it is like a clear-eyed person dwelling in great darkness. Though the essential nature is wondrously pure, the mind has not yet emitted light. This is called the region of the form skandha.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Dwelling in this state and entering samadhi</strong>.</p>
<p>Ananda should know: when cultivating this samadhi, dwelling in the pure state where still and moving do not change and remembering and forgetting are as one &#8211; <strong>like a clear-eyed person</strong> recognizing himself <strong>dwelling in great darkness</strong>: like one with bright eyes living in a dark house &#8211; though the inherent nature is purely bright and wondrous, the mind has not yet emitted the illumination of <strong>wisdom</strong> &#8211; <strong>though the essential nature is purely wondrous, the mind has not yet</strong> given forth the shining of true <strong>bright</strong> <strong>wisdom</strong>. Then <strong>this is called the region of the form skandha.</strong></p>


<p style="text-align:center">TRANSCENDING THE FORM SKANDHA: SEEING ITS ROOT</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhược mục minh lãng, thập phương đỗng khai, vô phục u ám, danh sắc âm tận. Thị nhân tắc năng siêu việt kiếp trược. Quán kì sở do, kiên cố vọng tưởng dĩ vi kì bản.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: If the eyes become bright and clear, the ten directions open through, and darkness no more remains, this is called the exhaustion of the form skandha. Such a person can then transcend the defilement of eons. Contemplate its cause: solid false thought is its root.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>If the eyes become bright and clear, the ten directions open through, and darkness no more remains.</strong> What is this state called? <strong>It is called the exhaustion of the form skandha</strong>: among the five skandhas &#8211; form, feeling, thinking, activity, consciousness &#8211; the form skandha has been dissolved. <strong>Such a person can then transcend the defilement of eons</strong>. <strong>Contemplate its cause: solid false thought is its root</strong>. When the cultivator transcends the defilement of eons, he sees that all his conduct issued from solid false thought (3).</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE TEN DISTINCTIVE STATES OF THE FORM SKANDHA</strong></p>

<p style="text-align:center"><strong>(IF DELUDED AND UNABLE TO TRANSCEND THE FORM SKANDHA)</strong></p>
<p><strong><em> </em></strong></p>

<ol>
<li><strong> THE MIND FREED FROM ITS OBSTRUCTION</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Sutra text: A-nan, đương tại thử trung, tinh nghiên diệu minh, tứ đại bất chức. Thiểu tuyển chi gian, thân năng xuất ngại. Thử danh tinh minh lưu dật tiền cảnh. Tư đản công dụng tạm đắc như thị, phi vi thánh chứng. Bất tác thánh tâm, danh thiện cảnh giới. Nhược tác thánh giải tức thọ quần tà.  </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Ananda, right within this state, intently investigating the wondrous brightness, the four elements no longer intertwine. In a brief moment the body can transcend obstructions. This is called the pure brightness overflowing into the external environment. It is merely a temporary state gained through effort &#8211; not sage realization. If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a good state; but if you interpret it as sagehood, you fall among the deviant.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: We shall now take up the connection between demonic occurrences and the five aggregates (skandhas): form, feeling, thinking, activity, consciousness. There are ten kinds of demons in each skandha, making fifty in all &#8211; the <em>Fifty Skandha-Demon Phenomena</em>. The cultivator must have a clear understanding of these <em>Fifty Skandha-Demon Phenomena</em>. If you do not clearly know these phenomena, you will easily end your wisdom-life among Mara's retinue without knowing why you fell therein. That is why you must be extremely cautious in cultivation.</p>
<p><strong>Ananda</strong>, <strong>right within this samadhi</strong>, <strong>intently investigating the wondrous bright nature</strong> &#8211; when the form skandha is about to dissolve, you will see the ten directions empty and vast and sense a kind of light, because you have applied effort investigating the subtle, wondrous, bright state.</p>
<p><strong>The four elements no longer intertwine</strong>; <strong>in a brief moment</strong> the <strong>body</strong> <strong>can</strong> <strong>transcend all obstructions</strong>.</p>
<p>The four elements no longer combine with one another and no longer obstruct. Before long the body becomes unobstructed, empty and vast as space. This is called the <em>mind-made body</em> &#8211; another body separate from the physical body (manifesting at will, unobstructed by matter).</p>
<p>I once told the story of my disciple who visited the heavens in his Dharma-body; that body is also called a <em>mind-made body</em>. <em>Mind</em> here relates to consciousness &#8211; one of the six consciousnesses: eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mind. Just as the physical body is made by mental consciousness, so too can one transcend the obstructions of the physical body compounded of the four elements.</p>
<p><strong>This state is called <em>pure brightness overflowing into the external environment</em></strong>: the wondrous bright nature also overflows into outer realms (4).</p>
<p><strong>It is merely a temporary state gained through effort</strong>.</p>
<p>This realm comes only from effort in cultivation &#8211; it is not permanent, only incidental. Indeed, at any time one may enter or leave that state at will: wishing to go somewhere, one goes; wishing to return, one returns &#8211; completely at ease:</p>
