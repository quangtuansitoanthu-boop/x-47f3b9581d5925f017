<p>The Buddha replied: &#8220;Go back and tell your soldiers to recite the Prajna line, &#8216;Maha Prajna Paramita.&#8217; I guarantee everything will turn out well.&#8221; Shakra returned and did as instructed, and the asuras surrendered unconditionally. That is how Shakra won the battle &#8211; but the asuras still constantly contend for rank and authority.</p>

<p><strong>Ananda, there is another group of inferior asuras, born in the heart of the great sea, diving beneath the waters' caves. By day they roam in empty space; by night they return to sleep in the waters. Such asuras are moisture-born, belonging to the animal destiny.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda, there is another group of inferior asuras, born in the heart of the great sea, diving beneath the waters' caves.&#8221; These asuras belong to the animal category &#8211; dragons and such are of this kind. Dwelling in thoughts of water and the sea, they are finally reborn in it.</p>
<p>&#8220;By day they roam in empty space; by night they return to sleep in the waters.&#8221; They return to their underwater caves at night. &#8220;Such asuras are moisture-born, belonging to the animal destiny.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>A-nan! Như thị địa ngục, ngạ quỷ súc sanh, nhân cập thần tiên. Thiên kịp tu la, tinh nghiên thất thú. Giai thị hôn trầm, chư hữu vi tưởng. Vọng tưởng thọ sanh, vọng tưởng tùy nghiệp. Ư diệu Viên Minh, vô tác bản tâm. Giai như không hoa, nguyên vô sở hữu. Đãn nhất hư vọng, cánh vô căn tự.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda! The seven destinies above &#8211; hells, hungry ghosts, animals, humans, spirit-immortals, gods, and asuras &#8211; are all dark, conditioned appearances: born of false thought, following karma into rebirth. In truth they are all like flowers in space, falsely appearing within the wondrous, bright, uncreated, inherent mind, originally having nothing at all &#8211; only a single falseness, with no root at all.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda! The seven destinies above &#8211; hells, hungry ghosts, animals, humans, spirit-immortals, gods, and asuras.&#8221; These still have form-bodies. They &#8220;are all dark, conditioned appearances: born of false thought, following karma into rebirth,&#8221; and false thought will carry them into the next birth-and-death realm. &#8220;In truth they are all like flowers in space, falsely appearing within the wondrous, bright, uncreated, inherent mind, originally having nothing at all.&#8221; The seven kinds of beings are like flowers in space, with nowhere to rest. They are &#8220;only a single falseness, with no root at all.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>A-nan! Thử đẳng chúng sanh. Bất thức bản tâm, thọ thử Luân-hồi. Kinh vô lượng kiếp, bất đắc chân tịnh. Giai do tùy thuận, sát đạo dâm cố. Phản thử tam chủng, hựu tắc xuất sanh, vô sát đạo dâm. Hữu danh quỷ luân, vô danh thiên thú. Hữu vô tướng khuynh, khởi Luân-hồi tánh.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda! These beings, not recognizing the inherent mind, undergo this turning wheel through measureless eons without attaining true purity &#8211; all because they comply with the three acts of killing, stealing, and lust; or, reversing these three, they produce non-killing, non-stealing, non-lust. Falsely seeing &#8220;existence,&#8221; one belongs to the ghost wheel; falsely seeing &#8220;nonexistence,&#8221; one belongs to the heavenly destiny. Existence and nonexistence toppling into each other give rise to the nature of the turning wheel.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda, the beings of the seven destinies, not recognizing the inherent mind, must undergo the turning wheel through measureless eons.&#8221; They do not know the true nature inherently present without any doing, and so keep passing through the six paths of rebirth: born, then dying; dead, then reborn. In one life they are oxen or cows, in the next horses, and perhaps in a life after that they return as humans. That is the turning wheel &#8211; and for how long does it continue? There is no way to count how many eons they must pass through thus.</p>
<p>They &#8220;do not attain true purity&#8221; &#8211; they never open the true mind and inherent nature. Why? &#8220;All because they comply with the three acts of killing, stealing, and lust.&#8221; They sink into them and cannot stop. Or else, reversing these three evil acts, they are born into non-killing, non-stealing, and non-lust. If they commit killing, stealing, and lust, they fall into the ghost realm, where their friends and kin are also ghosts. If they escape these evil acts, they are reborn as gods. The successive turning between the presence and absence of karma gives rise to the nature of the turning wheel: the constant struggle between creating karma and not creating it goes on and on &#8211; at times they create evil karma, at times they do not. That is the nature of the turning wheel.</p>
<p><strong>Nhược đắc diệu phát, tam ma đề giả, tức diệu thường tịch. Hữu vô nhị vô, vô nhị diệc diệt. Thượng vô bất sát, bất thâu bất dâm. Vân hà cánh tùy, sát đạo dâm sự.</strong></p>
<p><strong>If one attains the wondrous samadhi, the wondrous mind is ever still; existence and nonexistence are both gone, and even non-duality is extinguished, so that even non-killing, non-stealing, and non-lust do not exist &#8211; how much less complying with killing, stealing, and lust!</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: For those who have awakened to the samadhi Dharma, the wondrous mind is ever still, and existence and nonexistence are non-dual. Some among the seven kinds of beings can awaken to the samadhi Dharma &#8211; the Great Shurangama Samadhi: turning back to the hearing-nature, turning the light around to illumine within, until the inherent nature reaches the right path. They cultivate the perfect penetration of the ear organ and attain samadhi power and genuine wisdom. If all three evil acts are present, they must pass through the three lower realms of hells, ghosts, and animals; if the three evil acts are absent, they may be reborn as humans, sages, immortals, or asuras. But in this realm, karma neither exists nor vanishes: &#8220;even non-duality is extinguished&#8221; &#8211; the absence that divides the three evil acts and the four paths also vanishes. &#8220;Even non-killing, non-stealing, and non-lust do not exist &#8211; how much less complying with killing, stealing, and lust!&#8221; If fundamentally there is nothing, how could there be participation in evil acts? How could evil karma be created? It cannot.</p>
<p><strong>A-nan! bất đoạn tam nghiệp, các các hữu tư. Nhân các các tư, chúng tư đồng phần. Phi vô định xứ, tự vọng phát sanh. Sanh vọng vô nhân, vô khả tầm cứu.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda, if the three acts are not ended, each being has its own private karma, and because of each one's private karma, beings share a common portion &#8211; it is not that there is no fixed place. It is falseness arising of itself: falseness born without cause, whose origin cannot be traced.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda, if the three acts are not ended, each being has its own private karma.&#8221; Beings who do not end the three acts of killing, stealing, and lust will bear their own private karma. &#8220;Because of each one's private karma, beings share a common portion &#8211; it is not that there is no fixed place.&#8221; When beings create their own private karmic obstructions, they must undergo a shared retribution &#8211; this is called collective karma. It is no coincidence, no arbitrary circumstance. Yet however exactly things happen, that exactness is the result of ignorance. The origin is beginningless, so the sutra says: although they &#8211; private and collective karma &#8211; arise from self-created false karma, falseness fundamentally has no cause. Falseness has no form and no source; its root is unreal and untrue. It &#8220;cannot be traced to its origin.&#8221; You may search but you will not find its source: it is created from falseness and extinguishes of itself. You may try to seek its origin, but it does not exist &#8211; it is like a shadow, and you will not succeed in finding the shadow's source. You might say its source is the form-body, but the shadow is still not its form-body; the shadow is only an illusion.</p>
