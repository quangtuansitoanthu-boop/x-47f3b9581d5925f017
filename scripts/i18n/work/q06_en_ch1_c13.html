Translation: All activities are impermanent; The nature of mindfulness is within birth and death. Cause and effect are now vastly different — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Mahasthamaprapta Bodhisattva cultivated the Seeing element. He gathered in all six sense roots without selection, with pure mindfulness in succession.
All activities are impermanent.
Mindfulness is by nature within birth and death.
Both the nature of the mindfulness and the object of mindfulness belong to birth and death. When you recite, it appears; when you stop reciting, it vanishes.
Cause and effect are now vastly different.
We hope to transcend the idea of cause and effect.
How can one attain perfect penetration?
So the Buddha-recitation dharma-door is also unsuitable for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 我今白世尊 佛出娑婆界 此方真教體 清淨在音聞 欲取三摩提　　 實以聞中入。

Transliteration: Ngã kim bạch Thế tôn Phật xuất ta-bà giới Thử phương chơn giáo thể Thanh tịnh tại âm văn Dục thủ tam ma đề Thật dĩ văn trung nhập.

Translation: I now address the World Honored One: The Buddha appears in the Saha world; The true teaching-substance of this land Dwells purely in sound-hearing. Wishing to attain samadhi, One truly enters through hearing.

Commentary: At this point, Manjushri Bodhisattva has finished presenting the dharma-doors the sages certified to. He continues:
I now address the World Honored One.
The Buddha appears in the Saha world.
The true teaching-substance of this land
Dwells purely in sound.
In this world called "endurance," where the Buddha has manifested out of compassion, the true method for teaching beings is the purity of sound. That refers to the dharma-door of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature.
Wishing to attain samadhi,
One truly enters through hearing.
The effort of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature is truly the most sublime, the best of all dharma-doors.

Sutra text: 離苦得解脫 良哉觀世音

Transliteration: Ly khổ đắc giải thoát Lương tai Quán Thế Âm

Translation: Leaving suffering, attaining liberation — Excellent, O Avalokiteshvara!

Commentary: Leaving suffering, attaining liberation. We can leave suffering far behind and attain liberation. Leaving suffering is liberation. Liberation is precisely leaving suffering far behind. Excellent, O Avalokiteshvara! Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's dharma-door is truly sublime!

Sutra text: 於恒沙劫中 入微塵佛國 得大自在力 無畏施眾生。

Transliteration: Ư hằng sa kiếp trung Nhập vi trần phật quốc Đắc đại tự tại lực Vô uý thí chúng sanh.

Translation: Through kalpas as numerous as Ganges' sands, Entering Buddha-lands as numerous as dust motes, Attaining the power of great ease, Bestowing fearlessness upon beings.

Commentary: Why does Manjushri Bodhisattva praise Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva? Through kalpas as numerous as Ganges' sands, entering Buddha-lands as numerous as dust motes. Through many kalpas, Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva entered countless Buddha-lands as numerous as dust motes. Attaining the power of great ease.
Bestowing fearlessness upon beings. With spiritual powers and great ease, Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva enabled all beings to be without fear.
