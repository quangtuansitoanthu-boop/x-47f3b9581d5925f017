<p><strong>Sutra text: Thử danh trụ thế tự tại thiên ma, sử kì quyến thuộc, như giá- văn-trà cập Tứ thiên vương, Tì xá đồng tử, vị phát tâm giả, lợi kì hư minh, thực bỉ tinh khí. Hoặc bất nhân sư, kì tu hành nhân, thân tự quan kiến, xưng chấp kim cương, dữ nhữ trường mệnh. Hiện mĩ nữ thân, thạnh hành tham dục. Vị du niên tuế, can não khô kiệt,  khẩu kiêm độc ngôn, thính nhược yêu  mị. Tiền nhân vị tường, đa hãm vương nạn. Vị cập  ngộ hình, tiên dĩ càn tử. Não loạn bỉ nhân, dĩ chí tồ vẫn.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is called the world-dwelling self-existent heavenly demon, sending such unawakened followers as chamunda ghosts and pishacha youths of the Four Heavenly Kings &#8211; exploiting his empty brightness to devour his essence and energy. Or, without a master, the cultivator himself sees them claiming to be vajra-holders granting long life. Manifesting beautiful women's bodies, ardently practicing greed and lust. Within less than a year, liver and brain dried up &#8211; the mouth muttering alone, sounding like goblins and sprites. Outsiders, not knowing the cause, mostly fall into legal peril. Before undergoing punishment, already dead and desiccated. Harassing the cultivator to death itself.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>This is called the world-dwelling self-existent heavenly demon, sending such unawakened followers as chamunda ghosts</strong> &#8211;</p>
<p>Chamunda (Skt. <em>chamunda</em>) means &#8220;<em>pestilence-ghost</em>: <em>slave-ghost</em>&#8221; &#8211; demons and ghosts working like slaves. It also means <em>jealousy-ghost</em> &#8211; ghosts forever jealous of every good thing others attain, always seeking to hinder people wanting to study the Buddhadharma: if someone wishes to become good, they drag him back toward evil. That is a kind of evil demon. The heavenly demon of the world-dwelling self-existent heaven sends such demons to harass the right-samadhi effort of cultivators &#8211; or sends <strong>pishacha youths</strong> from <strong>the Four Heavenly Kings' realm</strong>.</p>
<p>Pishacha ghosts especially love devouring essence and energy &#8211; devouring the essence and energy of plants and vegetation, and people's all the better. When men and women join in lustful acts, a kind of essence-energy is emitted, which they devour &#8211; a great many ghosts waiting nearby to devour this essence-energy: most perilous.</p>
<p><strong>Exploiting his empty brightness, to devour the cultivator's essence and energy.</strong></p>
<p>Exploiting that person's empty brightness, coming to devour the cultivator's essence and energy &#8211; these ghosts, unawakened (that is, dispatched by the heavenly demon) &#8211; like pishacha ghosts and others &#8211; love exploiting the cultivator's empty brightness: loving to devour the cultivator's essence and energy, yet the cultivator knows nothing of it.</p>
<p><strong>Or, without a master (the possessed person), the cultivator himself sees them claiming to be vajra-holders granting long life.</strong></p>
<p>Or, apart from any master (the possessed person), the cultivator sees someone claiming: &#8220;I am a vajra-holding spirit (Dharma protector), come to grant you long life &#8211; now I come to grant you long life; you shall live forever.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>Or they manifest beautiful women's bodies, ardently practicing greed and lust with the cultivator.</strong></p>
<p>Or, having spoken, it turns into a beautiful woman, ardently practicing lust with the cultivator &#8211; both constantly doing so without rest.</p>
<p><strong>Within less than a year, the cultivator's liver and brain dried up; the mouth muttering alone, sounding like goblins and sprites.</strong></p>
<p>Within a year, vitality exhausted by such unbridled lust &#8211; vitality, essence, energy, spirit dried up within a year &#8211; through excess. The most important word is &#8220;<em>ardently practicing.</em>&#8221; The term is hard to convey in ordinary terms &#8211; but surely the woman says: &#8220;The more intoxicated in lust, the longer you live &#8211; soon attaining eternal life.&#8221; Craving boundless lifespan, he does not realize his life is being squeezed out, shortening minute by minute &#8211; within less than a year, life ends.</p>
<p><strong>Muttering alone.</strong> Then its mouth mutters alone &#8211; in fact not talking to itself but talking with demons. <strong>Sounding like goblins and sprites</strong>: talking with demons, though people around see no demons.</p>
<p>I have met this kind of demon before: once I met a man visited by a woman every night &#8211; never visiting by day. But every evening she came, everyone in the apartment heard the clatter of her wooden clogs striking the wooden floor &#8211; hearing her walk, yet seeing no form. Whenever the woman came, he stripped off his clothes and crawled into bed &#8211; doing so regardless of people around. That is an example of this kind of demon.</p>
<p>Later an aged local spirit possessing a healing medium came to exorcise that female demon. That night, when the female demon came, it spoke with that spirit. The demon said: &#8220;Good! You wish to cure this man? Fine &#8211; from now on he shall recover. But from here, I shall come to you &#8211; we shall contest our magic together.&#8221;</p>
<p>Afterward it indeed went to that spirit, no longer visiting the man with whom it had done improper things morning to night.</p>
<p>I have told you many times: this kind of demon and ghost is most formidable.</p>
<p><strong>Outsiders, not knowing the cause, mostly fall into legal peril. Before undergoing punishment, already dead and desiccated. Harassing the cultivator to death itself.</strong></p>
<p>Outsiders cannot understand what happens &#8211; wholly unaware of this condition. In every case, such people fall into legal peril &#8211; the demon's acts arrested by law.</p>
<p>But before undergoing punishment &#8211; before being brought to trial &#8211; dead and desiccated: dying in prison, all essence, energy, and spirit dried up. The demon harasses and enchants the cultivator to death &#8211; destroying the cultivator's samadhi power until the person dies desiccated.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đương tiên giác, bất nhập luân hồi. Mê hoặc bất tri, đọa vô gián ngục.   </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel. Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: Ananda, <strong>you should clearly know this condition</strong> &#8211; thoroughly understand this condition &#8211; <strong>lest you enter the turning wheel</strong>: recognizing these demonic states clearly, you will avoid the heavenly demon's trap &#8211; need not become the demon's household retinue &#8211; but <strong>deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell</strong> &#8211; even the slightest blessing of human rebirth utterly lost.</p>
