<p><em>Released, it fills the six directions;</em></p>
<p><em>Gathered in, it hides within the secret.</em></p>
<p>Released, it fills all heaven and earth; and then one can gather it back at any time. If you cannot do this whenever you wish, then yours is only temporary effort &#8211; a state you will attain when your cultivation matures. Yet this state is only temporary: you will not permanently have a body transcending all obstructions. This is <strong>not the manifestation of sage realization.</strong></p>
<p>You have not yet attained the sage fruition; one who has not attained fruition does not abide in such a state.</p>
<p><strong>If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a good state.</strong> That is, you should not say: &#8220;Oh, I have a little cultivation skill &#8211; I can send my soul out of my body through a point on the crown of my head.&#8221; If you become so self-satisfied, what will happen? It rests entirely with you. However, if you do not regard this state as proof of sage realization, then it is no great obstruction and no grave error.</p>
<p><strong>But once you think you have realized sagehood, you fall into deviant views.</strong></p>
<p>If you think: my cultivation skill is now superb &#8211; how marvelous I am, a world-transcending sage, an Arhat &#8211; demons and ghosts will attack you at once. Once demons come to sabotage, you are the first to fall into the hells.</p>


<ol start="2">
<li><strong> PICKING PARASITES OUT OF THE BODY</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Sutra text: A-nan, phục dĩ thử tâm tinh nghiên diệu minh, kì thân nội triệt. Thị nhân hốt nhiên ư kì thân nội thiệp xuất nhiêu hồi, thân tướng uyển nhiên, diệc vô thương hủy. Thử danh tinh minh lưu dật hình thể. Tư đãn tinh hành tạm đắc như thị, phi vi thánh chứng. Bất tác thánh tâm, danh thiện cảnh giới. Nhược tác thánh giải, tức thọ quần tà.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Further, Ananda, with this mind intently investigating the wondrous brightness, the body becomes thoroughly clear. Suddenly one can pick out roundworms from within one's own body, while the body remains intact and unharmed. This is called pure brightness overflowing into the physical form. This is only a temporary state from vigorous practice &#8211; not sage realization. If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a good state; but if you interpret it as sagehood, you fall among the deviant.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: This is called the second state of the <em>Five Skandha-Demon</em> phenomena. This demonic state appears because you cultivate vigorously; if you are not vigorous in practice, you cannot attract this demonic occurrence &#8211; even if you wished, demons would pay you no heed. In their eyes you are but a pauper, giving them no convenient pretext to harass you.</p>
<p>But now, in the midst of your effort, you have just stored up a little precious wealth &#8211; that is why demons follow you to harass.</p>
<p>So what should you do when they come? You must dwell in the state of thusness, unmoving, wakeful and clear &#8211; unattached, not pursuing the forms appearing outside; completely unattached to everything. Nor should you think: &#8220;That state was so wonderful &#8211; I wish it would appear again.&#8221;</p>
<p>Neither long for it nor chase it away &#8211; simply act as if nothing happened. If you do not regard yourself as a sage, that is a good situation. But if you think you are already a sage and suddenly exclaim:</p>
<p>&#8220;Oh! I am extraordinary &#8211; I can pick every kind of being out of my own body.&#8221;</p>
<p>If you think you have attained free spiritual powers, you are truly deluded: with that single self-satisfied thought, demons slip into your mind and take charge, controlling you until you have lost all samadhi power.</p>
<p>Therefore you must clearly understand the principles of cultivation &#8211; only so can you keep from falling into demonic traps or straying onto deviant paths. If you do not understand the Buddhadharma, you will easily go astray. Have you any skill? That does not matter. But once your effort gains a little samadhi power, the demon kings constantly keep their eyes on you, and given a convenient opening they come to harass you.</p>
<p><strong>Further, Ananda, if one uses this mind to investigate the wondrous bright nature, one will see clearly throughout the body</strong>.</p>
<p>If you diligently persevere in cultivation, in the end you will be able to see everything inside your own body &#8211; that is ordinary, if you wish to see so. But if you can see this realm only for a moment, that is the state of the form skandha.</p>
<p><strong>Suddenly one can pick out roundworms from within one's own body, while the body remains intact and unharmed.</strong></p>
<p>In this state, suddenly you can pick worms and parasites out of your own belly &#8211; all kinds, long and short, big and small. You need only place your hand and draw them out &#8211; the worms drawn out are real, yet the belly cavity is unharmed. How, you ask, are the worms taken out? If you reached your hand into the belly to extract them, the cavity would be injured &#8211; yet the belly is not torn at all; the body remains whole. This case can be explained in two ways:</p>
<p>One reading is: the body remains whole &#8211; that is, the bodies of the worms and parasites remain whole, unharmed in the least, still alive and well. The second reading is: the belly cavity within one's own body remains whole and uninjured.</p>
<p><strong>This state is called<em>: pure brightness overflowing into the physical form.</em></strong></p>
<p>A very transparent light fills your whole body. In fact you do not reach your hand into your belly to pick the worms out &#8211; the worms emerge of themselves.</p>
<p><strong>This is only a temporary state from vigorous practice, not sage realization</strong>: this state is not something that occurs regularly. If this were a constantly occurring realm, the situation would be quite different. This is not the realm of sage fruition.</p>
<p><strong>If you do not think you have realized sagehood, it is a good state.</strong></p>
<p>It will be fine, causing no grave problem. <strong>But if you think you have realized sagehood, you fall into deviant views.</strong></p>
<p>If you think: &#8220;I have just realized the sage fruition &#8211; I can take these worms out of my own stomach. Is that not marvelous? None of you can do this!&#8221;</p>
<p>If you become self-satisfied, clinging to the state just experienced, the demon kings will entice you into becoming one of their retinue.</p>

<li><strong> HEARING VOICES PREACHING THE DHARMA IN SPACE</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu dĩ thử tâm, nội ngoại tinh nghiên. Kì thời hồn phách, ý chí tinh thần, trừ chấp thọ thân, dư giai thiệp nhập, hỗ vi tân chủ. Hốt ư không trung, văn thuyết pháp thanh, hoặc văn thập phương, đồng phu mật nghĩa. Thử danh tinh phách, đệ tương li hợp, thành tựu thiện chủng, tạm đắc như thị, phi vi thánh chứng.
