THE PERFECT PENETRATION OF THE EAR ROOT
Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva


Sutra text: 爾時觀世音菩薩即從座起, 頂禮佛足而白佛言。

Transliteration: Nhĩ thời Quán Thế Âm Bồ-tát tức tùng toà khởi, đảnh lễ Phật túc nhi bạch Phật ngôn:

Translation: At that time Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva rose from his seat, bowed at the Buddha's feet, and said to the Buddha:

Commentary: In the previous section, Mahasthamaprapta Bodhisattva presented the method of cultivating the Buddha-recitation dharma-door. That is a method most suitable for everyone in the present age. That method is most effective. Why? The sutra tells us that in the Dharma-Ending Age, among a million people who cultivate, not even one will become enlightened. Many cultivate, but few attain certification. So what should we do? Do not worry. The sutra teaches, "It is solely through the Buddha-recitation dharma-door that all living beings are taken across to liberation." The Buddha-recitation method is very easy to practice. By practicing Buddha-recitation, we escape the three realms horizontally and are reborn with our karma intact.

What is meant by escaping the three realms horizontally? It is like an insect living inside a bamboo stalk: if it bores its way out lengthwise through the stalk, it must pass through each joint, which takes a very long time. But if that insect knows how to gnaw a hole through the side of the stalk, it can get out of the bamboo tube very easily. People who recite the Buddha's name are like the insect that escapes through the side of the bamboo; they escape the three realms horizontally — right from their own level. "Rebirth with karma" — the karma people carry with them is karma from past lives, not of this life; it is karma already created, not new karma. This means that before you knew of the Buddha-recitation method, you had already created karmic obstructions.

You may carry that karma with you when you are reborn in the Pure Land. But you must not continue creating evil karma once you have learned Buddha-recitation, for you cannot carry that evil karma with you. Once you know how to recite the Buddha's name, you should change your way of life. Do not deliberately create bad karma. If you do, you will pile up evil karma and increase your obstructions. That is called "knowingly and deliberately transgressing." In that case, your karmic obstructions triple. You may carry your old karma to rebirth, but now that you understand the Buddhadharma, you cannot say, "Oh! I can recite the Buddha's name on the one hand, and create evil karma on the other, because in the future I can carry the karma I create to the Land of Ultimate Bliss with me."
