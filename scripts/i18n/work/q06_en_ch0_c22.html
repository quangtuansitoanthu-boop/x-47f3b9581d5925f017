Yakshas are male ghosts; rakshasas are female ghosts. Both kinds of ghosts are most vicious. Their food is human flesh. They have mantras capable of dispelling the stench of corpses so they can eat the flesh. Kumbhandas are also the name of a kind of ghost, shaped like a round barrel, bringing nightmares to humans. For instance, while asleep, people see strange apparitions appear; though in the dream their spirits remain most alert, still they cannot move their bodies. They become paralyzed under the control of this nightmare-bringing ghost. Sometimes, when a person's yang energy is weakened and yin energy prevails, the person may be paralyzed for quite a long time, and that ghost may cause the person to die. This is a kind of ghost very numerous in the world.

Pishachas are ghosts that feed on human essence and also on the essence of grains. Putanas. They can make people fall ill and run fevers. If you cultivate the effort of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature, or if you recite Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name, then these ghosts cannot see you, though they come beside you — because you emit a light that frightens them. Indeed, owls and bats can see at all times. Since ghosts belong to yin, they cannot see you if you emit yang light. They see you only when you emit yin energy.

Sutra text: 七者音性圓銷。觀聽返入離諸塵妄。能令眾生禁繫枷鎖所不能著。

Transliteration: Thất giả âm tánh viên tiêu, quán thính phản nhập, ly chư trần vọng. Năng linh chúng sanh cấm hệ già toả sở bất năng trước.

Translation: Seventh, the nature of sound completely dissolved, my regarding and hearing turned back inward, apart from all false sense-objects. I can enable beings to be untouched by fetters, shackles, cangues, and locks.

Commentary: Seventh, the nature of sound completely dissolved, my regarding and hearing turned back inward, apart from all false sense-objects. There is no more sound, not even the nature of hearing, once the cultivator has turned the regarding-nature and the hearing-nature back into the self-nature. The cultivator leaves behind all false sense-objects and false thoughts. Then I can enable beings to be untouched by fetters, shackles, cangues, and locks. If you recite Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name, all locks and fetters fall away by themselves. This happens very often; a number of people have had this experience. That is not merely talk — if a person sincerely recites Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name, there will be such a response.

Someone had committed a crime and was locked in prison. He and seven or eight other prisoners together recited Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name. How did they know to do this? They knew a monk and begged the monk to rescue them from their miserable plight. The monk told them: "If you want me to rescue you, you must single-mindedly uphold Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name; you will escape this perilous situation." The prisoners all recited Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name for three days and three nights; locks, fetters, and chains suddenly came undone and they were free. But they still did not leave, thinking: "What meaning is there in our own escape when so many others remain imprisoned here?" So they told the other prisoners to recite Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name. After two more days of reciting together, the locks on those prisoners' shackles also came undone entirely. They all returned home. After that, they all single-mindedly recited Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name. They recited so sincerely that they moved the villagers to bring forth the resolve to cultivate.
