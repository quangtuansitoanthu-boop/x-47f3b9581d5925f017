For instance, there are some deviant teachers and outsiders who only know how to cheat people with their doctrines. They speak much about relations between men and women, claiming the more lust, the sooner Buddhahood. This is utterly wrong. Utterly deviant! You must note this. Do not let these people cheat you. In the right Dharma, every mention of lust as approval is wrong. Methods teaching people so are demons' exploitation. Ananda vows to help people of the Dharma-Ending Age dwell in the bodhi resolve. Some people study the Buddhadharma for a while, then retreat from the bodhi resolve. "I shall study no more Buddhadharma. It is too hard! I must abandon all my faults. But what if I cannot abandon them? Better that I do not study at all."
They lose their zeal. They accept retreat. They are subdued by demons.
Ananda wishes to know how to keep this from happening.

Sutra text: 爾時世尊於大眾中,稱讚阿難。善哉!善哉!如汝所問,安立道場救護眾生末劫沈溺。汝今諦聽,當為汝說。阿難大眾唯然奉教

Transliteration: Nhĩ thời Thế tôn ư đại chúng trung, xưng tán A-nan Thiện tai! Thiện tai! Như nhữ sở vấn, an lập đạo tràng cứu hộ chúng sanh mạt kiếp trầm nịch. Nhữ kim đế thính, đương vị nhữ thuyết. A-nan đại chúng duy nhiên phụng giáo.

Translation: Thereupon the World Honored One, amid the great assembly, praised Ananda: "Excellent! Excellent! As you ask, about establishing Way-places to rescue beings drowning in the final kalpa. Now listen attentively; I shall speak for you." Ananda and the assembly respectfully accepted the teaching.

Commentary: Ananda says he wishes beings of the Dharma-Ending Age never to retreat from the bodhi resolve. Ananda wishes them to have firm faith, vowing to protect the Buddhadharma, study the Buddhadharma, and never retreat from the bodhi resolve. But how is Ananda to help them do so? When Shakyamuni Buddha heard Ananda ask thus, he was most delighted. The Buddha dwells in the realm of thusness, unmoving, yet when someone brings forth the resolve to protect the Buddhadharma and help all beings become Buddhas, it still delights him.

Thereupon the World Honored One, amid the great assembly, praised Ananda: "Excellent! Excellent!"
The Buddha was pleased. "Ananda! You are most good!" He praised Ananda twice.
As you ask, about establishing Way-places to rescue beings drowning in the final kalpa.
You wish to rescue beings of the Dharma-Ending Age drowning in water and fire.
