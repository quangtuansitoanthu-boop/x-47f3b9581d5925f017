Hoang Te
---TOMTAT---
Great Master Hoang Te, who calmed the sea dikes by mantra power and devoted his old age to Buddha-recitation; Great Master De Qing (Old Man Hanshan), who awakened at Mount Wutai, suffered exile, and taught the Pure Land gate; and Dharma Master Di Xian, restorer of the Tiantai school, who lectured widely, practiced daily recitation, and passed away peacefully in 1932 amid supporting recitation.
