<h3 style="text-align:center"><strong>The Fifty Skandhas</strong>
<h3>

<p><strong><em>Sutra text: </em></strong><strong>Tức thời Như lai tương bãi pháp tòa. Ư sư tử sàng, lãm thất bảo kỷ, hồi tử kim sơn, tái lai bằng ỷ, phổ cáo đại chúng, cập A-nan ngôn: Nhữ đẳng hữu học Duyên giác Thanh văn , kim nhật hồi tâm thú đại bồ-đề vô thượng diệu giác. Ngô kim dĩ thuyết chân tu hành pháp.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Translation:</em></strong><strong> At that time the Tathagata was about to leave the Dharma seat. From the lion throne he drew near the seven-jeweled chair, turned his purple-golden mountain-like body about, leaned back against the chair, and announced to the great assembly and to Ananda: You who are Hearers and Pratyekabuddhas still in need of study, today you have turned your minds toward the unsurpassed, wondrous awakening of great Bodhi. I have now taught you the true method of cultivation.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary:</em></strong> <strong>At that time the Tathagata was about to leave the Dharma seat</strong>: this was when the Buddha had nearly finished teaching the Shurangama Sutra assembly.</p>
<p><strong>From the lion throne </strong><strong>he </strong><strong>drew near the seven-jeweled chair</strong>. The Buddha was sitting on the Dharma seat, the lion throne: the Tathagata's Dharma voice is likened to a lion's roar &#8211; when the lion roars, all beasts tremble in fear &#8211; hence the place where the Tathagata sits to teach the Dharma is called the lion throne. The chair on which the Tathagata sat was adorned with seven kinds of jewels.</p>
<p><strong>The Tathagata turned his purple-golden mountain-like body about, then leaned back against the chair</strong>. The Buddha's body was like a mountain of purple gold, its light shining everywhere; <strong>he announced to the great assembly and to Ananda: you are Hearers and Pratyekabuddhas still in need of study</strong>. Before attaining the four fruitions of Arhatship, they are still regarded as those in need of study.</p>
<p>Hearers are those awakened through cultivating the Dharma of the Four Noble Truths; Pratyekabuddhas are those awakened through cultivating the Dharma of the Twelve Links of Conditioned Origination. <strong>Today you have turned your minds toward the unsurpassed Bodhi</strong>: Ananda and the Hearers have now turned their minds from the Small Vehicle fruition toward the Great Vehicle. Ananda and the Two Vehicles have resolved to seek unsurpassed Bodhi, an awakening so wondrous nothing can compare to it.</p>
<p><strong>I have now taught you the true method of cultivation.</strong> The Buddha had already clearly taught them the true method of cultivation.</p>
<p><strong><em>Sutra text:</em></strong> <strong>Nhữ do vị thức tu xa-ma-tha , tì-bà-xá-na vi tế ma sự. Ma cảnh hiện tiền, nhữ bất năng thức. Tẩy tâm phi chính, lạc ư tà kiến.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: But you still do not know the subtle demonic occurrences that arise during the cultivation of shamatha and vipashyana. When demonic states appear before you, you will not recognize them, for your cleansing of the mind is not in accord with the true Dharma, and you will fall into wrong views.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>But you still do not know the subtle demonic occurrences that will arise during the cultivation of shamatha and vipashyana.</strong></p>
<p>Earlier Ananda had asked the Buddha to teach the method of cultivation, requesting the Dharma on behalf of beings of future ages.</p>
<p>Although Ananda now understands the principles of cultivation, he still lacks experience in practice: he has grasped the theory but, lacking experience, does not know what will occur during practice &#8211; hence the Buddha's instruction here. Cultivating shamatha means cultivating the Great Shurangama Samadhi; cultivating <em>vipashyana</em> is a very subtle contemplative Dharma. Subtle demonic occurrences will appear at this point: in the course of cultivation, many demonic states will manifest. Not every phenomenon appears openly &#8211; they are very subtle.</p>
<p><strong>When demonic states appear before you and you do not recognize them.</strong> When you apply effort in the method of <em>returning the hearing to hear the self-nature</em> (turning the hearing inward to recognize your inherent self-nature), demonic states will appear. If you do not recognize the demons and do not know what demons are, <strong>it is because your cleansing of the mind is not in accord with the true Dharma</strong>.</p>
<p>For you have cleansed your own mind, but with a slight deviation, not in accord with right views &#8211; hence falling into wrong views. If your right views are not truly correct (not in accord with the right Dharma), you will get caught in demonic states.</p>
<p><strong><em>Sutra text:</em></strong> <strong> Hoặc nhữ ấm ma, hoặc phục thiên ma. Hoặc trước quỷ thần, hoặc tao quỷ mị. Tâm trung bất minh, nhận tặc vi tử.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: You will be disturbed by a demon of your own five skandhas, or by a heavenly demon, or be possessed by ghosts and spirits, or meet goblins and sprites. With the mind unclear, you will mistake a thief for your own son.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>You will be disturbed by a demon of your own five skandhas.</strong></p>
<p>That is a kind of demon arising from one's own mind &#8211; for instance, one of the ten kinds of demons arising from the form skandha, which also come from your own mind.</p>
<p><strong>Or by a heavenly demon</strong>. Why would a demon from the heavens come to harass you? Because in cultivating you aim at attaining samadhi power.</p>
<p>Attaining a little samadhi power in cultivation is no great matter in itself, but it can shake the demon king's palace as in an earthquake. Since the demon king has spiritual powers, the moment his palace trembles he investigates: &#8220;Why is my palace suddenly shaking?&#8221; He discovers that someone in the world is about to accomplish the Way; that person's samadhi power will shatter his palace. The heavenly demon then thinks: &#8220;You want to destroy my palace &#8211; I shall destroy your samadhi power first.&#8221; And so it comes to sabotage the cultivator's samadhi power.</p>
<p><strong>Or be possessed by ghosts and spirits</strong>: when ghosts and spirits see you about to attain results in cultivation, they grow jealous, thinking: &#8220;Oh! So you are about to realize the Way.&#8221; So first they come to destroy your cultivation effort &#8211; entering your mind or seizing your body so your samadhi power cannot come to perfection.</p>
<p>It makes you &#8220;go astray and fall under demonic possession,&#8221; becoming dependent on it. I have already lectured before on being possessed by ghosts and spirits, have I not? That is very important. Why do you become the demon's property? Because <strong>your cleansing of the mind is not true, not in accord with the right Dharma</strong>, and because the motive is not upright &#8211; with even a single thought of a tiny deviant intention, you will become possessed by ghosts and spirits. That is what is called &#8220;going astray and falling under demonic possession.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>Or meet goblins and sprites</strong> &#8211; or wang-liang goblins. These demons are all kinds of goblins and monsters<strong>. With the mind unclear, you will mistake a thief for your own son.</strong> If you meet this kind of demon without recognizing it or understanding anything about it, you will come to &#8220;mistake a thief for your own son.&#8221; Think this over carefully, lest it steal your property: if you let the thief into your house, all the precious property in the house will be carried off by him.</p>
