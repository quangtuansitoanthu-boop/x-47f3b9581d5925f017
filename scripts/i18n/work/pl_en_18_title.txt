Duong Lien Hang
---TOMTAT---
Layman Duong Lien Hang, of shallow practice but sincere repentance, reborn through fellow practitioners' supporting recitation; the poor woman of Cixi who learned to sell suffering through Buddha-recitation; and the servant Little Vuong, whose stutter was cured and whose silent recitation formed a single mass, departing with wondrous signs at cremation.
