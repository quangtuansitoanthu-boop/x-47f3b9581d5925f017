They trade in the Tathagata. They use the Buddhadharma for barter. They trade in the Buddhadharma. All they need do is think of ways to make money. They say, "It is all Buddhadharma." They claim everything is Buddhadharma. Dancing is Buddhadharma, drinking wine is Buddhadharma, playing music is Buddhadharma. These are among the 84,000 dharma-doors."

They are truly most glib. "Smoking is Buddhadharma, gambling is Buddhadharma, do whatever you wish." They are most casual, to the point that whatever anyone wishes to do, they approve. Yet they slander fully-precepted monastic bhikshus as followers of the Small Vehicle way.

If someone calls them and asks, "Have you received the complete precepts?" They do not even understand what you are talking about. They do not even understand the five prohibitive precepts, let alone the eight precepts, or the ten major and forty-eight minor precepts. They themselves do not deserve the world's trust as monastics. Their outlook is most narrow and full of self. Thereby deluding countless beings into falling into the Uninterrupted Hells.

They delude others, and fundamentally they themselves do not understand. At first their followers all have good intentions, but once entangled with such an upside-down teacher, their lives will end in the state described before:
If a deluded person
Teaches delusion to others,
When all is said and done,
Then no one understands.
The teacher falls into the hells;
The student must follow along.

In the Uninterrupted Hells, suffering is never interrupted. One person fills the entire hell with suffering, and many people likewise fill the hell in the same way. With only one person in the hell, there is no room to spare. And no matter how many people are in it, the hell contains them all. Beings in those hells never leave the place. So it is most dangerous to plant affinities with that place.

Sutra text: 若我滅後,其有比丘發心決定修三摩提。能於如來形像之前。身然一燈燒一指節。及於身上爇一香炷。我說是人無始宿債一時酬畢。長挹世間,永脫諸漏。雖未即明無上覺路。是人於法已決定心。

Transliteration: Nhược ngã diệt hậu, kỳ hữu tỷ-khưu phát tâm quyết định tu tam-ma-đề. Năng ư Như Lai hình tượng chi tiền, thân nhiên nhất đăng, thiêu nhất chỉ tiết, cập ư thân thượng nhiệt nhất hương chú. Ngã thuyết thị nhân vô thuỷ túc trái nhất thời thù tất. Trường ấp thế gian vĩnh thóat chư lậu. Tuy vị tức minh vô thượng giác lộ, thị nhơn ư pháp dĩ quyết định tâm.

Translation: After my extinction, if there are bhikshus who resolve to cultivate samadhi with determination, who before the Tathagata's image can burn a lamp upon their bodies, burn off a finger joint, or burn a stick of incense upon their bodies, I say such people, beginningless old debts repaid at once, long bid farewell to the world, forever free of all outflows. Though they have not yet brightened the unsurpassed enlightened road, they already have determination toward the Dharma.
