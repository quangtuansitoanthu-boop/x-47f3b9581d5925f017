That place is now called Moonlight Cliff on Tiantai Mountain — where Han Shan and Shi De vanished. The official hurried back to Guoqing Temple to bow to Abbot Feng Gan — Amitabha Buddha. But when he entered the temple, he found the abbot already seated serenely in extinction. He had entered nirvana. The official now knew the abbot was precisely Amitabha Buddha manifesting, but it was too late! He had not seen what was true right before his eyes. Amitabha Buddha had gone.

Why do Buddhas and Bodhisattvas, once vanished, let people know who they are? If people knew, they would come to bow to them all day until it became too troublesome. There would be no time left to cultivate. So they do not wish to let it be known who they are. That is the way in the Way. People never say: "Behold! I am enlightened! I am a Buddha!"

Such a person is no different from those mentioned in this sutra. I have never met anyone who admitted to being enlightened. Elder Master Xu Yun likewise, and the other enlightened Elder Masters of China never said a word that they were enlightened, even when asked directly. That never happens in Buddhism. Besides, perhaps there is in new-age Buddhism. Those described in the sutra proclaim they are superior people. "Do you know who I am? I am Maitreya Bodhisattva. Do you know who I am? I am Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva. Now that you know, do not miss the opportunity. Bow to me as teacher. If you will not bow to me, then bow to my teacher. I shall issue you a certificate and 65 dollars. I shall transmit Dharma to you."

They go about deceiving the ignorant. They cheat the credulous. I have met this kind a great deal. They say: "I have a Dharma treasure; I shall sell it to you, 300 dollars each. It is because I like you so much that I shall reserve it for you. If I did not like you, I would not transmit it to you." So the disciple gives the teacher 300 dollars for the Dharma treasure. Some demand as much as 1000 dollars from you. Before long, the old teacher's wallet is heavy. When he moves his property from one safe place to another, he must use a train! Many have been disappointed by this. When you speak true Dharma for them to hear, as telling them "Do not kill." They do not believe it. "Do not steal." They do not believe it either. "Do not lust." They do not believe it either. But if you tell them you have something that will benefit them, they will pay you at once. They frighten them into losing their resolve.

They make you lose utterly the wisdom you inherently have. They delude you. Wherever they go, families are ruined. They are truly filthy rich, yet wherever they go, they accumulate still more wealth, stripping householders of property, keys, securities, and purses.

Sutra text: 我教比丘循方乞食,令其捨貪成菩提道。諸比丘等不自熟食,寄於殘生旅泊三界。示一往還去已無返。

Transliteration: Ngã giáo tỷ-khưu tuần phương khất thực, linh kỳ xả tham, thành bồ-đề đạo. Chư tỷ-khưu đẳng bất tự thục thực, ký ư tàn sanh lữ bạc tam giới. Thị nhất vãng hoàn khứ dĩ vô phản.
