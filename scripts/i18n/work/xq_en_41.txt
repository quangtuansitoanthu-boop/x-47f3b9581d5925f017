TITLE: Story 41: DIVIDED INTO 13 SECTIONS
TOMTAT: In the impermanent cycle of human life, people often mistakenly believe that death is the end, that their wrongdoings while alive, as long as they are cleverly hidden, no one will notice...

{align:center|**DIVIDED INTO 13 SECTIONS**}
In the impermanent cycle of human life, people often mistakenly believe that death is the end, that their wrongdoings while alive, as long as they are cleverly hidden, no one will know. But few people understand that, three meters above the head there is a god, below the feet is the book of life and death, all good and evil actions are recorded without a single mistake. Especially adultery and adultery - karma that both destroys family happiness, brings suffering to others, and digs a hole to bury oneself. And now, together, we follow the Journey of the Redemption Mission, listen to the scary story of a female ghost named Lan, who died at the age of 33, to clearly see the fierce retribution of adultery in the dark hell. Those who are having an affair, please practice hugging the hot copper pole to get used to it, so that if you have to commit a crime in the future, you will be less surprised...![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-466-7eff32e94a98.webp)
When she was alive, Lan was a normal woman, beautiful, and had a family. She fell in love with a man but was forbidden by her parents, because she had a hunch that this man was promiscuous and untrustworthy. However, because of the call of her heart, Lan disregarded her parents' advice and disobeyed her parents to marry that man. The first years of marriage passed in fake rosy terms. The husband shows love, pampering, and no lack of material things. But the needle that had been in the bag for a long time finally came out, and the husband's philandering nature gradually revealed itself. 
 

![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-467-aab2a106b0eb.webp)
He cheated again and again, leaving Lan in pain and humiliation. Because she loved her so much, because she still had two children, Lan continuously forgave, deceiving herself that if she was patient, the family would be peaceful. But the more she endured, the more she was trampled on. The husband still suffers from the same disability, neglects his family, and falls in love with another woman. At some point, an unwholesome thought arises. Lan thought: "If he eats spring rolls, she eats spring rolls." And from there, she slid down the path of adultery.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-468-c1f80cafad2c.webp)
After divorce, the door of freedom opens, but it is not freedom of liberation, but freedom of depravity. Lan dated a series of men, from single to married. Whoever calls comes, whoever invites follows, sex and sexuality have no boundaries. According to the book of life and death in the underworld, Lan had relationships with 13 men, 9 were married, 4 were single. Not only did she destroy the happiness of 9 families, Lan also spread social diseases, causing many men to fall into despair, resentment, and deadlock.
Betrayed wives are filled with anger and sow deep resentment. And then, walking at night sometimes meets ghosts. During a secret affair with a married man, Lan was discovered by his wife. Not just once, but many times. 
 

![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-469-33f2fcc3f22d.webp)
The betrayed woman's jealousy was suppressed to the extreme, turning into evil thoughts of revenge. She prepared high concentration acid, and in a cruel moment, sprayed Lan from head to toe. Heart-wrenching screams, burning skin, and a strong smell of death. Lan fainted in pain, and died on the way to the hospital. The body was deformed, burned black, and the human figure was no longer recognizable.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-470-9cbf9885276c.webp)
As soon as her last breath ended, a ghost appeared, dragging Lan's soul straight down into the underworld. 
 

![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-471-92dce3e777ba.webp)
Tại điện Diêm Đài, sổ nghiệp được mở ra. Every act of adultery, every secret orgy, is clearly visible. Lan knelt down, cried and complained, but Diem Chu coldly said: "Adultery - adultery, no one can escape the Harsh Reflection Mirror. Even a single unwholesome thought is recorded, let alone the action." Behind Lan is a long line of male and female sinners, most of whom are guilty of adultery. The gates of hell are wide open, because this karma is too much in this world. 
 

![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-472-6652004000b2.webp)
Lan bị quỷ sai lôi vào cửa ngục đầu tiên. Here, she was roasted alive, turned around like a roast pig on earth. The skin cracked, the flesh fell, and the burning smell spread throughout the prison.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-473-9e7c03f27786.webp)
Lan is still alive, still feeling extreme pain. The ghost used an iron pitchfork to blind his eyes and said: "So that you will no longer look at men and start thinking about it." 
When the body was "ripe", the ghost ordered Lan to be cut into 13 pieces. 
Each segment corresponds to a man she has harmed.  9 songs for 9 broken families, 4 songs for 4 sick, desperate people. Die – then live again. Live – then continue to suffer punishment. 
 

![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-474-42227ef09246.webp)
Before stopping, Lan was pushed to the next prison door. Here, the devil ordered her to rub her body on a sharp iron table, like washing clothes on wood grain. Rub until there is only a white skeleton left. The punishment repeated endlessly, dying and then coming back to life, the pain was beyond description.
 

![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-475-b00b922275a4.webp)
In prison with Lan was another woman - one who left her husband and children with her lover, then was murdered by her lover.
Both have been guilty for more than twenty years, with no release date known. Lan choked up and confessed: "When I was alive, I didn't believe in cause and effect. I thought death was the end. Now I know... it's too late to regret."![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-41-image-476-06754d49221b.webp)
After a long period of suffering, Lan was sent to earth to become a hungry ghost. Fortunately, thanks to Tien Ong's guidance, my group followed the instructions and came to enlighten us, brought the dead to the temple to practice, and relied on Buddha's door to partly repent of their karma. Lan's story is a wake-up call for those who are disregarding morality and cause and effect. Adultery is never a small matter, it destroys the family, sow resentment, and in the end the karma returns to the perpetrator.
Death is not the end.  Cause and effect does not leave out anyone who is faithful is a blessing - adultery is a disaster. If you believe, reflect to correct yourself. If you don't believe it, consider it a scary story for entertainment. But I hope that after hearing this, everyone will know how to avoid being afraid, and know how to keep their morals as human beings, live well - live rightly - live faithfully, so that they don't have to meet each other at the hot bronze pillar.

COMMENTS:
After reading the story of DIVIDED INTO 13 SECTIONS, I sat quietly for a long time. Not because of fear, but because we see one thing clearly: in each of us there are times of weakness, times when due to circumstances, pressure, or for some reason we have chosen paths that we can't help but feel sad when looking back on.This story reminds me that Karma is not something far away or only exists after death. It is present in the small, everyday choices. A word, an action, a decision for "love" or for "money" - all are sowing seeds. And sooner or later, we will be the ones reaping the harvest.

But what warms my heart after every story like this is: it's never too late. Even though they have caused heavy karma, even though they have endured suffering for many years and many lifetimes, the spirits in the book still have the opportunity to let go, have the opportunity to be guided, repented and transformed. That is the power of mindfulness and compassion.

For those of you reading these lines, perhaps you are not someone who has caused great karma like the character in the story. But each of us has times when we make mistakes, times when we hurt others because of selfishness or ignorance. This story is a gentle reminder: stop while you still can. Let's choose a way to live so that when night comes, we can sleep peacefully with a peaceful conscience.

An act of kindness, a sincere apology, a decision to live a good life today - no matter how small - can change the fate of ourselves and those around us for many lifetimes to come. That's what I want to convey after listening to this story.
