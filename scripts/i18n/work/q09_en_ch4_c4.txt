

<p style="text-align:center"><strong><em>3 &#8211; GREED FOR UNION</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu thiện nam tử, thọ ấm  hư diệu, bất tao tà lự. Viên định phát minh, tam-ma-địa trung, tâm ái miên vẫn, trừng kì tinh tư,  tham cầu  khế hợp.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed &#8211; within samadhi the mind fond of hidden accord, settling thought, greedily seeking union.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed.</strong></p>
<p>Again, in the empty, penetrating, wondrous state gained after the feeling skandha has dissolved, the cultivator no longer has deviant concerns, dwelling in the bright, perfect samadhi. <strong>Within samadhi, the mind fond of hidden accord</strong> &#8211; the mind fond of spiritual union.</p>
<p>The mind wishes to draw near the holy sages. <strong>Settling thought, greedily seeking union</strong>: the cultivator strives to settle the mind, reaching purity. Because of a hidden thought greedily seeking union, the cultivator has given the demon king a fine chance to destroy him.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhĩ thời thiên ma, hậu đắc kì tiện, phi tinh phụ nhân, khẩu thuyết kinh pháp.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Then the heavenly demon gets its convenient chance, sending its essence to possess another person, using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Then the heavenly demon gets its convenient chance. Sending its essence to possess another person, using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma.</strong></p>
<p>The heavenly demon sees its convenient chance, and so swiftly sends its essence to possess that person, using his mouth to expound sutras and Dharma.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Kì nhân diệc bất giác tri ma trước, diệc ngôn tự đắc, vô thượng niết-bàn. Lai bỉ cầu hợp thiện nam tử xứ, phu tòa thuyết pháp. Kì hình cập bỉ, thính pháp chi nhân, ngoại vô thiên biến, linh kì thính giả, vị văn pháp tiền, tâm tự khai ngộ. Niệm niệm  di dịch, hoặc đắc túc mệnh, hoặc hữu tha tâm, hoặc kiến địa ngục, hoặc tri nhân gian, hảo ác chư sự, hoặc khẩu thuyết kệ, hoặc tự tụng kinh. Các các hoan ngu, đắc vị tằng hữu.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: That person, likewise unaware of demonic possession, likewise claims to have attained unsurpassed nirvana. Coming to the place of the good man seeking union, it spreads its seat and preaches the Dharma. Its form and those listening to the Dharma show no outward change &#8211; yet it makes its listeners, before hearing the Dharma, feel their minds self-awakened &#8211; thought after thought shifting &#8211; some gaining knowledge of past lives, some possessing knowledge of others' minds, some seeing the hells, some knowing good and evil matters in the human realm, some speaking verses from the mouth, some reciting sutras of themselves. Each rejoicing, gaining what never was.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>That</strong> possessed <strong>person, likewise unaware of demonic possession,</strong> <strong>likewise claiming to have attained</strong> the wondrous fruition of unsurpassed nirvana. <strong>Coming to the place of the good man seeking spiritual union, at once</strong> <strong>spreading</strong> the Dharma <strong>seat</strong>, mounting it, <strong>preaching the Dharma</strong>. <strong>The form of that person and of the Dharma-listeners outwardly shows no change.</strong></p>
<p>Its bodily form and the Dharma-listeners' show no change &#8211; nothing happening to the outside of their bodies. This passage differs from the earlier sutra passage, where listeners suddenly saw themselves seated on jeweled lotus thrones or saw their bodies emitting blazing purple-golden light; nor like seeing the preacher transform into Shakra, bhikshus, bhikshunis, women. In this case there is no such transformation.</p>
<p>But instead, <strong>it makes its listeners, before hearing the Dharma, feel &#8220;awakened.&#8221;</strong> After that awakened feeling, they feel as if just sobered from drink or just awakened from a dream in which they grew rich. This awakened feeling is not true awakening &#8211; only a kind of illusion, like watching a movie or taking a hallucinogenic pill. They see everything in phantasmal transformation, so their <strong>thought after thought shifts.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Some gain knowledge of past lives</strong> &#8211; though that knowledge of past lives is also unreal; all they see is illusion. <strong>Some possess knowledge of others' minds, or see all the states of the hells</strong>, <strong>or know good and evil matters in the human realm</strong>, or can speak verses in their own style at will, or recite sutras on impulse. <strong>Everyone rejoices,</strong> feeling <strong>as gaining what never was.</strong></p>
<p>They all say: &#8220;He is truly a Bodhisattva &#8211; he is a Buddha &#8211; he awakened me, he gave me knowledge of past lives.&#8221; Yet that feeling lasts only while they listen to the demon lecture the Dharma &#8211; that is the key telling you the Dharma is not true.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thị nhân ngu mê, hoặc vi bồ-tát, miên ái kì tâm, phá Phật luật nghi, tiềm hành tham dục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: That person, deluded, mistakes it for a Bodhisattva; the mind entangled in fond love, breaking the Buddha's moral code, secretly practicing greed and lust.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>That person, deluded, mistakes it for a Bodhisattva</strong>: that person is extremely deluded, imagining the possessed one a Bodhisattva. <strong>The mind entangled in fond love</strong>: he befriends the demon, <strong>breaking the Buddha's moral code</strong>, no longer observing the precepts. <strong>Secretly practicing greed and lust</strong>: quietly committing lustful acts.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Khẩu trung hiếu ngôn, Phật hữu đại tiểu;mỗ Phật tiên Phật, mỗ Phật hậu Phật. Kì trung diệc hữu chân Phật giả Phật, nam Phật nữ Phật; bồ-tát diệc nhiên. Kì nhân kiến cố, tẩy địch bản tâm, dị nhập tà ngộ.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Its mouth delights in saying: Buddhas great and small; this Buddha first, that Buddha after; among them true Buddhas and false Buddhas, male Buddhas and female Buddhas; Bodhisattvas likewise. On seeing this, that person washes away his inherent mind, easily entering deviant awakening.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>It delights in saying: Buddhas great and small</strong>.</p>
<p>What does the demon love to say? It says: &#8220;You have studied the Buddhadharma &#8211; but do you know what Buddhas are? There are great Buddhas and small Buddhas, old Buddhas, young Buddhas,&#8221; explaining in further detail.</p>
<p><strong>This Buddha is first, that Buddha after; this Buddha true, that Buddha false</strong>: the demon proclaims itself the true Buddha while the others are false Buddhas. It further speaks of <strong>male Buddhas, female Buddhas</strong>, insisting &#8220;the union of male and female produces Buddhas &#8211; that is the Buddhas' origin, that is the Bodhi mind.&#8221;</p>
<p>Of course this deludes people: they think &#8220;Oh, that is how one becomes a Buddha,&#8221; then abandon themselves to dissolute, depraved living &#8211; preferring death to a life without wanton lust.</p>
<p>It distinguishes <strong>Bodhisattvas likewise.</strong></p>
