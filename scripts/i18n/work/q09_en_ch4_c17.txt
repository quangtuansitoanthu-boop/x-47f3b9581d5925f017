<p><strong>Keeping them from demonic entanglement</strong>: save and deliver beings, making them give rise to faith in you, lest they be deluded by the demon king, lest they follow the demon king, using the demon's means to harass beings anymore.</p>
<p><strong>Helping them</strong>, beings of the Dharma-ending age, <strong>gain right views</strong> &#8211; meaning you and I and the beings right now, no one else. You should remind and awaken yourselves &#8211; you must have right views.</p>
<p>Shakyamuni Buddha further taught:</p>
<p>Ananda, the Tathagata has helped you out of birth and death: you have ended birth-and-death in the turning wheel, realized the second Arhat fruition, and known the path to the fourth fruition. So through your effort's result, it can be said you will transcend birth and death.</p>
<p><strong>Obeying the Buddha's words now is called repaying the Buddha's kindness.</strong></p>
<p>Now listen to the Buddha's words &#8211; practice what the Buddha taught and do not forget. By revering and obeying the Buddha's teaching, you can repay the Buddhas' profound kindness.</p>
<p>What does repaying the Buddha's kindness mean? If we revere and practice the Buddha's words, that is repaying the Buddha's kindness. To repay the Buddha's kindness, we must obey what the Buddha taught. Ananda, to repay the Buddha's kindness, must also follow the Buddha's teaching. We should obey Shakyamuni Buddha as well as the Dharma masters who taught us to study the sutras and principles. That is why from the start I ask you to faithfully follow the Buddha's words. I am not a Buddha, so I too obey the Buddha's teaching. In whatever we do, we should never forget the Buddha's words.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: A-nan như thị, thập chủng thiền na hiện cảnh, giai thị tưởng ấm, dụng tâm giao hỗ, cố hiện tư sự.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Ananda, these ten states appearing in dhyana all come from the thinking skandha and the mind's applied effort interweaving, hence these occurrences.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>Ananda, the ten states</strong> explained &#8211; they only <strong>appear</strong> <strong>when</strong> the cultivator cultivates <strong>dhyana</strong>: striving with utmost effort, applying effort to reach the goal. Whence do these states appear? They occur <strong>all from the thinking skandha</strong> <strong>and the applying mind interweaving</strong> &#8211; formed by the contemplating mind's joint coordination.</p>
<p>These are transformations occurring while dissolving the thinking skandha, as the result of effort applied to the utmost. When you apply effort to the maximum, such states arise. But when they arise, do not foolishly mistake a thief for your son &#8211; do not be deluded by these states. When you sit in Chan, the effortful contemplating mind interweaves with the thinking skandha, forming a battlefield, as in warfare: if your samadhi power is stronger, the thinking skandha is conquered. But if the thinking skandha prevails and your samadhi effort weakens and scatters, the cultivator falls into demonic states and these ten states appear.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Chúng sanh ngoan mê, bất tự thốn lượng, phùng thử nhân duyên, mê bất tự thức, vị ngôn đăng thánh, đại vọng ngữ thành, đọa vô gián ngục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Obstinate and deluded beings, not assessing themselves, encountering such conditions, deluded and unaware, claim to have ascended to sagehood &#8211; committing the great falsehood, they fall into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>Obstinate and deluded beings</strong>, <strong>not assessing their own measure</strong> &#8211; beings usually with attached minds: very stubborn, inflexible rather than yielding, foolish, lacking wisdom, unable to deliberate anything truly right by themselves.</p>
<p><strong>Encountering such conditions, deluded and unaware</strong>: meeting such demonic states, lacking the clarity to assess. Most important: if you can assess the occurring state, demons cannot turn you. If you clearly recognize it, you will not be mistaken. Failing to recognize, you are deluded &#8211; delusion comes from unclear recognition. And what happens when the cultivator does not clearly understand this state?</p>
<p><strong>Claiming to have realized sagehood</strong>: speaking such words as: &#8220;Do you know me? I have become a Buddha, and I shall tell you: for me becoming a Buddha was easier than a slab of bean curd.&#8221;</p>
<p>So very easy, is it not? Being easy, such people often say they have become Buddhas, realized the Way, awakened, investigated a meditation topic and understood those topics.</p>
<p>For instance, an American who came here today &#8211; a kind of &#8220;American Sixth Patriarch&#8221; who came some days ago: he does not bow to Buddhas, does not listen to Dharma &#8211; only eats lunch and leaves. The reason he leaves is demons dragging him away: with too much demonic aura in him, he feels ill at ease staying here even a minute past lunch. You should have a clear, general judgment of the demon-possessed: its style shows in thinking itself already a Buddha, hence no need to bow to Buddhas.</p>
<p><strong>Committing the great falsehood, falling into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p>In the future, it is sure to fall into the uninterrupted hell &#8211; expect not a moment's respite: at least nine lives, at most a hundred generations. Not heeding the Buddhadharma because demonic power has seized it entirely: though wishing to listen, body and mind feel most uneasy, unable to sit still.</p>
<p>I have told you this story before &#8211; you should most attentively heed it. When you visit any monastery or Buddha-recitation hall, you must follow that Way-place's Chan rules: do as everyone does &#8211; do not stand while everyone bows to Buddhas. Those behaving so will never learn the Buddhadharma. Learning the Buddhadharma, you must be most humble, reverent, and sincere.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đẳng tất tu, tương Như Lai ngữ, ư ngã diệt hậu, truyền thị mạt pháp, biến linh chúng sanh, khai ngộ tư nghĩa. Vô linh thiên ma đắc kì phương tiện. Bảo trì phúc hộ, thành vô thượng đạo.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: In the Dharma-ending age, after the Tathagata's nirvana, you must all take the Tathagata's words and proclaim them to beings, awakening them to these meanings. Do not let the heavenly demons gain their advantage. Protect, shelter, and help beings accomplish the unsurpassed Way.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>In the Dharma-ending age, after the Tathagata's nirvana, you must all take the Tathagata's words and proclaim them to beings.</strong></p>
<p><em>You all</em> &#8211; Ananda, the great Bodhisattvas, great Bhikshus in the assembly, and great Elders: this is the Buddha's exhortation. &#8220;You must transmit the Tathagata's teaching continuously into the Dharma-ending age. <strong>Awakening them to these meanings</strong>: teaching them to understand the Shurangama Sutra; teaching them the <em>Fifty Skandha-Demons</em> &#8211; each demonic mark within the <em>form, feeling, thinking, activity, and consciousness skandhas</em> that I have explained to you. Wholeheartedly propagate this teaching for beings to understand.</p>
