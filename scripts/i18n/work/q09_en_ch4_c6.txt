<p>Father begets son; son grows up becoming father, in turn begetting sons &#8211; the continuity of generations is ever-abiding, the ever-abiding, undying Dharma-body. What you see right now is precisely the Buddha-land; this very place right now is the Buddha-land. <strong>There is no separate Pure Abode, nor any golden-colored form.</strong></p>
<p>There is no Pure Land to dwell in, nor any other golden form &#8211; all those are false. From its words, know clearly the mark of demons, the mark of ghosts. I often use the word demonic aura to speak of demons &#8211; this is what is called demonic aura: the demon's latent power possessing it, so everyone accepts whatever the demon lectures.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Kì nhân tín thọ, vong thất tiên tâm, thân mạng quy y, đắc vị tằng hữu. Thị đẳng ngu mê hoặc vi bồ-tát. Suy cứu kì tâm, phá Phật luật nghi, tiềm hành tham dục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: That person believes and accepts, losing the former mind &#8211; body and life taking refuge, gaining what never was. Such deluded ones mistake it for a Bodhisattva. Pursuing such fancies in mind, they break the Buddha's moral code, secretly practicing greed and lust.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>That person believes what the demon says, losing the original faith.</strong></p>
<p>Hearing the demon say such things, the cultivator thinks: &#8220;So true &#8211; father begets son, son grows up as father, in turn begetting sons, generation succeeding generation &#8211; that is the ever-abiding, undying Dharma-body, always so since time without beginning.&#8221; The cultivator fully believes it.</p>
<p>Losing the original faith, the cultivator forgets right views &#8211; believing the demon more strongly than the right Dharma. When heavenly demons and outer paths tell him to do things, he dares not disobey; when a true good teacher teaches him to do good, he wavers between doubt and trust. The demon king harasses him with latent demonic power, so he believes whatever the demon says.</p>
<p>If you ask the demon king to lecture this Sutra (the Shurangama Sutra), it cannot lecture it: for the moment it speaks, its characteristic marks show. That is why I say, in America, if you ask anyone claiming mastery of Buddhist learning to lecture this Sutra, they will not dare &#8211; fearing exposure.</p>
<p><strong>Giving body and life in refuge to the demon, feeling as gaining what never was</strong>: the cultivator <strong>deluded, deeming himself a Bodhisattva</strong> &#8211; truly pitiable, thinking the demon is a Bodhisattva. <strong>Pursuing such fancies</strong>, he studies with the demon, learning its magical arts. He no longer keeps the precepts; instead he <strong>breaks the Buddha's moral code, secretly practicing lustful acts</strong> &#8211; secretly abandoning himself to pleasure unknown to anyone.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Khẩu trung hảo ngôn nhãn nhĩ tỉ thiệt, giai vi tịnh độ. Nam nữ nhị căn, tức thị bồ-đề niết-bàn chân xứ. Bỉ vô tri giả, tín thị uế ngôn.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Its mouth delights in saying: eyes, ears, nose, tongue are all Pure Lands; the male and female organs are truly the place of Bodhi and nirvana. The ignorant believe such filthy words.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: This kind of demon king is truly vile! <strong>Its mouth delights in saying: eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, mind are all Pure Lands</strong> &#8211; you need seek the Pure Land Dharma-door nowhere else. It tells people: &#8220;That Dharma-door lies right in your own six organs.&#8221; It also says <strong>the male and female organs are truly <em>Bodhi and nirvana</em></strong> &#8211; the seeds of Bodhi, the true abode of <em>Bodhi and nirvana</em>. Can you imagine such words?</p>
<p><strong>The ignorant believe such filthy words</strong>: people lacking knowledge and wisdom hear this, saying: &#8220;Oh! Never before heard or seen anything like this &#8211; <em>Bodhi and nirvana</em> are just so.&#8221;</p>
<p>Despising their whole lives, they race down the road to death as life's most worthwhile deed, having believed such filthy, defiled words.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thử danh cổ độc, yểm thắng ác quỷ, niên lão thành ma ,não loạn thị nhân. Yểm túc tâm sanh, khứ bỉ nhân thể. Đệ tử dữ sư, câu hãm vương nạn.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is called gu-poison and oppressive evil ghosts, aged into demons, harassing this person. Sated, they arise and leave that person's body. Both disciple and teacher fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>This is called <em>gu-poison</em> ghosts and <em>oppressive</em> ghosts</strong>, also called kumbhanda ghosts, <strong>aged into demons</strong>. <strong>They harass the cultivator</strong> cultivating right samadhi. But <strong>sated</strong> at last, <strong>they leave the possessed person's body</strong>, possessing people no more. Hence <strong>disciple and teacher both fall into legal peril</strong> &#8211; interfered with by the authorities, activities restricted, teacher and disciple both arrested and jailed.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đương tiên giác, bất nhập luân hồi. Mê hoặc bất tri, đọa vô gián ngục</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel. Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel. Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p>You should awaken early, lest you enter the turning wheel &#8211; do not fall into the demon king's temptations. Deluded and unknowing, one is sure to fall into the uninterrupted hell.</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong><em>5 &#8211; GREED FOR SECRET RESPONSE</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu thiện nam tử, thọ ấm hư diệu. bất tao tà lự, niên định phát minh, tam-ma-địa trung, tâm ái huyền ứng, chu lưu tinh nghiên, tham cầu minh cảm.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed. Within samadhi the mind fond of mysterious response, thoroughly, pervasively investigating, greedily seeking secret resonance.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed.</strong></p>
<p>Again, in the empty, penetrating, wondrous state after the feeling skandha has dissolved, the cultivator cultivating right samadhi no longer has deviant concerns, dwelling in the bright, perfect right samadhi.</p>
<p>At this point the cultivator can no longer be harmed by demons or outer paths. <strong>Within samadhi</strong> the <strong>mind</strong> suddenly <strong>longs to know matters before and after</strong> &#8211; mysterious matters occurring very far away. For example, you are in San Francisco wishing to know what is happening in New York: if you can know it, you have attained what is called &#8220;<em>mysterious response</em>&#8221; (knowing distant secret matters).</p>
<p>Hence <strong>thoroughly, pervasively investigating, greedily seeking secret resonance.</strong> Focusing all the mind's refined power in study, greedily seeking secret resonance.</p>
<p>He focuses all his strength studying this mysterious matter, longing for mysterious resonance: for instance, sitting in meditation in San Francisco, he can clearly see relatives and friends working in New York and clearly hear what they are saying. Later he discovers what he heard occurred exactly at that time. That is the kind of <em>mysterious response</em> he wishes to attain.</p>
