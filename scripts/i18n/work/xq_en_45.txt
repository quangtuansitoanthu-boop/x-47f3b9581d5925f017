TITLE: Story 45: CONSIDERATIONS
TOMTAT: In the human world, there are evil deeds that do not shed blood, do not take lives, but silently accumulate karma throughout a lifetime. When people look at us, they only see plenty of food, warm clothes, family...

{align:center|**CONSIDERATION**}
In the human world, there are evil deeds that do not shed blood, do not take lives, but silently accumulate karma throughout a lifetime. When people look at them, they only see plenty of food and warm clothes, a warm family, and many children and grandchildren, but behind that is every gram of deceit, every ounce of deceit, every penny tainted with karma.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-45-image-509-cb8fbafd3e84.webp)
Scales - a seemingly small act in business - is an invisible knife that gradually cuts off a person's blessings and virtue, so that when the blessings run out and retribution comes, there is no way back. Today, in the journey of the Mission to Save the Dead, we listen to the story of a female ghost who lived nearly a century ago in modern times, who spent 40 years cheating and selling short, then had to face terrible punishments at prison door number 15 of the underworld. Believe it or not is everyone's right, but cause and effect never leaves anyone behind. 
The deceased girl was born into a poor family, with three siblings. At the age of 29, she got married in a foreign country and gave birth to five children. Times were difficult, her family had many mouths to feed, and she was forced to scrape by every job to survive. Initially, it was a small business, selling bamboo shoots and agricultural products, then gradually expanded to include rice, wine, dry goods, and purchasing goods from people in the area for resale. It is here that evil seeds begin to arise. 
She ordered a craftsman to custom-make a bronze scale that had been tampered with. Customers buy a kilo, but actually receive only seven or eight ounces. A little cheating today, a little shortcoming tomorrow, accumulates into forty years of endless suffering. Người mua nghèo khó, mất vài lạng gạo; She sold it, making a few more dollars. But she did not know that every time the balance tilted, the blessings and virtues in the book of the underworld also tilted. On the outside, her life remained peaceful. The family is peaceful, the children and grandchildren are happy, and the old age is comfortable. People look at it and admire it. But they don't know that because the blessing is great, the fruit hasn't bloomed yet, but it doesn't mean it doesn't exist. When your blessings run out, even if you live to be eighty years old, you will not be able to escape the day of being called by name. 
When she turned eighty years old, during a sales session like any other day, she held a familiar bronze scale to weigh for customers. Suddenly, a violent cough came over me. Máu từ trong miệng bà trào ra như suối, không sao cầm lại được. Her hand still held the scale tightly, blood flowed from her mouth, her body trembled, then in a panic, she slammed the scale against her chest as an unconscious reflex. Finally, she collapsed, her head hit the ground, and died on the spot.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-45-image-510-3c6be233eb42.webp)
The strange thing that shook the whole village was that the scale was stuck to her hand, the blood mixed with the metal, there was no way to remove it. Relatives tried to pull and remove it, but to no avail. In the end, she was buried with the scale - an object that had followed her throughout forty years of deceitful business. At that time, her soul was still floating in her body, and she could clearly hear people whispering: "If you weigh a bird, you will have to carry it with you when you die." Those words were like the last knife cut into my mind. And just as the cries of her children and grandchildren reached their peak, her soul burst out of her body. 
Before she could regain her composure, two demons ordered her to use an iron chain to drag her straight to the underworld. No explanation, no chance to escape. At the judgment hall, the crime was read clearly: torture for forty years, appropriating the property of countless people, including the poor and needy. She knelt down and cried, citing her large family and difficult life. But Lord Diem just coldly said: "Difficulties are not a reason to sow evil. To weigh an ounce of evil is to cut off a portion of happiness."![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-45-image-511-592c18183a95.webp)
With the amount of money appropriated so large, she was banished to prison door number 15 - a place that specializes in punishing those who do dishonest business. Ngay khi bước vào cửa ngục số 15, bà đã hiểu rằng mọi lời hối hận đều quá muộn. Before her eyes was a giant iron scale, heavy and cold. Quỷ sai ép bà nằm xuống, rồi chiếc cân từ từ ép từ trên xuống, nghiền nát thân thể như máy thủy lực. Bones are broken, flesh is crushed, pain is so much that the soul feels like it is being torn apart. But before she could completely die, she was revived to endure further pressure, repeated countless times. 
Not stopping there, she was forced to suck on red-hot iron coins. Each coin represents the illegal profits that have been appropriated. The red-hot metal burns the mouth and throat, but you still have to swallow the right amount. This punishment lasted seemingly indefinitely, because the amount of money she appropriated was so large. 
Finally, the punishment that haunted her the most: cutting off every fiber of flesh and placing it on a scale. The devil used a sharp knife to cut pieces of flesh from her body, placing them on a scale to "measure them fairly". Each cut made her scream in despair.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-45-image-512-da1610d7c413.webp)
Next to her, she saw an acquaintance - a person who mixed bad rice and sold it at a high price - whose intestines were slowly pulled out by an iron dog. The sound of metal chewing and moaning mixed together, making hell even more terrifying. She did not dare to continue talking. 
According to the words shared with my group: “There are no words on earth that can describe the horror down there.” Sau một thời gian dài chịu phạt, bà tạm được lên dương gian chờ ngày đầu thai. Trong 20 ngày lang thang, bà trú ngụ trên một chiếc võng người trần căng bỏ lại, đung đưa trong đêm vắng. Chính tại đó, bà gặp nhóm tôi tìm đến theo chỉ điểm của Tiên Ông. Được khai thị, bà chấp nhận theo về chùa tu học, mong chuộc lại phần nào nghiệp lực còn sót. 
Câu chuyện của vong nữ cân điêu là một lời cảnh tỉnh lạnh người cho những ai đang buôn bán gian dối. Đừng nghĩ rằng cân thiếu một chút, lời thêm một ít là vô hại. Một lạng gian hôm nay có thể đổi bằng cả đời đau khổ ngày mai. Phúc lớn thì quả đến chậm, nhưng không bao giờ mất. Làm ác không ai thấy, nhưng âm ti ghi đủ. Đừng đợi xuống địa ngục rồi mới hối hận, vì khi ấy đã không còn đường quay đầu. Death is not the end. Chết có khi chỉ là bước vào phiên tòa công bằng nhất của đời người.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-45-image-513-0851886b8e41.webp)
We hope that each of us, especially those who are doing business, should weigh enough - sell right away - live right, so that we don't have to meet each other in the darkness of prison. Finally, I wish everyone to stay awake, live a good life, practice diligently, so that this life and the next will be peaceful.

COMMENTS:
After reading CAN DIEU's story, I sat quietly for a long time. Not because of fear, but because we see one thing clearly: in each of us there are times of weakness, times when due to circumstances, pressure, or for some reason we have chosen paths that we can't help but feel sad when looking back on.

This story reminds me that Karma is not something far away or only exists after death. It is present in the small, everyday choices. A word, an action, a decision for "love" or for "money" - all are sowing seeds. And sooner or later, we will be the ones reaping the harvest.

But what warms my heart after every story like this is: it's never too late. Even though they have caused heavy karma, even though they have endured suffering for many years and many lifetimes, the spirits in the book still have the opportunity to let go, have the opportunity to be guided, repented and transformed. That is the power of mindfulness and compassion.For those of you reading these lines, perhaps you are not someone who has caused great karma like the character in the story. But each of us has times when we make mistakes, times when we hurt others because of selfishness or ignorance. This story is a gentle reminder: stop while you still can. Let's choose a way to live so that when night comes, we can sleep peacefully with a peaceful conscience.

An act of kindness, a sincere apology, a decision to live a good life today - no matter how small - can change the fate of ourselves and those around us for many lifetimes to come. That's what I want to convey after listening to this story.
