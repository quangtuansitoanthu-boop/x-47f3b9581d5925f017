{align:justify|Before I came to know the Buddha's teachings, I was very cruel by nature. Toward animals I felt no compassion, no pity, no sympathy whatsoever.}
{align:justify|More than twenty years ago, my whole family worked at a shoe factory, where a technical instructor, Mr. X, was very fond of eating dog meat. Once, Mr. X went out, bought a dog, and asked me to kill it for him. Feeling not the slightest hesitation, I smashed its head hard with an iron mallet. The dog howled pitifully, yet I remained utterly unmoved and kept striking blow after blow until its skull split open, blood gushed out, and it stopped twitching—only then did I stop.}
{align:justify|Moreover, whenever I happened upon a snake, I would devise every possible scheme to catch it, then crush its head with a rock. Before dying, the snake would writhe and roll in agony, while I watched calmly as though seeing nothing, my heart feeling not a shred of pity nor the slightest remorse for my inhumane cruelty.}
{align:justify|Later, I was afflicted with one painful illness after another. Seven years ago, while working on a road crew, a construction vehicle suddenly backed up; caught off guard, I was struck and suffered a brain injury. I had to be hospitalized and took a very long time to recover.}
{align:justify|After that, stomach pains flared up. I visited countless hospitals large and small and took every kind of Eastern and Western medicine, all to no avail.}
{align:justify|Finally, having no choice, I resorted to folk remedies people recommended. These recipes called for the flesh of many kinds of beings—dogs, cats, snakes, turtles, centipedes, chickens, ducks, and so on—steamed, boiled, stewed, or roasted together to take effect. As a result, I caused the deaths of even more living creatures. Yet after long years of taking all sorts of medicines, my illness not only failed to improve but grew steadily worse.}
{align:justify|Then I went to major hospitals for examination, where the doctors said that without immediate surgery my life could hardly be saved—so I had to undergo an operation. More than two-thirds of my stomach was removed, along with a section of my intestines, showing just how critical my condition was.}
{align:justify|Last year, while working at a factory, I carelessly let a stamping machine crush my finger to a bloody pulp. Blood flowed without ceasing and the pain pierced my heart; I wailed miserably and nearly fainted. Looking at my mangled, bleeding hand, my finger trembling uncontrollably, I suddenly recalled how I had once beaten snakes and killed dogs, with blood staining the whole yard red. And so I began to ask myself: "For more than ten years I have been tormented ceaselessly by strange illnesses and have met with every kind of crippling accident—can all of this be retribution for my past killing?"…}
