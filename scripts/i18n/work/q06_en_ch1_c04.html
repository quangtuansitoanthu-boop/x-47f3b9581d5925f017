Commentary: The sea of enlightenment, its nature clear and perfect. The sea of enlightenment is immensely vast, containing within it mountains, rivers, and lands, grasses, trees, and the myriad things. The nature of the sea of enlightenment is both still and perfect. Perfect, clear, the enlightened source sublime. The stillness and perfection of the sea of enlightenment are originally most sublime. But precisely within this sublimity, a single subtle thought of true-and-false arose, and the original brightness shone and produced something like an object (subject/object). The original brightness shone and produced the "object." The "object" established, the shining nature was lost. Fundamentally, the sea of enlightenment contains everything within it. There is nothing that is not within the Tathagatagarbha nature. But the moment a thought of ignorance arises, falseness springs forth. Once there is falseness, there is its object — sense-objects, false objective marks. Because of this false shining, the originally present brightness within the enlightened nature no longer shines, as when the sky is covered by clouds. Once the false objective realm is established, the true shining nature is also lost — that is, the originally present nature within the Tathagatagarbha is turned into the ignorance store-consciousness.

Sutra text: 迷妄有虛空 依空立世界 想澄成國土 知覺乃眾生。

Transliteration: Mê vọng hữu hư không Y không lập thế giới Tưởng trừng thành quốc độ Tri giác nãi chúng sanh.

Translation: In confused falseness there is empty space; Relying on space, worlds are established; Solidified thought becomes lands; Conscious knowing becomes beings.

Commentary: In confused falseness there is empty space. Because of falseness, subject and object (duality, objective/subjective) take shape. Originally, both aspects are false. Within the substance of the sea of enlightened nature, not a bit of phenomena manifests. Originally there is not a single thing. There is no subject or object. Only relying on the true does falseness arise — darkening into empty space, deluded about the nature of emptiness, turning it into stubborn emptiness, until there is entirely empty space; relying on empty space, all phenomenal worlds are falsely established.

"Gazing long, one grows weary." After extending for a time, the prolonging causes weariness. And then falseness and delusion arise into stubborn emptiness. Yet within the sea of enlightened nature there is originally nothing at all, not even empty space. The verse below describes:
Empty space arises within great enlightenment
Like a single bubble in the sea.
How obscure it truly is! And so we think of empty space as something immensely vast. Relying on space, worlds are established. The ten directions and three periods come into being.
Solidified thought becomes lands.
When time and space have arisen from empty space, false thought hardens; it makes dry land.
Conscious knowing becomes beings.
Solidified false thought結 forms lands. Conscious false thought of knowing forms beings.

Sutra text: 空生大覺中 如海一漚發 　有漏微塵國 皆從空所生 　漚滅空本無 況復諸三有。

Transliteration: Không sanh đại giác trung Như hải nhất âu phát, Hữu lậu vi trần quốc, Giai tòng không sở sanh, Âu diệt không bổn vô, Huống phục chư tam hữu.

Translation: Empty space arises within great enlightenment, Like a single bubble rising in the sea; Outflow lands as numerous as dust motes All arise from space. When the bubble bursts, space fundamentally is no more — How much less the Triple Existence!
