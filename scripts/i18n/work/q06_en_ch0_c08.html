This is the door of flowing — the direction of outflow.
When ignorance ceases, activity ceases;
When activity ceases, consciousness ceases;
When consciousness ceases, name-and-form cease;
When name-and-form cease, the six entrances cease;
When the six entrances cease, contact ceases;
When contact ceases, feeling ceases;
When feeling ceases, craving ceases;
When craving ceases, grasping ceases;
When grasping ceases, becoming ceases;
When becoming ceases, birth ceases;
When birth ceases, old age and death cease.

This is the door of cessation (the direction of still extinction). Arhat cultivators of the Way become very clear about ignorance and the connections leading to arising. From birth to death, beings are born and then die in turn. We end the wheel of birth and death by first extinguishing ignorance. When ignorance is entirely cut off, the other links successively dissolve. When they sever these links, they give rise to a superior nature. "They become those whose superior nature is perfected. I (Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva) appear before them in the body of a Condition-Enlightened One, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to attain liberation."

Sutra text: 若諸有學得四諦空。修道入滅勝性現圓。我於彼前現聲聞身。而為說法令其解脫。

Transliteration: Nhược chư hữu học đắc tứ đế không. Tu đạo nhập diệt thắng tánh hiện viên. Ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Thanh văn thân. Nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ giải thoát.

Translation: If there are learners who have attained the emptiness of the Four Noble Truths, cultivate the Way, enter extinction, with superior nature manifest in perfection, I appear before them in the body of a Hearers, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to attain liberation.

Commentary: If there are learners who have attained the emptiness of the Four Noble Truths, cultivate the Way, and enter extinction. They may be Hearers, Arhats, who have realized the emptiness-nature of the Four Noble Truths, cultivated the Truth of the Way, and certified to the wonderful bliss of nirvana. They have attained superior nature manifest in perfection; they have revealed the state of perfect penetration. I appear before them in the body of a Hearer, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to attain liberation.

Why does the Bodhisattva manifest a Hearer's body? Because only thus does he become like them, and mutual understanding comes easily. The Bodhisattva becomes a good friend, and much mutual trust is established. If one has no relationship with people, then no matter what one says, they will not believe. The Bodhisattva, as it were, befriends them in order to teach them. Hearers certify to the Way by hearing the Buddhas' discourses. They are those of the Two Vehicles.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生。欲心明悟。不犯欲塵欲身清淨。我於彼前現梵王身。而為說法令其解脫。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh, dục tâm minh ngộ, bất phạm dục trần, dục thân thanh tịnh. Ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Phạm vương thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ giải thoát.

Translation: If there are living beings whose minds wish for clear awakening, who do not offend against sense-objects of desire and wish their bodies to be pure, I appear before them in the body of a Brahma King, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to attain liberation.
