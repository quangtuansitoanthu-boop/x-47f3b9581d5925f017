Once you have certified to the Dharma-nature of a sage, there is nothing you do not understand. Whatever accords is expedient, and whatever opposes is also expedient. Whatever suits your capacity and circumstances, you may apply in cultivation. What is "opposing" — what does not suit one's circumstances yet is still applied in cultivation. Both accord and opposition in one's cultivation effort are expedient dharmas.

Beginners entering samadhi
Differ in quickness and slowness.

Some cultivate very quickly, some slowly. So they cannot be regarded as of one kind.

Sutra text: 色想結成塵 精了不能徹 如何不明徹 於是獲圓通。

Transliteration: Sắc tưởng kết thành trần Tinh liễu bất năng triệt, Như hà bất minh triệt, Ư thị hoạch viên thông

Translation: Form and thought combine to make objects; Their essence cannot penetrate. How from what is not penetrating Can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Form is made from false thought. Form and false thought combine together into "objects." That is form-objects. Their essence cannot penetrate. It is not clear and cannot be fully known.
How from what is not penetrating
Can one attain perfect penetration?
Could you hope to certify to perfect penetration with a method that is neither clear nor thorough? That is impossible. The verse tells us that using form-objects to cultivate perfect penetration is fundamentally not the best method. Some people may certify to fruition through this method, but only because they have special affinities with that particular dharma-door. That is not something everyone can cultivate.

Sutra text: 音聲雜語言 但伊名句味 　一非含一切 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Âm thinh tạp ngữ ngôn Đãn y danh cú vị Nhất phi hàm nhất thiết Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Sound mixed with speech Relies only on words and phrases; Since the one does not contain the all, How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Some people certify to the Way by means of sound-objects. However,
Sound mixed with speech
Relies only on words and phrases.
If the one does not contain the all —
No single word, no single name, no single sentence can speak of everything. Then
How can one attain perfect penetration?
So sound-objects cannot be chosen as the method for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 香以合中知 離則元無有 不恒其所覺 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Hương dĩ hợp trung tri Ly tắc nguyên vô hữu Bất hằng kỳ sở giác Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Fragrance is known only in union; Apart, it fundamentally does not exist. Its object of awareness does not constantly abide — How can one attain perfect penetration?
