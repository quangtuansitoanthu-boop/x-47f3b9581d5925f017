<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ bối tu thiền, sức tam ma địa. Thập phương bồ-tát cập chư vô lậu đại A-la-hán , tâm tinh thông vẫn, đương xứ trạm nhiên. </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: When you cultivate Chan and attain samadhi, the minds of the Bodhisattvas and the great unoutflowing Arhats of the ten directions, their essences spiritually interfused, abide in stillness right where they are.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: When <strong>you</strong> concentrate on <strong>cultivating Chan</strong> and <strong>attain samadhi</strong>, your true <strong>mind becomes spiritually interfused, uniting with the true minds of the Bodhisattvas and the great unoutflowing Arhats throughout the ten directions who abide in stillness</strong>.</p>
<p>You need not seek still purity anywhere else &#8211; it is right where you are. The still purity right in your own body and mind is precisely the Tathagata-store nature, pervading the entire Dharma realm.</p>
<p>Why do the minds of the Bodhisattvas and Arhats interfuse with those who cultivate Chan? Because they cultivate the same great samadhi &#8211; all using the method of &#8220;<em>returning the hearing to hear the self-nature</em>&#8221; &#8211; turning the hearing inward to recognize one's own nature until the Buddha Way is accomplished.</p>
<p>They all cultivate and practice the Great Shurangama Samadhi, and so their minds are completely interfused, secretly united &#8211; as if an electric current connected them to one another.</p>
<p>This connection occurs not only at the level of Buddhas, Bodhisattvas, and Arhats, but often among ordinary people as well. For example, when you think of someone, your thought contacts that person like sending a telegram. Does the other person know? Ask that person's inherent nature &#8211; it clearly knows, though their conscious mind may know nothing. On the level of substance, one can indeed know another's mind.</p>
<p>&#8220;Then if I think of someone, does that person likewise think of me?&#8221;</p>
<p>You may think until you die, to no avail. It is like the infatuated love I lectured on before: if a person is infatuated, he dwells on scenes of lustful love all day long, never able to stop those false thoughts &#8211; alone he thinks until he dies. How does he die? He thinks of a woman until the two marry. Once married they become muddled and confused; after living a while in muddle and confusion, they die. Marriage means living in muddle and confusion, without a shred of clarity.</p>
<p>In Chinese you can infer the meaning of the two characters for <em>marriage</em>, 結 婚 (jie hun): <em>hun</em> 婚 means darkness.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhất thiết ma vương, cập dữ quỷ thần, chư phàm phu thiên, kiến kỳ cung điện vô cố băng liệt, đại địa chấn sách, thủy lục phi đằng, vô bất kinh nhiếp. Phàm phu hôn ám, bất giác thiên ngoa.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: All demon kings, ghosts, spirits, and ordinary gods, seeing their palaces for no reason crumble and fall, the earth quaking and splitting, creatures of water and land flying and leaping in terror, are all seized with fright. Dull ordinary people are unaware of these upheavals.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: When a person attains the Great Shurangama Samadhi, the true great samadhi, the demon hosts tremble in terror. <strong>All demon kings, ghosts, spirits, and ordinary gods</strong> of the six Desire heavens and the heavens of the Four Dhyanas <strong>see their palaces for no reason crumble and fall</strong> &#8211; a truly incomprehensible situation, with no clear cause for their palaces suddenly falling into ruin.</p>
<p>I once told you about a young disciple of mine in Manchuria: only about fourteen years old, yet his spiritual powers were no small matter &#8211; he could ascend to the heavens and descend to earth at will. Although his five eyes were opened, he had not yet realized the six powers; he had five spiritual powers, lacking only the extinction of outflows. When a person attains the extinction-of-outflows power, he is called an Arhat.</p>
<p>One day he went up to the heavens to play. Up there the heavenly demons were delighted and lured him into their palace &#8211; a magnificent castle made of transparent crystal. The young novice fell into the trap.</p>
<p>Since he had the five eyes, he could see that his Dharma-body had been detained by the King of the Paranirmita-Vashavartin Heaven. Yet he still found a way to inform me: &#8220;Master, I went up to the heavens to play and now cannot return.&#8221;</p>
<p>&#8220;So you are stuck in the heavens! Hmph! Who told you to go up there in the first place?&#8221;</p>
<p>&#8220;I thought it would be great fun, so I went up to have a look. Now the people here do not want to let me back.&#8221;</p>
<p>I said: &#8220;If you want amusement, do not go up there looking for fun. The heavenly demons of the Desire Realm heavens all seek opportunities to destroy a cultivator's samadhi power.&#8221; I explained: &#8220;Do not fear &#8211; I shall bring you back.&#8221;</p>
<p>I tried to bring him back, but the demon king would not let him go. By then he was truly frightened and said: &#8220;It won't let me return. What now?&#8221; I said: &#8220;Do not worry &#8211; I have a way to bring you right back.&#8221;</p>
<p>Then I used the Shurangama Mantra &#8211; the fivefold heart mantras for dispelling demonic obstructions that I had already taught you. The demon king's palace instantly disintegrated, and the young novice returned at that very moment. This is a true story.</p>
<p>At that time the demon king's palace crumbled and collapsed. <strong>The earth quaked</strong> and split in many places. <strong>Creatures of water and land flying and leaping in terror.</strong></p>
<p>All creatures in the water, on the ground, flying and leaping, were frightened into uncontrollable panic.</p>
<p><strong>Dull ordinary people are unaware of these upheavals</strong>.</p>
<p>Ordinary people lack keen perception; they do not notice the earth undergoing such transformations, lacking sufficient sensitivity to perceive the six kinds of quaking occurring within the earth.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Bỉ đẳng hàm đắc ngũ chủng thần thông, duy trừ lậu tận, luyến thử trần lao. Như hà linh nhữ tồi liệt kỳ xứ? Thị cố quỷ thần cập chư thiên ma, võng lượng yêu tinh, ư tam muội thời thiêm lai não nhữ.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: These beings all possess five kinds of spiritual powers, lacking only the extinction of outflows, and cling to this worldly toil. How could they let you destroy their dwelling? Therefore ghosts, spirits, heavenly demons, goblins, sprites, and sprites all wish to come torment you while you cultivate samadhi.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: Now do you understand why demons come to harass? Only because these <strong>beings all possess five kinds of spiritual powers</strong> &#8211; the heavenly eye, heavenly ear, knowledge of others' minds, knowledge of past lives, and spiritual travel &#8211; <strong>lacking only the extinction of outflows</strong>. Had they the extinction-of-outflows power, they would no longer trouble you. But lacking it, they remain evil and still wish to come sabotage cultivators of the Way&#8230; What does that mean? I shall explain this very thoroughly.</p>
<p>What is called <em>outflow</em> is scattered, wandering thoughts: men thinking of women, women thinking of men. If you do not end these wandering thoughts, you can never escape outflows by yourself.</p>
