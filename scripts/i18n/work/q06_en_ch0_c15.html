Sutra text: 若有女人內政立身以修家國。我於彼前現女主身。及國夫人命婦大家。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược hữu nữ nhơn nội chánh lập thân, dĩ tu gia quốc, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện nữ chủ thân, cập quốc phu nhân mạng phụ đại gia, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there is a woman who establishes herself in domestic administration to regulate the family or the nation, I appear before her in the body of a queen, or in the bodies of a king's consort, a titled lady, or a noblewoman, speak Dharma for her, and help her accomplish her wish.

Commentary: If there is a woman who establishes herself in domestic administration to regulate the family or the nation. Sometimes these women manage household affairs; sometimes they hold power in the court, administering the nation's business. I appear before her in the body of a queen, or in the bodies of a king's consort, a titled lady, or a noblewoman, speak Dharma for her, and help her accomplish her wish.

Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva may manifest as a woman administering state affairs, or as the wife of a ruler. Sometimes the Bodhisattva manifests as a powerful titled lady or an elegant, refined woman of society. In all these cases, Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva responds to their wishes.

Sutra text: 若有眾生不壞男根。我於彼前現童男身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược hữu chúng sanh bất hoại nam căn, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện đồng nam thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings whose male faculty is intact, I appear before them in the body of a young boy, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings whose male faculty is intact. For young men who have never known women and are still pure, I appear before them in the body of a young boy, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Sutra text: 若有處女愛樂處身不求侵暴。我於彼前現童女身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược hữu xử nữ ái nhạo xử thân, bất cầu xâm bạo, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện đồng nữ thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there is a maiden who delights in remaining a virgin and does not seek violation, I appear before her in the body of a young girl, speak Dharma for her, and help her accomplish her wish.

Commentary: If there is a maiden — meaning a woman who has never known a man and has never married. Who delights in remaining a virgin and does not seek violation. They do not wish to be intimate with men, to marry, or to undergo the experience of being violated. I appear before her in the body of a young girl, speak Dharma for her, and help her accomplish her wish.

Sutra text: 若有諸天樂出天倫。我現天身而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược hữu chư thiên nhạo xuất thiên luân, ngã hiện thiên thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are gods who delight in transcending their heavenly kind, I manifest a heavenly body, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are gods who delight in transcending their heavenly kind. These goddesses and gods no longer wish to remain in the heavens; they wish to escape the Triple Realm. I manifest a heavenly body, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish. Because they wish to transcend the heavens, I manifest before them, using every expedient to help them obtain what they desire.
