&#8220;Form is not different from emptiness; emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness; emptiness is form.&#8221;</p>
<p>The bliss of still extinction &#8211; of still emptiness &#8211; is more than ten million times greater than material pleasure. If you wish to obtain it, you must first renounce craving for material things. If you refuse to let go of outer objects, the true emptiness within you cannot appear.</p>
<p>Here, the gods have reached the realm of pure emptiness, and their body and mind have become light and at ease. They have attained the stage and understood: &#8220;Form is not different from emptiness; emptiness is not different from form. Form is emptiness; emptiness is form.&#8221;<br />
This cannot be faked. You cannot claim to have attained this stage if you have not. It cannot be experienced through words &#8211; only by actually attaining the stage. It is like a person drinking water: only the one who drinks knows whether the water is cold or hot. Those who have attained this stage of still extinction dwell in the Limitless Purity Heaven.</p>


<ol start="3">
<li><strong>Thế giới thân tâm, nhất thiết viên tịnh. Tịnh đức thành tựu, thắng thác hiện tiền, quy tịch diệt lạc. Như thị nhất loại, danh biến tịnh Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>The world, the body, and the mind are all perfectly pure; pure virtue accomplished, superior states manifesting before them, returning to the bliss of still extinction; such a class is called the Pervasive Purity Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;The world, the body, and the mind are all perfectly pure; pure virtue accomplished.&#8221; In the heavens discussed before, only the body and mind were pure. The cultivators in the heaven spoken of here have attained spiritual powers such that they can transform the very ground: the earth is full of defilement, yet they can turn it into ultimate bliss. This principle is the same as in the saying:<br />
The true mind is the Pure Land;<br />
One's own nature is Amitabha Buddha.</p>
<p>If the mind is pure, the Pure Land is pure. If the mind is not pure, you will not see the purity of the Pure Land. If you dwell in the Pure Land yet do nothing but weep all day long, where is the happiness in that? If you are in the Saha world and are joyful from morning to night, then you are as if in the Land of Ultimate Bliss. The principle here is &#8220;all dharmas are mind-only.&#8221; The truth remains right there: if you can see through things and let go of the myriad things, you will be at ease and free. If you cannot see through things to recognize their nature and cannot put them down, you will not find tranquillity in happiness.</p>
<p>When the gods attain this stage, they gain purity in body, mind, and will, and in the nature of the myriad things &#8211; the light of their own nature. Then &#8220;superior states manifest before them.&#8221; How are these superior states to be explained? They are superior states appearing within the mind.</p>
<p>They &#8220;return to the bliss of still extinction; such a class is called the Pervasive Purity Heaven.&#8221; There is nothing that is not real. They can purify everything among the myriad dharmas.</p>
<p><strong>A-nan! Thử tam thắng lưu, cụ đại tùy thuận. Thân tâm an ổn, đắc vô lượng lạc. Tuy phi chánh đắc, chân tam-ma-địa. an ổn tâm trung, hoan hỉ tất cụ, danh vi tam Thiền.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda! These three superior levels are replete with great compliant conduct; body and mind are peaceful and secure, attaining limitless bliss. Though it is not a true attainment of genuine samadhi, in the peaceful and secure mind joy is fully present; this is called the Third Dhyana.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda! These three superior levels are replete with great compliant conduct.&#8221; This means they can understand the intentions of others and make others happy. &#8220;Body and mind are peaceful and secure, attaining limitless bliss.&#8221; They no longer have false thoughts in mind, and their bodies no longer chase after false thoughts. They have gained purity in body and mind. Those in the First and Second Dhyanas had let go of affliction, suffering, worry, and anxiety; those in the Third Dhyana no longer experience such afflictions at all. Their body and mind are pure, peaceful, and blissful.</p>
<p>They are also trustworthy. How are they trustworthy? They have no lustful thoughts. They no longer have to think of seeking the opposite sex in every thought and every act the way ordinary beings do. When the innermost mind is not yet peaceful, false thought arises upon false thought as each one ceases.</p>
<p>&#8220;Oh, she is so beautiful,&#8221; or &#8220;He is so handsome&#8221; &#8211; thinking of such things all day long. But when body and mind are pure &#8211; the purity described here &#8211; such fantasies no longer arise. Everything comes back to this one matter. I speak of it again and again, but in the final analysis it all comes down to karma because of this one thing: the single thought of ignorance, the first ignorant thought, which has stirred up so many calamities with their consequences. Love and lust begin from ignorance. That is why the first link mentioned in the twelve links of conditioned origination is ignorance. From ignorance comes volitional action, and from action comes consciousness; when consciousness appears, name and form appear. It all starts right there.</p>
<p>Although those described here have not yet extinguished ignorance, they have gained boundless happiness. &#8220;Though it is not a true attainment of genuine samadhi, in the peaceful and secure mind joy is fully present.&#8221; They do not yet have the great samadhi, but in purity they have bliss. They are called the Third Dhyana. When you attain the stage of the Third Dhyana, ideas no longer arise; you realize the truth of still extinction. There is a saying:<br />
When not a single thought arises, the whole substance appears;<br />
When the six sense organs stir, they are covered by clouds.</p>
<p>When not a single thought arises, the inherent Buddha-nature appears &#8211; hence &#8220;the whole substance appears.&#8221; When the eyes see and the ears listen and are moved, they become covered. As soon as the six sense organs, six sense objects, and six consciousnesses stir, they are covered by clouds, obscuring one's own nature, the true self. Before attaining the stage now under discussion, a person's thinking is still active. For example, when the pulse stops, thought still remains,</p>
<p>&#8220;How did the pulse stop?&#8221; &#8211; and with that single thought arising, the pulse starts beating again. When the breath stops, the thought &#8220;I have stopped breathing&#8221; arises, and with that thought the breath returns. That is what happens before thought has stopped. Now, in the Third Dhyana, there is no such thing: whether the pulse stops beating or the breath stops, whatever happens, they pay no attention. Thoughts no longer arise &#8211; not a single notion; none can be found. Hence the saying, &#8220;When not a single thought arises, the whole substance appears.&#8221; This is the state of the Third Dhyana stage.</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE FOURTH DHYANA</strong></p>

<ol>
<li><strong>A-nan! Thứ phục Thiên Nhân. Bất bức thân tâm, khổ nhân dĩ tận. Lạc phi thường trụ, cửu tất hoại sanh. Khổ lạc nhị tâm, câu thời đốn xả. Thô trọng tướng diệt, tịnh phước tánh sanh. Như thị nhất loại, danh phước sanh thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Further, Ananda! In this heaven body and mind are not oppressed; the causes of suffering have ended. Knowing that bliss is not permanent, that decay is certain after long abiding, both states of mind &#8211; suffering and bliss &#8211; are simultaneously renounced; coarse and heavy appearances cease, and the nature of pure blessings arises; such a class is called the Blessed Birth Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Further, Ananda! In this heaven body and mind are not oppressed; the causes of suffering have ended.&#8221; By this stage, suffering, hardship, worry, and obstruction no longer oppress these cultivators physically or mentally.</p>
