In this world, every kind has its disciples. So there is a saying:
Good people seek one another.
Bad people form cliques.
Birds of a feather flock together.
So even these deviant demons and goblins gather together and have their retinues.
They all claim to have perfected the unsurpassed Way.

They do not recognize what is truly unsurpassed; instead, they suppose their own way of life is best. They say they have attained the unsurpassed Way, to the point of saying reciting the Buddha's name is wasted effort, and saying they are just so. They argue: "Behold my spiritual powers."
In fact, they are demons, ghosts, and goblins. They entirely follow deviant conduct.

Sutra text: 我滅度後末法之中,多此妖邪熾盛世間, 潛匿姦欺,稱善知識。各自謂已得上人法。詃惑無識,恐令失心。所過之處其家耗散。

Transliteration: Ngã diệt độ hậu mạt pháp chi trung, đa thử yêu tà xí thạnh thế gian, tiềm nặc gian khi, xưng thiện tri thức. Các tự vị dĩ đắc thượng nhơn pháp. Huyễn hoặc vô thức, khủng linh thất tâm. Sở quá chi xứ kỳ gia hao tán.

Translation: After my extinction, in the Dharma-Ending Age, many such demons and deviants will blaze across the world, secretly cheating, calling themselves good spiritual teachers. Each claims to have attained the dharmas of superior people. Deceiving the ignorant, frightening them into losing their resolve. Wherever they pass, families are ruined.

Commentary: I have met very many such deviant demons. Westerners are not very familiar with such goblins, but not because Chinese believe in ghosts and spirits. Indeed, more and more, strange phenomena occur in the world.

After my extinction, in the Dharma-Ending Age, many such demons and deviants will blaze across the world.

Shakyamuni Buddha in this sutra told us that the age we are living in will be plagued by such deviant demons. We should not look only at what we see to believe they exist. Simply because there are many things in the world we have not seen. If we must wait until we see each thing with our own eyes, we could never see them all in this life. There are some things you must learn from others' words.

They blaze across the world. They are like fire burning people. Those who do not recognize such deviant demons will become entangled with them, as if stepping into a blazing fire. These people will be burned. "Secretly" means they go about everywhere cheating people.

They secretly cheat, calling themselves good spiritual teachers. Each claims to have attained the dharmas of superior people.

They proclaim themselves bright-eyed good spiritual teachers. "Superior people" refers to Bodhisattvas. In other words, they proclaim themselves Bodhisattvas. In Buddhism, though you are a Bodhisattva, even a Buddha manifesting, you should not say you are a Buddha or a Bodhisattva. You must keep silent about it while you live, until your last breath.
