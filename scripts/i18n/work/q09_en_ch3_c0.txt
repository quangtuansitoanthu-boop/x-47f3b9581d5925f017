<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE FEELING SKANDHA</strong></p>

<p style="text-align:center">THE MENTAL STATE BEFORE ENTERING THE FEELING SKANDHA</p>
<p><strong><em>Sutra text: A-nan, bỉ thiện nam tử, tu tam-ma-đề, xa ma tha trung, sắc âm tận giả, kiến chư Phật tâm, như minh kính trung, hiển hiện kì tượng.</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Ananda, that good man cultivating samadhi, within shamatha, with the form skandha exhausted, sees the Buddhas' minds as images appearing in a bright mirror.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Ananda, that good man cultivating samadhi.</strong></p>
<p>Cultivating the effort of &#8220;<em>returning the hearing to hear the self-nature,</em>&#8221; turning the hearing inward to recognize one's own nature &#8211; cultivating <em>samadhi</em> means cultivating right concentration: cultivating <em>the perfect penetration of the ear organ</em>, the effort of &#8220;<em>returning the hearing to hear the self-nature,</em>&#8221; turning the hearing inward to recognize one's own nature &#8211; the effort for attaining perfect, penetrating wisdom through the ear organ. When <strong>cultivating the shamatha Dharma</strong>, purifying the mind, transforming all false thoughts to focus on contemplation &#8211; when the <strong>form skandha is utterly exhausted</strong>, one can <strong>see the Buddhas' mind-seal Dharma as if seeing oneself in a mirror</strong>.</p>
<p>What happens when you dissolve the entire form skandha? You can see the Buddhas' mind-seal Dharma as if seeing yourself in a mirror.</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE SCOPE OF THE FEELING SKANDHA</strong></p>
<p><strong><em> Sutra text: Nhược hữu sở đắc, nhi vị năng dụng. Do như yểm nhân, thủ túc uyển nhiên kiến văn bất hoặc, tâm xúc khách tà, nhi bất năng động. Thử tắc danh vi thọ ấm khu vũ.</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: As if having gained something, yet unable to use it &#8211; like a person pressed by a nightmare-demon: limbs intact, seeing and hearing undeluded, yet the mind afflicted by an intruding evil, unable to move. This is called the region of the feeling skandha.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>As if having gained something, yet unable to use it</strong>. <strong>Like a person pressed by a nightmare-demon.</strong></p>
<p>I spoke before of the kumbhanda ghosts &#8211; a kind of nightmare ghosts that often use mantras to spell people sick to death. When a person is possessed by such a ghost, the limbs remain intact, not lost; seeing and hearing are undeluded and unconfused, yet the mind is controlled by the flock of evils to the point of being unable to move. This is called the region of the <em>feeling skandha</em>.</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE ROOT OF THE FEELING SKANDHA</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Sutra text: Nhược yểm cữu yết, kì tâm li thân, phản quán kì diện, khứ trụ tự do, vô phục lưu ngại. Danh thọ ấm  tận. Thị nhân tắc năng siêu việt kiến trược. Quán kì sở do, hư minh vọng tưởng, dĩ vi kì bản.</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: When the nightmare lifts, the mind leaves the body, looks back upon one's own face, going and staying at will, with no more obstruction. This is called the exhaustion of the feeling skandha. Such a person can then transcend the defilement of views. Contemplate its cause: empty, bright false thought is its root.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>When</strong> the paralysis of the nightmare <strong>eases and the mind leaves the body</strong>, <strong>looking back upon one's own face</strong>.</p>
<p>While still within the region of the feeling skandha, it is like being paralyzed by a kumbhanda ghost, so you cannot be at ease. If this condition vanishes and the feeling skandha is wholly removed, the mind escapes the body and you can see your own face, <strong>going and staying at will</strong> &#8211; going or not going as you wish, entirely unbound, unobstructed. <strong>This is called the exhaustion of the feeling skandha</strong>.</p>
<p>When the feeling skandha has dissolved, <strong>such a person can transcend the defilement of views</strong> &#8211; one of the five turbidities of the evil age.</p>
<p><strong>Contemplate its cause: empty, bright false thought is the root of the feeling skandha</strong>. When the feeling skandha is utterly destroyed, its root vanishes as well.</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE 10 STATES OF THE FEELING SKANDHA</strong></p>

<p style="text-align:center">* * *</p>

<p style="text-align:center"><strong> 1 &#8211; EXCESSIVE COMPASSION (THE DEMON OF COMPASSION)</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Sutra text: A-nan bỉ thiện nam tử, đương  tại thử trung, đắc đại quang diệu, kỳ tâm phát minh, nội ức quá phần. Hốt ư kì xứ, phát vô cùng bi, như thị nãi chí, quán  kiến văn manh, do như xích tử , tâm sinh lân mẫn, bất giác lưu lệ.</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Ananda, that good man, right within this state, gaining great bright light, his mind enlightened, inwardly suppressing excessively &#8211; suddenly, right there, gives rise to boundless compassion, to the point of seeing mosquitoes and gnats as newborn babes, his heart filling with pity, weeping unawares.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Ananda, that good man, right within this state, his mind very bright, inwardly suppressing excessively.</strong> A kind of emotion suddenly arises: he has continuously restrained thought to excess, and with inner repression overdone, <strong>suddenly gives rise to boundless compassion</strong> and fondness for all beings, <strong>to the point of seeing mosquitoes and gnats as newborn babes</strong> &#8211; <em>newborn babe</em> in Chinese is <em>chi zi</em>, because a newborn's skin is red.</p>
<p><strong>His heart filling with pity, weeping unawares</strong> &#8211; not knowing why he bursts into tears.</p>
<p><strong><em>Sutra text: Thử danh công dụng  ức tồi quá việt. Ngộ tắc vô cữu, phi vi thánh chứng. Giác liễu bất mê, cửu tự tiêu yết. Nhược tác thánh giải, tắc hữu bi ma  nhập kì tâm phủ, kiến nhân tắc bi  đề khấp vô hạn. Thất ư chánh thọ, đương tòng luân trụy.</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is called excessive repression of the mind in the course of effort. Recognized, it is without fault &#8211; not sage realization. Awakened and undeluded, in time it vanishes of itself. But if interpreted as sagehood, a demon of compassion enters the very heart, and on seeing people one pities them, weeping without restraint. Losing right samadhi, one is sure to fall.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>This is called excessive repression of the mind in the course of effort</strong>.</p>
<p>This state can arise during effort in cultivation &#8211; it occurs because you have repressed the mind excessively. <strong>Recognized, it is without fault,</strong> <strong>not sage realization. If awakened and undeluded, in time it vanishes of itself</strong> &#8211; if you realize you have erred and silently ask yourself why you weep so groundlessly, then change at once. Realizing this, the tension will no longer remain.</p>
<p>This state is not sage realization &#8211; it does not mean you have realized the mind of <em>same-substance great compassion</em>. Do not think that pitying mosquitoes, gnats, and tiny insects as your own newborn babes is the mind of <em>same-substance great compassion</em>.</p>
