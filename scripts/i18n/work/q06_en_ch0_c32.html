Commentary: Whatever beings of the six paths in the ten directions wish for is all bestowed. Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva knows that everyone wishes for a virtuous wife. Those who seek wives obtain wives; if they hope for an obedient, virtuous wife, they at once obtain an obedient, virtuous wife. That is the highest wish of all beings. Once they have a virtuous wife, they then wish for children. Thus the second thing Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva does is bestow children upon them. Those who seek children obtain children; if they hope to bear sons, at once they obtain sons; if they hope for daughters, at once they obtain daughters.

Third, he says: those who seek samadhi obtain samadhi; who seek long life obtain long life. If people who already have wives and children feel that human life is truly meaningless, and so bring forth the resolve to cultivate to escape the world and hope for samadhi, then they will obtain samadhi. Those who hope for long life will obtain long life. They wish to become immortal and never die; they will have it so. Thus even unto seeking great nirvana, they obtain great nirvana. If any beings hope to become Buddhas, they can attain Buddhahood.

Sutra text: 佛問圓通,我從耳門圓照三昧, 緣心自在。因入流相,得三摩 提,成就菩提。斯為第一。

Transliteration: Phật vấn viên thông, ngã tòng nhĩ môn viên chiếu tam muội, duyên tâm tự tại. Nhân nhập lưu tướng, đắc tam-ma-đề, thành tựu bồ-đề. Tư vi đệ nhất.

Translation: The Buddha asks about perfect penetration; from the ear-door I attained the samadhi of perfect illumination, and my conditioned mind became at ease. Through the mark of entering the flow, I attained samadhi and perfected bodhi. This is foremost.

Commentary: The Buddha asks about perfect penetration. The Buddha asked his disciples by what expedient they first certified to perfect penetration. From the ear-door I attained the samadhi of perfect illumination. I cultivated at the hearing-nature and perfected samadhi power. And my conditioned mind became at ease. Through the mark of entering the flow, I attained samadhi and perfected bodhi. This is foremost. My conditioned mind is no more; I am at ease. I turned the hearing back to recognize the self-nature, and perfected the unsurpassed Way. This is the best method.

Sutra text: 世尊彼佛如來。歎我善得圓通法門。於大會中授記我為觀世音號。

Transliteration: Thế tôn bỉ Phật Như Lai thán ngã thiện đắc viên thông pháp môn. Ư đại hội trung thọ ký ngã vi Quán thế âm hiệu.

Translation: World Honored One, that Buddha, the Tathagata, praised me for well attaining the dharma-door of perfect penetration. In the great assembly he bestowed upon me the name Avalokiteshvara.

Commentary: World Honored One, that Buddha, the Tathagata, praised me for well attaining the dharma-door of perfect penetration. "That Buddha, the Tathagata" refers to the Ancient Buddha, the Tathagata Avalokiteshvara. He praised me for having certified to perfect penetration at ease. Thus in the great assembly he bestowed upon me the name Avalokiteshvara. He called me the one who "regards the sounds of the world" — the same name as his own.

Sutra text: 由我觀聽十方圓明,故觀音名遍十方界.

Transliteration: Do ngã quán thính thập phương viên minh, cố quán âm danh biến thập phương giới.

Translation: Because my regarding and hearing are perfectly luminous throughout the ten directions, the name Guanshiyin pervades the realms of the ten directions.
