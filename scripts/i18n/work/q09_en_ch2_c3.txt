<li><strong> JEWEL-COLORED LIGHT FILLING SPACE</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Sutra text:  Hựu dĩ thử tâm, tinh nghiên diệu minh, quán  sát bất đình. Ức án, hàng phục, chế chỉ  siêu việt. Ư thời hốt nhiên, thập phương hư không, thành thất bảo sắc, hoặc bách bảo sắc. Đồng thời biến mãn, bất tương lưu ngại. Thanh hoàng xích bạch, các các thuần hiện. Thử danh ức án, công lực du phận. Tạm đắc như thị, phi vi thánh chứng. Bất tác thánh tâm, danh thiện cảnh giới. Nhược tác thánh giải, tức thọ quần tà.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Again with this mind, intently investigating the wondrous brightness, contemplating without cease &#8211; suppressing, subduing, restraining to excess. Then suddenly the empty space of the ten directions turns the colors of seven jewels, or the colors of a hundred jewels, appearing everywhere at once without obstructing one another. Blue, yellow, red, white each distinctly appear. This is called excessive effort in suppressing the mind. Temporarily gained thus, it is not sage realization. If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a good state; if you interpret it as sagehood, you fall among the deviant.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Again with this mind, intently investigating the wondrous brightness, contemplating without cease &#8211; suppressing, subduing, restraining to excess.</strong> Striving to restrain and subdue one's false mind until it cannot reach transcendence.</p>
<p><strong>Suddenly the empty space of the ten directions turns the colors of seven jewels, or the colors of a hundred jewels, appearing everywhere in space at once without obstructing one another</strong> &#8211; without hindering one another at all.</p>
<p><strong>Blue, yellow, red, white colors all distinctly appear, each displaying its own distinct hue. This state is called: excessive effort in subduing the false mind.</strong></p>
<p>You apply effort subduing the false mind, not letting false thoughts intrude, not letting stray thoughts arise. After such restraint goes on a long time, it becomes excessively tense &#8211; you have exceeded the normal measure that should be.</p>
<p><strong>Temporarily gained thus, it is not sage realization.</strong> You only temporarily see the seven-jeweled colors in space &#8211; so this state does not mean you have realized the sage fruition.</p>
<p><strong>If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a good state; but if you think you have realized sagehood, you fall into deviant views.</strong></p>
<p>It will be completely fine, not a bad situation. But if you think you are a sage, you fall into deviant views &#8211; surrounded by a flock of deviant views, and you will fall.</p>


<ol start="6">
<li><strong> NIGHT TURNED INTO DAY</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu dĩ thử tâm, nghiên cứu trừng triệt tinh quang bất loạn. Hốt ư dạ bán, tại ám thất nội, kiến chủng chủng vật, bất thù bạch trú, nhi ám thất vật, diệc bất trừ diệt. Thử danh tâm tế, mật trừng kỳ kiến, sở thân đỗng u. Tạm đắc như thị, phi vi thánh chứng. Bất tác thánh tâm, danh thiện cảnh giới. Nhược tác thánh giải, tức thọ quần tà.</strong></p>
<p><strong> Translation: Again with this mind, investigating to pure clarity, the essential light unconfused. Suddenly at midnight, within a dark room, one sees all kinds of things no differently than in daytime, while the things in the dark room do not disappear. This is called the mind refined to see through the dark dwelling. Temporarily gained thus, it is not sage realization. If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a good state; if you interpret it as sagehood, you fall among the deviant.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Again with this mind, investigating to pure clarity, the essential light unconfused.</strong></p>
<p>Again, when one settles the mind to contemplate all states until the spiritual light of the inherent mind becomes very calm and samadhi power is attained.</p>
<p><strong>Suddenly at midnight, one can see all kinds of things appearing within a dark room.</strong></p>
<p>In a house with light, at night <strong>things are no different than in daytime, while the things in the dark room do not disappear.</strong></p>
<p>Not only can you see the things inside the house &#8211; you can also see things outside the house. Not only can you see the objects already in the house &#8211; you can also see very clearly things coming from outside appearing in the house.</p>
<p>This state is called: <strong>&#8220;the mind settled, vision clarified, seeing through the dark dwelling.&#8221;</strong></p>
<p>Your mind is refined to the utmost and your vision purified to the extreme; with such transparent vision you can see everything even in darkness.</p>
<p><strong>Temporarily gained thus, it is not sage realization</strong>.</p>
<p>But it is only a temporary state, not sage realization. You should not think it is the fruition of a sage &#8211; surely the sage fruition is not like this.</p>
<p><strong>If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a wholesome realm; if you think you have realized sagehood, you fall into deviant views</strong>.</p>
<p>If you give rise to pride, thinking you have attained great skill in cultivation, you will draw demonic obstructions upon yourself.</p>
<p>Some cultivators of the Way, reaching an extremely pure and transparent state, suddenly see everything &#8211; because they have opened the Buddha eye. Yet not everyone can open the Buddha eye; and even if someone attains the Buddha eye, it is not necessarily permanent. The state described here is that the cultivator's Buddha eye opens only temporarily, enabling him to see objects in a dark house as if it were lit.</p>
<p>Note further that the cultivator can see objects coming from outside appearing in the house. What will be seen? For example, you may see gods, ghosts and demons, Bodhisattvas, or the Buddha coming from outside appearing in the dark house &#8211; no fixed image necessarily.</p>
<p>Not everyone passes through this state, but some will. This is a state that appears at some point while the cultivator applies effort. But do not think all cultivators of the Way must pass through the same states &#8211; they are not of a fixed pattern.</p>
<p>Some have the Buddha eye opened permanently &#8211; called &#8220;spiritual powers gained as blessings.&#8221; These are people who in past lives diligently cultivated the Dharma-door of a thousand hands and a thousand eyes, and as a result can naturally open the Buddha eye in this life and into later lives. Others can open the Buddha eye only temporarily, because their minds do not abide in constant purity. If their minds are usually pure and they cultivated the Great Compassion Dharma-door in past lives, they too may open the Buddha eye permanently. These are differing cases in cultivation.</p>

<li><strong> THE BODY WITHOUT FEELING, LIKE PLANTS AND TREES</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu dĩ thử tâm, viên nhập hư dung, tứ chi hốt nhiên đồng ư thảo mộc, hỏa thiêu đao chước, tằng vô sở giác. Hựu tắc hỏa quang, bất năng thiêu nhiệt, túng cát kì nhục, do như tước mộc. Thử danh trần tịnh, bài tứ đại tính, nhất hướng nhập thuần. Tạm đắc như thị, phi vi thánh chứng. Bất tác thánh tâm, danh thiện cảnh giới. Nhược tác thánh giải, tức thọ quần tà. </strong></p>
