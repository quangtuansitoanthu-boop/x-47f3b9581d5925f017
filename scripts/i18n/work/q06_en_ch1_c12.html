Commentary: The Vajra Fire Chief Ucchushma cultivated the contemplation of the Fire element, because he had a very strong lustful mind. The Buddha taught him to contemplate the fire of lust within his body.
Contemplating the fire element to dispel the fire of lust
Comes from weariness with suffering, not true departure.
This resolve to abandon the lustful mind is not in itself true departure. This is not an expedient for beginners. Duality is not the unsurpassed bodhi dharma. How can one attain perfect penetration? This method cannot be chosen as the dharma-door for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 若以風性觀 動寂非無對 對非無上覺 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Nhược dĩ phong tánh quán Động tịch phi vô đối Đối phi vô thượng giác Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: If one contemplates the nature of wind, Movement and stillness are not without duality; What is dual is not the unsurpassed enlightenment — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: The Prince Vaidurya Light used the Wind element to cultivate perfect penetration. If one contemplates the nature of wind —
Movement and stillness are not without duality.
Movement and stillness are two dualities, not absolutes.
What is dual is not the unsurpassed enlightenment.
Duality is not the supreme enlightened dharma.
How can one attain perfect penetration?
This method cannot be given for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 若以空性觀　 　昏鈍先非覺 　無覺異菩提　　 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Nhược dĩ không tánh quán Hôn độn tiên phi giác Vô giác dị bồ đề Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: If one contemplates the nature of space, Space, dull and dark, is fundamentally unaware. Unawareness differs from bodhi — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: If one contemplates the nature of space. The Bodhisattva Space Treasury cultivated by contemplating his body as vast as empty space and became enlightened. But in this verse, Manjushri Bodhisattva says: I regard the nature of the Space element and see that space, dull and dark, is fundamentally unaware. This factor lacks the brightness that would suit beginners.
Unawareness differs from bodhi —
How can one attain perfect penetration?
This method is not selected as the dharma-door for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 若以識性觀 觀識非常住 存心乃虛妄 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Nhược dĩ thức tánh quán, Quán thức phi thường trụ, Tồn tâm nãi hư vọng, Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: If one contemplates the nature of consciousness, Consciousness contemplated is not constantly abiding; Dwelling on mind is falseness — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Maitreya Bodhisattva cultivated from the Consciousness element. He contemplated the nature of the six consciousnesses. If one contemplates the nature of consciousness, consciousness contemplated is not constantly abiding. Just look at the discriminating mind-consciousness: it does not dwell long. Dwelling on mind is falseness. Wherever there is an object to contemplate and a contemplating thought, there is falseness. How can one attain perfect penetration? So this method is not regarded as perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 諸行是無常 念性無生滅 　因果今殊感 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Chư hành thị vô thường, Niệm tánh vô sanh diệ, Nhân quả kim thù cảm, Vân hà hoạch viên thông?
