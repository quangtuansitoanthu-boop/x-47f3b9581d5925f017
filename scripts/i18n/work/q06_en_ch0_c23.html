Sutra text: 八者滅音圓聞,遍生慈力。能令眾生經過嶮路,賊不能劫。

Transliteration: Bát giả diệt âm viên văn, biến sanh từ lực, năng linh chúng sanh kinh quá hiểm lộ, tặc bất năng kiếp.

Translation: Eighth, the object of sound extinguished and hearing perfected, compassionate power arises everywhere, enabling beings passing along dangerous roads not to be robbed by bandits.

Commentary: Eighth, the object of sound extinguished and hearing perfected, compassionate power arises everywhere. When sound has vanished and the hearing-nature has achieved perfect penetration, compassionate power arises vastly. That is the sound of the ordinary world, and through returning the hearing to hear the self-nature one's hearing-nature achieves perfect penetration — meaning one does not use the mind-consciousness to recognize hearing, yet one still hears every sound.

Sutra text: 九者薰聞離塵,色所不劫。能令一切多婬眾生遠離貪欲。

Transliteration: Cửu giả huân văn ly trần, sắc sở bất kiếp, năng linh nhất thiết đa dâm chúng sanh viễn ly tham dục.

Translation: Ninth, through perfuming hearing, apart from sense-objects, forms cannot captivate; I can enable all lustful beings to leave greed and desire far behind.

Commentary: The ninth fearlessness power is separation from lust. Through perfuming hearing into purity, apart from sense-objects, forms, sounds... cannot drag one away. If a person daily cultivates the effort of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature until perfection, they can leave the ordinary sense-object realms far behind. In particular, they can leave the defiled realm of form-objects.

We should not think beauty wonderful to get involved with. If you delight in beauty, it will rob your household of all its treasures. It will devour the precious ancient treasures and steal them all away. Rather, one should:
Nhãn kiến sắc trần nội vô hữu
Nhĩ văn thế sự tâm bất tri.
The eye sees forms, yet within nothing remains.
The ear hears worldly affairs, yet the mind does not discern.

When you see beauty, your mind should be as if seeing nothing at all. If you see beauty and your mind stirs, then ask yourself why your mind did not stir before you saw it. Do not let beauty rob you of the wealth in your own home.

Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva says: I can enable lustful beings to leave greed and desire far behind.

A passage in the Universal Door of Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva in the Dharma Flower Sutra says: "If there are beings with much lust who constantly revere and recite Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name, they at once leave lust behind."

That is also the representative meaning of the sutra text. "Much greed and desire" refers especially to sexual desire. The greatest problem in human life, the nearly insoluble problem, is sexual desire. Seeing through the entanglement of men and women and setting it aside — that is true freedom. If you cannot see through it and set it aside, you can have no freedom, and you cannot become enlightened. If you are truly honest in your effort, then when eating, you do not know you are eating; when dressing, you do not know you are wearing clothes. If you can forget eating and dressing, then you will all the more easily renounce everything external.
