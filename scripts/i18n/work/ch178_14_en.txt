TITLE: 03. THE HIGH OFFICIAL NAMED DANG
TOMTAT: A high official named Dang wished to run for Minister. Three others (named Zhao, Tran, and Luong) were contesting the post.... Mr. Dang asked me: "Can I win election as Minister?" I answered: "Yes!" About half a year...
-----
{align:justify|A high official named Dang wished to run for Minister. Three others (named Zhao, Tran, and Luong) were contesting the post…}
{align:justify|Mr. Dang asked me:}
{align:justify|"Can I win election as Minister?"}
{align:justify|I answered:}
{align:justify|"Yes!"}
{align:justify|About half a year later, the one elected Minister was not Dang but Tran. Mr. Dang flew into a rage, came to interrogate me, blaming me for first saying he could become Minister—so why had it not come true?}
{align:justify|"What kind of fortune-telling is this? Is it accurate at all? Aren't you deceiving people? Explain yourself!" Mr. Dang snapped irritably.}
{align:justify|I blushed crimson, unable to utter a word.}
{align:justify|Mr. Dang asked again:}
{align:justify|"Didn't you once predict that I… 'would make it'? What is this, then?"}
{align:justify|I could only answer:}
{align:justify|"Truly, back then I heard the God of Fortune confirm it—whatever he said, I relayed exactly!…"}
{align:justify|"The God of Fortune? So where is this 'God of Fortune'?"}
{align:justify|"The God of Fortune is invisible! He oversees people's fortune and rank! I can see and speak with him, but you cannot…"}
{align:justify|"Utter nonsense!"}
{align:justify|Mr. Dang sneered, his extreme displeasure written all over his face.}
{align:justify|Just as I stood being scolded and questioned, you can picture the "sorry spectacle": utterly flustered at the time, completely dispirited, I could only gaze silently at the sky, crying out to heaven alone… But suddenly a beam of light shot into my eyes—the God of Fortune had appeared. He looked at me with a gentle smile and wrote out a single character: "LUST." Beneath the character he even recorded the exact day and month of the offense…}
{align:justify|I promptly told Dang:}
{align:justify|"You lost the election because you violated the precept against lust!"}
{align:justify|Mr. Dang answered:}
{align:justify|"No!"}
{align:justify|"On that very day… of that month!"}
{align:justify|Mr. Dang insisted:}
{align:justify|"There's really no such thing!"}
{align:justify|I was stunned: the God of Fortune had clearly written "LUST" with the offending day and month noted in plain detail, his instruction perfectly clear—how could it not exist? Unconvinced, I pressed:}
{align:justify|"Think it over carefully?!"}
{align:justify|Mr. Dang pondered, counting the days and months on his fingers for a while, and finally still answered:}
{align:justify|"No, there isn't!"}
{align:justify|At this point the God of Fortune explained to me: "On that day and month, Dang peeked at the neighbor girl bathing"…}
{align:justify|Hearing the God of Fortune charge him with "spying on a young lady bathing," I found it so funny inside I could hardly keep from laughing. I told Dang:}
{align:justify|"It wasn't adultery or lovemaking… but peeking at… the neighbor girl bathing!"}
{align:justify|Mr. Dang, hearing this, froze for a moment, said nothing, then slunk away with bowed head!}
{align:justify|As far as I know, Mr. Dang's offense went like this: "Rightly, Mr. Dang deserved to become Minister, but a few months earlier a young woman had moved in next door to him. She was very beautiful—tall and lovely. Mr. Dang noticed her and kept staring. His house had a window facing the girl's bathroom. On that day… of that month… the neighbor girl entered the bathroom, forgot to draw the curtain, and was seen by Mr. Dang… He fetched his binoculars, raised them, and gazed at her from head to toe and toe to head… his heart wildly excited, his mouth sighing in admiration, murmuring his wish: If only I could spend one night with her, my life would not be wasted! At that moment Mr. Dang's eyes looked, his mind fantasized, his heart brimming with wicked thoughts…}
{align:justify|The God of Fortune said: Although Mr. Dang committed no act amounting to a crime with the neighbor girl, still—had he glimpsed her bathing, a decent man would have turned away at once. Instead, not only did Mr. Dang fail to turn away, he picked up his telescope and studied her minutely from head to toe; not only did his eyes stir and his heart stir, but lustful intent blazed up fiercely… though it led to no act of adultery, it still counted as violating the precept against lust! That is why he lost the ministerial post! Tell him: he must cultivate virtue and reform himself for about six more years before he can serve as Minister…}
