The highest grade becomes demon kings in the heaven of the Six Desires; the middle grade becomes demon people — ordinary kinds of demons. The lowest grade becomes demon women. They are most beautiful, but most vicious. Wise people should be careful. Intelligent people should note this passage of sutra. Do not let cleverness in the end turn back to harm yourselves. Do not take the attitude: "You do not know, but I know. You are not clear, but I am clear." That is only petty cleverness, vulgar understanding. Do not let your promising future be ruined.

Sutra text: 彼等諸魔亦有徒眾。各各自謂成無上道。

Transliteration: Bỉ đẳng chư ma diệc hữu đồ chúng. Các các tự vị thành vô thượng đạo.

Translation: Those demons also have retinues. Each claims to have perfected the unsurpassed Way.

Commentary: These people, with a little knowledge, unable to cut off the lustful mind, are always talking about lust. "I love you, and you love me." And they love back and forth until they become demons. Then what happens? Those demons also have retinues. Each claims to have perfected the unsurpassed Way.

They too have disciples and supporters. They are utterly shameless, always loudly proclaiming they have perfected the supreme Way. "I am a Buddha! We are all unsurpassed and none compare!" Fundamentally such people are demons, but they do not admit it; they proclaim themselves in place of the Buddhas. You see, there are still false Buddhas. But they do not see themselves as false. They see themselves as true. They believe: Above heaven, below earth, I alone am honored.

Sutra text: 我滅度後末法之中,多此魔民熾盛世間。廣行貪婬為善知識,令諸眾生。落愛見坑失菩提路。

Transliteration: Ngã diệt độ hậu mạt pháp chi trung, đa thử ma dân xí thạnh thế gian. Quảng hành tham dâm, vi thiện tri thức, linh chư chúng sanh lạc ái kiến khanh, thất bồ-đề lộ.

Translation: After my extinction, in the Dharma-Ending Age, many of these demon people will blaze across the world. Committing lustful acts far and wide, posing as good spiritual teachers, they cause beings to fall into pits of love and views, losing the road to bodhi.

Commentary: Shakyamuni Buddha says: "While the Tathagata is still in the world, these demons dare not display themselves, but after the Tathagata's extinction, many of these demon people will blaze across the world."

That is precisely our own age being mentioned in the sutra. As the teaching approaches ruin, there will be very many such demons about, going everywhere proclaiming lust. And they themselves are intoxicated with lust, whether as men or as women. At the same time, they think they are enlightened and have become Buddhas.

How can I recognize such people — they say they have become Buddhas — I no longer pay attention to their names. He says he is a Buddha. I say he is a demon.

He asks back: "Who is a demon?"
I answer: "You are a demon."

How do I know? Because he is like what I just described above. He is always talking about feelings and love. "Love, love. I love everyone."

How shameful. By what right do you love everyone? These demons commit lustful acts far and wide.
