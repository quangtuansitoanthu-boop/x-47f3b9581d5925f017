Commentary: Mind-hearing penetrates the ten directions. The dharma-door Samantabhadra Bodhisattva cultivated is ear-consciousness. But the hearing-nature pervades only through the power of a great causal condition.
This dharma-door requires power from a most supreme causal condition. Only then can this dharma-door be cultivated. Beginners' minds cannot enter it. Beings who have just brought forth the resolve to cultivate the Bodhisattva Way will not suit this dharma-door. Since beginners cannot cultivate this dharma-door, then How can one attain perfect penetration? This dharma-door is also not selected.

Sutra text: 鼻想本權機 秖令攝心住 　住成心所住 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Tỵ tưởng bổn quyền cơ Kỳ linh nhiếp tâm trụ Trụ thành tâm sở trụ Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Contemplating the nose-tip is but a provisional device, Only to gather the mind to dwell. Dwelling makes the mind something dwelt-in — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Contemplating the nose-tip is but a provisional device. Sundarananda cultivated the contemplation of the white mark on the nose-tip. Cultivating this dharma-door involves thought, so it is a skillful device. Only to gather the mind to dwell. That method serves to settle the mind.
If the object contemplated becomes "something dwelt-in" —
The goal of this method is to bring the mind to dwell in one place, but since there continues to be a place for the mind to dwell, then How can one attain perfect penetration? So this dharma-door is also not selected.

Sutra text: 說法弄音文 開悟先成者 名句非無漏 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Thuyết pháp lộng âm văn Khai ngộ tiên thành giả Danh cú phi vô lậu Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Speaking Dharma employs sounds and words; It awakens only those already accomplished. Names and phrases are not unconditioned — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Purna Maitrayaniputra spoke Dharma and became enlightened through the tongue root. Speaking Dharma employs sounds and words. When speaking Dharma and lecturing sutras, the spoken sounds must be beautiful and must be perfectly expressed in writing. So it only awakens those who have already planted Buddha seeds in the past. Names and phrases are not unconditioned. How can one attain perfect penetration?

Since these are objects of conditioned dharmas, that dharma-door cannot be chosen as the means to cultivate perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 持犯但束身 非身無所束 元非遍一切 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Trì phạm đãn thúc thân Phi thân vô sở thúc Nguyên phi biến nhất thiết Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Keeping or breaking precepts only binds the body; Apart from the body, what is there to bind? Fundamentally not pervading all — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: The Elder Upali specialized in upholding precepts. Keeping precepts only binds the body. Keeping or breaking precepts lies with the body. This is a body-related dharma concerning body and mind. Apart from the body, what is there to bind?
If you had no body, there would be nothing to restrain or control.
Fundamentally not pervading all —
How can one attain perfect penetration?
This method is not complete. So it is not selected as the means to cultivate perfect penetration.
