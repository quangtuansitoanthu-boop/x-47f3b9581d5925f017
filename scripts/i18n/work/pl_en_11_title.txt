Vuong Nhut Huu
---TOMTAT---
Layman Vuong Nhut Huu (Longshu) of the Song dynasty, author of the widely circulated Longshu Pure Land Writings, who stood upright at his passing; Layman Vien Liet Tinh, who defended the Pure Land gate; and Lady Viet Quoc, who through a dream journey beheld the lotus pond where each reciter's flower blooms or withers with diligence or sloth.
