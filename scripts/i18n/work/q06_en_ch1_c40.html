Sutra text: 阿難,我令比丘食五淨肉。此肉皆我神力化生本無命根。汝婆羅門地多蒸濕,加以沙石,草菜不生。我以大悲神力所加。因大慈悲假名為肉。汝得其味。奈何如來滅度之後,食眾生肉名為釋子。

Transliteration: A-nan, ngã linh tỷ-khưu thực ngũ tịnh nhục. Thử nhục giai ngã thần lực hóa sanh, bổn vô mạng căn. Nhữ Bà-la-môn địa đa chưng thấp, gia dĩ sa thạch, thảo thái bất sanh. Ngã dĩ đại bi thần lực sở gia. Nhân đại từ bi giả danh vi nhục. Nhữ đắc kỳ vị. Nại hà Như Lai diệt độ chi hậu, thực chúng sanh nhục danh vi Thích tử?

Translation: Ananda, I permit bhikshus to eat the five kinds of pure meat. Such meat is all transformed by my spiritual power, fundamentally without a life-root. Your brahman land is mostly damp and steamy, and moreover sandy and rocky; vegetables do not grow. I, with the aid of great compassionate spiritual power, provisionally call it meat out of great kindness. You obtain its flavor. How, after the Tathagata's extinction, can those who eat beings' flesh call themselves Shakya's sons?

Commentary: Ananda, I permit bhikshus to eat the five kinds of pure meat.

The Buddha's teaching permits consuming five kinds of meat, as follows:
1) Meat of animals one did not see killed.
2) Meat of animals one did not hear killed.
3) Meat of animals one is certain were not killed for one's own eating.
4) Meat of animals already dead (killed by no one).
5) Meat left over after birds have pecked at it.

Such meat is all transformed by my spiritual power, fundamentally without a life-root. The Buddha himself created these kinds of meat; they did not come from living beings at all. They have no life-root — meaning no consciousness, no warmth, no breath. Your brahman land is mostly damp and steamy, and moreover sandy and rocky; vegetables do not grow.

You are practitioners of pure conduct, yet live in a land full of sand, rock, damp, and heat. I, with the aid of compassionate spiritual power, provisionally call it meat out of great kindness. You obtain its flavor. That is in substance what you are eating. Now the Tathagata permits you to eat such meat. But after the Tathagata's extinction, how can those who eat beings' flesh call themselves Shakya's sons? They do not eat the five kinds of pure meat; they entirely eat beings' flesh. Can they be called the Buddha's disciples? They do not deserve to be regarded as Shakyamuni's disciples — that is, they do not deserve to be monastics.

Sutra text: 汝等當知是食肉人,縱得心開似三摩地,皆大羅剎。報終必沈生死苦海 。非佛弟子。如是之人相殺相吞,相食未已。云何是人得出三界?

Transliteration: Nhữ đẳng đương tri thị thực nhục nhân, túng đắc tâm khai, tợ tam-ma-đề, giai đại la-sát. Báo chung tất trầm sanh tử khổ hải. Phi Phật đệ tử. Như thị chi nhân tương sát tương thôn, tương thực vị dĩ. Vân hà thị nhân đắc xuất tam giới?

Translation: You should know that such meat-eaters, though their minds open as if in samadhi, are all great rakshasas. When their retribution ends, they must sink into the bitter sea of birth and death. They are not the Buddha's disciples. Such people kill and devour one another, eating without end. How can such people transcend the Triple Realm?
