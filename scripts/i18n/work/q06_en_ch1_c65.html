Translation: What I say thus is spoken by the Buddhas. What is not spoken thus is spoken by Papiyan.

Commentary: What I say thus is spoken by the Buddhas. If you explain as the Tathagata explained in this sutra, that is precisely the teaching the Buddhas spoke. What is not spoken thus is spoken by Papiyan.
Whoever does not expound this teaching, but proclaims doctrines contrary to this teaching — that is precisely the demon kings' speech." Papiyan refers to the Demon King.
