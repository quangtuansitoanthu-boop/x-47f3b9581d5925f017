{align:justify|If you say the Buddha is compassionate, why does He wait until sentient beings cry out and invoke His name before saving them? It is like the sun and moon shining their light for everyone, but whoever wishes to receive it must open the door. If the door stays tightly shut, the light cannot shine in.}
{align:justify|Radio and television stations broadcast on every channel for us to watch, yet we must tune in to the right frequency to see anything. Likewise, to connect with the Buddhas and Bodhisattvas we must invoke Their names: during the time we recite, our scattered thoughts subside, our minds grow clear, and communion with the Buddhas becomes easy. That is why we must invoke Their names.}
{align:justify|As for the father in this story, he is a model Buddhist disciple. Ordinarily, those who do not know the Dharma tend to chase after material desires, wealth and beauty, and pay little attention to their children and families. But the father here, besides cultivating himself through recitation, cares deeply and loves his child dearly. When his child went to sit for the exams, the father vowed to discipline himself, do good deeds, create blessings and accumulate hidden merit, dedicating it all to his child.}
{align:justify|Thus, once one becomes a Buddhist disciple, even without leaving the household life, one finds much greater happiness: thanks to the resolve to keep the precepts, husband and wife treat each other with faithfulness, the family lives in harmony and warmth, and life overflows with love. Because they create no evil and their hearts remain wholly wholesome, they enjoy blessings and joy right in this very life. This is precisely the benefit of Buddhist families.}

He affirms that these stories are entirely true—real people, real events, with solid evidence—and asks that they be published so the world may take warning. He hopes that through them everyone will learn many good lessons and deepen their faith in cause and effect, so as to "Do no evil, and practice all that is good," and thereby improve the moral climate of society.}
