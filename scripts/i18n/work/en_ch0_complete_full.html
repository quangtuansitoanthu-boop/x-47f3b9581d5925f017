THE REALM OF FORM

THE FIRST DHYANA

A-nan! Thế gian nhất thiết, sở tu tâm nhân. Bất giả Thiền-na, vô hữu trí tuệ.

Ananda! All those who cultivate the mind in the world, without Dhyana, have no wisdom.

Commentary: Ananda, now we shall speak of the Realm of Form, the heavens of the Four Dhyanas. All cultivators in the world cannot have wisdom without the aid of Dhyana. They are not yet accustomed to cultivating meditative concentration — they cannot yet restrain all their false thoughts, and therefore they have no wisdom yet.

  1. Đãn năng chấp thân, bất hành dâm dục. Nhược hành nhược tọa, tưởng niệm câu vô. Ái nhiễm bất sanh, vô lưu dục giới, thị nhân ưng niệm, thân vi phạm lữ. Như thị nhất loại, danh Phạm Chúng Thiên.

If one can restrain the body from sexual desire, whether walking, standing, sitting, or lying down, with no thought or recollection whatsoever; lust and defilement do not arise; without outflow from the desire realm — this person is called the Brahma Multitudes Heaven.

Commentary: One can control one's own body and mind and guard against sexual desire. "Whether walking, standing, sitting, or lying down, with no thought or recollection whatsoever" — at all times, whether walking, standing, sitting, or lying down, and even in dreams — "lustful defilement does not arise." Even when encountering a situation or a person, no thoughts of lust arise. Thus one "cultivates the pure precepts without outflow."

Such people can freely associate with Brahma heavens. They make friends with the Brahma heavens. And they no longer have any lustful desires. Such people are called the Brahma Multitudes Heaven. They are reborn as pure sages in those heavens. They become a celestial being among the great multitudes of Brahma heavens — one among the great multitude.

  1. Dức tập ký tự, ly dục tâm hiện. Ư chu luật nghi, ái lạc tự thuần. Thí nhân ứng thời, năng hành phạm độc. Như thị nhất loại, danh Phạm Phụ Thiên.

When virtue has long been accumulated, "the mind is exhausted of desire," following the precepts and rules, acting in accordance with the rules — this is called the Brahma Ministers Heaven.

Commentary: "Virtue has long been accumulated, the mind is exhausted of desire, following the precepts and rules." The basic nature of humans is to like food and sex. These are innate habits. But at this point, these beings have left behind the habits of lustful desire. When they no longer have lust or defilement, wisdom begins to arise. They uphold the precepts and follow the rules of discipline. They delight in the precepts and maintain purity in their cultivation. Such people can carry out the unique conduct in accordance with the rules — this is called the Brahma Ministers Heaven. At all times and in all circumstances, they cultivate according to the true Way. Their conduct is pure and clean. "Brahma Ministers" means purity. These beings are reborn in the Brahma Ministers Heaven. Upholding the Dharma, they assist the Great Brahma Minister Heaven.

  1. Thần tâm diệu viên, uy nghi bất khuyết. Thanh tịnh cấm giới, gia dĩ minh ngộ. Thí nhân ứng thời, năng thượng phạm chúng, vi Đại Phạm Vương. Như thị nhất loại, danh Đại Phạm Thiên.

Body and mind wondrously perfect, awesome deportment fully complete; precepts maintained purely, with spiritual power; leading the Brahma multitudes as the Great Brahma King — this is called the Great Brahma Heaven.

Commentary: Those who have "body and mind wondrously perfect, awesome deportment fully complete, precepts maintained purely, with spiritual power." These are beings who no longer have thoughts of lust, and who fully possess the 3,000 awesome deportments and 80,000 subtle manners. They perfect all conduct. They uphold the precepts purely and completely according to what the Buddha taught. Not only do they uphold the precepts perfectly, but they also have a comprehensive understanding of the precepts. They have penetrated through body, matter, and dharmas. They "lead the Brahma multitudes as the Great Brahma King — this is called the Great Brahma Heaven." When their lives end, they are reborn in the Brahma heavens and can rule over all in the Brahma realm.

A-nan! Thứ tam thắng lưu. Nhất thiết khổ não, sở bất năng bác. Tuy phi chính tu, chân tam-ma-đề. Thanh tịnh tâm trung, chử lậu bất động, danh vi sơ Thiền.

Ananda! These three are supreme and cannot be surpassed. Though not true cultivation, they are genuine Dhyana. Pure in mind, outflows cannot move them — this is called the First Dhyana.

Commentary: "Ananda! These three are supreme and cannot be surpassed." These three are the Brahma Multitudes Heaven, the Brahma Ministers Heaven, and the Great Brahma Heaven. These are supreme celestial ranks — the heavens of the First Dhyana — where defilement and affliction do not disturb the cultivator. "Though not true cultivation, they are genuine Dhyana." They have not yet cultivated true Dhyana, but their minds are pure, and afflictions have been suppressed. They have not cut off outflows, but within the First Dhyana, they are not moved by outflows. "Pure in mind, outflows cannot move them — this is called the First Dhyana."

THE SECOND DHYANA

  1. A-nan! Cử thrice Phạm thiên. Thường nhận Phạm nhân, viên mãn Phạm hành. Trưởng tâm bất động, tích trạm sinh quang. Như thị nhất loại, danh Thiếu Quang Thiên.

Ananda! The Brahma Multitudes Heaven above. They always receive the Brahma beings, perfecting Brahma conduct. The mind is unmoved; accumulated light is produced. This is called the Lesser Light Heaven.

Commentary: Those dwelling at the summit of the Brahma Multitudes, Brahma Ministers, and Great Brahma heavens have the ability to receive and absorb the beings in those three heavens. This is because they have perfected Brahma conduct. "The mind is unmoved; accumulated light is produced." Their minds are extremely calm and peaceful, not moving. When the mind is purified and still, a light will naturally shine forth. Those cultivators who attain this are reborn in the Lesser Light Heaven.

  1. Quang quang tường nhiên, chiếu diệu vô tận. Ánh tháp phương giới, biến thành lưu ly. Như thị nhất loại, danh Vô Lượng Quang Thiên.

Light shines brilliantly, illuminating the wondrous without end. Light pagodas pervade the realms, transforming into lapis lazuli. This is called the Infinite Light Heaven.

Commentary: "Light shines brilliantly, illuminating the wondrous without end, illuminating all directions of the realms like lapis lazuli." The light of these beings shines upon each other. They mutually illuminate each other. Has anyone ever seen lamp lights illuminating each other? No object exists that light does not illuminate. If you hold a lamp, and another person also holds a bright lamp, the two light sources do not conflict with each other. There is no confusion in light. This is what is described when speaking of light shining brilliantly. Light illuminates without end, without limit. It illuminates all directions, and every place is illuminated like lapis lazuli. Those cultivators who attain this are reborn in the Infinite Light Heaven.

  1. Hạp trĩ viên quang, thành tấu giáo thể. Phát hóa thanh tịnh, ức dụng vô tận. Như thị nhất loại, danh Quang Âm Thiên.

Uniting to perfect the light, it becomes the teaching body of a tathagata. Transforming into pure purity, its function is without end. This is called the Light Sound Heaven.

Commentary: "The united light is like sound, becoming the teaching body of a tathagata." They together attain and maintain the light and sound, and in this way they become the teaching body of a tathagata. In the Light Sound Heaven, no one needs to speak loudly to converse. They use the light and sound for communication. They communicate by emitting light. They do not communicate by mouth, they do not use language. Light and sound are the means of teaching the Dharma in that heaven. This is truly a wonderful state:
— Minds understand each other without speaking a word.
When they emit light and sound to each other in that heaven, the other person understands what the first is saying. They exchange thoughts through light and sound. "Transforming into pure purity, its function is without end; this is called the Light Sound Heaven." The subtle function of the light and sound is truly without end.

A-nan! Thứ tam thắng lưu. Nhất thiết lầu buồn, sở bất năng bác. Dữ phi chính tu, chân tam-ma-đề. Thanh tịnh tâm trung, thỏ lậu dĩ phục, danh vi nhì Thiền.

Ananda! These three are all supreme, none can surpass them. Though not true cultivation, they are genuine Dhyana. Pure in mind, outflows are exhausted — this is called the Second Dhyana.

Commentary: "Ananda! These three are all supreme and cannot be surpassed." In the heavens of the Second Dhyana, they are not suppressed by afflictions or suffering. Although they still have a trace of worry. If something comes up, they still have a bit of difficulty letting go of it. They are bound by matters. But in the Second Dhyana heaven, there are no more afflictions or worries, and no more matters to be bound by. They have nothing in their minds. They are different from ordinary people in this world who worry back and forth about things day and night. "Bound" means being dragged along, unable to let go. "Though not true cultivation, genuine Dhyana. Pure in mind, outflows are exhausted." These cultivators, though not yet true cultivation of the First Dhyana, have attained a wonderful state where the mind is purified. ... (continued)

THE THIRD DHYANA

  1. A-nan! Như thí Thiên nhân. Viên quang thành âm, phi âm lự điều. Phát thành tinh hành, thông tích tích lạc. Như thị nhất loại, danh Thiếu Tịnh Thiên.

Ananda! Like those heavenly beings. Perfect light becomes sound; it is not sound or mere expression. Producing the pure conduct, pervading the accumulated bliss. This is called the Lesser Purity Heaven.

Commentary: "Ananda! The light and sound become sound, using sound to express the wonderful, becoming the pure conduct of the pure essence." For those cultivators in that great realm, the light and sound have become sound, and when they can distinguish this sound even more clearly, it reveals a level of pure essence and ultimate purity of cultivation. "Pervading with the bliss of cessation" — through the pure essence and ultimate purity of cultivation. Cessation (nirodha) is the extinction of false thoughts. False thoughts and dharmas have ceased, and they are called the Lesser Purity Heaven. Those cultivators are reborn in the Lesser Purity Heaven. Body and mind have attained a degree of purity. Not yet completely pure.

  1. Tịnh không hiện tiền, dấn phát vô tề. Thần tâm khinh an, thành tích tích lạc. Như thị nhất loại, danh Vô Lượng Tịnh Thiên.

The state of "Pure Emptiness" manifests; it produces the infinite. Body and mind are light and peaceful, achieving the bliss of cessation. This is called the Infinite Purity Heaven.

Commentary: "The state of Pure Emptiness manifests, without end." They have attained purity, and then they awaken to the true emptiness. When understanding emptiness, true emptiness leads to no-end. The cessation is perfect. "Body and mind are light and peaceful, achieving the bliss of cessation." At this time, they are completely at ease. They have self-mastery. They have attained the bliss of cessation. In birth there is cessation, in cessation there is birth. Something disappears and no one knows what happens. People are greedy for external objects and chase after them. The nature of those beings produces feelings; feelings produce desire. The desire for sex is created. They become a fixed object in the discriminating mind, and then the mind of greed burns and endlessly pursues the object of desire. Is it necessary to have something external to have this joy? No, because in Emptiness there is Form. If you understand, in Form there is Emptiness. You will perceive the Emptiness and the bliss of cessation from within. The Diamond Sutra says: "Form is not different from Emptiness, Emptiness is not different from Form. Form is precisely Emptiness, Emptiness is precisely Form." The bliss of Cessation — the Bliss of Emptiness — exceeds material joy by more than ten million times. If you want to attain it, you must first let go of material desire. If you cannot let go of external objects, the true Emptiness within you cannot manifest.

Here, those heavenly beings have reached the state of Pure Emptiness, and body and mind have become light and peaceful. They have attained the wonderful state and understand: "Form is not different from Emptiness, Emptiness is not different from Form. Form is precisely Emptiness, Emptiness is precisely Form." This cannot be spoken. You cannot say you have attained this state if you have not. It cannot be experienced through words. Only when you attain it. Just like a person drinking water knows if it's cold or hot. Those who have attained this state of cessation are reborn in the Infinite Purity Heaven.

  1. Thế giới thân tâm, nhất thiết viên tịnh. Tịnh đức thành tấu, thượng thác hiện tiền, quy tích lạc lực. Như thị nhất loại, danh Biến Tịnh Thiên.

The world, body, and mind are all perfectly pure. Pure virtue becomes the tathagata; the supreme state manifests; returning to the bliss of cessation. This is called the Universal Purity Heaven.

Commentary: "The world, body, and mind are all perfectly pure; pure virtue becomes the tathagata." In the heavens previously discussed, only body and mind were purified. But those cultivators in this heaven speak of having attained the spiritual powers to change the mind of the earth. The earth is so filthy, yet they can change it into purity. True suchness is like the verse: "True mind is the pure land; the pure mind is Amitabha Buddha." If the mind is pure, the pure land is also pure. If the mind is not pure, you cannot see the purity of the pure land. If you are in the Pure Land and do nothing but cry all day, what happiness is there? If you are in this Saha world and are happy from morning to night, that is also like being in the hells. The true principle here is "Dharmas are mind-only." The true principle remains there; if you can see through it and let go of things, you will be free and at ease. If you cannot see through things and cannot let them go, you cannot attain the peace of happiness. Those heavenly beings who have attained this state have the purity of body, mind, and the nature of things, the light of the true nature. When the "supreme state manifests" — what is the supreme state? It is the state manifesting in the mind. They can purify all things in all dharmas.

"Returning to the bliss of cessation, this is called the Universal Purity Heaven." There is no falsehood in them. They can purify all things in all dharmas.

A-nan! Thứ tam thắng lưu, chử đại tự thuần. Thần tâm an ổn, đắc vô lượng lạc. Tuy phi chính đắc, chân tam-ma-đề. An ổn tâm trung, hoan hỷ thỏ chử, danh vi tam Thiền.

Ananda! These three are all the great perfected self-mastery. Body and mind are peaceful, attaining boundless joy. Though not true attainment, they are genuine Dhyana. Peaceful in mind, all rejoice — this is called the Third Dhyana.

Commentary: "Ananda! These three are all the great perfected self-mastery..." (continues with detailed commentary on the Third Dhyana, including the verse "A single thought does not arise, the whole true nature appears; the power of the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind all conceal it...")
