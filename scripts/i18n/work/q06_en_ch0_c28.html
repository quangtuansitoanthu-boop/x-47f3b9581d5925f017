Eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, and mind all unobstructed and perfectly interfused with one another. All things merge into one. The six sense roots function interchangeably. Like a great mirror revealing the empty, still treasury of the Tathagatas.

I receive and follow the secret Dharma-doors of the Tathagatas of the ten directions, as numerous as dust motes, without loss, enabling childless beings of the Dharma Realm who seek daughters to give birth to daughters of upright marks, blessings, virtue, and gentleness, loved and respected by all.

If people wish to bear daughters, they will obtain daughters upright and gentle. Everyone meeting such daughters at once loves and respects them. They will be virtuous and perfect daughters.

Sutra text: 十四者此三千大千世界百億日月。現住世間諸法王子。有六十二恒河沙數修法垂範。教化眾生隨順眾生。方便智慧各各不同。

Transliteration: Thập tứ giả, thử tam thiên, đại thiên thế giới, bá ức nhật nguyệt. Hiện trụ thế gian chư pháp vương tử, hữu lục thập nhị hằng hà sa số, tu pháp thuỳ phạm, giáo hóa chúng sanh, tuỳ thuận chúng sanh. Phương tiện trí huệ các các bất đồng.

Translation: Fourteenth, in this great trichiliocosm with its hundred koṭis of suns and moons, the Dharma Princes now dwelling in the world, sixty-two Ganges' sands in number, cultivate the Dharma as models, teach beings, and accord with beings. Their expedient wisdom each differs.

Commentary: The fourteenth fearlessness power is: in this great trichiliocosm with its hundred koṭis of suns and moons, the Dharma Princes now dwelling in the world, sixty-two Ganges' sands in number — they are now dwelling right in this world — all cultivate the Buddhadharma, serve as models, teach beings, and accord with beings. Their expedient wisdom each differs. They use different skillful expedients and provisional wisdom to teach each different kind of being.

Sutra text: 由我所得圓通本根發妙耳門。然後身心微妙含容遍周法界。能令眾生持我名號。與彼共持六十二恒河沙諸法王子。二人福德正等無異。

Transliteration: Do ngã sở đắc viên thông, bổn căn phát diệu nhĩ môn. Nhiên hậu thân tâm vi diệu hàm dung biến chu pháp giới. Năng linh chúng sanh trì ngã danh hiệu, dữ bỉ cộng trì lục thập nhị hằng hà sa chư Pháp vương tử. Nhị nhơn phước đức chánh đẳng vô dị.

Translation: Because I attained perfect penetration, the sublime ear-door of my original root opened. Then my body and mind, subtle and wonderful, contained and pervaded the Dharma Realm. I can enable beings who uphold my name, compared with those who uphold the names of the sixty-two Ganges' sands of Dharma Princes above, to have merit and virtue exactly equal, without difference.

Commentary: Because I attained perfect penetration, the sublime nature of my ear root opened. I, Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, certified to the perfect penetration of the ear root, and obtained the subtle, wonderful powers from the ear root. Then my body and mind, subtle and wonderful, pervade the Dharma Realm. This subtle state pervades all ten directions, throughout the Dharma Realm. Thus I enable beings who uphold my name — those who recite Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name — compared with those who uphold the names of the sixty-two Ganges' sands of Dharma Princes above, to have merit and virtue exactly equal, without difference.

A person who recites only Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name, and another who recites the names of Bodhisattvas as numerous as the sands of sixty-two Ganges Rivers — each one's meritorious retribution is equal. This shows just how great the merit of reciting Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name is.
