You help people become enlightened through the principle you have certified — that is what the Buddhas did.
Though I am not yet delivered, I vow to deliver all beings of the final kalpa.
Though I have not yet certified to the Way, I wish to help all beings of the Dharma-Ending Age be delivered. I wish them all to receive benefit in the Buddhadharma.

Sutra text: 世尊,此諸眾生去佛漸遠。邪師說法如恒河沙。欲攝其心入三摩地。云何令其安立道場遠諸魔事,於菩提心得無退屈?

Transliteration: Thế tôn, thử chư chúng sanh khứ Phật tiệm viễn. Tà sư thuyết pháp như hằng hà sa. Dục nhiếp kỳ tâm nhập tam-ma-địa, vân hà linh kỳ an lập đạo tràng, viễn chư ma sự, ư bồ-đề tâm đắc vô thối khuất?

Translation: World Honored One, these beings grow ever farther from the Buddha. Deviant teachers speaking Dharma are as numerous as Ganges' sands. Wishing to gather their minds into samadhi, how shall they be made to establish a Way-place, stay far from demonic deeds, and never retreat in the bodhi resolve?

Commentary: World Honored One, these beings grow ever farther from the Buddha. Beings of the Dharma-Ending Age gradually grow distant from the Buddha and even from the Buddhadharma.

Deviant teachers speaking Dharma are as numerous as Ganges' sands. That is the situation now. Deviant teachers proclaim they understand what they really do not understand. Unenlightened, they say they are enlightened. Uncertified to sagely fruition, they say they are certified. I ask them: "Have you certified to the first, second, third, fourth Arhat fruitions? Which fruition have you certified to? Have you become enlightened? What have you awakened to?" They fall silent. I continue: "You are a Buddha, yet you cannot say which fruition you have certified to. How did you leap into Buddhahood?" Still they persist, saying that not only are they Buddhas, but "Everyone is a Buddha." That is their tune. This is great false speech. When you have not reached that state, you cannot speak at will. Nothing is worse than a deviant teacher. Take care never to become such a person, whether or not you know the Buddhadharma. Do not be like one of them who, when someone comes asking about Dharma, answers, "I am old; I have forgotten."

What does forgetting mean? He does not know where to begin. That way of speaking deliberately deceives people. If you know, say you know. If you do not know, say you do not know. One cannot say one has forgotten when one never understood what was asked.

When deviant teachers proclaim their methods, hoping for offerings, the teaching they speak is deviant. For instance, lust is wrong, but they say it is good. "That is the best dharma-door." They praise it, deluding people so they cannot distinguish the true teaching. What is wrong, they call right; what is right, they call wrong. They hold deviant knowledge and deviant views. Their outlook is incorrect.

Ananda says, "In the Dharma-Ending Age, such deviant teachers are very many."

Wishing to gather their minds into samadhi —
Though at that time there will be as many deviant teachers as Ganges' sands, still I vow that beings will give rise to right knowledge and right views.

How shall they be made to establish a Way-place, stay far from demonic deeds, and never retreat in the bodhi resolve?
