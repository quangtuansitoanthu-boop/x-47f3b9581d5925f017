<p><strong>Here there are also five kinds of Non-Returning Heavens, in which the nine grades of habit-energies of the lower realms are all extinguished together; suffering and bliss both forgotten, with no dwelling below; therefore they establish their dwelling among the renunciant minds shared by the assembly.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Here there are also five kinds of Non-Returning Heavens.&#8221; These upper five levels are sometimes counted among the heavens of the Fourth Dhyana. However, these five realms are the dwelling of sages, and thus differ from the heavens of the First, Second, Third, or Fourth Dhyana. Those who have been assured of Arhatship dwell in these five Non-Returning Heavens.</p>
<p>They have &#8220;extinguished together the nine grades of habit-energies of the lower realms; suffering and bliss both forgotten, with no dwelling below.&#8221; The nine kinds of habits refer to the first nine of the eighty-one kinds of delusions of thought. We shall not go into detail here. Such beings need not return anywhere at this point; they will not return to any lower realm.</p>
<p>They &#8220;establish their dwelling among the renunciant minds shared by the assembly.&#8221; Countless beings who reach this stage dwell together in emptiness, upon a cloud sheltering the earth. Those there are sages who have attained the stage of the Non-Returning Heavens.</p>

<p><strong>Thus both suffering and bliss are extinguished, and neither liking nor disliking arises; such a class is called the No Affliction Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: They have no thoughts of suffering and no thoughts of bliss, and so take no part in the struggle between suffering and bliss. Those who do not experience the battle between suffering and bliss are the class called the No Affliction Heaven. The beings in this heaven have no afflictions at all.</p>


<ol start="2">
<li><strong>Kỳ quát độc hành, nghiên giao vô địa. Như thị nhất loại, danh vô nhiệt Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Freely renouncing self, with no subject or object, the mind without opposition; such a class is called the No Heat Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Freely renouncing self, with no subject or object, the mind without opposition&#8221; describes beings from the previous heaven who have progressed in cultivation. In the first of the Five Pure Abodes, they took no part in the dispute between thoughts of suffering and thoughts of bliss &#8211; which basically means they have no such thoughts, though occasionally a trace of such thought may still arise; at times they are still slightly distracted. But in this realm, the No Heat Heaven, they investigate deeply into such meaningless involvements until they reach the point where they can no longer give rise to such thoughts in the mind. For these gods, such thoughts never arise. They are counted among the No Affliction gods, free of anger and very calm in all circumstances.</p>


<ol start="3">
<li><strong>Thập phương thế giới, diệu kiến viên trừng. Cánh vô trần tượng, nhất thiết trầm cấu. Như thị nhất loại, danh thiện kiến Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>The worlds of the ten directions, wondrously seen with perfect clarity, with no more dusty appearances, all defilements sunk away; such a class is called the Good View Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;The worlds of the ten directions, wondrously seen with perfect clarity, with no more dusty appearances, all defilements sunk away.&#8221; Their vision is subtle and perfect as well as completely clear &#8211; not turbid or confused, holding no prejudiced opinions. Their vision is said to be perfect and clear because it is no longer defiled; all slanderous dharmas are extinguished, and ignorance and false thoughts as numerous as dust and sand have been removed. Such heavenly beings are called the Good View Heaven.</p>


<ol start="4">
<li><strong>Tinh kiến hiện tiền, đào chú vô ngại. Như thị nhất loại, danh thiện hiện Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>The wondrous view manifesting before them, forged and fashioned without obstruction; such a class is called the Good Manifestation Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: Everything the gods in the heaven above see is good. Now, with the manifestation of this pure vision, everything they see surpasses anything they ever saw before; this heaven is far purer than the heavens described earlier. The term &#8220;forged and fashioned&#8221; refers to the process of smelting, casting, and shaping &#8211; like heating done in a furnace or shaping on a whetstone. What is forged and fashioned here is the mind and nature of a sage, until the mind becomes solid, unobstructed, and at ease in all circumstances. These are the sages dwelling in the Good Manifestation Heaven.</p>


<ol start="5">
<li><strong>Cứu cánh quần kỷ, cùng sắc tánh tánh, nhập vô biên tế. Như thị nhất loại, danh Sắc cứu kính Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Form-dusts, from their restless appearances to their ultimate stillness, exhausting the nature of form and entering the boundless; such a class is called the Ultimate Form Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Form-dusts, from their restless appearances to their ultimate stillness.&#8221; &#8220;Ultimate&#8221; carries the sense of the summit, perfection. &#8220;Restless appearances&#8221; refers to involvement with desire, from which they are now detached. Reaching the end of the nature of emptiness and the nature of form, they enter what exhausts the nature of form and has no bounds; &#8220;such a class is called the Ultimate Form Heaven.&#8221; They have reached the final stage of the Realm of Form.</p>
<p><strong>A-nan! Thử Bất hoàn Thiên. Bỉ chư tứ Thiền, tứ vị Thiên Vương. Độc hữu khâm văn, bất năng tri kiến. Như kim thế gian, khoáng dã thâm sơn, Thánh đạo tràng địa. Giai A-la-hán, sở trụ trì cố. Thế gian thô nhân, sở bất năng kiến.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda! As for these Non-Returning Heavens, only the Four Heavenly Kings of the Four Dhyanas can hear of them, but cannot know or see them. It is like in the present world, in deep mountains and remote wilds, the holy way-places where Arhats preside, which coarse worldly people cannot see.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda! As for these Non-Returning Heavens, only the Four Heavenly Kings of the Four Dhyanas can hear of them.&#8221; The god-kings of the Fourth Dhyana heavens only know of the Non-Returning Heavens by hearing their fame; they hear but cannot see or know them.</p>
<p>It is like in the present world, where in deep mountains and remote wilds, the holy way-places are presided over by Arhats, whom coarse worldly people cannot see. Sages with the bodhi mind dwell in places people cannot reach. Those who live in such places are great Arhats and great Bodhisattvas, whose presence is a supporting influence upon the regions where they live. Ordinary people never see these sages. Although all live in the same world, people cannot see the sages. So too, the sutra says, the Five Pure Abodes dwell in a distant, tranquil realm, and the gods of the Fourth Dhyana do not know the dwelling of the sages residing there.</p>
<p><strong>A-nan! Thị thập bát Thiên, độc hành vô giao, vị tận hình lụy. Tự thử dĩ hoàn, danh vi sắc giới.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda! These eighteen heavens above, though free from the realm of desire, have not yet escaped the encumbrance of form; all up to this point is called the Realm of Form.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda! These eighteen heavens above, though free from the realm of desire, have not yet escaped the encumbrance of form.&#8221; The eighteen heavens are the three heavens in each of the First, Second, and Third Dhyanas (3x3=9), the four heavens of the Fourth Dhyana, and the five Pure Abode heavens &#8211; together making up the Realm of Form.</p>
