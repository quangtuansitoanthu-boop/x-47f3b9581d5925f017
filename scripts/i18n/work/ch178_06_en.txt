{align:justify|I have an elder relative who loved meat, most of all pig's trotters, chicken wings and chicken feet. Not only did he eat them at all three meals every day, but whenever he drank with a few friends the table always had to include those dishes. One could say he loved meat to extremes and never tired of it.}
{align:justify|In old age, excess uric acid left this elder's hands and feet twisted and deformed, and frightful sores broke out across his face. Several patches of skin even began to rot. Though he sought cures everywhere, nothing healed him. Once, he carelessly fell and fractured his thigh; he groaned in pain for days before passing away. According to his family, in the days before his death he would often tremble and cry out in terror:}
{align:justify|"Someone has come to seize me! Someone wants to seize me! They want me to join them!"—chilling all who heard it.}
