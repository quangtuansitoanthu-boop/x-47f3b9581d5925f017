They blaze fiercely in the world. Deluded people become entangled with them, supposing everything they say is reasonable. Especially will it agree with the thinking of young people. As the saying goes, "Those with the same atrocious tastes flock together." They often praise one another in falling into this path. If they do not approve someone's views, they will not act so.

If the other person's way does not suit, they will not cooperate together. But if their thinking is alike, then the blind lead the blind. How pitiful. Here I am not scolding anyone, but:

If a deluded person
Teaches delusion to others,
When all is said and done,
Then no one understands.
The teacher falls into the hells;
The student must follow along.
The teacher also falls into the hells, and when the disciple also appears there, the teacher asks in surprise, "Why have you come here too? This is a most terrible place."

The disciple answers: "You came here first, and you are our teacher; of course we must follow you."
Posing as good spiritual teachers.
They boast, "I teach in many places to very many people." How ridiculous!
Causing beings to fall into pits of love and views, losing the road to enlightenment.
They cause all who associate with them to fall into pits of lustful love, making them lose the bodhi mind and end their lives in the hells.

Sutra text: 汝教世人修三摩地先斷心婬。是名如來先佛世尊第一決定清淨明誨。

Transliteration: Nhữ giáo thế nhân tu tam-ma-địa, tiên đoạn tâm dâm. Thị danh Như Lai tiên Phật Thế tôn đệ nhất quyết định thanh tịnh minh hối.

Translation: Teach people of the world to cultivate samadhi by first cutting off the lustful mind. This is called the former Buddhas, the World Honored Ones' first decisive, pure, clear instruction.

Commentary: Demon kings promote love. The difference between demon kings and the Bodhisattvas' teaching is most subtle — like turning the palm up or down. How do they differ?

Bodhisattvas also love people, but their love is sheltering compassion, far from lust. But a current of lust runs through everything demon kings say. Demon kings openly approve lust, to the point of saying the more indulgent in lust, the sooner one reaches certification. Such deviant doctrine harms people. Bodhisattvas no longer have lust; they make no distinction between beings and themselves. Demon kings have their own motives; they are greedy for wealth. Bodhisattvas have no motives behind and are not greedy. Related to this is the Buddha's teaching on the Twelvefold Conditioned Origination.

Teach people of the world to cultivate samadhi by first cutting off the lustful mind. Teach them to rid themselves of lustful thoughts. This is called the former Buddhas, the World Honored Ones' first decisive, pure, clear instruction.

This is the teaching method the Tathagata Buddhas employed. This is the decision of the Buddhas of past lives. This is the clear instruction teaching people how to attain a pure mind. Everyone must rid themselves of the lustful mind. There is not a shred of leeway. One cannot say one may both indulge lust and not. Everyone must rid themselves of it. If you wish to reach enlightenment yet retain lustful thoughts, then surely you are a disciple of demons.
