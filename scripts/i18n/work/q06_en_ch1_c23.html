Translation: The six roots are likewise: Originally relying on one refined brightness, Divided into sixfold union; When one dwelling attains rest and return, The six functions all cease to be; Defiling dust vanishes in a thought, Becoming perfect, luminous purity.

Commentary: Why does Manjushri Bodhisattva speak of dreams and illusions? Because the six roots are like illusory tricks.
The six roots are likewise.
The six roots are like puppet shows performed by puppeteers. It is as if a single mechanism controls them. But if one root becomes pure, the other roots also become at ease.
Originally relying on one refined brightness.
Originally just one refined-bright nature within the Tathagatagarbha. It divides into sixfold union.
Divided into eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, and mind.
When one dwelling attains rest and return — when the knots have been untied — then the six functions all cease to be.
If one root no longer functions, the other roots will also stop; they no longer have functions.
Defiling dust vanishes in a thought.
Every connection between the six roots and six objects ceases.
Becoming perfect, luminous purity.
They become the luminous, subtle true mind, the ever-abiding pure substance of the Tathagatagarbha.

Sutra text: 餘塵尚諸學 明極即如來。

Transliteration: Dư trần thượng chư học Minh cực tức Như Lai.

Translation: Remaining dust still means learners; Utmost brightness is precisely the Tathagata.

Commentary: Remaining dust still means learners.
When ignorance is first removed, there still remains subtle ignorance called birth-mark ignorance. This means remaining dust — afflictions remaining like dust motes. Bodhisattvas must still eliminate these subtle afflictions.
Utmost brightness is precisely the Tathagata.
That is the nature of the Tathagatagarbha.

Sutra text: 大眾及阿難 旋汝倒聞機 反聞聞自性 性成無上道 圓通實如是。

Transliteration: Đại chúng cập A-nan Triền nhữ đảo văn cơ Phản văn văn tự tánh Tánh thành vô thượng đạo Viên thông thật như thị.

Translation: Great assembly and Ananda: Turn back your inverted hearing mechanism; Return the hearing to hear the self-nature; The nature realized is the unsurpassed Way. Perfect penetration truly is just so.

Commentary: Great assembly and Ananda.
Turn back your inverted hearing mechanism.
You should cultivate by this method. Do not run seeking outside. Turn back. Look within and find yourselves again.
Return the hearing to hear the self-nature.
The nature realized is the unsurpassed Way.
Your nature can perfect the Unsurpassed Way.
Perfect penetration truly is just so.
