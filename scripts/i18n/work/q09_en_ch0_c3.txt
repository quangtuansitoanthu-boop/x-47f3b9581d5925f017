<p>They plant no causes of suffering, yet they cannot rely on blissful blessings forever; in the end they will decay. Suddenly they &#8220;know that bliss is not permanent &#8211; that decay is certain after long abiding &#8211; and both states of mind, suffering and bliss, are simultaneously renounced.&#8221; When they reach the Blessed Birth Heaven and cling to the bliss found there, they are mistaken. They should let go of both bliss and suffering until no false thought of suffering and no false thought of bliss remains. When they can do this, they will have the bliss of still extinction.</p>
<p>&#8220;Coarse and heavy appearances cease, and the nature of pure blessings arises.&#8221; True blessings appear when false thoughts do not arise &#8211; no suffering, no bliss. Only then do they gain the true, eternal, blissful blessings. Such cultivators dwell in the Blessed Birth Heaven.</p>


<ol start="2">
<li><strong>Xả tâm viên dung, thắng giải thanh tịnh. Phước vô già trung, đắc diệu tùy thuận, cùng vị lai tế. Như thị nhất loại, danh Phước ái Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>With the mind of renunciation perfectly interfused, superior understanding pure; blessings unobstructed, attaining wondrous compliance extending to the bounds of the future; such a class is called the Blessed Love Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;With the mind of renunciation perfectly interfused, superior understanding pure.&#8221; When they can perfectly interfuse affliction and bliss, true blessings appear. Having understood this truth of perfect interfusion, their wisdom opens and they gain genuine wisdom. They &#8220;attain wondrous compliance extending to the bounds of the future.&#8221; Nothing can obstruct these blessings; their magnitude is immense. These blessings bring the capacity to comply with the lives of beings. Such compliance is like this:<br />
If turned toward the east, it flows to the east;<br />
If turned toward the west, it flows to the west.</p>
<p>It is like a river: everything accords with their conduct, and every conduct is right, without error. Everything fits people's intentions; everything perfectly suits the wishes of others.</p>
<p>Wherever they go to do something, it is fitting &#8211; no obstruction arises. In whatever they do, no trouble befalls their work; every problem is resolved. That is the meaning of wondrous compliance extending to the bounds of the future. What are the bounds of the future? They are just that &#8211; the limits of the future; there is nothing more to say. Such gods dwell in the Blessed Love Heaven.</p>

<li><strong>A-nan! Tùng thị Thiên trung, hữu nhị kì lộ. Nhược ư tiên tâm, vô lượng tịnh quang. Phước đức Viên Minh, tu chứng nhi trụ. Như thị nhất loại, danh Quảng quả Thiên.</strong></li>

<p><strong>Ananda! From this heaven there diverge two paths: if, using the former mind of boundless pure light, perfect and bright with blessings and virtue, one cultivates realization and dwells in it, such a class is called the Extensive Fruition Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda, from this heaven &#8211; that is, from the second heaven of the Fourth Dhyana, the Blessed Love Heaven &#8211; there are two paths to take.&#8221; One path leads to the Extensive Fruition Heaven and the other to the No Thought Heaven; there is no other road.</p>
<p>&#8220;Those who expand their former thought&#8221; &#8211; the state of the Blessed Love Heaven &#8211; &#8220;using the former mind of boundless pure light, perfect and bright with blessings and virtue, cultivate realization and dwell in it.&#8221; Such cultivators dwell in the Extensive Fruition Heaven.<br />
Their conduct is complete and vast and their blessings and virtue very great, so they can abide in this heaven.</p>


<ol start="4">
<li><strong>Nhược ư tiên tâm, song yếm khổ lạc. Tinh nghiên xả tâm, tương tục bất đoạn. Viên cùng xả đạo, thân tâm câu diệt. Tâm lự hôi ngưng, Kinh ngũ bách kiếp. Thị nhân ký dĩ, sanh diệt vi nhân. Bất năng phát minh, bất sanh diệt tánh. Sơ bán kiếp diệt, hậu bán kiếp sanh. Như thị nhất loại, danh vô tưởng Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>If, using the former mind, one detests both suffering and bliss, intently investigating the mind of renunciation without interruption, perfecting the way of renunciation until both body and mind vanish, the mental functions stilled and frozen, passing through five hundred eons; yet since such a person has taken arising and ceasing as the cause, he cannot discover the nature of non-arising and non-ceasing, and so for the first half of the eon there is cessation and for the latter half arising; such a class is called the No Thought Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;If, using the former mind, one detests both suffering and bliss, intently investigating the mind of renunciation without interruption&#8221; &#8211; this opens up this new path. If those in the Blessed Love Heaven described before can detest suffering and bliss and renounce both, intently investigating suffering and bliss without interruption until they perfect the way of renunciation, body and mind vanish and the mental functions are stilled. At that point they have attained the samadhi of no thought, which they experience for five hundred eons. &#8220;Yet since such a person has taken arising and ceasing as the cause, he cannot discover the nature of non-arising and non-ceasing.&#8221; Their lifespan is five hundred great eons, but because the cause they planted is one of arising and ceasing.</p>
<p>&#8220;And so for the first half of the eon there is cessation.&#8221; This speaks of detesting suffering and bliss: when they recognize the truth of suffering and bliss, false thoughts no longer arise. But after two hundred and fifty great eons, false thoughts arise again. &#8220;For the latter half there is arising.&#8221; The blessings of cultivating the no-thought samadhi are exhausted. When the blessings run out, they hold wrong views about the Three Jewels. When false thoughts arise, that is the first step toward the end of their lifespan; when their samadhi is shattered, they blame the Three Jewels. What do they say? &#8220;The Buddha said the fourth dhyana stage is the end of birth and death, free from rebirth. Now I have attained the Fourth Dhyana &#8211; why must I still drift into the six paths of rebirth?&#8221;</p>
<p>That is how they blame the Three Jewels. In fact, the Fourth Dhyana they attained is not the Fourth Dhyana of the Arhat; it does not even equal the first fruition of Arhatship. They mistakenly thought they had entered the fourth stage of Arhatship &#8211; attaining the Fourth Dhyana stage, they thought it was the fourth stage of Arhatship. Cultivators without knowledge make such mistakes. Those who follow this path dwell in the No Thought Heaven.</p>
<p><strong>A-nan! Thử tứ thắng lưu, nhất thiết thế gian. Chư khổ lạc cảnh, sở bất năng động. Tuy phi vô vi, chân bất động địa. Hữu sở đắc tâm, công dụng thuần thục, danh vi tứ Thiền.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda! These four superior levels cannot be moved by any states of suffering or bliss in the world. Though it is not the true unmoving ground of the unconditioned way, with a mind that has something to attain, their skill is mature and refined; this is called the Fourth Dhyana.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary: </strong>&#8220;Ananda! These four superior levels cannot be moved by any states of suffering or bliss in the world.&#8221; Whether they undergo suffering or blessings and bliss, their minds do not change; whether they undergo suffering or bliss, their minds are unmoved.</p>
<p>&#8220;Though it is not the true unmoving ground of the unconditioned way.&#8221; They can still be self-possessed and unmoved, but it is forced &#8211; they have not attained a higher stage &#8211; because &#8220;with a mind that has something to attain.&#8221; In the Fourth Dhyana they still have thoughts of attainment &#8211; for instance, the thought of one beyond study that they have attained the fourth fruition of Arhatship. &#8220;Their skill is mature and refined.&#8221; They have reached the highest level of cultivation for their particular practice. They are called the Fourth Dhyana. This is the realm of the Fourth Dhyana. <strong><br />
</strong></p>

<p><strong>A-nan! Thử trung phục hữu, ngũ bất hoàn thiên. Ư hạ giới trung, cửu phẩm tập khí, câu thời diệt tận. Khổ lạc song vong, hạ vô bốc cư. Cố ư xả tâm, chúng đồng phần trung, an lập cư xử.</strong></p>
