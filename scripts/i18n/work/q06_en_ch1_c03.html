The beings mentioned here are precisely ourselves. We who are hearing Dharma in the Buddhist Lecture Hall are exactly those the Buddha means. Any of you reading this sutra are also included in this group. So do not stand apart and say, "I have no part in it." That way, you only confuse yourselves more. If you place yourselves outside that number, you will fall into a later life, and if you become horses, pigs, cows, dogs, chickens, it will not be easy to hear the Shurangama Sutra. For one thing, you could never leap four flights of stairs in one bound to reach the lecture hall! In Hong Kong, there were ducks that came to hear my sutra lectures, but that was on the ground floor.

Long ago, Shakyamuni Buddha already arranged seats for us in this Dharma assembly, so that it unfolds as it does now. Do not slight yourselves. Of course, if you do not come here now to hear the sutra, then you will have no part.

"Among these dharma-doors, which is most suitable for beings of later ages with great roots who bring forth the resolve seeking the Unsurpassed Way?" the Buddha asks Manjushri. "Which dharma-door most easily leads to accomplishment? Manjushri, take care to select one dharma-door."

Sutra text: 文殊師利法王子奉佛慈旨,即從座起頂禮佛足。承佛威神說偈對佛。

Transliteration: Văn-thù-sư-lợi Pháp vương tử phụng Phật từ chỉ, tức tòng toà khởi đỉnh lễ Phật túc. Thừa phật oai thần, thuyết kệ đối Phật.

Translation: Manjushri, Prince of Dharma, receiving the Buddha's compassionate instruction, at once rose from his seat, bowed at the Buddha's feet, and, relying on the Buddha's awesome spirit, answered the Buddha in verse:

Commentary: Manjushri, Prince of Dharma, receiving the Buddha's compassionate instruction, at once rose from his seat.

The Bodhisattva Wonderfully Auspicious was entrusted by the Buddha with selecting, from among the eighteen realms and the seven elements, one method of cultivation to attain perfect penetration. He will find the dharma-door most suitable for Ananda and for all of us to cultivate. The six sense roots, six sense-objects, and six consciousnesses make up the eighteen realms. Earth, water, fire, wind, together with space, consciousness, and perception, make up the seven elements as explained in full detail before.

Hearing the Buddha's compassionate instruction, Manjushri Bodhisattva at once rose from his seat, bowed at the Buddha's feet, and, relying on the Buddha's awesome spirit, answered in verse:

The Bodhisattvas and Arhats all had seats around the Buddha, unlike the Vajra Fire Chief, who had to stand in the Dharma assembly. A verse may consist of many syllables and many lines, but it always contains the principles of the sutra.

Sutra text: 覺海性澄圓 圓澄覺元妙 　元明照生所 所立照性亡。

Transliteration: Giác hải tánh trừng viên Viên trừng giác nguyên diệu Nguyên minh chiếu sanh sở Sở lập chiếu tánh vong.

Translation: The sea of enlightenment, its nature clear and perfect; Perfect, clear, the enlightened source sublime; The original brightness shone and produced the "object"; The "object" established, the shining nature was lost.
