<p>Or saying: &#8220;This eon is about to perish.&#8221; Or threatening that war is about to break out here, or that Russia is about to go to war with this or that country.</p>
<p><strong>Terrifying people, causing them to pointlessly squander their family wealth.</strong></p>
<p>He constantly says things to draw people's attention, then unsettles them: once people hear such news, they panic: &#8220;Oh! No! War is coming &#8211; my life is in danger!&#8221;</p>
<p>Or the demon says: &#8220;War is coming. If you hand money to me at once, I shall guarantee your life safe; if you do not give me money, you will lose your life.&#8221; It always speaks so to cheat people.</p>
<p>If you wish to tell who is genuine and who is false &#8211; whether someone is a Bodhisattva or a heavenly demon &#8211; you need only look at these points: first, see whether the person has a mind of lust; second, see whether the person craves money. If the person cheats people to satisfy greed for wealth and fondness for beauty, that person is not genuine. What is greed for wealth? For example, the person may say: &#8220;A great disaster is about to befall us &#8211; the world is coming to its end. An atom bomb is about to explode over there; an H-bomb is about to drop on that place.&#8221;</p>
<p>Speaking so, making people panic, then bringing money to offer it. If you stay alert to its tricks &#8211; even if it emits light, shakes the earth, or does other extraordinary things &#8211; you should not believe it, for it has a greedy mind. One without a greedy mind is genuine; one greedy is false. The method I now teach is practical and tested.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thử danh quái quỷ, niên lão thành ma, não loạn thị nhân. Yểm túc tâm sanh, khứ bỉ nhân thể. Đệ tử dữ sư, câu hãm vương nạn.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is called a strange ghost, aged into a demon, harassing this person. Sated after its disturbance, it arises and leaves that person's body. Both disciple and teacher fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>This is called a strange ghost</strong>, living a long time, <strong>aged into a demon</strong> &#8211; specializing in <strong>harassing cultivators</strong>. <strong>Sated after its disturbance, it arises and leaves the possessed person's body</strong>: at last it tires of this game &#8211; having seen enough, it stops possessing others. When this happens, <strong>both disciple and teacher fall into legal peril</strong> &#8211; wanted by the authorities, accused of inciting the masses, liable to execution or equivalent prison terms.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đương tiên giác, bất nhập luân hồi. Mê hoặc bất tri, đọa vô gián ngục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel. Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the demon king's turning wheel of birth and death</strong> &#8211; you must awaken to this early, and not fall into the demon king's wheel of birth and death.</p>
<p><strong>Deluded and unknowing</strong>, in the future <strong>one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong><em>2 &#8211; FOND OF TRAVELING</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: A-nan, hựu thiện nam tử, thọ ấm  hư diệu, bất tao tà lự. Viên định phát minh, tam-ma-địa trung, tâm ái du đãng, phi kì tinh tư, tham cầu kinh lịch.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Ananda, again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed &#8211; within samadhi the mind fond of roaming, letting thought fly out, greedily seeking to travel about.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>Ananda</strong>, <strong>again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns. In</strong> the empty, penetrating, wondrous state gained after the feeling skandha has dissolved &#8211; the cultivator cultivating samadhi no longer has deviant concerns &#8211; disclosed, dwelling in the bright, perfect samadhi. <strong>Within samadhi</strong> the <strong>mind</strong> has false thoughts <strong>fond of roaming</strong>.</p>
<p>That is &#8220;exiting through the crown of the head and wandering with the soul&#8221; &#8211; the soul roaming everywhere: hence thought flies out, greedily seeking to roam. <strong>Letting</strong> the <strong>spirit fly out, wishing to go everywhere</strong> sightseeing at ease.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhĩ thời thiên ma, hầu đắc kì tiện, phi tinh phụ nhân, khẩu thuyết kinh pháp.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Then the heavenly demon, waiting for its convenient chance, sends its essence to possess another person, using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Then the heavenly demon waits for its convenient chance</strong>: the demon king in the heavens again grows jealous, waiting for a lapse to possess.</p>
<p><strong>Sending its essence to possess another person</strong> &#8211; again sending a trusted follower to possess that person. <strong>Using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma</strong>: it comes to harass the cultivator. Why is the cultivator possessed by demons? Simply because he greedily seeks roaming, wishing to travel and wander about: the moment he gives rise to the thought longing to roam, he at once gives the heavenly demon a convenient chance to torment him. Demons can possess nowhere unless the cultivator gives rise to such greedy false thoughts.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Kì nhân diệc bất giác tri ma trước, diệc ngôn tự đắc, vô thượng niết-bàn. Lai bỉ cầu du, thiện nam tử xứ, phu tòa thuyết pháp, tự hình vô biến. Kì thính pháp giả, hốt tự kiến thân, tọa bảo liên hoa, toàn thể hóa thành, tử kim quang tụ. Nhất chúng thính nhân, các các như thị, đắc vị tằng hữu.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: That person, likewise unaware of demonic possession, likewise claims to have attained unsurpassed nirvana. Coming to the place of the good man seeking to roam, it spreads its seat and preaches the Dharma, its own form unchanged. But its listeners suddenly see themselves seated on jeweled lotuses, their whole bodies transformed into masses of purple-golden light. Each listener alike, gaining what never was.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>That person, likewise unaware of demonic possession,</strong> <strong>likewise claiming to have attained unsurpassed Bodhi</strong> &#8211; proclaiming to have reached the wondrous fruition of unsurpassed nirvana. When <strong>the demon comes to the place of the one seeking to roam</strong> &#8211; the good man who has given rise to the false thought wishing his spirit to exit through the crown of his head and go wandering &#8211;</p>
<p>The demon <strong>spreads its seat, preaching the Dharma</strong>, its own <strong>preaching form unchanged.</strong></p>
<p>In the sutra passage lectured before, the possessed person's body transformed into a bhikshu, bhikshuni, Shakra, laywoman, or countless other shapes. But in this case the demon-possessed person's body does not change. There are many kinds of demons, and you should not mistake ordinary matters for wholesome Dharma: if that happens and you do not recognize it correctly, demons will possess you at once.</p>
<p><strong>But its listeners suddenly see themselves seated on jeweled lotus thrones, their whole bodies transformed into masses of purple-golden light.</strong></p>
