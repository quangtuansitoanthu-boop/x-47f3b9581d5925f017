Sutra text: 汝以婬身求佛妙果,縱得妙悟皆是婬根。根本成婬,輪轉三途,必不能出。如來涅槃,何路修證?

Transliteration: Nhữ dĩ dâm thân cầu Phật diệu quả, túng đắc diệu ngộ giai thị dâm căn. Căn bổn thành dâm, luân chuyển tam đồ, tất bất năng xuất. Như Lai niết-bàn, hà lộ tu chứng?

Translation: Seeking the Buddhas' sublime fruition with a lustful body, though you attain sublime awakening, it is all the lust-root. The root become lust, you must turn in the three paths, surely unable to escape. By what road shall the Tathagata's nirvana be cultivated and certified?

Commentary: The Buddha says to Ananda, "Your fondness for Matanga's daughter concerns not only lustful thoughts, but your lust-root."

Seeking the Buddhas' sublime fruition with a lustful body, though you attain sublime awakening, it is all the lust-root. Though you certify to the quintessence of the sublime principle, you still have not rid yourself of the lust-root.

The root become lust, you must turn in the three paths, surely unable to escape. In the future, you will surely fall into the hells. The "three paths" are the realms of animals, hungry ghosts, and hells. You will surely be involved with these three realms and never escape.

By what road shall the Tathagata's nirvana be cultivated and certified?
Within these realms, which road leads you to the fruitions?

Sutra text: 必使婬機,身心俱斷 。斷性亦無。於佛菩提,斯可希冀。

Transliteration: Tất sử dâm cơ, thân tâm câu đoạn. Đoạn tánh diệc vô. Ư Phật bồ-đề, tư khả hy ký.

Translation: One must cause the lust-mechanism to be cut off in both body and mind. Even the nature of cutting off is gone. Then the Buddhas' bodhi may be hoped for.

Commentary: One must cause the lust-mechanism to be cut off in both body and mind. You must rid yourself of every lustful thought, however slight and subtle. That means ignorance itself must be cut off entirely. It must be done with both body and mind. Even the nature of cutting off is gone. Even the idea of cutting off ignorance must be cut off. Then the Buddhas' bodhi may be hoped for.

Sutra text: 如我此說名為佛說。不如此說即波旬說

Transliteration: Như ngã thử thuyết, danh vi Phật thuyết. Bất như thử thuyết tức ba-tuần thuyết.

Translation: What I say thus is spoken by the Buddhas. What is not spoken thus is spoken by Papiyan.
