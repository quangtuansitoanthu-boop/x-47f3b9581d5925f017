-----
{align:justify|A young man who did not believe in predictions was dragged to my place by his family. He sat huddled in a corner, wanting nothing to do with it. Urged by his family, he shouted:}
{align:justify|All you fortune-telling prophets are frauds—why believe a bunch of wandering charlatans peddling scams? Every one of them is a swindler!}
{align:justify|I calmly told him:}
{align:justify|"In this world there is the true and the false! There are indeed many swindlers. Why not use your wisdom and think for yourself, to tell the false from the true…?"}
{align:justify|He answered:}
{align:justify|"I don't care! You're just a swindler anyway!"}
{align:justify|I said: I know all about your affairs!}
{align:justify|"I don't believe it!" he retorted bluntly.}
{align:justify|I smiled and said: Yesterday you fell off your bike! Your right leg bled. Right?}
{align:justify|His eyes widened in astonishment, and he said:}
{align:justify|"I never told anyone—not even my family knew. Only I knew. How do you know so well?"}
{align:justify|Then he rolled up his trouser leg and showed everyone the wound. His right leg was indeed injured, the blood already dried.}
