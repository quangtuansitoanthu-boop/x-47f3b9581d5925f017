<p>Suddenly everyone feels as if seated on a jeweled lotus throne, the whole body transformed into a mass of blazing purple-golden color.</p>
<p><strong>Every listener feels so, deeming themselves to have gained what never was.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thị nhân ngu mê, hoặc vi bồ-tát, dâm dật kì tâm, phá Phật luật nghi, tiềm hành tham dục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: That person, deluded, mistakes it for a Bodhisattva; the mind turned lustful, breaking the Buddha's moral code, secretly practicing greed and lust.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>That person, deluded, mistakes it for a Bodhisattva</strong>.</p>
<p>Not only this person &#8211; all the listeners are very deluded, their minds grown doubtful, thinking: &#8220;He must be a Bodhisattva &#8211; he can make me sit on a seven-jeweled lotus throne and turn my body into a mass of blazing purple-golden color.&#8221;</p>
<p>You should ask yourselves: &#8220;Am I truly seated on a lotus throne blazing purple-golden?&#8221; How could you, in an ordinary body, have such a state? Is this not a heavenly demon come to delude? Why would a Bodhisattva display such a state? What is that Bodhisattva possessing you for?</p>
<p><strong>The mind turned lustful</strong>.</p>
<p>The minds of those hearing its sutra-lectures grow unsettled, beginning to give rise to lustful thoughts.</p>
<p><strong>Breaking the Buddha's moral code</strong>.</p>
<p>They no longer keep the Buddha's precepts. You must all remember: after receiving the precepts, do not be deceived by such deluding states. Even if a Dharma master displays great spiritual powers, you must observe him carefully to see whether he has a greedy mind: if he craves money or harbors lust, he is not one with a true mind &#8211; he is a fraud. But if the person no longer has lust or greed, hiding no inner designs, he is genuine. If he destroys the Buddha's precepts, he is a cheat.</p>
<p><strong>Secretly practicing lustful acts</strong>.</p>
<p>Indulging wantonly, dissolutely, in secret.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Khẩu trung hảo ngôn, chư Phật ứng thế. Mỗ xứ mỗ nhân, đương thị mỗ Phật, hóa thân lai thử. Mỗ nhân tức thị, mỗ bồ-tát đẳng lai hóa nhân gian. Kì nhân kiến cố, tâm sanh khát ngưỡng, tà kiến mật hưng, chủng trí tiêu diệt.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Its mouth delights in saying Buddhas are appearing in the world: that person, that place, is precisely such a Buddha, manifesting its transformation-body here; that person is precisely such a Bodhisattva come to teach the human realm. On seeing this, that person gives rise to thirsting admiration; wrong views secretly flourish, and the seed-wisdom is destroyed.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Its mouth delights in saying Buddhas are appearing in the world.</strong> The demon's mouth loves saying Buddhas are manifesting here and there.</p>
<p>The possessed person loves wandering about, then exploits that roaming to speak of mysterious, uncanny things &#8211; things he says so fantastically people hardly believe. He says: &#8220;Now Buddhas are manifesting in the world to save beings.&#8221;</p>
<p>He says <strong>that person, that place, is precisely such a Buddha, manifesting its transformation-body</strong> &#8211; that Buddha has manifested in the world in that person, at that place.</p>
<p>It says he is precisely such-and-such a Buddha&#8230; manifesting at that place to save all beings. <strong>Or saying that person is precisely a Bodhisattva manifesting here to teach the human realm</strong>.</p>
<p>For example, a disciple of mine named Gary (Cát-lợi) reported: Samalosa (?) said his teacher (Samalosa) is a Bodhisattva. How absurd! What kind of Bodhisattva? A demon-Bodhisattva? They only deceive people. I shall tell you plainly: a Buddha or a Bodhisattva never claims to be a Buddha or Bodhisattva &#8211; still less lets his disciples go advertising him.</p>
<p>&#8220;My teacher is a Bodhisattva&#8221; &#8211; never speak of your teacher so. Why would you say your teacher is a Buddha? Never speak of me so. Have I not told you I am a demon? Speak of your teacher only as a demon. I do not wish to be called a Buddha or Bodhisattva. How pitiable these people are! Here they say: &#8220;This is a Bodhisattva manifesting to teach beings in the Dharma-ending age.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>On seeing this, the cultivator gives rise to thirsting admiration</strong>.</p>
<p>Because such people have immature understanding, not truly understanding the Buddhadharma. They think:</p>
<p>&#8220;Oh! He is a Bodhisattva &#8211; wonderful! Quickly go hear him lecture and ask to become his disciple.&#8221;</p>
<p>As yesterday, when some people came to steal the Dharma by listening: I asked who their teacher was, and they answered so-and-so&#8230; That is a case in point. Their minds brim with thirsting admiration.</p>
<p>&#8220;I should ask that Bodhisattva to be my teacher, and draw near to learn the Bodhisattva Dharma from him.&#8221;</p>
<p>Since he is a Bodhisattva, of course disciples learning from him will also become Bodhisattvas &#8211; as a necessary result.</p>
<p><strong>Wrong views secretly flourish</strong>: gradually wrong views grow thicker, and the seed-wisdom is destroyed.</p>
<p><strong>Seed-wisdom</strong> &#8211; the Buddha's and all kinds of wisdom &#8211; is destroyed; the seeds of wisdom <strong>destroyed</strong> &#8211; truly perilous.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thử danh bạt quỷ, niên lão thành ma, não loạn thị nhân, yểm túc tâm sanh, khứ bỉ nhân thể. Đệ tử dữ sư, câu hãm vương nạn.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is called a drought-ghost, aged into a demon, harassing this person; sated, it arises and leaves that person's body. Both disciple and teacher fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>This is called a <em>drought-ghost</em>, aged into a demon</strong>.</p>
<p>Wherever this kind of <em>drought-ghost</em> goes, there is no rain &#8211; drought lasting at least six months, sometimes one, two, or five years. Wherever this demon goes, there is no rain. When young, it is called a demon; grown old, it grows more vicious and treacherous, turning into a ghost. There is an ancient saying:</p>
<p>&#8220;<em>Growing old without dying makes one a villain</em>&#8221; &#8211; old yet undying is cunning. All the more cunning for a ghost: a demon old yet undying turns into a ghost.</p>
<p>It <strong>harasses the cultivator. Sated after a time</strong>.</p>
<p>After playing its cheating game a long time, the demon tires of it &#8211; nothing amusing anymore. <strong>It arises and leaves the possessed person's body. Then both teacher and disciple fall into legal peril</strong> &#8211; both arrested and jailed, liable to execution or life imprisonment &#8211; life as if in hell.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đương tiên giác, bất nhập luân hồi. Mê hoặc bất tri, đọa vô gián ngục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel. Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel</strong> &#8211; within the demon king's wheel, not becoming the demon king's retinue. <strong>Deluded and unknowing</strong>: failing to recognize the demon king's deeds, one follows the demon king, <strong>falling into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
