Translation: Second, because my hearing and contemplation transcend the six sense-objects, like sound passing through a wall unobstructed, my sublime function can manifest each and every form and recite each and every mantra. Those forms and those mantras can bestow fearlessness upon beings. Therefore the lands of the ten directions, as numerous as dust motes, all call me the Bestower of Fearlessness.

Commentary: The second non-doing wondrous virtue is: Second, because my hearing and contemplation transcend the six sense-objects. I cultivated the wisdom of hearing and the wisdom of contemplation, transcending the six sense-objects of form, sound, scent, taste, touch, and mental objects. I am no longer attached to them. It is like sound passing through a wall, unobstructed. Therefore my sublime function can manifest each and every form and recite each and every mantra.

The forms I manifest and the mantras I recite can all bestow the power of fearlessness upon beings. They all have the power to grant fearlessness. Therefore the lands of the ten directions, as numerous as dust motes, all call me the Bestower of Fearlessness.

Sutra text: 三者由我修習本妙圓通清淨本根,所遊世界,皆令眾生,捨身珍寶求我哀愍。

Transliteration: Tam giả do ngã tu tập bổn diệu viên thông thanh tịnh bổn căn, sở du thế giới, giai linh chúng sanh, xả thân trân bảo cầu ngã ai mẫn.

Translation: Third, because I cultivated the originally sublime, perfectly interfused, pure original root, in whatever world I travel, I cause beings to renounce their bodies and precious treasures to seek my pity.

Commentary: Third, because I cultivated the originally sublime, perfectly interfused, pure original root. He is speaking of the pure original nature of the ear root — the hearing-nature. In whatever world I travel, I cause beings to renounce their bodies and precious treasures to seek my pity. Beings all sacrifice their lives and give up all their precious possessions to seek my help.

Sutra text: 四者我得佛心證於究竟。能以珍寶種種供養十方如來, 傍及法界六道眾生。

Transliteration: Tứ giả ngã đắc Phật tâm, chứng ư cứu cánh. Năng dĩ trân bảo chủng chủng cúng dường thập phương Như Lai, bàng cập pháp giới lục đạo chúng sanh.

Translation: Fourth, because I have attained the Buddha's mind and certified to the ultimate, I can with all kinds of precious treasures make offerings to the Tathagatas of the ten directions, and extensively to the beings of the six paths throughout the Dharma Realm.

Commentary: The fourth non-doing wondrous virtue is: Because I have attained the Buddha's mind and certified to the ultimate. I have certified to the true mind, the Tathagatagarbha. "Ultimate" means the highest station of Buddhahood. Thus I can with all kinds of precious treasures make offerings to the Tathagatas of the ten directions, and extensively to the beings of the six paths throughout the Dharma Realm. Whatever beings wish for, I grant them to fulfillment.

Sutra text: 求妻得妻求子得子。求三昧得三昧。求長壽得長壽。如是乃至求大涅槃得大涅槃。

Transliteration: Cầu thê đắc thê cầu tử đắc tử, cầu tam-muội đắc tam-muội. Cầu trường thọ đắc trường thọ. Như thị nãi chí cầu đại niết-bàn đắc đại niết-bàn.

Translation: Those who seek wives obtain wives; who seek children obtain children; who seek samadhi obtain samadhi; who seek long life obtain long life. Thus even unto seeking great nirvana, they obtain great nirvana.
