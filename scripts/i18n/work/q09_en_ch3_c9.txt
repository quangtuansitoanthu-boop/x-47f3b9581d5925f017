<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>After the Tathagata's extinction into nirvana, you should transmit the Tathagata's words, so that beings of the Dharma-ending age all awaken to these meanings.</strong></p>
<p>Ananda, after the Tathagata enters nirvana, you should transmit the Tathagata's words &#8211; proclaim the words the Tathagata taught.</p>
<p>So that beings of the Dharma-ending age all awaken to these meanings &#8211; enabling beings to hear the principles, understand, and awaken.</p>
<p><strong>Do not let the heavenly demons gain their advantage</strong>. <strong>Do not let demons get a convenient chance to disturb cultivators.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Protect and shelter beings in cultivation until the unsurpassed Way is accomplished.</strong></p>
