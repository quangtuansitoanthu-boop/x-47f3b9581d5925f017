Commentary: You should know that such meat-eaters, though their minds open as if in samadhi, are all great rakshasas. With no regard for what kind of meat they are eating. They do not care whether it is among the three kinds of pure meat or the five kinds of pure meat. If it is meat, they eat it. Ananda, you should know that after the Tathagata's extinction, such people will disguise themselves as the Buddha's disciples, and they will use both wine and meat. They are most unrestrained, saying everyone is free to do whatever they wish. Though they have a little awakening or a little wisdom, they only resemble being in samadhi." In truth it is not so. They are like those who came here and proclaimed themselves the Sixth Patriarch.

I asked: "What proof do you have for calling yourself the Sixth Patriarch?"
The person answered: "I have no proof that I am not." He thought that a clever retort. In truth, he belongs to the same kind as those described in this sutra passage. Such people seem to have a little samadhi power, but in fact they are great rakshasas, great demon kings, great ghosts and spirits. When their retribution ends, they must sink into the bitter sea of birth and death. They are not the Buddha's disciples. Though such people wear the Buddha's kashaya and eat the Buddha's food, they are not the Buddha's disciples. Such people kill and devour one another, eating without end. They live and eat meat, minding nothing at all. They keep devouring one another. They kill one another; you eat me, I eat you back; killing and eating; then in turn being killed and eaten.

How can such people transcend the Triple Realm? Thus they create an endless wheel of birth and death. This life you eat my flesh; next life I eat yours. The life after, back again you eat mine, and on it goes. How can such beings transcend the Desire Realm, Form Realm, and Formless Realm?

Sutra text: 汝教世人修三摩地,次斷殺生。是名如來先佛世尊第二決定清淨明誨。

Transliteration: Nhữ giáo thế nhân tu tam-ma-địa, thứ đoạn sát sanh. Thị danh Như Lai Tiên Phật Thế Tôn đệ nhị quyết định thanh tịnh minh hối.

Translation: Teach people of the world to cultivate samadhi by next cutting off killing. This is called the Tathagatas, the former Buddhas, the World Honored Ones' second decisive, pure, clear instruction.

Commentary: Teach people of the world to cultivate samadhi by next cutting off killing. First, cut off lust; then also restrain killing thoughts. This is called the Tathagatas, the former Buddhas, the World Honored Ones' second decisive, pure, clear instruction. This is the teaching the Buddha proclaims. Both the Buddhas of the past and the Buddhas of this age proclaim this decisive, pure, clear teaching. You must surely honor this teaching. Otherwise, you can never transcend the three realms.

Sutra text: 是故阿難,若不斷殺修禪定者,譬如有人自塞其耳,高聲大叫求人不聞。此等名為欲隱彌露。

Transliteration: Thị cố A-nan, nhược bất đoạn sát tu thiền định giả, thí như hữu nhân tự tắc kỳ nhĩ, cao thanh đại khiếu, cầu nhân bất văn. Thử đẳng danh vi dục ẩn di lộ.

Translation: Therefore, Ananda, cultivating dhyana without cutting off killing is like a person who stops his own ears, shouts loudly, and hopes no one hears. Such ones are called those who, wishing to hide, reveal all the more.
