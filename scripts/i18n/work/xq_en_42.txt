TITLE: Story 42: SPIRIT CAT
TOMTAT: 

{align:center|**Spirit CAT**}
Trong cõi nhân gian, con người thường cho rằng chỉ có con người mới biết hận thù, mới mang oán niệm. Thế nhưng, ít ai hiểu rằng vạn vật đều có linh, từ cây cỏ, sông núi cho đến muông thú quanh ta. When the spiritual energy gathers long enough, that creature is no longer an inanimate object, but has entered another level of existence - a spirit animal, even a star. Và một khi đã mang linh tính, thì oán nghiệp sinh ra cũng sâu nặng chẳng kém gì con người. Today's journey in the Salvation Mission is a scary story, told by the deceased himself to my group, then compiled and sent to Duc.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-42-image-477-8f5d13fd8ac5.webp)
No additions or subtractions, no fabrications. If you believe, think about it to correct yourself. If you don't believe, then consider it as a spiritual story for entertainment. But hopefully, after hearing this, no one will dare to take killing lightly. The main character of the story is a dead man who died at the age of 37, died in strange circumstances, related to a black cat turned into a ghost and an inescapable life debt. 
When he was alive, this man had a wife and three children. Although his life was not abundant, his life was peaceful. He does all kinds of jobs, as long as he has money to take care of his family, not afraid of suffering, not afraid of hardship. He is not an evil person, nor is he a deceitful person. Thế nhưng, chỉ một khoảnh khắc nóng giận và thiếu hiểu biết, đã đẩy anh vào vòng xoáy nhân quả nghiệt ngã. Năm anh tròn 37 tuổi, trong xóm có một người đàn ông qua đời vì bệnh nan y. Tang lễ được tổ chức theo phong tục, thi thể đặt trong mùng. On the third day, when many guests came to pay their respects, a terrible incident happened. A black cat came out of nowhere and jumped straight into the mosquito net and jumped over the dead man's body.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-42-image-478-927ddcc51315.webp)
At that moment, the corpse that had been lying for three days jumped up, its body convulsed, its mouth opened as if it wanted to scream. The scene caused chaos at the funeral, people ran away, others were so scared that they collapsed. According to folklore, cats jumping over corpses is a big taboo, carrying extremely heavy evil spirits. In the midst of the chaos, the man - who later died - out of fear and panic, jumped into the mosquito net and grabbed the black cat's tail. In excitement, he threw the cat several times until it died on the spot. The cat's death was extremely tragic. The body convulsed, teeth bared, eyes widened, and blood flowed. 
According to legend, before the cat stopped breathing, the cat's eyes still stared at him, not leaving for even a moment, that look seemed to be engraved straight into his soul. The funeral then continued, the mosquito net was tied tighter, no animal dared to come near. Everyone thought it was over. But in reality, resentment has only just begun.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-42-image-479-d6bdc4154e8d.webp)
Hai ngày sau, vào ban đêm, anh vô tình nhìn thấy ba con mèo trắng đứng ngoài sân, nhìn anh chằm chằm trong bóng tối. I don't care. Đến sáng, mới phát hiện con của mình đã bắt ba con mèo ấy về nuôi. Since the three white cats appeared, his family has continuously encountered misfortunes: - His parents suddenly fell ill - The children are constantly sick - The family is in turmoil and quarrels endlessly - He himself lost his job and was unemployed for a long time. 
Tai họa nối tiếp tai họa, như có một bàn tay vô hình bóp nghẹt vận khí của cả gia đình. Một ngày nọ, khi đi chợ, anh gặp một người đàn ông lạ mặt. This person looked at him for a long time and then said: "You once killed a black cat that jumped over a human body, right? That cat was not an ordinary cat, but a mature cat. The three white cats you are raising are related to it." The stranger gave him a talisman, telling him to burn it and mix it with water for the cats to drink and then let them go somewhere else, otherwise the family would face great disaster. He half believed and half doubted. Trên đường về thì gặp mưa, lá bùa bị ướt.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-42-image-480-44b49c359e91.webp)
Instead of drying it and doing as instructed, he forgot the talisman in the basin of water and let everything pass. That is the last chance to resolve resentment. Three days later, while sleeping, he fell into a semi-conscious state. Suddenly I saw a figure entering the house. He tried to open his eyes and saw a human body, but the face was that of a black cat, exactly like the cat he had killed. Its hands tightened around his neck, the strength was cold and cruel. He couldn't scream, couldn't struggle. And dying right in the dream, the soul leaves the body. The three white cats then disappeared without a trace.![](https://media.quangtu.vn/content/smdv-1ab0570c44ac47b5bb43f328aed7464b/chapter-42-image-481-ebfe52a94d75.webp)
After death, he was not led by ghosts or Black White Impermanence. Instead, he met the divine mandarin who governed the area. This person said: "Your fate has not yet ended, your yang life is still alive. Because of the karma of killing spiritual objects, you must wander the earth until your yang lifespan is complete." 
During that time, thanks to Tien Ong's guidance, my group came to enlighten me and took me back to the temple to study. But studying does not mean the end of sin. When enough time has passed, the soul must return to the underworld to continue examining karma and reincarnation. 
The story of mistakenly killing a cat to become a ghost is a profound warning. It's not just humans who have resentment. Mascots are unjustly killed, especially those with high spirituality and extremely strong resentment. Network debt, in the end still has to be paid with the network. Through this story, each person should: - Limit killing, if you can stop, the better - Do not arbitrarily kill dogs, cats, and wild animals - Do not bring strange objects, roadside stones, old trees home - Respect the life of all things Cause and effect is not wrong one inch. Whatever you sow – reap. Can't hide from people - can't hide from heaven. If you believe, think about it and fix it. If you don't believe it, it seems like a scary story. Finally, I wish everyone to live a good life - less killing - more happiness, so that the path of life will be peaceful, and not meet each other in the dark underworld.COMMENTS:
After reading the story of CAT TINH, I sat quietly for a long time. Not because of fear, but because we see one thing clearly: in each of us there are times of weakness, times when due to circumstances, pressure, or for some reason we have chosen paths that we can't help but feel sad when looking back on.

This story reminds me that Karma is not something far away or only exists after death. It is present in the small, everyday choices. A word, an action, a decision for "love" or for "money" - all are sowing seeds. And sooner or later, we will be the ones reaping the harvest.

But what warms my heart after every story like this is: it's never too late. Even though they have caused heavy karma, even though they have endured suffering for many years and many lifetimes, the spirits in the book still have the opportunity to let go, have the opportunity to be guided, repented and transformed. That is the power of mindfulness and compassion.

For those of you reading these lines, perhaps you are not someone who has caused great karma like the character in the story. But each of us has times when we make mistakes, times when we hurt others because of selfishness or ignorance. This story is a gentle reminder: stop while you still can. Let's choose a way to live so that when night comes, we can sleep peacefully with a peaceful conscience.An act of kindness, a sincere apology, a decision to live a good life today - no matter how small - can change the fate of ourselves and those around us for many lifetimes to come. That's what I want to convey after listening to this story.
