He inhales and exhales like a storm. He breathes in and out very rapidly, as if he had become an immortal who can exhale clouds and spit out mist. An alcoholic swears he will never touch another drop. But when he gets angry about something, he buys a bottle and drinks to drown his sorrow. Who would guess that the more he drinks, the more desperate he becomes. When he wakes after a drunken binge, every joint in his body aches. Ask anyone who has drunk alcohol, and they will tell you the next morning is truly terrible. If he is broke at the time, he can do nothing but endure. But if a little money remains, of course he will buy another bottle and start drinking again. He knows only one thing: to be drunk.

In general, when afflictions arise, you will do things you would never ordinarily do. This happens even when someone has sworn never to kill — yet when affliction flares up, he utterly disregards everything and decides to kill everyone in the world. He grabs a knife and sets to work! Thus afflictions are to be avoided. Do not give rise to afflictions. You must leave the home of afflictions. We also wish to leave the home of the Triple Realm, and so leaving home must fully embody these three meanings.

Sutra text: 若有女子好學出家持諸禁戒。我於彼前現比丘尼身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược hữu nữ tử hiếu học xuất gia trì chư cấm giới. ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện tỷ-khưu-ni thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there is a believing woman who wishes to study to leave the home-life and uphold the precepts, I appear before her in the body of a bhikshuni, speak Dharma for her, and help her accomplish her wish.

Commentary: If there is a believing woman who wishes to study to leave the home-life and uphold the precepts. She wishes to study the Buddhadharma. She also wishes to leave the worldly home, leave the home of afflictions, and leave the home of the Triple Realm. Women must uphold more precepts than men. They must keep 348 precepts. It is commonly said that women have bodies with five kinds of outflows. Hence they must keep more precepts. I appear before her in the body of a bhikshuni, speak Dharma for her, and help her accomplish her wish.

Sutra text: 若有男子樂持五戒。我於彼前現優婆塞身。而為說法令其成就。若[1]復女子五戒自居。我於彼前現優婆夷身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược hữu nam tử nhạo trì ngũ giới, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Ưu-bà-tắc thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu. Nhược phục nữ tử ngũ giới tự cư, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Ưu-bà-di thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there is a good man who delights in upholding the five precepts, I appear before him in the body of an upasaka, speak Dharma for him, and help him accomplish his wish. If there is a believing woman who abides by the five precepts, I appear before her in the body of an upasika, speak Dharma for her, and help her accomplish her wish.

Commentary: If there is a good man who delights in upholding the five precepts — no killing, no stealing, no sexual misconduct, no lying, no alcohol — I appear before him in the body of an upasaka, speak Dharma for him, and help him accomplish his wish. I shall manifest as a layman to speak Dharma for them. If there is a believing woman who delights in the five precepts — she too wishes to keep these precepts — I appear before her in the body of an upasika. I shall manifest as a laywoman and speak Dharma for her to help her accomplish her wish.
