<p><strong>With light shining upon light, illuminating without end, shining through the realms of the ten directions so that everything becomes like crystal; such a class is called the Limitless Light Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;With light shining upon light, illuminating without end, shining through the realms of the ten directions so that everything becomes like crystal.&#8221; Their halos shine upon one another, illuminating together. Have you ever noticed how lamplight shines together? There is nothing that lamplight does not illuminate together. If you hold one lamp and another person also holds a bright lamp, the two lamps do not clash with each other, do they? Light does not clash with light. There is never a moment when a bigger lamp suppresses a smaller lamp. There is no confusion in lamplight. That is what is described here by &#8220;light shining upon light.&#8221; The light shines without end, illuminating throughout the ten directions, and everything is illumined as if made of crystal. Such cultivators dwell in the Limitless Light Heaven.</p>

<li><strong>Hấp trì viên quang, thành tựu giáo thể. Phát hóa thanh tịnh, ưng dụng vô tận. Như thị nhất loại, danh Quang âm Thiên.</strong></li>

<p><strong>Absorbing and maintaining the perfect light so as to accomplish their teaching-substance, they emit pure teaching and respond without end; such a class is called the Light-Sound Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Absorbing and maintaining the perfect light so as to accomplish their teaching-substance.&#8221; Together they attain and maintain the halo of light, and in this way they accomplish their teaching-substance. In the Light-Sound Heaven, people need not shout to converse with one another; they use the halo of light as sound. They converse by flashing light. They do not talk with mouth and tongue; they use no language. The halo of light is the medium of the Dharma in this heaven. It is truly a case of:<br />
&#8211; Minds understanding each other without a single word spoken.</p>
<p>When they flash light to one another in this realm, each understands what the other is saying; ideas are exchanged through light. &#8220;They emit pure teaching and respond without end; such a class is called the Light-Sound Heaven.&#8221; The functioning of light is truly limitless.</p>
<p><strong>A-nan! Thử tam thắng lưu. Nhất thiết ưu sầu, sở bất năng bức. Tuy phi chánh tu, chân tam-ma-địa. Thanh tịnh tâm trung, thô lậu dĩ phục, danh vi nhị Thiền.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda! These three superior levels cannot be oppressed by any sorrow or anxiety. Though they have not truly cultivated the genuine samadhi, in their pure minds the coarse outflows are already subdued; this is called the Second Dhyana.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda! These three superior levels cannot be oppressed by any sorrow or anxiety.&#8221; In the heavens of the First Dhyana, they are not oppressed by affliction and suffering, yet they still have a little worry. When something comes up, they still have a little difficulty letting go of everything; they get entangled in matters. But in the Second Dhyana there is no more worry or affliction, nothing left to get entangled in. They keep nothing in mind. They are quite unlike ordinary people of this world, who get entangled in all kinds of comings and goings and spend day and night worrying over things. To get &#8220;entangled&#8221; means to be obstructed and hindered.</p>
<p>&#8220;Though they have not truly cultivated the genuine samadhi, in their pure minds the coarse outflows are already subdued.&#8221; Although such cultivators have not yet truly cultivated the genuine samadhi, they have attained a stage in which the mind is pure. The afflictions have been brought under control &#8211; though they still cannot entirely control the false thoughts in the mind. Outwardly, those in the Second Dhyana appear to live without affliction or worry.</p>
<p>When such cultivators sit in meditation and enter the samadhi of the Second Dhyana, their breathing stops. In the First Dhyana the pulse stops; in the Second Dhyana the breath stops. Although it is said here that the breath stops, do you remember what I told you in an earlier lecture about little boys and little girls?</p>
<p>The cultivation methods of outer paths and of the Buddha's path have some points in common. Outwardly they look alike in many respects, differing only in a few details. When the pulse stops, does that mean the person is dead? No, it is not so, for although the outer pulse has stopped beating, the pulse of the true mind still remains. The same applies to the breath: the breath and pulse of the true mind remain and take over, so the bodily pulse may stop beating. In the same way, when the outer signs of breathing cease in the Second Dhyana, it does not mean the cultivator has died. They have entered the samadhi of the Second Dhyana and attained purity and bliss. Only outer things and matters have been brought under control, however &#8211; not everything within has been controlled. Such beings are called the Second Dhyana. <strong><br />
</strong></p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE THIRD DHYANA</strong></p>

<ol>
<li><strong>A-nan! Như thị Thiên Nhân. Viên quang thành âm, phi âm lộ diệu. Phát thành tinh hạnh, thông tịch diệt lạc. Như thị nhất loại, danh thiểu tịnh Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Ananda! The perfect light becoming sound, using sound to reveal the wondrous principle and accomplish vigorous practice, penetrating into the bliss of still extinction; such a class is called the Lesser Purity Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda! The perfect light becoming sound, using sound to reveal the wondrous principle and accomplish vigorous practice.&#8221; For cultivators at this great stage, the halo of light has become sound, and the more clearly they can distinguish this sound, the more vigorous and wondrous their level of cultivation.</p>
<p>&#8220;Penetrating into the bliss of still extinction&#8221; through vigorous and wondrous cultivation. Still extinction is the absence of false thoughts: false thoughts and the myriad dharmas have become still and extinct. They are called the Lesser Purity Heaven; such cultivators dwell in the Lesser Purity Heaven. Body and mind have attained partial purity &#8211; not yet complete purity.</p>

<li><strong>Tịnh không hiện tiền, dẫn phát vô tế. Thân tâm khinh an, thành tịch diệt lạc. Như thị nhất loại, danh vô lượng tịnh Thiên.</strong></li>

<p><strong>The realm of &#8220;pure emptiness&#8221; manifesting before them, boundless and without limit, with body and mind at ease, accomplishing the bliss of still extinction; such a class is called the Limitless Purity Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;The realm of pure emptiness manifesting before them, boundless and without limit.&#8221; They have attained purity and then awakened to the truth of the unconditioned. Understanding emptiness, the truth leads them to what has no bounds &#8211; limitless purity.</p>
<p>&#8220;With body and mind at ease, accomplishing the bliss of still extinction.&#8221; At this point they are completely at ease and have mastery over themselves. They have attained the joy of cessation of birth and death: within birth there is extinction, within extinction there is birth &#8211; something lost of which most people do not know is possible. Most people are greedy for outer material things and so chase after them.<br />
Their nature flows out as emotion;<br />
Their emotion flows out as desire.</p>
<p>The thought of sexual desire is created; it hardens into the notion that an object must be found, and then they let the fire of lust blaze and spend their whole lives pursuing desire. That is sexuality. Is anything outside the body needed for this bliss? No &#8211; for within emptiness there is form.
