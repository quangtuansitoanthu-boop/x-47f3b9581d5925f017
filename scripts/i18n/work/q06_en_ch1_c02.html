Sutra text: 於是如來告文殊師利法王子: 汝今觀此二十五無學諸大菩薩及阿羅漢,各說最初成道方便,皆言修習真實圓通。彼等修行實無優劣前後差別。

Transliteration: Ư thị Như Lai cáo Văn-thù-sư-lợi Pháp vương tử: “Nhữ kim quán thử nhị thập ngũ vô học chư Đại Bồ-tát cập A-la-hán, các thuyết tối sơ thành đạo phương tiện, giai ngôn tu tập chơn thật viên thông. Bỉ đẳng tu hành thật vô ưu liệt tiền hậu sai biệt.

Translation: Thereupon the Tathagata said to Manjushri, Prince of Dharma: "Now regard these twenty-five great Bodhisattvas and Arhats beyond study; each has described the expedient of his initial accomplishment of the Way, all speaking of cultivating true perfect penetration. In their cultivation there is truly no superiority or inferiority, no difference of earlier or later."

Commentary: Thereupon the Tathagata — Shakyamuni Buddha — said to Manjushri, Prince of Dharma: "Now regard these twenty-five great Bodhisattvas and Arhats beyond study."

Now examine the expedient dharma-doors these twenty-five sages employed in cultivation. "Beyond study" refers to those who have certified to the fourth fruition of Arhatship or higher. Each has described the expedient of his initial accomplishment of the Way; all speak of cultivating true perfect penetration. They have truly certified to the perfect penetration of the sense roots. In their cultivation there is truly no superiority or inferiority, no difference of earlier or later. Indeed, there is no way to distinguish superiority or inferiority, earlier or later, difference among them.

Sutra text: 我今欲令阿難開悟,二十五行,誰當其根。兼我滅後,此界眾生 入菩薩乘,求無上道。何方便門得易成就?

Transliteration: Ngã kim dục linh A-nan khai ngộ, nhị thập ngũ hạnh, thuỳ đương kỳ căn? Kiêm ngã diệt hậu, thử giới chúng sanh, nhập bồ tát thừa cầu vô thượng đạo. Hà phương tiện môn đắc dị thành tựu?

Translation: Now I wish to enable Ananda to become enlightened: among the twenty-five practices, which accords with his root? And after my extinction, when beings of this realm enter the Bodhisattva Vehicle seeking the unsurpassed Way, through which expedient door will accomplishment come easily?"

Commentary: Shakyamuni Buddha says: Now I wish to enable Ananda to become enlightened. Ananda is still at the first fruition of Arhatship. The Tathagata wishes him to become enlightened and certify to the second, third, and fourth fruitions of Arhatship. Then among the twenty-five practices, which accords with Ananda's root? Which dharma-door accords with Ananda's root? Among the eighteen realms and the seven elements, which dharma-door corresponds to Ananda? And after the Tathagata's extinction, when beings of this realm enter the Bodhisattva Vehicle seeking the unsurpassed Way, through which expedient door will accomplishment come easily?
