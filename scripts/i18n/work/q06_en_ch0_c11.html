Sutra text: 若諸眾生愛生天宮驅使鬼神。我於彼前現四天王國太子身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh ái sanh thiên cung, khu sử quỷ thần, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Tứ thiên vương quốc Thái tử thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who wish to be born in heavenly palaces and command ghosts and spirits, I appear before them in the body of a prince, son of one of the Four Heavenly Kings, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who wish to be born in heavenly palaces, delighting in the blessings and bliss of the heavens. While in the heavens, they can command ghosts and spirits. They can order ghosts and spirits to do various tasks for them. They summon ghosts and spirits and assign them work. In fact, the ability to command ghosts and spirits is quite ordinary. People find it strange, but it really is not so. For those who wish to command ghosts and spirits, I appear before them in the body of a prince, son of one of the Four Heavenly Kings, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生樂為人主。我於彼前現人王身,而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh nhạo vi nhơn chủ, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Nhơn vương thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who delight in being rulers of people, I appear before them in the body of a human king, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are people who delight in ruling the human realm, I at once appear before them in the body of a human king to speak Dharma for them.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生愛主族姓世間推讓。我於彼前現長者身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh ái chủ tộc tánh, thế gian suy nhượng, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện trưởng giả thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who wish to head a clan of noble family, respected by the world, I appear before them in the body of an elder, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who wish to head a clan of noble family, respected by the world. They delight in wealth and in presiding over a great clan. They wish to be respected by everyone in the world. I appear before them in the body of an elder, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish. I shall manifest the body of a wealthy elder before them to speak Dharma for them.

A person must have ten virtues to earn the title of elder. They are:
1. Noble nature: born of a great noble family.
2. High position: holding the post of a senior official.
3. Great wealth: abundant possessions, rich.
4. Awesome courage: dignified and valiant.
5. Profound wisdom: lofty and deep wisdom.
6. Senior age: advanced in years, weighty in virtue.
7. Pure conduct: lofty, pure character, worthy to be a model.
8. Complete propriety: in dealing with people and things, always courteous.
9. Praised above: praised by kings above.
10. Respected below: accorded the hearts of the people below.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生愛談名言清淨其 居。我於彼前現居士身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh ái đàm danh ngôn, thanh tịnh kỳ cư. Ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện cư sĩ thân. Nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.
