Each kind has more or less than twenty varieties, making one hundred in all. Once they had eaten the hundred grains, "their feet never left the ground." We attribute this to gravity, the earth's gravity, but the deeper reason is that humans ate this food. One must cause body and mind, toward beings' bodies or body-parts, neither to wear nor eat them. We humans, wishing to keep our body and mind free from karma-making through relationships with beings' lives or anything made from their bodies, should not take their lives, nor harm them in spirit. We should wear nothing made from animals' lives, nor eat their flesh. Such people, the Tathagata truly regards as liberated. The Tathagata calls such people truly liberated.

Sutra text: 如我此說名為佛說。不如此說即波旬說

Transliteration: Như ngã thử thuyết danh vi Phật thuyết. Bất như thử thuyết tức ba-tuần thuyết.

Translation: What I say thus is spoken by the Buddhas. What is not spoken thus is spoken by Papiyan.

Commentary: What I say thus is spoken by the Buddhas. The Tathagata's exposition is the teaching spoken by the Buddhas of the ten directions and three periods. What is not spoken thus is spoken by Papiyan.

Every teaching according with the principle spoken by the Tathagata is the Buddhas' teaching. Every doctrine not according with the principle the Tathagata proclaimed here is the demon kings' speech. Now you have heard the Shurangama Sutra. You can use this sutra as a demon-detecting mirror. If someone disguises himself as a person, when you shine this mirror upon him, he will reveal his original shape — a demon. Sometimes they are pig demons, cow demons, or horse demons, or mountain spirits, or water monsters. Sometimes they are demon kings. Whatever they are, the mirror clearly shines them forth. Now that you have heard the Shurangama Sutra, you will recognize who speaks true Dharma by comparing what is spoken in the sutra. So the sutra is like a demon-detecting mirror.

That is why before, I said that the deaf, blind, and mute have no opportunity to hear my sutra lectures. The deaf fundamentally cannot hear, but here I do not mean the ear-deaf; I mean those who hear Dharma yet know nothing. Silent, forgetting the points I lectured. I hope that all of you who have heard the Shurangama Sutra will become good spiritual teachers in the future; you will become truly sure in understanding the Buddhadharma. Then you will teach the blind, the deaf, the mute. When you lecture sutras, though you use a sound system, and even though they are outside, they will still hear and no longer be deaf or mute. Those who do not understand the Buddhadharma are most pitiful. So when you have mastered the Buddhadharma, you should go teach them. That means, at that level, you should have special attentiveness as if you were studying.

3. ENDING THE MIND OF STEALING

Sutra text: 阿難又復世界六道眾生其心不偷。則不隨其生死相續。

Transliteration: A-nan, hựu phục thế giới lục đạo chúng sanh kỳ tâm bất thâu. Tắc bất tuỳ kỳ sanh tử tương tục.
