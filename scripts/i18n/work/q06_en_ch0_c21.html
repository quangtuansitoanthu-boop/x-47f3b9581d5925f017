Transliteration: Tứ giả đoạn diệt vọng tưởng, tâm vô sát hại, linh chư chúng sanh nhập chư quỷ quốc, quỷ bất năng hại.

Translation: Fourth, having cut off and extinguished false thoughts, with a mind free of killing, I enable beings entering ghost countries not to be harmed by ghosts.

Commentary: If false thoughts have been entirely cut off, and you no longer have a single thought of killing, and if you recite Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's name, then beings entering the dwellings of rakshasa ghosts can avoid being murdered by them.

Sutra text: 五者薰聞成聞。六根銷復同於聲聽。能令眾生臨當被害刀段段壞。使其兵戈猶如割水,亦如吹光性無搖動。

Transliteration: Ngũ giả huân văn thành văn. Lục căn tiêu phục đồng ư thanh thính. Năng linh chúng sanh lâm đương bị hại đao đoạn đoạn hoại. Sử kỳ binh qua du như cát thuỷ, diệc như xuy quang tánh vô dao động.

Translation: Fifth, through perfuming hearing into accomplished hearing, the six sense roots dissolve and return to be one with sound and hearing. I can enable beings, at the point of being harmed, to have knives break into pieces, making weapons that touch the body like cutting water, or like blowing upon light — the nature unmoved.

Commentary: Fifth, through perfuming hearing into accomplished hearing. The six sense roots all dissolve, returning to the original nature, one with the nature of hearing — meaning, when the effort of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature has been perfected, the six roots function interchangeably. Thus I can enable beings, at the point of being harmed, to have knives break into pieces. For instance, suppose someone takes a knife intending to cut off another's head. The moment the knife touches the head, the knife shatters into fragments. I can make sharp weapons touching a person's body like cutting water, like blowing upon light — the nature unmoved. I can make a sharp weapon about to chop someone's shoulder like cutting into water; that is, when the knife passes through the shoulder, it simply passes through, and no wound remains. Or I can make the knife cut like blowing upon a light — that is, utterly without effect — for no matter how you blow upon a light, the light does not flicker.

Sutra text: 六者聞薰精明明遍法界, 則諸幽暗性不能全。能令眾生藥叉羅剎鳩槃茶鬼,及毘舍遮富單那等,雖近其傍目不能視。

Transliteration: Lục giả văn huân tinh minh minh biến pháp giới, tắc chư u ám tánh bất năng toàn. Năng linh chúng sanh dược-xoa, la-sát, cưu-bàn-trà quỷ, cập tỳ-xá-già, phú- đan-na đẳng, tuy cận kỳ bàng, mục bất năng thị.

Translation: Sixth, through perfuming hearing into luminous essence shining throughout the Dharma Realm, all dark natures can no longer remain whole. I enable beings — though yakshas, rakshasas, kumbhanda ghosts, and pishachas, putanas… come close beside them — to remain unseen by their eyes.

Commentary: Sixth, through perfuming hearing into luminous essence — meaning when the effort of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature has been perfected — shining throughout the Dharma Realm, all dark natures can no longer remain whole. I enable beings — though yakshas, rakshasas, kumbhanda ghosts, and pishachas, putanas… come close beside them — to remain unseen by their eyes.
