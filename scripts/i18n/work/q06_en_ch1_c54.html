Commentary: If bhikshus, beyond robes and bowl, store not an inch. Bhikshus have only three robes, one bowl, and a sitting cloth. They need nothing more. They accumulate no wealth.
Give alms of begged food's remainder to hungry beings. They give surplus begged items to those with nothing to eat. At great assemblies join palms bowing to the assembly.

Bhikshus join palms reverently bowing to everyone in the assembly. When beaten or reviled, take it as praise. Take scolding as praise. They do not react to reviling. Sharing body, flesh, bones, and blood with beings.

Their minds harbor no arrogant thoughts, and their bodies do not react in displays of arrogance and self-love. When someone scolds you, you should take it as if they are singing for you. If you scold no one yet they scold you, best is you need not understand what they are saying. Those words mean nothing to you. As if they spoke some language you do not understand — like Japanese, English, or Chinese — so that you understand nothing. When someone truly scolds you, think: "Oh! He is speaking such fine things to me!" Look at it reversely. If someone beats you, just pretend you bumped into a wall. Pretend you carelessly ran into a wall and left a big bump on your head.

If you turn around and punch the wall saying, "Why did you bump into me?" You only end with a sore hand. When someone beats you, if you take it as bumping into a wall, then everything ends right there. Bhikshus with true bodhi resolve should renounce their bloody bodies for other beings if they need your sharing. Once Shakyamuni Buddha, on the causal ground in cultivation, saw a hungry tiger and offered his body to the hungry tiger. Tigers are the fiercest beasts in the world, so Shakyamuni Buddha in cultivation on the causal ground abandoned his own life, giving it to the hungry tiger.

Do not take the Tathagata's provisional teachings as their own understanding to mislead beginners. Bhikshus do not proclaim Small Vehicle teachings as if understood in their own way. In other words, if they do not speak as the Buddha taught, then they themselves have misunderstood and delude new students. If bhikshus do not fall into that fault, the Buddha certifies such a person attains true samadhi. The Buddha certifies such people. They truly certify to samadhi.

Sutra text: 如我所說名為佛說。不如此說即波旬說

Transliteration: Như ngã sở thuyết danh vi Phật thuyết. Bất như thử thuyết tức ba-tuần thuyết.

Translation: What I say thus is spoken by the Buddhas. What is not spoken thus is spoken by Papiyan.

Commentary: This is how the Buddha speaks Dharma. Every other exposition is the demon kings'.

Sutra text: 阿難如是世界六道眾生,雖則身心無殺盜婬,三行已圓若大妄語。即三摩提不得清淨。成愛見魔失如來種。

Transliteration: A-nan như thị thế giới lục đạo chúng sanh, tuy tắc thân tâm vô sát đạo dâm, tam hạnh dĩ viên, nhược đại vọng ngữ, tức tam-ma-đề bất đắc thanh tịnh, thành ái kiến ma, thất Như Lai chủng.
