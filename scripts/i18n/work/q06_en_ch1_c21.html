"Source" is the Tathagatagarbha nature. When one sense root returns to the Tathagatagarbha nature, all six roots merge together. The elder brothers return home and the younger siblings follow along. But if you do not bring one root back, none of the roots will follow along. They are related to one another, for fundamentally they are one, but they have been divided into six.

Sutra text: 見聞如幻翳 　三界若空花 聞復翳根除 　塵銷覺圓淨。

Transliteration: Kiến văn như huyễn ế Tam giới nhược không hoa Văn phục ế căn trừ Trần tiêu giác viên tịnh.

Translation: Seeing and hearing are like illusory films over the eyes; The Triple Realm like flowers in space. Hearing restored, the film-root removed; Objects dissolved, awareness perfectly pure.

Commentary: Why have we not yet become Buddhas? We are bound by the six roots and six objects. So we must find a method to attain liberation. It is not only we who are bound by roots and objects; at the same time, Ananda shares this problem. We are in the same dilemma. Ananda took this path to liberation, and we are now most fortunate to study that method. We, too, can walk this path and be liberated.
Seeing and hearing are like films over the eyes.

Seeing and hearing are something unreal. They are like an illusion, like a thin film veiling the eyes, like clouds in the sky.
The Triple Realm like flowers in space.
The Desire Realm, Form Realm, and Formless Realm make up the three realms. We are within them, staring into empty space until the staring wearies us. So it looks as if flowers are dancing in empty space. At the start of this verse, Manjushri said:
Empty space arises within great enlightenment
Like a single bubble in the sea.
Empty space arises within great enlightenment
Like a single bubble rising in the sea.

Empty space arises in the vast sea of the enlightened nature, likened to a bubble rising amid the sea of empty space.
Hearing restored, the film-root removed.

When we turn the hearing back to recognize our own nature, that thin film veiling the eyes vanishes.
Objects dissolved, awareness perfectly pure.

When sensory perceptions are no more, guest-objects no longer trouble you. So one's awareness-nature is entirely pure.

Sutra text: 淨極光通達 寂照含虛空 却來觀世間 猶如夢中事 摩登伽在夢 誰能留汝形。

Transliteration: Tịnh cực quang thông đạt Tịch chiếu hàm hư không Khước lai quán thế gian Du như mộng trung sự Ma-đăng-già tại mộng Thuỳ năng lưu nhữ hình?

Translation: Utmost purity, light penetrating; Still shining, containing empty space. Returning to regard the world, It is like a matter within a dream. Matanga's daughter is in the dream — Who can detain your form?
