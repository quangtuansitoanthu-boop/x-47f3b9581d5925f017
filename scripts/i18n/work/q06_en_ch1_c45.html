Translation: Further, Ananda, if beings of the six paths in the worlds had no stealing in their minds, they would not follow the continuity of birth and death.

Commentary: The Buddha calls Ananda again.
Further, Ananda, if beings of the six paths in the worlds had no stealing in their minds, they would not follow the continuity of birth and death. Gods, asuras, humans, animals, hungry ghosts, and those in the hells — if they did not steal, even in mental thought. They would take nothing from anyone, tangible or intangible, valuable or valueless. Not only would they truly not take, they would not even have thoughts of taking. That means no stealing. If those beings kept minds of no stealing, as well as minds of no killing and no lust, they would not follow the continuity of birth-and-death turning, and they would also have no continuous retribution in the world.

Sutra text: 汝修三昧本出塵勞。偷心不除塵不可出。

Transliteration: Nhữ tu tam-muội bổn xuất trần lao. Thâu tâm bất trừ trần bất khả xuất.

Translation: In cultivating samadhi, your fundamental aim is to transcend worldly toil. If the stealing mind is not removed, one cannot transcend the dust.

Commentary: In cultivating samadhi, your fundamental aim is to transcend worldly toil. You wish to develop true samadhi power. Your chief motive in cultivation is to transcend the turning wheel of birth and death in the world. But if you still have stealing ideas, if the stealing mind is not removed, one cannot transcend worldly toil.
"Worldly toil" here refers to the burning house in the three realms.

Sutra text: 縱有多智禪定現前,如不斷偷,必落邪道 。上品精靈,中品妖魅,下品邪人,諸魅所著。

Transliteration: Túng hữu đa trí thiền định hiện tiền, như bất đoạn thâu tất lạc tà đạo. Thượng phẩm tinh linh, trung phẩm yêu mị, hạ phẩm tà nhơn chư mị sở trước.

Translation: Though one has much wisdom and samadhi manifests, if stealing is not cut off, one will surely fall into deviant paths. The highest grade becomes essences and spirits; the middle grade becomes demons and sprites; the lowest grade becomes deviant people possessed by sprites.

Commentary: Though one has much wisdom and samadhi manifests, if stealing is not cut off, one will surely fall into deviant paths. Basically such people have no true wisdom, for if they had wisdom, they would not steal, nor lust, nor kill. Only from lack of wisdom do they do such things. But if we grant such people a little wisdom and just a bit more petty cleverness than ordinary people, and when they sit they slip into an oblivion more or less like samadhi, yet such people think they have certified to some extraordinary states others have never reached. He feels his effort surpasses everyone else's. His outlook is full of arrogance, and if he refuses to stop stealing, he will fall into deviant paths. Despite a little wisdom and a little samadhi power, they will fall into untrue realms because of stealing. In these deviant paths, they in turn teach beings in other paths full of darkness and impropriety. They in turn teach others deviant knowledge and deviant views.
