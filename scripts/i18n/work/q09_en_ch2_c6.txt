<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>After the Tathagata's extinction, in the Dharma-ending age, you should rely on this and proclaim these meanings.</strong></p>
<p>Ananda, you should wholeheartedly rely on and practice the Dharma I have taught you. In the Dharma-ending age, after the Tathagata's extinction, you must propagate this Dharma.</p>
<p><strong>Do not let the heavenly demons gain their advantage</strong>. <strong>Protect and shelter all beings, enabling them to accomplish the unsurpassed Way</strong>: you must maintain and protect the true Buddhadharma, and only then may the unsurpassed fruition of the Way be accomplished.</p>
