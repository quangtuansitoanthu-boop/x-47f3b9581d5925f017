TITLE: 02. THEN I DEDICATED MERIT TO MY MOTHER AS FOLLOWS:
TOMTAT: "May my mother's karmic obstructions dissolve, her blessings and wisdom grow, may she enjoy health and long life, give rise to faith and vow to recite the Buddha's name, and after this body is done, be reborn in the Western Pure Land of...
-----
{align:justify|"May my mother's karmic obstructions dissolve, her blessings and wisdom grow, may she enjoy health and long life, give rise to faith and vow to recite the Buddha's name, and after this body is done, be reborn in the Western Pure Land of Ultimate Bliss."}
{align:justify|I recited for one week, completing seven copies of the "Diamond Sutra." Since I recited at around five in the morning, fearing to disturb my family's sleep I only recited silently; the dedication verses too I intoned silently, each round clear in my mind, only never spoken aloud. But of this reciting of the "Diamond Sutra" dedicated to my mother, I breathed not a word to my mother or anyone.}
{align:justify|After a week I went to Zhongguancun to visit my parents. When I asked about my mother's illness, my father said excitedly:}
{align:justify|"How fortunate—all this week your mother's heart and blood pressure have been stable, so every day she sleeps wonderfully. Seven straight days without an attack—something that hasn't happened in nearly a year."}
{align:justify|Hearing this, I rejoiced inwardly, guessing quietly that my sutra recitation for my mother had taken effect… So I told everyone what had happened; all who heard were amazed, agreeing it was marvelous beyond measure!}
{align:justify|Only then did my mother reveal what she had hidden so long, and I was more shaken still. My mother confessed that before, fearing no one in the family would believe her, she had kept silent.}
{align:justify|As it happened, for nearly a year, every night her blood pressure soared, her heart labored terribly, she could not sleep, and worse, she kept seeing people walking back and forth inside the house. My mother stressed that she was not dreaming at all—fully awake, she still saw that crowd! Alarmed, I quickly asked:}
{align:justify|"What did they look like?"}
{align:justify|Mother answered:}
{align:justify|"Men and women, fairly old…"}
{align:justify|"And their appearance?"}
{align:justify|"Mostly I couldn't see their faces clearly. Not only that—at midnight I would also hear a woman weeping. Once, a big, brawny man with a ferocious face lunged at me and grabbed my arm; I wrestled free and escaped…"}
{align:justify|Mother explained:}
{align:justify|"Because I feared seeing those people, every night I dared not sleep facing outward. But even lying facing the wall, once—less than thirty centimeters before my face—there suddenly appeared a creature, old, ugly, unspeakably filthy and foul… I vomited violently, retching over and over, and only then did the monster vanish."}
{align:justify|Before 1949 my mother graduated from university; before retiring she worked at China's "Academy of Advanced Sciences" in Zhongguancun. As a senior intellectual with decades at the Academy of Sciences, she had never touched anything classed as superstition—naturally she could never accept phenomena (persons or things) of that realm; she never believed ghosts or spirits existed! Yet this time, perversely, she met ghosts face to face while still alive—and not by hallucination. Whether she liked it or not, my mother had to believe!}
{align:justify|I asked:}
{align:justify|"Why didn't you tell me sooner? Don't you know how dangerous and frightening this is? The frightening thing is, by then one of your feet had already stepped through the gates of ghosts!"}
{align:justify|I explained to my mother:}
{align:justify|"Among the six realms, the Human realm, the Animal realm, and the Ghost realm… all three share one space. We can see the animal realm, but the ghost realm is yin in nature, while ordinary people, their yang energy prevailing, cannot be approached by ghost-realm beings—hence we do not see them.}
{align:justify|But karmic creditors who died and now dwell in the Ghost realm lie ever in wait; when a person falls gravely ill or nears death, yin waxes and yang wanes, and they immediately draw close to collect their debts, tormenting and avenging themselves… So the very moment you saw ghost-realm beings, one foot had already stepped through the ghost gate. Had you told me everything sooner, I could have performed Buddhist rites to rescue you earlier…"}
{align:justify|Mother said:}
{align:justify|"But for about a week now I no longer see ghosts (it was from the very day I began reciting sutras and dedicating the merit to my mother and her karmic creditors that the ghosts vanished that very night and never came to trouble her for the whole week)."}
{align:justify|For the whole half year after, I single-mindedly recited the "Diamond Sutra" dedicated to my parents—about one hundred copies. From the summer Sunday of 2009 onward, my mother's heart condition and high blood pressure improved and changed for the better: each day she takes medicine once, the dose halved, less than usual.}
{align:justify|My parents are intellectuals, university graduates before 1949, both outstanding scientists at the Academy of Advanced Sciences—persuading them to believe in Buddhism was very hard.}
{align:justify|I have learned Buddhism for more than twenty years, tirelessly proclaiming the Buddha's teachings for my parents to understand, yet my father remained half-believing, half-doubting, while my mother flatly refused to believe.}
{align:justify|But ever since my recitation of the "Diamond Sutra" for my mother drew this marvelous response, my two headstrong scientists were shaken and moved—this time both were utterly convinced, and began to believe in Buddhism, resolving to recite the Buddha's name seeking rebirth in the West.}
{align:justify|My mother is eighty-eight this year. Every day, morning and evening, she spends an hour walking, reciting the Buddha's name as she walks, her spirit and health robust—as if a different person from half a year ago.}
{align:justify|My father is also eighty-eight; daily he practices walking meditation reciting the Buddha's name for over two hours. I even bought a Buddha-recitation machine, playing around the clock at home to help my parents gather their minds more strongly.}
{align:justify|My own miraculous response, judged even by science, still meets the conditions for proof:}
{align:justify|Clearly, throughout the week I recited the "Diamond Sutra" praying for my mother, I revealed this to neither my parents nor anyone, and while reciting I knew nothing at all of my mother's secret. So when everything was smoothly resolved, it cannot be concluded that it all came from my own "autosuggestion" or "hallucination." Hence I am fully convinced that the merit of the "Diamond Sutra" is truly inconceivably wondrous!}
{align:justify|A READER'S LETTER}
{align:justify|Dear Bhikkhuni, I wish to report a happy news so you may rejoice with me—and be cheered to know how immensely your translation work has served life and people.}
{align:justify|You surely remember that more than two years ago my left eye turned red and streamed tears, burning terribly. The eye doctor in America did his utmost yet could not cure it; only twenty percent of my vision remained—I was nearly totally blind. After reading of the Green and White Snakes in "The Flooding of Jinshan Temple" in your translated "Karmic Retribution in Present Life – Volume 3," a gleam of hope flashed… So I recited the Ksitigarbha Sutra and prostrated through about ten sets of the Liang Huang Repentance, and the eye disease gradually eased, then healed completely. It is now nearly three months. My late report comes from waiting to see whether I was truly healed—now it is certain the illness is fully cured; I see very clearly, even driving at night.}
{align:justify|The eye doctor who treated me is also a devout Buddhist; hearing my story, she hugged me in joy. Doctor and patient both shed tears.}
{align:justify|Once more, my deep gratitude to you, Bhikkhuni. I wish you good health.}
{align:justify|N.T – October 25, 2014}
{align:justify|Translator's note: Above is a letter from a female reader in the U.S.A., who once taught American college students and is now retired. I take the liberty of publishing it so readers may have verified proof and deepen their faith. (My apologies to Ms. N. T. for publishing without asking your permission. For the benefit of so many, I hope you will forgive me.)}
