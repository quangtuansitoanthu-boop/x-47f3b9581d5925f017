Why did Manjushri Bodhisattva choose the ear root? Because it is the easiest way to cultivate successfully. I believe some of you already know how to develop this cultivation effort, and some do not. What is meant by turning the hearing inward to recognize one's own nature? It is the effort used in investigating Chan. When investigating Chan, do not spend all your energy seeking outside. You must turn the light back to shine within. Then ask yourself: "Who is reciting the Buddha's name?" "Who?" Place the "Who?" in your mind and listen with your ears. Pursue the "Who?" Do this continuously, without stopping. Do not let your effort be interrupted. Hold this meditation topic in whatever you are doing.
Walking, standing, sitting, lying down, never leave this effort.
If you leave the effort, you have gone wrong.

What is "this"? That is the question. "Who is reciting the Buddha's name?" You need not recite aloud; just ask in your mind and listen with your ears. Listen within, not without. Once you have heard within, your mind and your hearing-nature will finally merge into one. And then, unexpectedly, at an unanticipated moment, you suddenly become enlightened. But you absolutely must bring your mind and your nature back into one. Do not let them scatter in many directions. Do not let them dissipate outward. Gather them inward. Turn the hearing inward to recognize your own nature. Eventually and naturally your effort will develop. Investigating Chan and the effort of turning the hearing inward to recognize your own nature are the same. And now everyone knows that using the ear root to cultivate perfect penetration is the easiest method. So put all your mind's strength into cultivating this method.

Sutra text: 此是微塵佛 　一路涅槃門 過去諸如來 　斯門已成就 現在諸菩薩 　今各入圓明 未來修學人 　當依如是法 我亦從中證 　非唯觀世音。

Transliteration: Thử thị vi trần Phật Nhất lộ niết-bàn môn Quá khứ chư Như Lai Tư môn dĩ thành tựu Hiện tại chư bồ tát Kim các nhập viên minh Vị lai tu học nhơn Đương y như thị pháp Ngã diệc tòng trung chứng Phi duy Quán Thế Âm.

Translation: This is the Buddhas as numerous as dust motes' Single road, the door to nirvana; The Tathagatas of the past Have already perfected this door. The Bodhisattvas of the present Now each enter perfect brightness. Future students Should rely on this Dharma. I too was certified through it — Not Avalokiteshvara alone.

Commentary: Now Manjushri Bodhisattva certifies the authenticity of the dharma-door he has selected.
This is the Buddhas as numerous as dust motes'
Single road, the door to nirvana.
He says: "Not only did Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva become enlightened using this cultivation — the cultivation effort leading to the perfect penetration I have explained — I myself, Manjushri, also certify to this ear-root perfect-penetration dharma-door. Not only I, but the Buddhas as numerous as dust motes in the past also found this road for entering nirvana."
The Tathagatas of the past
Have already perfected this door.
