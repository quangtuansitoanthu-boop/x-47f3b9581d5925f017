THE FIFTY SKANDHA-DEMONS

Lectured by Venerable Master Hsuan Hua at

THE FORM SKANDHA

  1. THE PURE MENTAL STATE BEFORE ENTERING THE REALM OF THE FORM SKANDHA

Sutra text: A-nan should know: sitting in the Way-place, you must dissolve all thoughts. When thoughts are exhausted, then apart from all thought there is utter purity and brightness. Still and moving unchanging, remembering and forgetting are as one.

Translation: Ananda should know: sitting in the Way-place, you must dissolve all false thoughts. When false thoughts are entirely exhausted, no more discriminating thoughts remain in your mind. The pure, bright state, full of wisdom and samadhi power, will dwell within you. When still and moving unchanging, remembering and forgetting are as one.

Commentary: Ananda should know, when you sit in the Way-place, you must dissolve all false thoughts. Ananda, you should know: you are one cultivating the Dharma of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature (turning the hearing inward to recognize your own nature).

With such effort you have dissolved all false thoughts. When false thoughts are entirely exhausted, no more discriminating thoughts remain in your mind. The pure, bright state, full of wisdom and samadhi power, will dwell within you. When still and moving unchanging, remembering and forgetting are as one, your mind does not change. Whether you remember or forget, it is all the same. It is not wrong or different.

THE BOUNDARY OF THE FORM SKANDHA

Sutra text: When you abide in that state and enter samadhi, it is like one opening eyes in a dark room. The true nature of the mind is pure and bright; the mind radiates light. This is called the boundary of the Form Skandha.

Translation: When abiding in that state and entering samadhi, it is like opening one's eyes in a dark room. Because the true nature is pure and bright, the mind emits light. This is called the boundary of the Form Skandha.

Commentary: When abiding in that state and entering samadhi.

Ananda should know: when cultivating this samadhi, abiding in the pure state, samadhi power unchanging, forgetting and remembering as one. Like one opening eyes to recognize one's own inside the dark room. Like one with bright eyes living in a dark room, with the true nature pure and bright, the mind emits light. The true nature, pure and bright, the mind not yet emitted radiates the light of wisdom, truly genuine. Then this is called the boundary of the Form Skandha. In the five skandhas: form, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness; the Form Skandha is exhausted. This person can transcend past kalpas. Examining the causes of the Form Skandha, seeing false thoughts as the root. When a practitioner passes through a kalpa, they see their own conduct all arising from false thoughts (the third of the three).

THE TEN FALSE STATES OF THE FORM SKANDHA

(IF ONE DOES NOT TRANSCEND THE FORM SKANDHA)

  1. THE SPIRIT'S DEPARTURE

Sutra text: Ananda, in that samadhi, the spirit intensely examines the true brightness; the seeing and the great become unobstructed. Insufficient illumination, the spirit leaves the body. This is called the spirit's brightness drifting to the fore. This is like concentrated effort, not the state of sagehood. If one does not raise the mind of sagehood, this is a good state. But if one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

Translation: Ananda, right in that samadhi, contemplating the true brightness, seeing the seeing and the great no longer obstructing each other. In a moment, the spirit leaves the body. This state is called the spirit's brightness drifting to the fore. This is only a temporary state from effort, not the state of sagehood. If one does not raise the mind of sagehood, this is a good state. But if one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

Commentary: Here we discuss the connected demonic states of the five skandhas: form, feeling, perception, volition, consciousness. There are five kinds of demons in each skandha. Thus there are Fifty Skandha-Demon States. A practitioner must have clear understanding of these Fifty Skandha-Demon States. If a practitioner does not understand these states, they will easily end their life in the demon king's power without knowing why. That is why practitioners must exhaust their spirit in cultivation.

Ananda, right in that samadhi, contemplating the true brightness, when the Form Skandha is about to dissolve, one will see all directions bright, and feel a kind of light. Because one has used effort to contemplate...

  1. WORMS APPEARING IN THE BODY

Sutra text: Ananda, again using that mind to contemplate the true brightness, seeing through the entire body. Suddenly worms and insects appear from within the body, while the body remains intact, not harmed. This state is called the spirit's brightness manifesting form. This is only a temporary state from concentrated effort, not sagehood. If the person does not think they have attained sagehood, this is a good state. But if they think they have attained sagehood, they fall into the demonic state.

Translation: Again, Ananda, if one uses that mind to contemplate the true brightness, seeing through the whole body. Suddenly worms and insects appear from within one's body, while the body remains intact, unharmed. This state is called the spirit's brightness manifesting form. This is only a temporary state from concentrated effort, not sagehood. If the person does not think they have attained sagehood, this is a good state. But if they think they have attained sagehood, they fall into the demonic state.

Commentary: This is called the second state of the demonic states of the Form Skandha. This demonic state appears because the cultivator has concentrated effort. If one does not have concentrated effort in practice, one cannot attract this demonic state. Even if one wants it, demons won't give it to you. Under its gaze, you are just a poor beggar; it has no greedy desire to cheat you.

But now because you are at the point of effort, you have just accumulated a little treasure. That is why the demon follows you to cheat you.

What should you do when it comes? You must abide in the unmoving, fixed enlightenment, bright and clear; do not fixate, do not attach to forms appearing outside. Completely do not attach to...

  1. HEARING DHARMA VOICES IN EMPTY SPACE

Sutra text: Again, if one uses that mind to contemplate the Form Skandha outside. When the body's pores are exhausted, the pores' spirit, from the pores of the body, emerges as many sounds of guests. Then one hears voices teaching Dharma in empty space, or hears all directions expounding profound meanings. This state is called: "Spirit and pores interchanging, becoming the tathagata's sound, contemplating the true, not sagehood." If one does not raise the mind of sagehood, this is a good state. If one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

Translation: Again, if one uses that mind to contemplate the Form Skandha outside, contemplating the fine pores without end, inside and outside, the light reaches the ultimate. No more cessation of cultivation. Pores, pores - the three pores in the human body. I have already taught these to you brothers. Some pores have only ears or eyes, or only eyebrows...

  1. MANIFESTING A BODY

Sutra text: Again using that mind, the inner pure, the pores emit light. All directions transform into udumbara color. All beings transform into Tathagatas. At that time one sees Tathagata Turyogina on the great lotus, surrounded by many Tathagatas, and lotus flowers appearing simultaneously. This state is called: the mind's brightness transmitted by the spirit, when contemplating the source of the mind, light from within illuminates all realms. This is only a temporary state, not sagehood. If one does not think one has attained sagehood, this is a good state. If one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

  1. JEWEL COLORS FILLING SPACE

Sutra text: Again using that mind, contemplating the fine pure colored light. Contemplating unceasingly. Like reciting, reciting, reciting, reciting beyond. At that time one sees empty space transform into seven jewel colors, or the colors of the hundred jewels. Appearing simultaneously, not obstructing each other. The colors green, yellow, red, white appear clearly. This state is called "Effort reciting, the mind's false thoughts excessive." If one attains such a state, it is not sagehood. Not raising the mind of sagehood is a good state. If one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

  1. NIGHT AS DAY

Sutra text: Again using that mind, contemplating the fine pure light, unceasing. Suddenly in the night one sees things appearing in the room, no different from day. The things in the room also do not disappear. This is called: "Samadhi mind knowing what it sees, therefore seeing all inside the dark room." The mind is thus, not sagehood. If one does not raise the mind of sagehood, it is a good state. If one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

  1. BODY WITHOUT SENSATION, LIKE WOOD AND STONE

Sutra text: Again using that mind, perfectly entering the fire samadhi. Things disappear, no longer knowing. Fire does not burn the body, even when cut it is like cutting wood. This is called: the body's state and the mind's great ending completely, only one pure entering. The mind is thus, not sagehood. If one does not think one has attained sagehood, this is a good state. If one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

  1. TRANSFORMING INTO BUDDHA REALMS

Sutra text: Again using that mind, becoming pure and pure. Mind's effort exhausted, seeing great earth, all directions become Buddha lands, seven jewels, light illuminating all. Again seeing the Tathagata Tathagata like Ganges sands, empty space all ancient lamp trees. Below seeing the earth, above seeing the heavenly palace, unobstructed. This is called: false thoughts liking, thinking deeply day by day, thoughts becoming thus, not sagehood. Not raising the mind of sagehood is a good state. If one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

  1. NIGHT SEEING FAR

Sutra text: Again using that mind, contemplating deeply, suddenly in the night seeing distant places. Seeing trees, rivers, paths, caves, spirits, and hearing them converse. This state is called: "From excessive mind pressure to the utmost, the mind flies out to see many things from afar." This is not sagehood. If one does not think one has attained sagehood, this is a good state. If one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

  1. SEEING SPIRITUAL TRANSFORMATIONS WITHOUT KNOWING CAUSE

Sutra text: Again using that mind, contemplating the fine extreme, seeing spirits and sages, forms changing. In a moment, their bodies transform into many forms, not knowing why. This is called: "The confused mind, the mad ghost enters, or the demon creates transformations, very penetrating." Not sagehood. If one does not think one has attained sagehood, the demons disappear. If one thinks one has attained sagehood, one falls into the demonic state.

CONCLUDING ADMONITION FROM THE BUDDHA FOR THE FORM SKANDHA

Sutra text: Ananda, as above, the demon states appear in samadhi, caused by the Form Skandha, mind and effort interacting.

Translation: Ananda, all states above appear in samadhi, caused by the Form Skandha and mind's effort interacting.

Commentary: Ananda, all states above have been described appear when concentrating contemplation in samadhi. These states relate to the Form Skandha, when mind's effort interacts with contemplation of the Form Skandha to the utmost. Anyone cultivating can experience these states.

Sutra text: Beings are foolish, not knowing their own strength. Meeting this cause, deluded, not knowing, claiming sagehood, great false views arise, falling into the hells without interval.

Translation: Beings are foolish, not knowing their own strength. Meeting this cause, deluded, not knowing, claiming sagehood, great false views arise, falling into the hells without interval.

Commentary: Beings are foolish, not knowing their own strength. Beings are stubborn, hard to transform, foolish, difficult to teach. They do not know to examine themselves to verify their own cultivation level from the start. Meeting this cause, meeting these demonic states, deluded, not knowing themselves, claiming sagehood.

They proclaim: "Oh! I am a Buddha. I have awakened. I..." (continues with detailed commentary on the importance of not mistaking states for sagehood, the nature of demonic states, and the proper attitude toward cultivation).
