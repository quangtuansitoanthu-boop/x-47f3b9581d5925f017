VUONG DIEN

Layman Vuong Dien, styled Vo Cong, lived under the Song, a native of Cixi county in Mingzhou. He was very intelligent and deeply learned, but twice failed the metropolitan examinations. From then on his ambition for fame faded like cold ashes; he ate vegetarian food, wore coarse cloth, and attended every sutra lecture. In old age he devoted himself to the Buddha-Recitation samadhi and authored the Record of the Self-Nature of the Pure Land, writing his own preface:

"The special wonder of the Pure Land gate taught by the World Honored One can be summed in a single phrase: 'It carries beings from the rank of ordinary persons directly across to the stage of non-retrogression!' Why? — In this world, when a practitioner realizes the First Faith of the Perfect Teaching or the First Fruit of the Lesser Vehicle, wrong views and the three poisons never again arise. That is the realm of severing delusion and awakening (karmic delusion extinguished, awakening arisen), directly entering the stream of sages: though passing through rebirth, one never loses what has been realized. That rank transcends the four evil destinies (hells, hungry ghosts, animals, asuras) and never loses a heavenly or human body. But all ordinary persons, even bodhisattvas of the initial resolve who have reached the stage of subduing delusion and awakening (delusion sunk into the store-consciousness, awakening arisen), once they pass through the extraordinary transformation of birth and death, at once forget what they realized and practiced. Hence such persons, meeting adverse conditions, may retrogress and fall into the three lower paths.

But in the Land of Common Residence of sages and ordinary beings in the Land of Bliss, though it too holds the tiers of the three realms, there are only humans and celestials. Thus all conscious beings born there are forever done with the four destinies and freed from rebirth. With supporting conditions complete and lifespan endless, even the dullest, in one lifetime of practice, all realize sagehood. How then could there be retrogression? The Buddhas' intent in urging and praising is nothing beyond this. If the superior capacity resolves upon Buddha-recitation, that is the supreme pure karma and will join a high grade. As for the most ignorant rustics, if they sincerely recite the Buddha's name and vow rebirth, none will fail to attain it.

Alas! Reflecting upon the Pure Land gate, one knows the Buddha abandons not a single being! Those who cling to emptiness in folly, imitate unobstructed conduct, then in pride seek to cut off others' Buddha-recitation — are they not deeply pitiable?".

Early on the night of the Dinh Mao day in the leap month of the sixteenth year of Shaoxing, strange fragrance suddenly filled the house. The layman said to his Dharma student, the monk Tu Te: "This is evoked by this old man's pure karma!". Having spoken, he bathed, changed his robes, sat upright facing west, joined his palms, and attained rebirth. At the cremation, one hundred and eight rice-grain relics were recovered.
