Sutra text: 阿難整衣服。望大眾中,合掌頂禮。心迹圓明,悲欣交集。欲益未來諸眾生故,稽首白佛大悲世尊。我今已悟成佛法門。是中修行得無疑惑。常聞如來說如是言。自未得度先度人者菩薩發心。自覺已圓能覺他者如來應世。我雖未度願度末劫一切眾生。

Transliteration: A-nan chỉnh y phục. Vọng đại chúng trung, hợp chưởng đảnh lễ. Tâm tích viên minh, bi hân giao tập. Dục ích vị lai chư chúng sanh cố, khể thủ bạch Phật: “Đại bi Thế tôn. Ngã kim dĩ ngộ thành Phật pháp môn. Thị trung tu hành đắc vô nghi hoặc. Thường văn Như Lai thuyết như thị ngôn: ‘Tự vị đắc độ, tiên độ nhân giả, Bồ-tát phát tâm. Tự giác dĩ viên, năng giác tha giả, Như Lai ứng thế. Ngã tuy vị độ, nguyện độ mạt kiếp nhất thiết chúng sinh’.”

Translation: Ananda arranged his robes. Facing the great assembly, he joined his palms and bowed. His mind's traces perfectly bright, grief and joy intermingled. Wishing to benefit beings of the future, he bowed his head and said to the Buddha: "Greatly compassionate World Honored One, I have now awakened to the dharma-door for becoming a Buddha. Cultivating within it, I attain freedom from doubt. I often hear the Tathagata say: 'One not yet delivered who first delivers others — that is a Bodhisattva's resolve. Self-enlightenment perfected, able to enlighten others — that is a Tathagata's advent in the world. Though I am not yet delivered, I vow to deliver all beings of the final kalpa.'"

Commentary: After Ananda heard Manjushri Bodhisattva's verse, he stood up and arranged his robes. Ananda straightened his collar and pulled his kashaya robe neat. Ananda never had a bit of carelessness or negligence, never letting his robes be sloppy. Facing the great assembly, he joined his palms and bowed. His mind's nature entirely bright. Ananda was no longer deluded as before. He felt both joy and sorrow. Wishing to benefit beings of future lives, he bowed his head to the Buddha:

Ananda did not know whether to laugh or cry. Do you remember how easily Ananda weeps? Ananda has wept several times since the sutra-lecture assembly began, and now wants to weep again. But Ananda also wants to laugh. What was Ananda's concern? It is because of the saying:

Extreme joy gives birth to sorrow — utmost joy is sorrow.
Ananda was most joyful, thinking: "Now I have obtained the Buddhadharma; I have penetrated the true teaching." Ananda had never been so joyful. But understanding for himself alone was not enough; Ananda wished to benefit beings in future lives. So Ananda bowed to the Buddha and said:

"Greatly compassionate World Honored One, I have now awakened to the dharma-door for becoming a Buddha. Cultivating within it, I attain freedom from doubt.
I shall rely on the Dharma to cultivate and shall never again have a shred of doubt. "I often hear the Tathagata say..."

Ananda often proved what he said by citing the Buddha's own teachings. Here Ananda says, "I hear the Buddha teach that, 'One not yet delivered who first delivers others — that is a Bodhisattva's resolve.'"
When oneself is not yet enlightened, yet one can aspire to teach others first — that is the way of Bodhisattvas.
"Self-enlightenment perfected, able to enlighten others — that is a Tathagata's advent in the world."
