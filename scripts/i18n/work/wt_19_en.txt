TITLE: 20. THE FEAR OF DARKNESS
TOMTAT: Mrs. Tran had feared darkness since childhood — because in a past life as a man she stole lamp oil from a kindly family's gate lantern, leaving travelers frightened in the night. Meeting Master Miaofa, she learned the cause and effect.
-----
{align:center|**THE FEAR OF DARKNESS**}

{align:justify|Mrs. Tran, past 40, came with her husband to honor Master Miaofa.}

{align:justify|She told how since childhood the mere fall of night frightened her. So before dark she always lit every room in the house. Else, once night fell, she no longer dared enter a room to light its lamp. And if anything fell from the bed into the dark, she never dared pick it up. For the mere sight of darkness made her shiver with fear. Since childhood she had visited many hospitals and many psychologists, but none found the cause. Her fear of darkness tormented her always, leaving her most wretched.}

{align:justify|The Master said that in a past life she was a man named X, his house so poor he could not even buy lamp oil.}

{align:justify|In the village where X lived, there was a kindly family who on moonless nights always hung an oil lamp outside their great gate, to light the road for foot travelers — helping them see their destination and sparing them fear in the dark night. But this poor X conceived a wicked greedy thought: late at night he would steal all the lamp's oil for his own home.}

{align:justify|The lamp snuffed out, travelers grew fearful and could no longer see their way.}

{align:justify|As the cause, so the fruit. Hence in this life she ever fears the dark night.}

{align:justify|COMMENTARY:}

{align:justify|The story THE FEAR OF DARKNESS teaches of "stealing light — retribution in fearing darkness." In a past life, the poor yet greedy man X secretly stole the lamp oil a kindly family hung outside their gate to light travelers' way — a selfish act harming others. Its fruit: in this life ever fearing darkness, lighting every room, never daring to retrieve things fallen in the dark. The story warns: "As the cause, so the fruit." Harming others' safety and light summons the retribution of "fearing darkness" — living in the darkness of one's own heart. Whoever fears darkness or ghosts, examine whether in a past life or this one you ever frightened others — and repent to transform it.}
