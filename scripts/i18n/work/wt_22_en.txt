TITLE: 23. ONLY VEGETARIANISM AND PRECEPTS BRING BENEFIT
TOMTAT: Mrs. Dong suffered diarrhea for a dozen years from meat-eating. Advised to eat vegetarian and recite Guanyin Bodhisattva's name, she healed in a single night; practicing diligently afterward, her back pain healed too.
-----
{align:center|**ONLY VEGETARIANISM AND PRECEPTS BRING BENEFIT**}

{align:justify|Mrs. Dong, living downstairs, past 40, plain and generous by nature. She had suffered diarrhea for a dozen years. Daily she went at least 4-5 times, often a dozen. The moment her belly pained she must dash to the latrine; the doctors called it chronic enteritis. Though she drank a great many medicines, none worked.}

{align:justify|Once upstairs, she asked if I knew any remedy.}

{align:justify|I joked:}

{align:justify|"I shall prescribe for you — only use my 'prescription' as directed, and I guarantee the illness shall vanish. And a 'doctor' like me charges not a cent."}

{align:justify|Plain Mrs. Dong said: "Prescribe away — I trust you! Dear or bitter, I shall buy and drink it!"}

{align:justify|I said:}

{align:justify|"My medicine costs you nothing. I see you must soon stop eating meat — chicken, duck, fish... For your bowels and stomach reject foul foods, and so every day you suffer diarrhea. From this day, except unfertilized hen's eggs, you must end meat-eating and eat wholly vegetarian."}

{align:justify|I added:}

{align:justify|"Starting tonight, before sleep, relax your body, sit well, breathe evenly, calm your mind, close your eyes and recite 'Nam mô Quán Thế Âm Bồ-tát' for half an hour. Only be sincere, and results shall swiftly come. If a month of recitation brings no effect, count my words false — I shall let you eat meat again. In any case this prescription costs nothing — will you try it?"}

{align:justify|Hearing me, her face full of doubt, she asked:}

{align:justify|"Say — a woman colleague of mine visits the pagoda to recite sutras every full and new moon. Daily she devoutly burns incense at home, yet her whole body is ill. She has had needling, injections, medicines for day upon day, yet her illness never ceases. How do you explain that?"}

{align:justify|I demanded:}

{align:justify|"Is she vegetarian?"}

{align:justify|"People told her: 'A lay Buddhist worshiping at home need only avoid killing — threefold-pure meat may be eaten'... so she eats on," Mrs. Dong answered.}

{align:justify|I asked:}

{align:justify|"Suppose one smokes into lung disease, or drinks into liver disease — must he abstain from nothing, smoking and drinking at will, and merely take injections and medicines to heal? Is that so?"}

{align:justify|"That is impossible," she answered.}

{align:justify|"It is exactly the same. Illness born of eating beings' flesh must first be met by stopping meat-eating. Reciting sutras and the Buddha's name then bears effect. Illness born of unfilial conduct toward parents or of evil karma must first be met by ending the evil karma, arousing repentance, and praying for the Buddhas' blessing to prove effective."}

{align:justify|"Buddhas and bodhisattvas are the best physicians in the world. The Buddha Dharma is the finest medicine in the universe. Yet most people neither understand nor know this supreme medicine. People wholly ignore that each possesses a treasure vault — a most grievous pity. If you cannot follow the Buddha's instructions, then whatever medicines you use, whatever doctors you see, your illness shall never heal. Do I speak truly?"}

{align:justify|"Your explanation I understand. From now, within one month I shall practice your 'prescription' of vegetarianism and Buddha-recitation. If truly my illness heals, then I vow from this day to eat meat no more, but to learn from you — daily vegetarian, daily reciting the Buddha's name."}

{align:justify|The next evening, radiant Mrs. Dong reported to me:}

{align:justify|"Last night home again my belly did not pain; at supper I ate no meat. Before sleep I sat in meditation, closed my eyes reciting the Buddha's name — reciting a little, my whole body grew warm. I wondered: 'Is this a sympathetic response?' The more I recited, the more joyful my heart. When my legs went numb I opened my eyes. Oh! More than a full hour had passed. I unfolded my legs, my whole body sweating — as though just bathed in hot water — feeling utterly light and free."}

{align:justify|Then she merrily said:}

{align:justify|"Who would have thought sitting meditation and Buddha-recitation so good! All today until now my belly has not pained. How strange — in a dozen years I have never known such an experience."}

{align:justify|To this day, Mrs. Dong's belly pain and diarrhea are not only gone — even her backache of many years healed without medicine.}

{align:justify|Now daily she sits in meditation reciting the Buddha's name, and has begun reciting the Kshitigarbha Sutra and the Great Compassion Mantra. She practices diligently of her own accord, her mind firm, vowing to follow the Buddha's teaching.}

{align:justify|COMMENTARY:}

{align:justify|The story ONLY VEGETARIANISM AND PRECEPTS BRING BENEFIT proves in life that "meat-eating — diarrheal illness; vegetarianism and Buddha-recitation — healing." Mrs. Dong suffered chronic enteritis, a dozen years of diarrhea no medicine cured — yet a single night of vegetarianism and reciting Guanyin Bodhisattva's name ended the diarrhea; afterward her back pain healed of itself. The story's fine comparison: "Suppose one smokes into lung disease... must he abstain from nothing, smoking at will... to heal?" — the answer is impossible. Likewise, illness born of eating beings' flesh must first be met by stopping meat-eating; only then do sutra recitation and Buddha-recitation bear effect. It reminds those who "recite the Buddha's name yet still eat threefold-pure meat": "Reciting the Buddha's name while eating beings' flesh means greed uncut, offenses uncut." For true benefit from the Buddha Dharma, eat vegetarian and keep the precepts in earnest.}
