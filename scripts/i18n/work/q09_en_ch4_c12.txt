<p>Commentary: <strong>That person, unaware of demonic possession, likewise claiming to have attained the wondrous fruition of unsurpassed nirvana. Coming to the place of the good man seeking spiritual powers, spreading its seat, preaching the Dharma.</strong></p>
<p><strong>That person may grasp firelight in hand</strong> &#8211; grasping fire in one hand &#8211; <strong>gathering the light with the other hand, then scattering the fire over the heads of the fourfold assembly listening</strong>: placing fire on each listener's head one by one. <strong>The firelight atop their heads several feet high</strong>: placed on the head small at first, then the fire grows gradually to several <em>feet</em>. <strong>Yet neither hot nor burning</strong> &#8211; though using nothing to shield from the fire.</p>
<p><strong>Or it</strong> (the possessed person) <strong>can walk on water as on level ground, or sit unmoving in the air</strong> &#8211; nothing holding him floating in the air, yet he can sit still upon it&#8230;</p>
<p><strong>Or enter into a jar or a bag, or pass through windows, through walls, without obstruction</strong>: doors and windows shut, yet it passes through with ease, unopened, without the least obstruction.</p>
<p><strong>Only with weapons it is not at ease</strong>: it fears being stabbed by knives, spears, swords &#8211; for it still has a material body obstructing. Though possessing five kinds of spiritual powers, it still fears injury from weapons.</p>
<p><strong>Claiming to have become a Buddha</strong>, <strong>yet clad in lay white</strong> &#8211; not wearing the left-home robes.</p>
<p><strong>Receiving bhikshus' homage</strong>: clad as a layperson, yet accepting bhikshus' homage. <strong>Slandering Chan samadhi and the Vinaya</strong> &#8211; deeming them useless and unreasonable.</p>
<p><strong>Scolding its disciples and exposing people's faults</strong>: exposing people's private faults. <strong>Heedless of ridicule and suspicion</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Khẩu trung thường thuyết, thần thông tự tại, hoặc phục linh nhân, bàng kiến Phật độ.  Quỷ lực cảm nhân, phi hữu chân thực. Tán thán hành dâm, bất hủy thô hạnh. Tương chư ổi tá, dĩ vi truyền pháp.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Its mouth constantly speaks of spiritual powers and self-mastery, or else making people see Buddha-lands nearby. Moved by ghost power, nothing truly real. Praising lustful conduct, not renouncing coarse acts &#8211; taking all defiled conduct as transmitting the Dharma.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>It constantly speaks of the various</strong> kinds of <strong>spiritual powers and self-mastery,</strong> <strong>or else making people see Buddha-lands pervading the ten directions. Those lands are unreal, only appearing through demonic power to delude people</strong>: in fact it has no real skill-power at all &#8211; what <strong>it praises</strong> most is <strong>lust</strong>, saying: &#8220;That is truly the most marvelous &#8211; the source of Bodhi and nirvana.&#8221; It <strong>does not renounce coarse acts</strong>, saying: &#8220;Nothing is wrong with that &#8211; do not bother keeping precepts.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>Taking all defiled conduct as transmitting the Dharma.</strong> It uses every defiled deed as a means of transmitting the Dharma.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thử danh thiên địa, đại lực sơn tinh, hải tinh phong tinh, hà tinh thổ tinh, nhất thiết thảo mộc, tích kiếp tinh mị. Hoặc phục long mị, hoặc thọ chung tiên, tái hoạt vi mị. Hoặc tiên kì chung, kế niên ưng tử, kì hình bất hóa, tha quái sở phụ. Niên lão thành ma, não loạn thị nhân. Yếm túc tâm sanh, khứ bỉ nhân thể. Đệ tử dữ sư, đa hãm vương nạn.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is called the mighty spirits of heaven and earth &#8211; mountain sprites, sea sprites, wind sprites, river sprites, earth sprites, and the essences of all plants and trees accumulated over eons; or dragon-goblins; or immortals whose lifespan ended, revived as goblins; or immortals whose retribution ended and who should have died, yet whose forms do not decay, possessed by other goblins. Aged long into demons, harassing this person. Sated, they arise and leave that person's body. Disciple and teacher mostly fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>This is called the mighty spirits of heaven and earth &#8211; mountain sprites, sea sprites, wind sprites, river sprites, earth sprites, and the accumulated-eon essences of all plants and trees. Or dragon-goblins (dragon-demons); or immortals whose lifespan ended, revived as goblins</strong> &#8211; sometimes a thousand, two, three, or up to five thousand years old, lifespan ended, revived as goblins. <strong>Or immortals whose retribution ended and who should have died</strong>, <strong>yet whose forms</strong> after death <strong>do not decay</strong> or change, <strong>hence possessed by other goblins</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>These kinds, long-lived, turn into ghosts, harassing</strong> the samadhi power of <strong>cultivators</strong>. At last, <strong>sated, no longer possessing people.</strong></p>
<p>While the demon still possesses the body, the possessed person has extraordinary samadhi power. But once it possesses no more, <strong>both disciple and teacher fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đương tiên giác bất nhập luân hồi. Mê hoặc bất tri đọa vô gián ngục </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel. Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: Ananda, <strong>you should know this matter beforehand, lest you enter</strong> the demon king's <strong>turning wheel</strong> &#8211; do not fall into the demon king's temptations.</p>
<p><strong>Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>

<p style="text-align:center"><strong><em>9 &#8211; GREED FOR PROFOUND EMPTINESS (THE DEMON OF SUNK EMPTINESS)</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu thiện nam tử,  thọ ấm  hư diệu, bất tao tà lự, viên định phát minh. Tam-ma-địa  trung, tâm ái nhập diệt, nghiên cứu hóa tánh, tham cầu thâm không.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed. Within samadhi the mind fond of entering extinction, studying the nature of transformation, greedily seeking profound emptiness.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed.</strong> In the empty, penetrating, wondrous state after the feeling skandha has dissolved, the cultivator no longer has deviant concerns, dwelling in the bright, perfect samadhi. <strong>Within samadhi the mind fond of entering extinction, studying the nature of transformation, greedily seeking profound emptiness.</strong></p>
<p>The cultivator studies how a body transforms from existence to nothingness, and how from nothingness it transforms back to existence &#8211; studying the manifestation of this transformation, longing to experience a wondrous, empty state.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhĩ thời thiên ma hậu đắc kì tiện, phi tinh phụ nhân, khẩu thuyết kinh pháp.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Then the heavenly demon gets its convenient chance, sending its essence flying to possess another person, using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma.</strong></p>
