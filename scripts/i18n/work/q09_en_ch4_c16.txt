<p><strong>Translation: Ananda should know: in the Dharma-ending age these ten demons &#8211; either leaving home to cultivate within my Dharma, or possessing human bodies, or manifesting their own forms &#8211; all claim to have accomplished right and universal awakening.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>Ananda should know</strong>, especially <strong>in the Dharma-ending age, these ten demons</strong> appearing in the ten demonic states connected with the thinking skandha &#8211; <strong>either leaving home to cultivate within the Tathagata's Dharma</strong>, <strong>or possessing human bodies</strong>: the demon kings may possess other people's bodies, <strong>or manifesting their own various forms</strong> &#8211; displaying spiritual powers like demon kings, appearing in all kinds of shapes: Buddhas, Bodhisattvas, Arhats, or people of the heavens &#8211; demon kings can appear in any shape.</p>
<p>They all claim to have accomplished right and universal awakening: Right and Universal Knowledge is one of the Buddha's ten epithets &#8211; the Buddha is one complete with <em>right knowledge</em> and <em>universal knowledge</em>: <em>right knowledge</em> means knowing that <em>the mind contains the myriad dharmas</em>, and <em>universal knowledge</em> means knowing that <em>the myriad dharmas are only mind</em>. When people gain such truly right understanding, they become Buddhas &#8211; ones of <em>Right and Universal Knowledge</em>.</p>
<p>The heavenly demons fake themselves as Buddhas, falsely proclaiming to have attained <em>Right and Universal Knowledge</em>. When Shakyamuni Buddha was about to enter nirvana, he summoned the demon kings and taught: &#8220;From now on you must dwell within the moral code &#8211; do not violate it.&#8221;</p>
<p>The demon kings replied: &#8220;So you want us to practice by the code you made. Very well! In the Dharma-ending age I shall wear your robes, eat your food, then defecate into your alms bowl.&#8221;</p>
<p>Those words mean they will destroy the Dharma from within.</p>
<p>Hearing this, the Buddha said: &#8220;Then there is no way to save you &#8211; your conduct is too vicious and destructive.&#8221;</p>
<p>Hence the saying: &#8220;Worms in the lion's body eat the lion's flesh&#8221; &#8211; meaning in the Dharma-ending age demons are strong and the Dharma weak. So cultivators must be very careful. What are demon kings like? Usually quite unlike other people &#8211; with demonic aura within, showing some abnormal outer phenomena to prove they are demon kings.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text:  Tán thán dâm dục, phá Phật luật nghi. Tiên ác ma sư, dữ ma đệ tử, dâm dâm tương truyền. Như thị tà tinh, mị kì tâm phủ, cận tắc cửu sanh, đa du bách thế. Linh chân tu hành, tổng vi ma quyến.   </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Praising lust, breaking the Buddha's moral code &#8211; former demonic teachers with demonic disciples, lustfully transmitting to one another. Such deviant essences enchanting their minds &#8211; near, at least nine lives; many, over a hundred generations &#8211; making true cultivators all become Mara's retinue.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>They praise lust</strong>.</p>
<p>How can we conclude someone is a demon? This: demons never praise the true method of cultivation &#8211; they praise lust and openly propagate lustful acts.</p>
<p><strong>Breaking the Buddha's moral code.</strong></p>
<p>It says: &#8220;The Buddha's precepts are utterly useless &#8211; do not keep them. Those precepts are only for Small Vehicle disciples to practice; we are Great Vehicle Bodhisattvas, needing to keep nothing!&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>Demonic teachers, the demon king's disciples, transmitting lustful acts to one another</strong>: practicing lustful acts together and praising them, saying: &#8220;That is the most wondrous, wonderful Dharma-door &#8211; true emptiness and wondrous existence lie right therein.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>Such deviant essences</strong> &#8211; praising lust &#8211; <strong>enchanting cultivators' minds</strong>: since cultivators' minds, possessed and enchanted by deviant demons, immerse themselves in lust, openly praising it &#8211; doing all such things because demons have seized complete mastery of their minds.</p>
<p><strong>Near, at least nine lives; many, over a hundred generations. Making true cultivators all become Mara's retinue.</strong></p>
<p>At least nine lives: what does a life mean? The lifespan of a person, from birth to death? Not only that &#8211; more: about a hundred years. So nine lives are nine hundred years, and at most a hundred generations &#8211; a generation thirty years, a hundred generations three thousand years. True cultivators lost into the demon king's retinue at least nine lives (nine hundred years), at most a hundred generations (three thousand years).</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Mệnh chung chi hậu, tất vi ma dân. Thất chánh biến tri, đọa vô gián ngục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: After life ends, they are sure to become Mara's subjects. Losing right and universal knowledge, they fall into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>After life ends</strong>: when the allotted span ends, at whatever point within the nine lives to a hundred generations, the cultivator dying, <strong>is sure to fall as one of Mara's subjects</strong> &#8211; unable to be a demon king, only a petty demon subject, an ordinary citizen among the demon folk.</p>
<p><strong>Losing right and universal knowledge</strong>: losing right views, left only wrong knowledge and wrong views, carrying out whatever the demon king commands &#8211; and at last <strong>falling into the uninterrupted hell</strong>: after enjoying the demon's blessings to the end and the demon-subject's life ending, falling into the uninterrupted hell.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ kim vị tu, tiên thủ tịch diệt, túng đắc vô học. Lưu nguyện nhập bỉ mạt pháp chi trung, khởi đại từ bi, cứu độ chánh tâm thâm tín chúng sanh. Linh bất trước ma, đắc chánh tri kiến. Ngã kim độ nhữ dĩ xuất sanh tử. Nhữ tuân Phật ngữ, danh báo Phật ân.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Ananda, you should not yet hasten to still extinction &#8211; though you have attained beyond study. Keep your vow to enter that Dharma-ending age, giving rise to great compassion to save and deliver beings of right mind and deep faith, keeping them from demonic entanglement, gaining right views. I have now delivered you, already out of birth and death. Obeying the Buddha's words is called repaying the Buddha's kindness.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>Ananda, you should not yet hasten to enter nirvana</strong> &#8211; not entering nirvana early &#8211; but replacing the Tathagata, remaining in this Saha world to teach beings.</p>
<p>Al<strong>though you have attained the <em>beyond-study</em> fruition</strong>. <strong>Keep your vow to enter that Dharma-ending age</strong>: though you have now attained the <em>beyond-study</em> fruition (at that time Ananda had realized the second Arhat fruition, not yet formally the <em>beyond-study</em> fruition; yet his path of effort toward realization was very bright, so he is regarded as having attained it).</p>
<p>The Buddha taught: you should keep the great-compassion vow &#8211; when the age of <em>right Dharma</em> has passed and the age of <em>semblance Dharma</em> is no more, the <em>Dharma-ending</em> age will come. Then <strong>give rise to great compassion to save and deliver beings of right mind and</strong> <strong>deep faith</strong>: Ananda, give rise to the great compassionate resolve to save all beings of true faith in the Dharma-ending age.</p>
