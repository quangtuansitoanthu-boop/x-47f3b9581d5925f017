<p><strong>Or it eats but a sesame seed, a wheat grain a day, yet looks hale</strong> &#8211; fatter than a pig. <strong>Upheld by ghost power.</strong> (27)</p>
<p><strong>It slanders bhikshus and scolds its followers, heedless of ridicule and suspicion</strong>.</p>
<p>It specializes in reviling Sangha Masters, the left-home ones: &#8220;Left-home people? What did they leave home for? They cultivate nothing &#8211; do left-home people not crave money? With them, the more the better!&#8221;</p>
<p>Or it says: &#8220;Why do you believe them? They are only people, too &#8211; you are utter fools in the end.&#8221;</p>
<p>When someone believes the bhikshus, it calls them &#8220;fools,&#8221; scolding its own followers as animals and such vile things, wholly heedless of slanderous words.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Khẩu trung hảo ngôn, tha phương bảo tạng, thập phương thánh hiền, tiềm nặc chi xứ. Tùy kỳ hậu giả, vãng vãng kiến hữu, kì dị chi nhân.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Its mouth delights in speaking of treasuries in distant places, or the hidden dwelling of holy sages of the ten directions. Those following it often see strange and unusual people.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>It delights in speaking of treasuries in strange lands</strong>: what does it love to speak of? It says: &#8220;Over there, in that place, are gold, silver, and jewels &#8211; do you want to get them?&#8221; It spreads such rumors to enchant people; or <strong>it loves speaking of the hidden dwelling of holy sages of the ten directions</strong>, telling people: &#8220;Buddhas and Bodhisattvas are cultivating in that place, wholly unknown to you.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>Those following it often see strange and unusual people</strong> &#8211; it can emit light or display very strange features.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thử danh sơn lâm, thổ địa thành hoàng, xuyên nhạc quỷ thần, niên lão thành ma. Hoặc hữu tuyên dâm, phá Phật giới luật, dữ thừa sự giả, tiềm hành ngũ dục; hoặc hữu tinh tiến, thuần thực thảo mộc, vô định hành sự, não loạn bỉ nhân. Yếm túc tâm sanh, khứ bỉ nhân thể. Đệ tử dữ sư, đa hãm vương nạn.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is called a demon &#8211; mountain and forest spirits, earth, city-gods, river and mountain ghosts and spirits, aged into ghosts. The possessed person advocates lust, breaking the Buddha's moral code &#8211; secretly practicing the five desires with its attendants; or else vigorously eating only herbs, acting without samadhi, harassing that person. Sated, it arises and leaves that person's body. Disciple and teacher mostly fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: What kind of being is this? <strong>This is a kind of demon &#8211; mountain and forest spirits, earth, city-gods, river and mountain, aged</strong> over a long time <strong>into ghosts</strong>. <strong>The possessed person advocates lust, breaking the Buddha's moral code</strong>: lustful thoughts make it break the precepts. It <strong>secretly practices lust with its disciples</strong> who likewise enjoy the five desires: wealth, beauty, fame, food, sleep.</p>
<p><strong>Or else it vigorously eats only herbs, acting erratically.</strong> It does not sit in meditation or apply any cultivation effort &#8211; only following useless asceticism.</p>
<p><strong>Harassing the cultivator</strong>, making them unable to cultivate the Way anymore.</p>
<p>But after a time, <strong>sated, it no longer possesses people; teacher and disciple both fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đương tiên giác, bất nhập luân hồi. Mê hoặc bất tri, đọa vô gián ngục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel. Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>You should know this matter beforehand, lest you enter the turning wheel.</strong></p>
<p>Awaken early to this situation, lest you enter the demon's trap &#8211; do not enter the demon-spread net of the turning wheel of birth and death.</p>
<p><strong>Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong><em>8 &#8211; GREED FOR SPIRITUAL POWERS</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu thiện nam tử,  thọ ấm hư diệu, bất tao tà lự, viên định phát minh. Tam-ma-địa trung, tâm ái thần thông, chủng chủng biến hóa. Nghiên cứu hóa nguyên, tham thủ thần lực. </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed. Within samadhi the mind fond of spiritual powers and all transformations &#8211; studying the origin of transformations, greedily seeking spiritual powers.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous,</strong> in the empty, penetrating, wondrous state after the feeling skandha has dissolved, <strong>untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed. Within samadhi the mind fond of spiritual powers.</strong> He has another false thought &#8211; determined to gain spiritual powers <strong>and all</strong> transforming abilities, to display the eighteen <strong>transformations</strong> in the air: water shooting from the upper body, fire issuing from the lower body; fire from above, water from below. He burns to be like the Arhats, displaying these eighteen spiritual transformations floating in the air.</p>
<p><strong>Hence studying the origin of such transformations,</strong> studying the fundamental source of transforming powers, <strong>greedily seeking spiritual powers.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhĩ thời thiên ma, hậu đắc kì tiện, phi tinh phụ nhân, khẩu thuyết kinh pháp.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Then the heavenly demon gets its convenient chance, sending its essence to possess another person, using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma. </strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>Then the heavenly demon gets its convenient chance, sending its essence to possess another person, using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma.</strong></p>
<p>The heavenly demon, long awaiting a convenient chance, at once sends its essence to possess another person, then commands that person to lecture Sutras and Dharma.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Kì nhân thành bất giác tri ma trước. Diệc ngôn tự đắc vô thượng niết-bàn. Lai bỉ cầu thông thiện nam tử xứ, phu tòa thuyết pháp. Thị nhân hoặc phục, thủ chấp hỏa quang, thủ toát kì quang, phân ư sở thính, tứ chúng đầu thượng, thị chư thính nhân,đỉnh thượng hỏa quang, giai trưởng sổ xích, diệc vô nhiệt tánh, tằng bất phần thiêu. Hoặc thượng thủy hành, như lí bình địa, hoặc ư không trung, an tọa bất động, hoặc nhập bình nội, hoặc xử nang trung, việt dũ thấu viên, tằng vô chướng ngại. Duy ư đao binh, bất đắc tự tại. Tự ngôn thị Phật, thân trước bạch y, thọ tỉ khưu lễ, phỉ báng thiền luật, mạ lị đồ chúng, yết lộ nhân sự, bất tị cơ hiềm.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: That person, truly unaware of demonic possession, likewise claims to have attained unsurpassed nirvana. Coming to the place of the good man seeking powers, it spreads its seat and preaches the Dharma. That person may grasp firelight in hand, gathering the light and scattering it over the heads of the fourfold assembly listening &#8211; the firelight atop each listener's crown several feet high, yet neither hot nor ever burning. Or walking on water as on level ground; or sitting unmoving in the air; or entering into a jar or a bag; or passing through windows, through walls, without obstruction. Only with weapons it is not at ease. Claiming to be a Buddha, its body clad in white, receiving bhikshus' homage &#8211; slandering Chan and the Vinaya, scolding its followers, exposing people's affairs, heedless of ridicule and suspicion.</strong></p>
