The Fifty Skandhas

Sutra text: Túc thời Như lai tương bãi pháp tòa. Ư sư tử sàng, lãm thất bảo ký, hồi tử kim sơn, tái lai bằng ỹ, phổ cáo đại chúng, cấp A-nan ngôn: Nhữ đẳng hữu học Duyên giác Thanh văn , kim nhật hồi tâm thú đại bồ-đề vô thượng diệu giác. Ngô kim dĩ thuyết chân tu hành pháp.

Translation: At that time the Tathagata was about to leave the Dharma seat. From the lion throne he drew near the seven-jeweled chair, turned his purple-golden mountain-like body about, leaned back against the chair, and announced to the great assembly and to Ananda: You who are Hearers and Pratyekabuddhas still in need of study, today you have turned your minds toward the unsurpassed, wondrous awakening of great Bodhi. I have now taught you the true method of cultivation.

Kinh Văc: Hựu phúc ư trung đắc tiêu vi túc. Như đai thệ thiên Vô Văn tiếu-khẩu vọng ngôn chủng thành. Thiên báo dĩ túc, tư tưởng hiện tiền, bằng A-la-hán, thần tao hựu hưu. Đỗ a-tỉ ngự ngọc.

Translation: Again, during cultivation, one feels joy and satisfaction when attaining a small result. Like the devaputra Vô Văn who falsely claimed to have attained the deva state, when the heavenly retribution is exhausted, thoughts arise, and one falls like an Arhat whose divine power is exhausted, falling into the Avici hell.

Commentary: Again, during cultivation... (continues with detailed commentary on the dangers of false attainment, the importance of genuine wisdom, the three essentials of essence/energy/spirit, and the nature of demonic states)

Kinh Văn: Do những vọng tưởng, mê lầm vi cưu, si ái phát sinh, sanh phát biến mê, cựu hưu không tính. Hóa mê bất tực, hựu thải giới sanh. Tắc thọ thụp phương vi trần quốc đô phi vô lâu giá, giai thỉ mê ngon vọng tưởng an lập.

Translation: Because of false thoughts, delusion gives rise to jealousy, which produces the great love/desire, creating deluded confusion, leading to the end of no-return. Transforming delusion without end, the realms of transmigration are exhausted. The states of the dust worlds are like dust in every direction, not eternal, all arising from delusion and false thoughts.
