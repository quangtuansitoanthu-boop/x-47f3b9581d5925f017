<p><strong>That flock of evils</strong> &#8211; demons, ghosts, and outer paths &#8211; <strong>all draw upon dark energy</strong>: all their (sabotaging) tactics rely on darkness. <strong>Since brightness can destroy darkness</strong> &#8211; if you attain samadhi power and genuine wisdom, the power of the light of wisdom will shine and dispel all gloom &#8211; <strong>they melt away of themselves on approaching you; how would they dare approach you to disturb your chan samadhi</strong>? If they wish to approach, let them &#8211; but they dare not harass you.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: <em>Nhược bất minh ngộ, bị ấm sở mê, tắc nhữ A-nan tất vi ma tử, thành tựu ma nhân. </em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: If you are not clearly awakened and are deluded by the skandhas, then you, Ananda, will surely become a child of Mara and accomplish the deeds of Mara.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>If you are not clearly awakened</strong> &#8211; if you do not understand and awaken but <strong>are deluded by the demons of the five skandhas</strong>, <strong>then you, Ananda</strong>, <strong>will surely become a child of Mara and accomplish the deeds of Mara</strong> &#8211; joining the retinue of the demon hosts.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Như Ma-đăng-già thù vi miễu liệt. Bỉ tuy chú nhữ phá Phật luật nghi. Bát vạn hạnh trung, kì hủy nhất giới. Tâm thanh tịnh cố, thượng vị luân nịch.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Like the Matangi difficulty you met &#8211; a trifling matter. She used a mantra to make you break the Buddha's moral code, hoping that among the eighty-four thousand subtle practices you would violate a single precept. But because your mind was pure, you did not drown in defilement.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Like the difficulty</strong> with Matangi's daughter that <strong>you met &#8211; a trifling matter</strong>: merely an ordinary entanglement with demonic deeds. <strong>She used a mantra to make you break the Buddha's moral code</strong> &#8211; she used the mantra of the Brahma god-immortal to delude you and make you violate the Buddha's moral code. <strong>Hoping that among the eighty-four thousand subtle practices you would violate a single precept</strong>. <strong>But because your mind was pure, you did not drown in defilement</strong>: since you had already realized the first Arhat fruition, you were completely untroubled by her and did not fall.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thử nãi huy nhữ bảo giác toàn thân. Như tể thần gia hốt phùng tịch một, uyển chuyển linh lạc vô khả ai cứu</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is an effort to utterly destroy your precious enlightenment. (Should it succeed,) you would be like a high minister suddenly stripped of power, his family left desolate with no one to pity or rescue them.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>This is an effort to utterly destroy your precious enlightenment</strong> &#8211; a conduct striving to make you fall. (Should it succeed,) <strong>you would be like a high minister suddenly stripped of power</strong>: when a high official's family property is confiscated by the King, <strong>his family is left desolate with no one to pity or rescue them</strong> &#8211; utterly alone, with no recourse, no one to pity or help.</p>
