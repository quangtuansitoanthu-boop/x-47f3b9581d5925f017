Commentary: Such atyantikas destroy the Buddha-seed. Those who speak great false speech — uncertified claiming certification, unattained claiming attainment, uncomprehending claiming comprehension — such people are called atyantikas, meaning "those who sever good roots." If you cut off your good roots, it means evil roots will pile up. Those who speak great false speech and cheat others in the world destroy the Buddha-seed within themselves, like a person cutting a tala tree with a knife.

The tala tree, grown in India, grows on high ground, but if its crown is cut off, it can no longer grow. Those who cut off the Buddha-seed are like cutting off the tala tree's crown; the Buddha-seed will never be regained. The Buddha predicts such people forever lose good roots, have no more right knowing and seeing. The Buddha's prediction about such people is that they destroy good roots and become stripped of all knowing or views. Sinking into the three seas of suffering, never perfecting samadhi.

The three seas of suffering spoken of here are:
1. The suffering of swords (sword path): referring to the sword-mountain hells.
2. The suffering of blood (blood path): referring to the blood hells, where people's entire bodies are made to bleed constantly.
3. The suffering of fire (fire path): referring to the fire-burning hells.
Such people will fall into these three terrible hells.

Sutra text: 我滅度後勅諸菩薩及阿羅漢。應身生彼末法之中。作種種形度諸輪轉。

Transliteration: Ngã diệt độ hậu, sắc chư Bồ-tát cập A-la-hán, ứng thân sanh bỉ mạt pháp chi trung. Tác chủng chủng hình độ chư luân chuyển.

Translation: After my extinction, I command the Bodhisattvas and Arhats to be born with response bodies in that Dharma-Ending Age, manifesting various forms to deliver those in the turning wheel.

Commentary: After my extinction, I command the Bodhisattvas and Arhats to be born with response bodies in that Dharma-Ending Age. They should use reward bodies and transformation bodies to be born in this world, where there is much suffering. In the Dharma-Ending Age, they must use many body-forms, manifesting in countless realms — perhaps as humans, perhaps as animals, or perhaps in some other way. They will accord with beings to deliver those still in the turning wheel.

They will widely rescue beings. Bodhisattvas will return to take animal bodies. Do not think saying so is disrespectful, for they truly do that. In Bodhisattva practices, they will come to teach animals, as when Shakyamuni Buddha in a past life took the body of the Deer King to rescue the herd of deer.

Sutra text: 或作沙門白衣居士,人王宰官,童男童女。如是乃至婬女寡婦姦偷屠販。與其同事稱歎 佛乘。令其身心入三摩地。

Transliteration: Hoặc tác sa-môn bạch y cư sĩ, nhân vương tể quan đồng nam đồng nữ. Như thị nãi chí dâm nữ quả phụ, gian thâu đồ phán. Dữ kỳ đồng sự xưng tán Phật thừa. linh kỳ thân tâm nhập tam-ma-địa.

Translation: Or as shramanas, white-robed lay followers, human kings, ministers, boys and girls; thus even as wantons, widows, cheats, thieves, butchers, and peddlers — joining them in the same deeds, praising the Buddha Vehicle, causing their body and mind to enter samadhi.
