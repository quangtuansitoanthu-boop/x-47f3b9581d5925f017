Commentary: Ah! Manjushri Bodhisattva speaks most forcefully. He needs not a bit of elegance. He is bluntly scolding Ananda:
"Utmost purity, light penetrating. Once your enlightened nature is perfect and pure, reaching the utmost, luminous wisdom pervades everywhere. You become enlightened. Still shining, containing empty space. When you attain this effort, empty space is right within you. Your own nature is precisely the all-pervading enlightened nature. When you return to the original nature that has always been yours, you pervade all of empty space.
Returning to regard the world,
It is like a matter within a dream.

As you continue applying this effort, you regard the world and will see it as nothing more than a dream.
Matanga's daughter is but a dream.
Who can detain you?
She is part of the dream. Who is it that could capture you? You lost face. You wanted to be there. You liked her. If you attained the level of effort I have just described, who could restrain you?" You see there is not a bit of elegance. Is there?

Sutra text: 如世巧幻師 幻作諸男女 雖見諸根動 要以一機抽 息機歸寂然 諸幻成無性。

Transliteration: Như thế xảo huyễn sư Huyễn tác chư nam nữ Tuy kiến chư căn động Yếu dĩ nhất cơ trừu Tức cơ quy tịch nhiên Chư huyễn thành vô tánh.

Translation: Like the clever illusionists of the world Who conjure up men and women — Though the roots are seen to move, It all hangs on a single mechanism; When the mechanism rests, all returns to stillness, And the illusions become without nature.

Commentary: Like the clever illusionists of the world.
This is like the puppet shows of old, when puppets danced and performed behind a colored screen. Behind were puppets made of donkey hide, attached to strings worked by the puppeteer, and the audience saw the puppets' shadows reflected upon the curtain. Today we have cinema. The puppets look like real people, but in fact they are worked by puppet-masters. They often fight noisily on stage with swords and guns. I remember watching from childhood.
Who conjure up men and women —
The puppets wear clothes like men and women, then dance upon the stage.
Though the roots are seen to move,
It all hangs on a single mechanism.

They seem able to move arms and legs and even to blink eyes and lips, but in fact it is all machinery. They are connected to a mechanism. Yet the viewers are still stunned.
When the mechanism rests, all returns to stillness,
And the illusions become without nature.
Ultimately, they are nothing at all. In this chapter, Manjushri Bodhisattva explains why all dharmas are like illusions and dreams. The previous chapter already explained clearly why we are within illusions and dreams.
