Sutra text: 若緊陀羅樂脫其倫。我於彼前現緊陀羅身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược khẩn-đà-la nhạo thoát kỳ luân, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện khẩn-đà-la thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are kinnaras who delight in transcending their kind, I appear before them in a kinnara's body, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are kinnaras who delight in transcending their kind,

"Kinnara" is Sanskrit, translated into Chinese as "doubtful spirits." They are so called because when they manifest in human form, they bear a horn upon their heads. They are also another kind of music spirit that often plays music for the Jade Emperor. I appear before them in a kinnara's body, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Sutra text: 若摩呼羅伽樂脫其倫。我於彼前現摩呼羅伽身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược ma-hô-la-già nhạo thoát kỳ luân, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện ma-hô-la-già thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are mahoragas who delight in transcending their kind, I appear before them in a mahoraga's body, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are mahoragas — "Mahoraga" is Sanskrit, translated into Chinese as "great python spirits," also called earth dragons. The dragons mentioned above can travel through the sky and are called heavenly dragons. These pythons, also called dragons, are confined to the ground. They have no spiritual powers. Mahoragas are also beings of the eight divisions of ghosts and spirits. If mahoragas delight in transcending their kind, I appear before them in a mahoraga's body, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生樂人修人。我現人身而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sinh nhạo nhân tu nhân, ngã hiện nhân thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who delight in the human state and cultivate to be human, I manifest a human body, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who delight in the human state and cultivate to be human. They wish, life after life, always to obtain a human body. Thus Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva says, "I manifest a human body, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to accomplish their wish." He will speak Dharma for these beings to help them accomplish their wish.

Sutra text: 若諸非人有形無形。有想無想樂度其倫。我於彼前皆現其身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư phi nhơn, hữu hình vô hình, hữu tưởng vô tưởng nhạo độ kỳ luân, ngã ư bỉ tiền giai hiện kỳ thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are non-human beings — with form or without form, with thought or without thought — who delight in transcending their kind, I appear before them all in bodies like theirs, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to accomplish their wish.
