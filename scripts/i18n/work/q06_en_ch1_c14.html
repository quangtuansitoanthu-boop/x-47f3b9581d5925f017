Transliteration: Diệu âm Quán Thế Âm Phạm âm hải triều âm Cứu thế tất an ninh Xuất thế hoạch thường trụ.

Translation: Sublime the sound of Guanshiyin — Pure sound, ocean-tide sound — Rescuing the world, all find peace; Transcending the world, attaining permanence.

Commentary: Sublime the sound of Guanshiyin.
Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva is infinitely sublime.
Pure sound, ocean-tide sound.
His pure and clear sound is like the roar of the ocean waves. Rescuing the world, all find peace. He rescues all beings in this world, bringing peace and contentment to all.
Transcending the world, attaining permanence.
He transcends the world and certifies to the ever-abiding enlightened nature. He attains indestructible spiritual powers of ease, and ever-abiding.

Sutra text: 我今啟如來 如觀音所說 譬如人靜居 十方俱擊皷 十處一時聞 此則圓真實。

Transliteration: Ngã kim khải Như Lai Như Quán Âm sở thuyết Thí như nhân tĩnh cư Thập phương câu kích cổ Thập xứ nhất thời văn Thử tắc viên chơn thật.

Translation: I now address the Tathagata: As Avalokiteshvara has described — Suppose a person dwelling in stillness, While in the ten directions drums are beaten; Heard in ten places at a single time — This is perfect truth.

Commentary: I now address the Tathagata.
As Avalokiteshvara has described his cultivation and certification of perfect penetration.
Suppose a person dwelling in stillness,
While in the ten directions drums are beaten,
Heard in ten places at a single time.
In each place of the ten directions drums are beaten at the same time. Though the drum sounds come from ten directions, a person in a quiet room can hear those drum sounds at the same time.
This is perfect truth.
This (hearing) nature is truly perfect.

Sutra text: 目非觀障外 口鼻亦復然 　身以合方知 心念紛無緒。

Transliteration: Mục phi quán chướng ngoại Khẩu tỵ diệc phục nhiên Thân dĩ hợp phương tri Tâm niệm phân vô tự

Translation: The eye cannot see beyond obstructions; Mouth and nose are likewise. The body knows only in union; Mind-thoughts are tangled without order.
