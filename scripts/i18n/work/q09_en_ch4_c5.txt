<p>The demon keeps saying: &#8220;There are great Bodhisattvas, small Bodhisattvas, male Bodhisattvas, female Bodhisattvas.&#8221; In fact &#8211; unless you do not wish to become a Buddha &#8211; if you wish to become a Buddha, you must have a male body; a female body cannot become a Buddha. To become a Bodhisattva you must also be male; a female body cannot become a Bodhisattva.</p>
<p><strong>On hearing this, the cultivator believes, washing away his inherent mind, easily falling into deviant awakening</strong>.</p>
<p>Hearing the demon say such things and thinking: &#8220;He speaks truly &#8211; I always see Guanyin Bodhisattva depicted as a woman; that is conclusive proof.&#8221; Such people do not understand the Buddhadharma: Guanyin Bodhisattva is neither male nor female &#8211; responding according to beings' vows, teaching each according to their faculties. Her responsive manifestations are hard to distinguish &#8211; except by the demon king's spiritual powers, for the demon king also has powers and can transform itself. How can you tell who is a demon? Observe whether they harbor lust.</p>
<p>The original faith utterly vanishes &#8211; the true mind of cultivation originally there now wiped clean&#8230;</p>
<p>Easily falling into deviant understanding.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thử danh mị quỷ, niên lão thành ma, não loạn thị nhân. Yểm túc tâm sanh, khứ bỉ nhân thể. Đệ tử dĩ sư, câu hãm vương nạn.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: This is called a deluding ghost, aged into a demon, harassing this person. Sated, it arises and leaves that person's body. Both disciple and teacher fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>This is called a deluding ghost</strong> &#8211; a demon among the li, mei, wang, liang kinds. <strong>Aged into a demon</strong>. <strong>It harasses the cultivator</strong> cultivating right samadhi. <strong>Sated, it leaves the possessed person's body. Both disciple and teacher fall into legal peril.</strong></p>
<p>When the demon mind has tired of it, at last possessing people no more &#8211; both disciple and teacher fall into legal peril, both arrested and jailed.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đương tiên giác, bất nhập luân hồi. Mê hoặc bất tri, đọa vô gián ngục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: You should be awakened beforehand, and not enter the turning wheel. Deluded and unknowing, one falls into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>You should be awakened at the very outset, lest you enter the demon king's wheel of birth and death</strong> &#8211; <strong>but</strong> lacking wisdom and <strong>deluded, unknowing</strong> what is happening, <strong>one falls into the uninterrupted hell</strong>.</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong><em>4 &#8211; GREED FOR KNOWLEDGE</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu thiện nam tử, thọ ấm hư diệu, bất tao tà lự, viên định phát minh. Tam-ma-địa  trung, tâm ái căn bản, cùng lãm vật hóa, tánh chi chung thủy. Tinh sảng kì tâm, tham cầu biện tích.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed. Within samadhi the mind fond of origins, exhaustively surveying beings' transformations, natures from end to end &#8211; the mind keen and bright, greedily seeking analysis.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed.</strong></p>
<p>Again, in the empty, penetrating, wondrous state after the feeling skandha has dissolved, the cultivator cultivating right samadhi no longer has deviant concerns, dwelling in the bright, perfect samadhi. <strong>Within samadhi the mind fond of knowing the origin of things</strong>: he decides: I shall study the physical origin of the universe.</p>
<p><strong>Hence the cultivator exhaustively surveys the nature of things</strong> and their transformation from root to tip, to find what they ultimately are &#8211; making <strong>thought keen and sharp through greedily seeking analysis of things</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhĩ thời thiên ma, hậu đắc kì tiện, phi tinh phụ nhân, khẩu thuyết kinh pháp.  </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Then the heavenly demon gets its convenient chance, sending its essence to possess another person, using the possessed person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Then the heavenly demon gets its convenient chance</strong> &#8211;</p>
<p>Then the heavenly demon knows and says: &#8220;Aha! You have just given rise to a greedy mind &#8211; splendid! Now I can send my disciple to tempt you.&#8221; Thus it gets its convenient chance &#8211; again the demon king sends one of his followers, <strong>sending its essence to possess another person, using the possessed person's mouth to expound</strong> the demon king's <strong>Dharma</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Kì nhân tiên bất giác tri ma trước, diệc ngôn tự đắc, vô thượng niết bàn.  Lai bỉ cầu nguyên thiện nam tử xứ, phu tòa thuyết pháp. Thân hữu oai thần, tồi phục cầu giả, linh kì tòa hạ, tuy vị văn pháp, tự nhiên tâm phục. Thị chư nhân đẳng, tương Phật niết-bàn bồ-đề pháp thân, tức thị hiện tiền, ngã nhục thân thượng. Phụ phụ tử tử, đệ đại tương sanh, tức thị pháp thân thường trụ bất tuyệt. Đô chỉ hiện tại, tức vi Phật quốc, vô biệt tịnh cư, cập kim sắc tướng.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: That person, first unaware of demonic possession, likewise claims to have attained unsurpassed nirvana. Coming to the place of the good man seeking origins, it spreads its seat and preaches the Dharma. Its body possessed of awesome spirit, subduing the seekers, so that beneath its seat, though not yet hearing the Dharma, they are naturally subdued in mind. It tells these people that the Buddha's nirvana, Bodhi, and Dharma-body are just the present fleshy body of self. Fathers and sons succeeding one another, generation after generation &#8211; this is the ever-abiding, unending Dharma-body. All points to the present: this very place is the Buddha-land; there is no separate Pure Abode, nor any golden-colored form.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>That person, unaware of being demon-possessed, likewise claims to have attained unsurpassed nirvana</strong> &#8211;</p>
<p>This is a case of the demon's stock line: &#8220;Everyone is a Buddha&#8221; &#8211; but a trick the heavenly demon often uses. This person truly does not know he is demon-possessed &#8211; having not a single notion of being possessed by the demon king, because he has grown deluded, all awareness lost when possessed: the demon takes total control, becoming the possessed person's announcer.</p>
<p>It claims to have attained the wondrous fruition of unsurpassed nirvana. When <strong>the demon comes to meet the one seeking to know the origin of things</strong> &#8211; seeking full knowledge of the physical phenomena of the universe &#8211; it <strong>spreads its seat, preaching the Dharma</strong>. <strong>Its</strong> (the demon king's) <strong>body possessed of awesome spirit, subduing the Dharma-seekers</strong> seeking the origin of all things. <strong>So that the assembly beneath its seat, though not yet hearing the Dharma, are naturally subdued in mind</strong> &#8211; their minds already subdued, deeply reverent.</p>
<p><strong>It tells them the Buddha's Bodhi, nirvana, and Dharma-body are just the demon's present fleshy body</strong>: it says: &#8220;What are Bodhi and nirvana? They are right here, in this fleshy body of mine.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>It says fathers and sons succeeding one another, generation after generation, are the ever-abiding, unending Dharma-body</strong>.</p>
