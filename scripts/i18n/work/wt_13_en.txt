TITLE: 14. READING MANY SUTRAS INCREASES WISDOM
TOMTAT: Mrs. Ho suffered heart disease, believing herself vegetarian while still eating shrimp and crab. Meeting Master Miaofa, she learned that "fish, shrimp, and crab are meat," repented, became truly vegetarian, and her heart recovered.
-----
{align:center|**READING MANY SUTRAS INCREASES WISDOM**}

{align:justify|Mrs. Ho, a laywoman, consulted Master Miaofa: she had suffered heart disease for three or four years; though treated in hospital, it was better at times, worse at others. Then someone introduced her, saying: "The Master expounds Buddhist principles wonderfully. If you believe, eat no meat, kill nothing, daily recite the Buddha's name and do good, your heart may recover!"}

{align:justify|So she hastily took refuge in Buddhism — but when would her illness heal?}

{align:justify|The Master smiled and asked her:}

{align:justify|"Are you not vegetarian, then?"}

{align:justify|"I am vegetarian!" she affirmed.}

{align:justify|"Yet I still sense you eating fish, shrimp, and crab! Above all you love crayfish. Not only do you love eating them yourself, you introduce friends to restaurant X and praise it, advertising how deliciously restaurant X cooks. Do you do this or not?"}

{align:justify|Hearing the Master, Mrs. Ho shed tears and asked:}

{align:justify|"Oh! Master — fish, shrimp, crab... are they meat too? I still thought I was vegetarian!"}

{align:justify|The Master said:}

{align:justify|"Fish, shrimp, crab... if not meat, what do you call them? Vegetables? A Buddha's disciple keeps the precepts: eats no meat and no onions, scallions, garlic... Know that 'no meat' includes all living animals of blood and flesh. And fish, shrimp, and crab are animals! They still possess life. You must end meat-eating and eat truly vegetarian — only then will your illness turn for the better."}

{align:justify|"Yes, Master. From now I shall begin anew — I shall eat no animals, including fish, shrimp, and crab," said Mrs. Ho.}

{align:justify|The Master taught further:}

{align:justify|"A Buddha's disciple must not only keep the killing precept himself, end meat-eating for good, drink no liquor, smoke nothing — he should never introduce others to meat-eating places, teach them smoking or drinking parties, still less trade in such things. For selling liquor, tobacco, meat... for others' use and eating is an offense! — just as not smoking oneself yet selling cigarettes to smokers bears the same guilt. Since you introduced others to meat-eating, you must recognize that fault and repent it."}

{align:justify|"Master, I understand clearly. Henceforth not only shall I myself eat vegetarian, I resolve never to urge others to eat meat again."}

{align:justify|Sitting beside the Master, I listened to Mrs. Ho and could not help adding:}

{align:justify|"Do you feel your heart easier?"}

{align:justify|She started a moment, then caught my meaning and declared eagerly:}

{align:justify|"Oh! So very light and easy. It has been so long since I felt this well!"}

{align:justify|One who honors the Buddha and eats vegetarian does so not only to heal illness — most important is to understand the essential truths of the universe and human life.}

{align:justify|You should study: the Heart Sutra, the Kshitigarbha Sutra, the Platform Sutra, and the Emperor Liang Repentance Sutra... Also the Shurangama Sutra — a sutra that opens wisdom, distinguishing the fifty demonic states of the aggregates. Through reading sutras you may examine whether your own conduct accords with the true Dharma. Hence: "Only reading sutras increases wisdom."}

{align:justify|COMMENTARY:}

{align:justify|The story READING MANY SUTRAS INCREASES WISDOM teaches "true vegetarianism." Mrs. Ho thought herself vegetarian while still eating shrimp and crab — and introducing friends to the seafood restaurant: "assisted killing." When Master Miaofa stated plainly that "fish, shrimp, and crab are living animals," she wept and repented. At once her heart grew well. The story also urges reading many sutras (the Heart Sutra, the Kshitigarbha Sutra, the Shurangama Sutra...) to increase wisdom and tell right from wrong. It reminds us: "Vegetarianism means not merely avoiding boned meat, but ending all creatures that possess life." Whoever recites the Buddha's name and would have illness turn for the better, first eat truly vegetarian and read sutras to open wisdom.}
