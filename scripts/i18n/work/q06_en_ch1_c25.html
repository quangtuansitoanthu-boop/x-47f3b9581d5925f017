They perfected enlightenment through the perfect-penetration cultivation arising from the effort of turning the hearing inward to recognize one's own nature.
The Bodhisattvas of the present
Now each enter perfect brightness.
The Bodhisattvas cultivating in this life all walk this same path.
Future students
Should rely on this Dharma.

People in future lives who have not met the Buddhadharma will choose this cultivation. I too was certified through this Dharma. Long ago I perfected this dharma-door and certified to perfect penetration. Not Avalokiteshvara alone.

Sutra text: 誠如佛世尊 詢我諸方便 以救諸末劫 求出世間人 成就涅槃心 觀世音為最。

Transliteration: Thành như Phật Thế tôn Tuân ngã chư phương tiện Dĩ cứu chư mạt kiếp Cầu xuất thế gian nhơn Thành tựu niết-bàn tâm Quán Thế Âm vi tối.

Translation: Truly as the World Honored Buddha Asks me about expedients, To rescue in the final kalpas Those who seek transcendence, To perfect the nirvana mind — Guanshiyin is supreme.

Commentary: Truly as the World Honored Buddha
Asks me about expedients.
O Buddha, I speak the truth; I speak what is truly so. The method to rescue in the final kalpas —
Those who seek transcendence,
To perfect the nirvana mind.
Among the eighteen realms and seven elements, which dharma-door is most perfectly penetrating? Which best suits the potentials of beings in the Dharma-Ending Age? Those who wish to leave the world and seek transcendence — how can they perfect their aspiration to cultivate the Way and certify to nirvana?
Guanshiyin is supreme.
The best method is regarding the sounds of the world.
Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, now manifesting in the Saha realm, best accords with this dharma-door. This dharma-door is most suitable, most fitting for everyone's potentials.

Sutra text: 自餘諸方便 皆是佛威神 即事捨塵勞 非是長修學 淺深同說法。

Transliteration: Tự dư chư phương tiện Giai thị Phật oai thần Tức sự, xả trần lao Phi thị trường tu học Thiển thâm đồng thuyết pháp.

Translation: As for all other expedients, They all rely on the Buddha's awesome spirit; Right within phenomena, abandoning worldly toil. They are not the long-term cultivation and study Equally taught to the shallow and deep.

Commentary: As for all other expedients,
They all rely on the Buddha's awesome spirit.
In cultivating any expedient dharma-doors other than ear-root perfect penetration, we must all rely on the Buddhas' protection to succeed. Without the Buddhas' support, we can accomplish nothing.
Right within phenomena, abandoning worldly toil.
They are not the long-term cultivation and study
Equally taught to the shallow and deep.
