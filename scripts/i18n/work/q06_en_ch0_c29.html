Transliteration: Thế tôn, ngã nhất hiệu danh dữ bỉ chúng đa danh hiệu vô dị, do ngã tu tập đắc chơn viên thông.

Translation: World Honored One, my single name is no different from those many names, because through cultivation I attained true perfect penetration.

Commentary: World Honored One, my single name — the name Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva — is no different from those many names, meaning the Bodhisattvas as numerous as the sands of sixty-two Ganges Rivers. How is this so? How can the merit of one name equal that of so many names? It is because through cultivation I attained true perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 是名十四施無畏力,福備眾生.

Transliteration: Thị danh thập tứ thí vô uý lực, phước bị chúng sanh.

Translation: These are called the fourteen powers of bestowing fearlessness, with which I fully bless all beings.

Commentary: These are called the fourteen powers of bestowing fearlessness, with which I fully bless all beings.

I come to help them; whenever anyone calls for rescue, I at once rescue them.

Sutra text: 世尊我又獲是圓通修證無上道故,又能善獲四不思議無作妙德。一者由我初獲妙妙聞心,心精遺聞。見聞覺知不能分隔,成一圓融清淨寶覺。故我能現眾多妙容。能說無邊祕密神呪。

Transliteration: Thế tôn, Ngã hựu hoạch thị viên thông tu chứng vô thượng đạo cố, hựu năng thiện hoạch tứ bất tư nghì vô tác diệu đức. Nhất giả do ngã sơ hoạch diệu diệu văn tâm, tâm tinh di văn. Kiến văn giác tri bất năng phân cách, thành nhất viên dung thanh tịnh bảo giác. Cố ngã năng hiện chúng đa diệu dung. Năng thuyết vô biên bí mật thần chú.

Translation: World Honored One! Further, having attained this perfect penetration to cultivate and certify to the unsurpassed Way, I have also well attained four inconceivable, non-doing, wondrous virtues: First, when I first attained the sublime hearing-mind, the essence of mind abandoned hearing. Seeing, hearing, perceiving, and knowing could no longer be separated, merging into one perfect, pure, precious enlightenment. Thus I can manifest many wonderful forms and speak countless secret spirit-mantras.

Commentary: World Honored One! Further, having attained this perfect penetration to cultivate and certify to the unsurpassed Way, I have also well attained four inconceivable, non-doing, wondrous virtues:

What are these four inconceivable merits?

First, when I first attained the sublime hearing-mind, the essence of mind abandoned hearing.

At first, when I certified through the effort of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature — the sublime within the sublime — my mind became utterly refined and all marks of the hearer dissolved. The true mind manifested, and I reached the state of refined subtlety. When the marks of the hearer had dissolved, seeing, hearing, perceiving, and knowing could no longer be separated. The other sense roots all became perfectly interfused and were no longer divided. I perfected the precious enlightenment, perfectly interfused and pure. This means he certified to oneness; there is no more distinction between subject and object.

Thus I can manifest many wonderful forms and speak countless secret spirit-mantras.
