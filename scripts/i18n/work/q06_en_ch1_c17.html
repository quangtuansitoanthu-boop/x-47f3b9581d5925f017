They do not recognize their inherent hearing-nature. Following sounds, thus they turn.
They do not know to turn the hearing back to recognize their own nature. They delight in seeking outside; they listen outwardly. They chase after sound-objects and seek outwardly. If one lets the ear root chase after sound-objects instead of turning the hearing back inward, one will fall into the flow of birth and death. That is also a knot. With birth and death, there are knots. It begins because you chase after sound-objects. You cannot pull it back.
Ananda, though of strong memory. Ananda is most intelligent and of strong memory. He could memorize by heart the sutras of the Tripitaka. Yet he could not avoid falling into deviant thought.

Though intelligent, Ananda still could not escape the calamity with Matanga's daughter and the Brahma-heaven mantra. Ananda was captured by the outsiders' deviant magic.
Is it not through what engulfs one
That, turning the flow, one attains freedom from falseness?

If you can return the hearing to hear the self-nature, then naturally you will have no falseness to resist. You will no longer make such mistakes. The reason Ananda was so easily caught by deviant magic is that Ananda was always seeking outside. Ananda studied this, then learned that, and mastered this sutra, then that sutra, but never turned the light back to shine within. Ananda never stopped to consider why he thought and acted as he did. All Ananda did was study. He studied so much he forgot what he himself was. This reminds me of Confucius's answer when someone asked:
"Is there anyone in the world like..." the questioner began.
"Like what?" Confucius asked.
"It goes, taking everyone along with it — taking along family relatives, except its own wife. It forgets to take its wife along. Is there such a kind of person?"
Can you guess how Confucius answered? Did Confucius answer that forgetting to take one's wife along is impossible? Or did he answer that forgetting to take one's wife along is possible for people?
Confucius answered: "Nowadays, people even forget themselves!" Not only do they forget their wives, they forget their own bodies. That is, they do not know that in the future their bodies will die. They forget that they will pass through old age, sickness, and death.

Because Ananda focused on studying things outside himself and on developing his scholarly ability, Ananda neglected cultivating to develop samadhi power. Because Ananda neglected samadhi power, he did not know how to apply the effort of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature; so Ananda chased after sound-objects. Ananda did not return sound-objects to his own nature, so Ananda fell into calamity with Matanga's daughter.

Sutra text: 阿難汝諦聽 我承佛威力 　宣說金剛王 如幻不思議 　佛母真三昧。

Transliteration: A-nan nhữ đế thính Ngã thừa Phật oai lực Tuyên thuyết Kim cang vương Như huyễn bất tư nghị Phật mẫu chơn tam muội.

Translation: Ananda, listen attentively! Relying on the Buddha's awesome power, I proclaim the Vajra King's samadhi — Illusory, inconceivable, the true samadhi that is mother of Buddhas.
