TITLE: 01. BACK IN MY ARMY DAYS
TOMTAT: Back then I joined Survey Unit 5802. At the time the deputy regiment commander, Wei Qingping, held a stack of money and told me to answer at once how much he was holding. I said: Fourteen stacks! Even he himself...
-----
{align:justify|Back then I joined Survey Unit 5802. At the time the deputy regiment commander, Wei Qingping, held a stack of money and told me to answer at once how much he was holding.}
{align:justify|I said: Fourteen stacks!}
{align:justify|He himself did not even know exactly how much he was holding, so hearing my answer he counted it—and his eyes bulged in amazement, for the result matched exactly. This softened his atheist mind, and from then on he took refuge in Buddhism, resolving to recite the Buddha's name and chant sutras.}
{align:justify|There was a man of blunt, forceful speech (I call him "Iron Mouth"), who used to mock and laugh whenever he saw people believing my predictions. One day he came to me, chattered on about heaven and earth at length, then asked:}
{align:justify|"Can you guess what I did last night?"}
{align:justify|I answered: Played mahjong!}
{align:justify|This "Iron Mouth" froze for a moment, hardly daring to believe it—"how could it be so right?" He said:}
{align:justify|"Right, I did play mahjong! But can you guess how much I lost or won?"}
{align:justify|This was a great challenge, with everyone staring fixedly at me.}
{align:justify|I answered: You lost eight hundred and eighty coins!}
{align:justify|Iron Mouth shouted:}
{align:justify|"Right! Right! Right! Truly a godlike guess! Unbelievable… How can such a thing exist in this world?"}
{align:justify|Then Iron Mouth explained:}
{align:justify|"Originally I had lost only eight hundred. Having lost, I was about to leave when the friend beside me offered to lend me 80. I thought: 'Borrow these 80—maybe I can win it back!'… In the end I lost that too, exactly 880. That's how it happened!"}
