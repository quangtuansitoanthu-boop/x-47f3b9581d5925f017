Commentary: Groves, trees, ponds, and marshes all proclaimed the sounds of Dharma. Flowing water, sighing wind, and rustling leaves were all displays of Dharma sounds. The interweaving lights formed like a jeweled gauze net. The light of the Buddhas of the ten directions poured upon Shakyamuni Buddha's crown, and the light from Shakyamuni Buddha issued forth again, pouring upon the crowns of the Tathagatas as numerous as dust motes in the ten directions; the pattern of those interweaving rays formed a most splendid net. The great assembly obtained what they had never had; all universally attained the Vajra samadhi door. They had never before seen such auspicious happenings, which caused everyone to attain the Vajra samadhi door.

Sutra text: 即時天雨百寶蓮華,青黃赤白間錯紛糅。十方虛空成七寶色。

Transliteration: Tức thời thiên vũ bá bảo liên hoa, thanh hoàng xích bạch gián thố phân nhữu. Thập phương hư không thành thất bảo sắc.

Translation: At once there rained from the heavens hundred-jeweled lotus blossoms, blue, yellow, red, and white intermingled in profusion. The empty space of the ten directions took on the colors of the seven precious things.

Commentary: At once — when everyone in the assembly attained the Vajra samadhi — there rained from the heavens hundred-jeweled lotus blossoms, blue, yellow, red, and white intermingled in profusion. These four colors appeared in various combinations upon the lotus petals. Blossoms of blue lotus stood out beside yellow hues. Blossoms of yellow lotus bore red specks within their petals. Blossoms of white lotus glistened with tints of pink. The empty space of the ten directions took on the colors of the seven precious things. The seven precious things are gold, silver, vaidurya, crystal, mother-of-pearl, red pearl, and carnelian. The whole world turned into these colors.

Sutra text: 此娑婆界大地山河俱時不現。唯見十方微塵國土合成一界。梵唄詠歌自然數奏

Transliteration: Thử ta-bà giới đại địa sơn hà câu thời bất hiện. Duy kiến thập phương vi trần quốc độ hợp thành nhất giới. Phạm bái vịnh ca tự nhiên sổ tấu.

Translation: The mountains, rivers, and great earth of this Saha world all vanished from sight at once. Only the lands as numerous as dust motes in the ten directions were seen, merged into a single realm. Sanskrit hymns and songs resounded of themselves.

Commentary: The mountains, rivers, and great earth of this Saha world all vanished from sight at once. Only the lands as numerous as dust motes in the ten directions were seen, merged into a single realm.

The world we dwell in is called Saha. That name means "endurance." It symbolizes that beings must endure suffering in this world. Indeed, it is most hard to bear, yet everyone must directly undergo it, not merely acknowledge suffering. They take suffering for joy. They think it is truly fine here.

Now, however, the Saha realm vanished all at once. That was through Shakyamuni Buddha's spiritual power, displayed for the assembly to see. Buddha-lands as countless as dust motes merged into one. Sanskrit hymns and songs resounded of themselves.

Pure Sanskrit sounds, hymns of praise, resounded everywhere. Before, groves, trees, rivers, and lakes had proclaimed Dharma sounds, but here, the myriad things all proclaimed Dharma sounds in most pure and subtle hymns and songs of praise.

Flowing waters and sighing winds all proclaim the Great Vehicle.
