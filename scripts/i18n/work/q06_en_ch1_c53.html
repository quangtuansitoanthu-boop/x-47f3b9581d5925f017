After the brahman became a Buddha, he had to take his five hundred bhikshu disciples to another country for the summer retreat. Outwardly, that country's king warmly welcomed him, but the king made no offerings to the Sangha for the retreat. Finally, a horse-trader in the country learned that the Buddha and the bhikshu Sangha had received no food offerings, so he shared with the Sangha the food used to feed horses. Though the brahman had become Shakyamuni Buddha, and his five hundred young disciples had become five hundred Arhats, the Buddha still had to repay past-life old karma through ninety days of eating horse-feed. So the Buddha says that if people do not perform such body-renouncing deeds, then in the future they must repay the karma created in past lives, as they caused it.

Sutra text: 汝教世人修三摩地,後斷偷盜。是名如來先佛世尊第三決定清淨明誨。

Transliteration: Nhữ giáo thế nhơn tu tam-ma-địa, hậu đoạn thâu đạo. Thị danh Như Lai tiên Phật Thế tôn đệ tam quyết định thanh tịnh minh hối.

Translation: Teach people of the world to cultivate samadhi by further cutting off stealing. This is called the Tathagatas, the former Buddhas, the World Honored Ones' third decisive, pure, clear instruction.

Commentary: Teach people of the world to cultivate samadhi by further cutting off stealing. When they wish to cultivate the Way, they must rid themselves of the stealing mind. "This is called the Tathagatas, the former Buddhas, the World Honored Ones' third decisive, pure, clear instruction."

Sutra text: 是故阿難若不斷偷修禪定者。譬如有人水灌漏巵 欲求其滿。縱經塵劫終無平復。

Transliteration: Thị cố A-nan, nhược bất đoạn thâu tu thiền định giả, thí như hữu nhân thuỷ quán lậu chi, dục cầu kỳ mãn. Túng kinh trần kiếp chung vô bình phục.

Translation: Therefore, Ananda, cultivating dhyana without cutting off stealing is like a person pouring water into a leaking vessel, hoping to fill it. Though passing dust-kalpas, in the end it can never be filled.

Commentary: Therefore, Ananda, cultivating dhyana without cutting off stealing is like a person pouring water into a leaking vessel, hoping to fill it. Though you try to pour water full into a bowl with a hole in the bottom, then though passing dust-kalpas in number, in the end it can never be filled.

Sutra text: 若諸比丘衣鉢之餘分寸不畜。乞食餘分施餓眾生。於大集會合掌禮眾。有人捶 罵同於稱讚。必使身心二俱捐捨。身肉骨血與眾生共。不將如來不了義說。迴為已解以誤初學。佛印是人得真三昧。

Transliteration: Nhược chư tỷ-khưu y bát chi dư, phân thốn bất súc. Khất thực dư phần thí ngạ chúng sanh. Ư đại tập hội, hợp chưởng lễ chúng. Hữu nhơn chuỷ mạ, đồng ư xưng tán. Tất sử thân tâm nhị câu quyên xả. Thân nhục cốt huyết, dữ chúng sanh cộng. Bất tương Như Lai bất liễu nghĩa thuyết, hồi vi dĩ giải dĩ ngộ sơ học. Phật ấn thị nhơn đắc chơn tam-muội.

Translation: If bhikshus, beyond robes and bowl, store not an inch; give alms of begged food's remainder to hungry beings; at great assemblies join palms bowing to the assembly; when beaten or reviled, take it as praise; cause body and mind both to be renounced; share body, flesh, bones, and blood with beings; do not take the Tathagata's provisional teachings as their own understanding to mislead beginners — the Buddha certifies such a person attains true samadhi.
