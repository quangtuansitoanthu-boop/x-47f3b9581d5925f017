THE FOUR CLEAR AND UNALTERABLE EXPOSITIONS
1. ONE MUST END LUST

Sutra text: 阿難,云何攝心我名為戒。若諸世界六道眾生其心不婬。則不隨其生死相續。

Transliteration: A-nan, vân hà nhiếp tâm, ngã danh vi giới? Nhược chư thế giới lục đạo chúng sanh kỳ tâm bất dâm, tắc bất tuỳ kỳ sanh tử tương tục.

Translation: Ananda, why do I call gathering the mind precepts? If beings of the six paths in all worlds had no lustful minds, they would not follow the continuity of birth and death.

Commentary: Ananda, why do I call gathering the mind precepts? The Tathagata will now tell you. If beings of the six paths in all worlds had no lustful minds. The six paths — the realms of gods, humans, asuras, animals, hungry ghosts, and beings in the hells. If those beings had no lustful thoughts, then they would not follow the continuity of birth and death. They would cut off birth and death.

Sutra text: 汝修三昧,本出塵勞。婬心不除,塵不可出。

Transliteration: Nhữ tu tam-muội, bổn xuất trần lao. Dâm tâm bất trừ trần bất khả xuất.

Translation: In cultivating samadhi, your fundamental aim is to transcend worldly toil. If the lustful mind is not removed, one cannot transcend the dust.

Commentary: The Buddha says to Ananda: In cultivating samadhi, your fundamental aim is to transcend worldly toil. You wish to transcend birth and death. If the lustful mind is not removed, one cannot transcend worldly toil. If you do not cut off lustful desire, you can never transcend the toil and affliction of the world. For lustful thought itself is defilement. It is itself worldly toil and affliction. Not to speak of engaging in lustful acts — the mere presence of this idea is already defilement. If you do not abandon the lustful mind, there is utterly no reason to hope for enlightenment and Buddhahood.

Keeping the lustful mind on the one hand and hoping to perfect enlightenment on the other is the most stupid thinking. Some think that method is hard to teach. Even if Shakyamuni Buddha himself manifested in the world right now, he would have no way to bring such people to enlightenment. Such people are of the dullest kind.

Sutra text: 縱有多智禪定現前,如不斷婬.必落魔道。上品魔王,中品魔民,下品魔女。

Transliteration: Túng hữu đa trí thiền định hiện tiền, như bất đoạn dâm, tất lạc ma đạo. Thượng phẩm ma vương, trung phẩm ma dân, hạ phẩm ma nữ.

Translation: Though one has much wisdom and samadhi manifests, if lust is not cut off, one will surely fall into the demonic paths. The highest grade becomes demon kings; the middle grade becomes demon people; the lowest grade becomes demon women.

Commentary: Though one has much wisdom and samadhi manifests, if lust is not cut off, one will surely fall into the demonic paths.

You may have intelligence, and when you sit in meditation you may feel light and at ease and feel most free — meaning you can enter meditative concentration. So do you suppose you have wisdom? If you do not cut off the lustful mind, in the end you will become a demon.
