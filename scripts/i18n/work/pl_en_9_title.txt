Vien Hoang Dao
---TOMTAT---
Layman Vien Hoang Dao of the Ming dynasty, author of the Joint Treatise on the West, who with his brothers turned from Zen to the Pure Land, and whose younger brother Trung Dao (Tieu Tu) journeyed in spirit to the Pure Land and recorded the celebrated Journey to the Pure Land, with the rebirth of the boy Vien Dang.
