TITLE: 02. OLD MASTER TRAN
TOMTAT: I have a female colleague named Tran. At a gathering, she told us that her father, Old Master Tran, had a peculiar fondness: he loved eating fish eyes. Whenever fish was served, he would always gouge out the eyes...
-----
{align:justify|A female colleague of mine named Tran once told us at a gathering that her father, Old Master Tran, had a peculiar fondness: he loved eating fish eyes. Whenever fish was served at a meal, he would always gouge out the eyes to eat, praising them as delicious, and he even instructed the family to buy plenty of fish—above all, the fish had to be alive, for only then did he find them truly tasty.}
{align:justify|Later, her father suddenly developed an inflammation in one eye. The condition broke out and worsened gravely, leaving many renowned doctors helpless. Soon that eye went completely blind, and the doctors removed it, replacing it with a glass eye.}
{align:justify|The following year, his remaining eye fell ill as well; before long he lost his sight entirely, and now he must live out his days unable to see the light.}
