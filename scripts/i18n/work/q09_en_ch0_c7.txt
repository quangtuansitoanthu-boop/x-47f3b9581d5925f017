<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda! These Four Stations of Emptiness&#8221; are:<br />
1. The Station of Emptiness<br />
2. The Station of Consciousness..<br />
3. The Station of Nothing Whatsoever<br />
4. The Station of Neither Thought nor Non-Thought.</p>
<p>This is &#8221;body and mind utterly extinguished, the nature of samadhi manifesting, with no karmically-requited form.&#8221; They no longer have a body, and they no longer use the mind but the unmoving consciousness. The nature of samadhi power manifests. They have escaped the Realm of Form and no longer pass through the heavens of the Realm of Form. They are beings of the &#8220;Formless Realm.&#8221; This is the last of the three realms; the four stations above are the heavens of the Formless Realm &#8211; the highest heavens of all.</p>
<p><strong>Thử giai bất liễu, diệu giác minh tâm. Tích vọng phát sanh, vọng hữu tam giới. Trung gian vọng tùy, thất thú trầm nịch. Bổ-đặc-già-la, các tùng kỳ loại.</strong></p>
<p><strong>All of this comes from not understanding the wondrous enlightened bright mind: accumulating falsehood, there falsely arise the three realms; in between, falsely following, the seven destinies sink and drown. Pudgala beings, each following its own kind.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: The beings of the three realms &#8220;all come from not understanding the wondrous enlightened bright mind.&#8221; They lack sufficient wisdom to penetrate and reach awakening. &#8220;Accumulating falsehood, clinging to the intermediate body, reborn according to kind, there falsely arise the three realms.&#8221; From a mind full of false thoughts, error appears; ignorance is born within the nature of truth, and the entire three realms are created by the ignorance of beings. Thus &#8220;in between, falsely following, the seven destinies sink and drown.&#8221; We usually speak of the six paths of rebirth, but here the sutra mentions seven, because the path of the Formless Realm is included. The seven are:<br />
Gods, asuras, immortals, humans, animals, hungry ghosts, and hells.</p>
<p>They are reborn up and down &#8211; suddenly reborn in the heavens, then fallen into the hells; passing from the human realm to the realm of hungry ghosts. That is &#8220;sinking and drowning.&#8221; &#8220;Pudgala beings, transmigrating according to karma&#8221; &#8211; &#8220;pudgala&#8221; is Sanskrit for sentient beings; &#8220;transmigrating according to karma&#8221; means beings transmigrate according to the karmic retribution of what they have done. If they are immortals, they follow their own kind; if of good fruition, they enter the heavens and gather with the other gods; if born as asuras, they gather with the other asuras &#8211; and so with all other beings.</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong>ASURAS</strong></p>

<p style="text-align:center">* * *</p>
<p><strong>Phục thứ A-nan, thị tam giới trung, phục hữu tứ chủng, A-tu-la loại.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Further, Ananda! Within the three realms there are four more kinds of asuras:</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Asura&#8221; is Sanskrit, sometimes translated as &#8220;not gods&#8221; &#8211; because some asuras have heavenly blessings but not the virtue of the gods. Another translation is &#8220;not upright in appearance,&#8221; though this holds only for the males; the females are extremely beautiful. Asuras are found in four destinies: womb-born, egg-born, moisture-born, and transformation-born. Each kind will now be discussed.</p>


<ol>
<li><strong>Nhược ư quỷ đạo, dĩ hộ pháp lực, thành thông nhập không. Thử A-tu-la, tùng noãn nhi sanh. Quỷ thú sở nhiếp.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>If, from the ghost realm, by the power of protecting the Dharma, one penetrates emptiness with spiritual powers, such an asura is egg-born, belonging to the ghost destiny.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: Some ghosts are good ghosts who act as Dharma protectors &#8211; General Guan Gong is such an example: a very great and awesome ghost. Such ghosts protect and uphold the Three Jewels, using spiritual powers to enter emptiness. Such asuras are &#8220;egg-born, belonging to the ghost destiny.&#8221;</p>


<ol start="2">
<li><strong>Nhược ư Thiên trung, hàng đức biếm trụy. Kỳ sở bốc cư, lân ư nhật nguyệt. Thử A-tu-la, tùng thai nhi xuất. Nhân thú sở nhiếp.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>If, from the heavens, one falls through loss of virtue, dwelling near the sun and moon, such an asura is womb-born, belonging to the human destiny.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;If, from the heavens, one falls through loss of virtue, dwelling near the sun and moon.&#8221; They start out in the heavens but lack the virtue to remain there; they fall and are cast out into empty space, dwelling in places near the sun and moon. Such &#8220;asuras are womb-born, belonging to the human destiny.&#8221;</p>


<ol start="3">
<li><strong>Hữu tu la Vương, chấp trì thế giới, lực đỗng vô úy. Năng dữ Phạm Vương, cập Thiên đế thích, tứ thiên tranh quyền. Thử A-tu-la, nhân biến hóa hữu, thiên thú sở nhiếp.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>There are asura kings who rule over all asuras in the world, fearless in power, able to contend for power with the Brahma Kings, Shakra, and the Four Heavenly Kings; such asuras are transformation-born, belonging to the heavenly destiny.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: There is another kind: the &#8220;asura kings who rule over all asuras in the world, fearless in power.&#8221; Their strength is truly immense &#8211; they can shake the whole world. If they grasped the peak of Mount Sumeru with their hands and pushed, they could topple the mountain. That is the strength they have. With spiritual powers they think they &#8220;can contend for power with the Brahma Kings, Shakra, and the Four Heavenly Kings,&#8221; using spiritual powers to contend.</p>
<p>The asura king had a daughter who became the reason for his war with Shakra. She was a beautiful celestial maiden descended to earth, and when Shakra caught sight of her, he went to the asura king to ask for her hand; the asura king agreed. She turned out to be jealous and selfish. Shakra liked listening to the Buddhadharma and often went to hear Dharma masters lecture. She noticed Shakra's frequent absences and grew jealous, thinking: &#8220;He married me but loves me no more &#8211; I am sure he goes out philandering.&#8221; With her jealous heart she secretly followed Shakra. One day she learned where Shakra usually went; when Shakra arrived at the lecture hall and took his seat, she saw a great many celestial maidens in the hall. The sight inflamed her jealousy further, and on the spot she revealed herself. When Shakra saw her, he asked: &#8220;What are you doing here?&#8221;</p>
<p>She answered in a jealous voice: &#8220;You come here to meet other women and do not want me to come, do you?&#8221;</p>
<p>Shakra slapped her, and she began wailing and ran home to the asura king to tattle: &#8220;Lord Shakra has broken the marriage vows and is always chasing other women.&#8221; She tattled: &#8220;When I followed him, he hit me.&#8221;</p>
<p>Of course, when the asura king heard this, he was furious. &#8220;We shall go to war! We shall overthrow him and seize the throne!&#8221; He commanded all the asura soldiers and called the generals to join the battle against Shakra. And what do you suppose happened?</p>
<p>Shakra's heavenly troops and the Four Great Heavenly Kings could not withstand the asura army; the palace was besieged and cut off. At last Shakra went to beg the Buddha for help. &#8220;What should I do?&#8221; he asked. &#8220;I cannot defeat the asura troops.&#8221;</p>
