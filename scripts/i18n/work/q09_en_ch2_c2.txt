<p><strong>Translation: Further, with this mind investigating thoroughly within and without &#8211; then the hun and po souls, the will, and the spirit, apart from the body that grasps and receives, all interfuse, alternately acting as host and guest. Suddenly in empty space one hears voices preaching the Dharma, or hears the ten directions all proclaiming profound and secret meanings. This is called the essences of spirit and soul alternately separating and uniting to accomplish good seeds &#8211; a temporary state, not sage realization. If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a good state; if you interpret it as sagehood, you fall among the deviant.</strong></p>
<p>Commentary: <strong>Further, if one uses</strong> <strong>this mind to investigate thoroughly within and without</strong> &#8211; investigating ever more deeply without cease, striving within and without to reach the summit, ceaselessly striving to refine that process &#8211; then the <strong>hun and po souls</strong> undergo a change. There are three hun and seven po souls in the human body; I have mentioned these ten siblings before. Some of these ten souls have only ears or eyes; some have only a nose but no lips, eyes, or ears. Each governs only one sense, lacking the other five, so they cannot function separately and independently &#8211; they must cooperate with one another like a team: those with ears help those without ears, those with eyes help those without eyes, supporting one another. (5)</p>
<p>These ten hun and po souls, together with the <strong>will and spirit</strong>, <strong>interfuse, alternately acting as host and guest</strong> &#8211; changing roles, mutually helping one another, alternately playing host and guest.</p>
<p><strong>Suddenly in empty space one hears voices preaching the Dharma</strong>: someone is preaching the Dharma in empty space. Who? You hear the voice but see no form. In fact it is only your own will and spirit alternately playing host and guest to preach the Dharma. <strong>Or one hears the ten directions all proclaiming profound and secret meanings</strong>: you can hear Dharma preaching from mid-air. Why? Because of your cultivation effort in past lives &#8211; you once heard a great many sutras and Dharmas preached, so they can never fade from your spirit, will, and thought. Hence in this life, when your effort reaches its peak, those past experiences well up.</p>
<p><strong>This state is called: the essences alternately separating and uniting to plant good seeds together.</strong></p>
<p>They cooperate, forming a single thread, grouping together and separating again. They can preach the Buddhadharma so you understand what you never understood before &#8211; thus you can plant good seeds for later lives.</p>
<p><strong>It is only a temporary state</strong>, not permanent, <strong>not sage realization</strong>. Do not give rise to the idea that this state is wondrous and say: &#8220;Look at me &#8211; I need not attend any Dharma assembly; I can hear the Dharma preached in space whenever I wish.&#8221; You may hear the Dharma preached, but that does not mean you have realized sagehood.</p>
<p><strong>If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a good state</strong> &#8211; if you do not become self-satisfied, do not deem yourself extraordinary, and do not wish to deceive people, it is a good state. Suppose you say: &#8220;Oh, I can hear the Dharma even when no one preaches it. Who else can do that? No one! Yet it comes to me.&#8221;</p>
<p>But if you advertise and boast of that state hoping people will believe you, what will happen?</p>
<p><strong>If you think you have realized sagehood, you fall into deviant views.</strong></p>

<li><strong> THE BUDDHA APPEARING IN PERSON</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong> Sutra text: Hựu dĩ thử tâm, trừng lộ hiệu triệt, nội quang phát minh. Thập phương biến tác, diêm-phù-đàn sắc. Nhất thiết chủng loại, hóa vi Như Lai. Ư  thời hốt nhiên, kiến Tì-lô giá-na, cư thiên quang đài, thiên Phật vi nhiễu, bách ức quốc độ, cập dữ liên hoa câu thời xuất hiện. Thử danh tâm hồn, linh ngộ sở nhiễm, tâm quang nghiên minh, chiếu chư thế giới. Tạm đắc như thị, phi vi thánh chứng. Bất tác thánh tâm, danh thiện cảnh giới. Nhược tác thánh giải, tức thọ quần tà.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Again with this mind, clarified and thoroughly penetrating, inner light shines forth brightly. The ten directions all turn the color of jambu gold. Every kind of being is transformed into a Tathagata. Then suddenly one sees Vairocana Buddha seated on a heavenly light platform, a thousand Buddhas surrounding him, a hundred lakhs of lands, together with lotuses, all appearing at once. This is called the mind-soul influenced by spiritual awakening: investigating the mind's light to its source, it illuminates all worlds. Temporarily gained thus, it is not sage realization. If you do not take it as sagehood, it is a good state; if you interpret it as sagehood, you fall among the deviant.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Again with this mind clarified and thoroughly penetrating, inner light shines forth brightly</strong> &#8211; when the mind is still, pure, manifest, and supremely clear, light from within the mind shines everywhere: <strong>the ten directions all turn the color of jambu gold</strong> &#8211; radiant light shining from within the mind covers the lands of the ten directions in the hue of jambu gold. <strong>Every kind of being</strong> (womb-born, moisture-born, transformation-born, egg-born) and all other kinds of beings <strong>are transformed into Tathagatas</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Then suddenly one sees Vairocana Buddha.</strong> Vairocana means &#8220;pervading everywhere&#8221; &#8211; an epithet of the Buddha: he does not dwell in any one place alone, so there is nowhere he is not; his body pervades everywhere. This person suddenly sees Vairocana Buddha, for the Buddha's body pervades everywhere.</p>
<p><strong>Seated on a heavenly light platform</strong> made of heavenly light, manifesting a body a thousand fathoms tall; Vairocana Buddha is <strong>surrounded by a thousand Buddhas, together with a hundred lakhs of lands and lotuses</strong> &#8211; blue, yellow, red, white &#8211; <strong>all appearing at once</strong>.</p>
<p>This state is called: &#8220;<strong><em>the mind-soul influenced by a kind of spiritual awakening</em></strong> &#8211; do not take this state to be real. Your mind-consciousness is affected by a kind of spiritual sense of awakening, and so it is not real &#8211; for <strong><em>investigating the mind's light to its source, it illuminates all lands.</em></strong></p>
<p>When your mind is bright, it will illumine all worlds everywhere.</p>
<p>It is only a temporary state, not sage realization: what is happening is no permanent mansion. You have not realized the sage fruition. If you do not take it as sagehood, it is called a good state; but if you deem yourself to have realized sagehood, you fall into deviant views.</p>
<p>If you say: &#8220;How marvelous! I just saw Vairocana Buddha. Did you see him? Your skill is not as deep as mine &#8211; I truly attained that.&#8221; Once you give rise to such a thought, the demon kings come to drag you into the hells.</p>
