TITLE: 04. THE LIEUTENANT GENERAL

-----
{align:justify|Once, Lieutenant General Lu Co came to see me and said:}
{align:justify|"Master Lien, I hear your predictions are brilliant, so today I have come to ask your advice… I recall that my parents once consulted Master Diep (also a king of diviners, famed for never-erring prophecy), asking him to read my fortune.}
{align:justify|Mr. Diep said that at eighteen I would place first nationwide, then in the army be selected for the Research Institute; at twenty-seven I would earn a doctorate, then spend three years in America earning another doctorate. At fifty-three I would rise in rank to Full General.}
{align:justify|Lu Co went on:}
{align:justify|"This Mr. Diep is unlike other masters: whoever wants him to read their fortune must pay a very high price and queue up with an appointment—walk in casually and he won't see you at all! And he won't read for anyone of small destiny either! Mr. Diep read mine with utter accuracy! True enough, at eighteen I took first place nationwide; as for his prediction that at twenty-seven I would earn my doctorate, he was off by a little (for it was two years later—only at twenty-nine—that I earned it). But his prophecy of my three years in America earning another doctorate was spot on! Yet he said that at fifty-three I would rise to Full General, and there he was wrong! For now I am fifty-six and still a Lieutenant General, nowhere near Full General. So I would ask Master Lien to see for me: why have I still not been promoted to Full General?"}
{align:justify|I used divination to read for Lu Co. I formed the mudra, finished reciting the mantra, and the God of Fortune appeared like a tiny star, growing and radiating brilliant light… I then asked about Mr. Lu's whole life. The God of Fortune answered exactly as Master Diep had predicted.}
{align:justify|I wondered:}
{align:justify|"But why was Mr. Lu two years late in earning his doctorate?"}
{align:justify|The God of Fortune explained:}
{align:justify|"Rightly, Mr. Lu could have graduated as predicted, but in his youth, after a drinking bout, he followed his friends to a brothel for amusement; egged on by them, and wanting to prove himself bold and dashing, he slept with a prostitute. Because of this fault his career was delayed by two years!"}
{align:justify|I asked:}
{align:justify|"Why should that be?"}
{align:justify|The God of Fortune said:}
{align:justify|"Never visit brothel prostitutes, never linger at the gates of painted beauties." A gentleman always cherishes his own person and guards his virtue bright as jade, and so dreads and shuns such filthy affairs! Through ignorance, Mr. Lu willingly sank, staining his own character and tainting his life. Because of this wrongful way of living he ruined his fortune and harmed his body; reckless indulgence not only infected him with gonorrhea but invited great calamity…}
{align:justify|The God of Fortune further explained:}
{align:justify|"A two-year delay in the degree is but a light punishment. The venereal poison he caught would surely kill him. Doctorate upon doctorate earned… yet hereafter he will become a doctor… of death!"}
{align:justify|My tongue shrank—I was speechless. Then I asked the God of Fortune:}
{align:justify|"Rightly, at fifty-three Lu Co should have risen to Full General—so why, at fifty-six, is he still a Lieutenant General? What blocks his promotion?"}
{align:justify|The God of Fortune silently wrote two characters: MAC THU.}
{align:justify|Bewildered, I asked:}
{align:justify|"Mac Thu?—What does that mean?"}
{align:justify|The God of Fortune answered:}
{align:justify|"It is a person's name!"}
{align:justify|"And what has this person to do with Mr. Lu?"}
{align:justify|"Everything!"}
{align:justify|The God of Fortune said:}
{align:justify|"Lu Co's destiny is excellent—he is a rare genius, excelling in both letters and arms. But alas, in youth his fault of sleeping with a prostitute delayed his career by two years—and that is only a light punishment! After middle age, unrepentant and unreformed, he again gave rise to wicked thoughts, craving male beauty: he fell into homosexual love with a man named Mac Thu. This man is handsome and talented, dashing and refined—his subordinate! Lu Co has lived with Mac Thu for eight years now. With this offense, keeping his Lieutenant General's rank is already lucky—how could he dream of Full General? He only knows how to crave rank for himself, utterly blind to the grave karma he is creating! Know that one rich in blessings who commits sexual misconduct loses power and rank, while one of scant blessings, with no rank left to lose, loses years of life…"}
{align:justify|I asked:}
{align:justify|"Then… what lies ahead for Lu Co?"}
{align:justify|"It will fall upon his descendants!"}
{align:justify|"What does that mean?"}
{align:justify|"Childlessness, early death!" the God of Fortune answered.}
{align:justify|"But each bears his own deeds—why must his descendants carry it?"}
{align:justify|"This is collective karma drawing them together. A father who stores up blessings also enriches himself and his children!"}
{align:justify|Hearing this, I was deeply shaken.}
{align:justify|First I told Lu Co only why his doctorate had come two years late.}
{align:justify|Lu Co said:}
{align:justify|"That happened. In youth, running with friends, they lured me along to buy pleasure. I never imagined it would bring such ill consequences—beyond all expectation!"}
{align:justify|Then I explained why he could not become Full General. I wrote the reason in two words: "Mac Thu." He glanced at it, then bowed his head in silence, saying nothing.}
{align:justify|I asked:}
{align:justify|"Is it true?"}
{align:justify|He nodded:}
{align:justify|"Yes!"}
{align:justify|Then Lu Co stood up and said to me:}
{align:justify|"Lien Sinh, you are truly the king of prophets—your readings are godlike! At last I understand: though a person's destiny is fixed, it can change! Shifting back and forth, all of it is made by the mind alone!"}
{align:justify|"Well said! I hope you understand, and from now on reform yourself well to avoid evil retribution!"}
{align:justify|As Lu Co left, I handed him a slip of paper with a few lines of counsel:}
{align:justify|Husband and wife, man and woman dwelling together}
{align:justify|That is the right way—no error allowed!}
{align:justify|Crooked lust invites deep harm…}
{align:justify|Both self and others are ruined; rank and fortune alike are lost}
{align:justify|Descendants cut off—such sorrow}
{align:justify|Wealth scattered, a thousand years of grief}
{align:justify|Halt and wake up, quickly, quickly}
{align:justify|Lest your children fall into calamity}
{align:justify|To be human, live upright and true}
{align:justify|Karmic retribution never errs—it is clear!}
{align:justify|Not long after, Lu Co's only son was struck and killed by a vehicle. As for Lu Co himself, calamity befell him too—childless indeed, just as foretold!}
