Commentary: If there are non-humans. This refers to animals and other creatures different from humans — beings either with form or without form, either with thought or without thought. If such beings as these delight in transcending their kind, I appear before them all in bodies like theirs, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to accomplish their wish.

"With form" means having an actual visible form that can be seen.

"Without form" means having no form.

There are many beings "with thought." Beings "without thought" include earth, stone, wood, iron... Beings "without form" are in fact also beings, but they have been dispersed into emptiness and fallen into oblivion. Such cases are usually of the intermediate-bardo body, and at some point they undergo rebirth again and return as humans.

Sutra text: 是名妙淨三十二應,入國土身。皆以三昧聞薰聞修, 無作妙力自在成就 。

Transliteration: Thị danh diệu tịnh tam thập nhị ứng, nhập quốc độ thân. Giai dĩ tam muội văn huân văn tu vô tác diệu lực tự tại thành tựu.

Translation: These are called the thirty-two sublimely pure response bodies, all entering lands, perfected at ease through the samadhi of hearing-perfumation and hearing-cultivation and the wondrous power of non-doing.

Commentary: These are called the thirty-two sublimely pure response bodies, all entering lands, perfected at ease through the samadhi of hearing-perfumation and hearing-cultivation. The cultivator develops the effort of returning the hearing to hear the self-nature through daily cultivation, and applies the wondrous power of non-doing. "Non-doing" refers to the subtle principle of "non-action" — with this principle, one need not pass through concepts of deliberation and processes of thought to find ideas, as everyone must do when wishing to do something. Bodhisattvas do not imagine or deliberate over such matters. In samadhi, they can do all things. In the samadhi of hearing-perfumation and hearing-cultivation they can attain the wondrous power of non-doing. And by this method, they perfect the power of ease. Quite naturally, all matters are settled well.

Sutra text: 世尊我復以此聞薰聞修金剛三昧無作妙力,與諸十方三世六道,一切眾生同悲仰故,令諸眾生於我身心,獲十四種無畏功德。

Transliteration: Thế tôn, ngã phục dĩ thử văn huân văn tu kim cang tam muội vô tác diệu lực, dữ chư thập phương tam thế lục đạo, nhất thiết chúng sanh đồng bi ngưỡng cố, linh chư chúng sanh ư ngã thân tâm, hoạch thập tứ chủng vô uý công đức.

Translation: World Honored One, further, through this Vajra samadhi of hearing-perfumation and hearing-cultivation and its wondrous power of non-doing, and because I share the same compassionate ngưỡng with all beings of the six paths in the ten directions and three periods, I enable living beings who merge into my body and mind to obtain fourteen kinds of fearlessness merit.

Commentary: World Honored One, further, through this Vajra samadhi of hearing-perfumation and hearing-cultivation and its wondrous power of non-doing, and because I share the same compassionate ngưỡng with all beings of the six paths in the ten directions and three periods — having a compassionate ngưỡng in the Buddhadharma toward beings of the six paths: gods, humans, asuras, animals, hungry ghosts, and hells.

I go throughout the ten directions and three periods — past, present, and future. I cultivate the Vajra samadhi of hearing-perfumation and hearing-cultivation, and I need not deliberate or calculate about a task before carrying it out.
