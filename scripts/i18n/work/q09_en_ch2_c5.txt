<p><strong>This state is called: &#8220;the mind forced to its utmost, flying out to see much through obstructions.&#8221;</strong></p>
<p>In your effort you restrained the mind indulging in false thought, forcing your mind to the utmost &#8211; then experiencing the mind bursting forth, seeing everything no matter how far, scenes that normally could not be seen however hard one tried to look. But now suddenly your mind bursts open and you can see everything.</p>
<p><strong>That is not sage realization</strong> &#8211; do not think this is a good state.</p>
<p><strong>If you do not think you have realized sagehood, it is a good state; but if you deem yourself a sage, you fall into deviant views.</strong></p>
<p>If you proclaim you have realized the sage fruition when you have not, or proclaim attainments you cannot attain, you will find yourself surrounded by demon hosts.</p>


<ol start="10">
<li><strong> SEEING A GOOD SPIRITUAL FRIEND TRANSFORM WITHOUT UNDERSTANDING WHY</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Hựu dĩ thử tâm, nghiên cứu tinh cực, kiến thiện tri thức, hình thể biến di. Thiểu tuyển vô đoan, chủng chủng thiên cải. Thử danh tà tâm, hàm thọ li mị, hoặc tao thiên ma, nhập kì tâm phúc, vô đoan thuyết pháp, thông đạt diệu nghĩa, phi vi thánh chứng. Bất tác thánh tâm, ma sự tiêu yết. Nhược tác thánh giải, tức thọ quần tà. </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Again with this mind, investigating to utter refinement, one sees a good spiritual friend's physical form transform and shift. In a brief moment, for no reason, it changes in all sorts of ways. This is called the deviant mind possessed by goblins and sprites, or met by a heavenly demon entering its very heart &#8211; preaching the Dharma for no reason, fluently expounding wondrous meanings &#8211; not sage realization. If you do not take it as sagehood, the demonic occurrences vanish; if you interpret it as sagehood, you fall among the deviant.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: This is the tenth demonic state of the form skandha, called &#8220;false seeing&#8221; and &#8220;false preaching.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>Again with this mind, investigating to utter refinement, one sees a good spiritual friend's physical form transform and shift.</strong> One from whom you studied the Dharma in the past. <strong>In a brief moment, for no reason, the body changes in all sorts of ways.</strong></p>
<p>The good friend transforms in shape right before you &#8211; suddenly an old man, moments later a middle-aged person, moments later a youth. Or if the good friend was originally male, he turns female, and you think: Oh, my good friend in a past life was a woman. In fact, what you see is all unreal; these transformations result from your false thoughts. In a brief moment, many changes occur inexplicably; everything changes and becomes abnormal.</p>
<p>This resembles what drug users experience: to them, everything turns glaringly colorful and they cannot see objects clearly. They paint bizarre pictures no one understands, merely splattering colors recklessly across the canvas. When finished, someone says: &#8220;Oh, truly a master painter&#8221; &#8211; and some praise it sincerely. You see! That is of the same kind.</p>
<p>You wonder: &#8220;But is this the case of a cultivator who has taken drugs?&#8221;</p>
<p>No. This state is caused by the demon kings. When you take drugs, the demon kings also get to work &#8211; making all your sensations transform likewise. (6)</p>
<p>The states within the form skandha can turn good or turn bad: if the cultivator is replete with good roots, the transformation goes toward the good; if the cultivator has few good roots, it turns toward the unwholesome. Such are all the transformations.</p>
<p><strong>This is called: &#8220;the deviant mind possessed by goblins and sprites, or met by a heavenly demon entering its heart &#8211; preaching the Dharma for no reason, fluently expounding wondrous meanings.&#8221;</strong></p>
<p>When goblins, sprites, or heavenly demons enter, you will deem yourself able to lecture sutras and preach the Dharma &#8211; deem yourself awakened, deem yourself an Elder. Such people begin preaching the Dharma without cause, not knowing what they say: they claim to fluently understand the wondrous meanings of the Buddhadharma, yet truly understand nothing of them; they are not expounding the Buddhadharma at all &#8211; taking wrong things, expounding them as right, proclaiming their Dharma wondrous, praising themselves while slandering others, styling themselves Elders.</p>
<p><strong>Not sage realization</strong>: this is not a good state.</p>
<p><strong>If you do not take it as sagehood, the demonic occurrences vanish.</strong> The demon hosts will disappear of themselves.</p>
<p><strong>But if you think you have realized sagehood, you fall into deviant views</strong>: thinking you have attained the sage fruition, you will find yourself in the hells &#8211; attaining your fruition in the hells.</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong> THE WORLD-HONORED ONE'S CONCLUDING EXHORTATION ON THE FORM SKANDHA</strong></p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: A-nan, như thị thập chủng, thiền-na hiện cảnh, giai thị sắc ấm, dụng tâm giao hỗ, cố hiện tư sự .</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Ananda, these ten states appearing in dhyana all come from the form skandha and the mind's applied effort interweaving, hence these occurrences appear.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Ananda, the ten states</strong> described above <strong>appearing</strong> when concentrating in contemplation <strong>in dhyana</strong> <strong>come from the form skandha</strong> and the impatient mind's <strong>applied effort interweaving to make them appear</strong>. This state involves the form skandha, when the applying mind mutually investigates the form skandha to the utmost. Any cultivator of the Way may pass through this state.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Chúng sanh ngoan mê, bất tự thổn lượng. Phùng thử nhân duyên, mê bất tự thức, vị ngôn đăng thánh, đại vọng ngữ thành, đọa vô gián ngục.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Obstinate and deluded beings, not assessing themselves, encountering such conditions, deluded and unaware, claim to have ascended to sagehood &#8211; committing the great falsehood, they fall into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Obstinate and deluded beings, not assessing themselves</strong> &#8211; beings stubborn and hard to transform, deluded and hard to awaken, who do not examine themselves to verify their level of study from the start. <strong>Encountering such conditions</strong> &#8211; meeting these demonic states &#8211; <strong>deluded and unaware</strong> of themselves, <strong>claiming to have realized sagehood</strong>.</p>
<p>They proclaim: &#8220;Oh! I am a Buddha. I am awakened. I have realized the sage fruition.&#8221;</p>
<p>Thus <strong>committing the great falsehood</strong> &#8211; they truly speak great falsehoods &#8211; and surely <strong>fall into the uninterrupted hell</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhữ đẳng đương y, Như lai diệt hậu, ư mạt  pháp trung, tuyên kì tư nghĩa. Vô linh thiên ma, đắc kì phương tiện. Bảo trì phúc hộ, thành vô thượng đạo.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: After the Tathagata's extinction, in the Dharma-ending age, you should rely on this and proclaim these meanings. Do not let the heavenly demons gain their advantage. Protect and shelter beings, enabling them to accomplish the unsurpassed Way.</strong></p>
