Commentary: The Child Fragrance-Adorned certified to the Way through fragrance-objects. But fragrance functions only in union. The nose must inhale the fragrance before it knows there is fragrance. Apart, it fundamentally does not exist. Fragrance does not exist.
Its object of awareness does not constantly abide —
How can one attain perfect penetration?
Because the nature of fragrance is neither constant nor unchanging, we do not always have awareness of it, and so fragrance-objects are not regarded as the best method for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 味性非本然 要以味時有 其覺不恒一 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Vị tánh phi bản nhiên Yếu dĩ vị thời hữu Kỳ giác bất hằng nhất Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: The nature of flavor is not inherent; Only when tasted does it exist. Its awareness is not constant and single — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Though some have become enlightened through flavor-objects, flavor is not inherently existent. The nature of flavor is not the perfect self-nature. For only when tasted does it exist. Flavor exists only when there is something flavored; only then do flavor-objects exist.
Its awareness is not constant and single —
How can one attain perfect penetration?
The knowing-nature of flavor is not always present. Therefore this method is not given as a dharma-door for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 觸以所觸明 無所不明觸 　合離性非定 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Xúc dĩ sở xúc minh Vô sở bất minh xúc Hợp ly tánh phi định Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Touch is known through what is touched; Without something, touch is unknown. Its nature in union and separation is indeterminate — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Touch is known through what is touched.
Though some become enlightened through cultivating touch-objects, touch exists only when there is something touching.
Without something, touch is unknown.
The only way to feel touch is for someone to create contact with something.
In union and separation, its nature is indeterminate.
How can one attain perfect penetration?
Therefore, touch-objects are likewise not used as a method for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 法稱為內塵 憑塵必有所 能所非遍涉 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Pháp xưng vi nội trần Bằng trần tất hữu sở Năng sở phi biến thiệp Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Dharmas are called inner objects; Relying on objects, there must be a "somewhere." Subject and object do not wholly interpenetrate — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Mahakashyapa cultivated the mind root through dharma-objects, and he certified to the samadhi of the cessation of feeling and thought.
Dharmas are called inner objects.
Dharma-objects are the objects of the mind root.
Relying on objects, there must be a "somewhere." (sense faculty)
Since they are objective realms, they must have a location. Since they have a location, they cannot be at the level of "empty yet not empty."
Subject and object do not wholly interpenetrate. The interfusion of subject and object (subject/object) is not yet thorough, not yet complete.
How can one attain perfect penetration?
