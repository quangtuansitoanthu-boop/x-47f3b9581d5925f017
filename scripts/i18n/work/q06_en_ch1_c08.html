Sutra text: 見性雖洞然 明前不明後 四維虧一半 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Kiến tánh tuy đỗng nhiên Minh tiền bất minh hậu Tứ duy khuy nhất bán Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: The seeing-nature, though clear and penetrating, Sees before but not behind; Of the four directions, half is lacking — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: The seeing-nature, though clear and penetrating. The eye can see clearly and penetratingly. But it sees before but not behind. If the eyes look forward, they cannot see behind. If they look right, they cannot see left. Of the four directions, half is lacking. One can see only two of the four directions at once.
How can one attain perfect penetration?
So that is not the method selected for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 鼻息出入通 現前無交氣 支離匪涉入 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Tỵ tức xuất nhập thông Hiện tiền vô giao khí Chi ly phỉ thiệp nhập Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: The nose's breath goes out and in; Between, at present there is no exchange of breath; Fragmented, not interfused — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: The nose's breath goes out and in.
This verse discusses the nose root. Between, at present there is no exchange of breath. Sometimes the breath is held. Breathing stops.

Fragmented, not interfused. This state is a deficiency and makes the nose root unsuitable too. It is not entirely perfect. How can one attain perfect penetration? Therefore it cannot be chosen as the method for cultivating perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 舌非入無端 因味生覺了 味亡了無有 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Thiệt phi nhập vô đoan Nhơn vị sanh giác liễu Vị vong liễu vô hữu Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: The tongue without flavor has no ground for entering; Through flavors awareness arises. When flavors vanish, awareness is utterly gone — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: The tongue without flavor has no ground for entering. Flavor must contact the tongue (the tongue root) before that taste-awareness exists. Without flavor, nothing is known. Through flavors awareness arises. The experience of awareness comes through flavor-objects. When flavors vanish, awareness is utterly gone.
How can one attain perfect penetration?
You cannot use this method to cultivate perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 身與所觸同 各非圓覺觀 　涯量不冥會 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Thân dữ sở xúc đồng Các phi viên giác quán Nhai lượng bất minh hội Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: The body aware of touch is the same as its object of touch; Neither is a perfect, enlightened regard. Limited, not darkly merged — How can one attain perfect penetration?
