Commentary: World Honored One, if there are Bodhisattvas who enter samadhi, advance in cultivation of the unconditioned — they have already attained right concentration (samadhi) and wish to continue their effort to reach the true realm of no-dwelling nirvana. They are those who have attained superior understanding and manifest perfection. Their wisdom is most subtle and wonderful, and they have revealed the perfect penetration of the six sense roots. I appear before them in a Buddha's body, speak Dharma for them, and enable those Bodhisattvas to attain liberation. When I meet such beings, I at once manifest a Buddha's body. Although I myself have not yet become a Buddha, my compassionate power is the same as the Buddhas'. Through this compassionate power I can manifest a Buddha's body and speak Dharma for the ranks of Bodhisattvas, helping them reach liberation.

Sutra text: 若諸有學寂靜妙明勝妙現圓,我於彼前現獨覺身而為說法令其解脫。

Transliteration: Nhược chư hữu học tịch tĩnh diệu minh, thắng diệu hiện viên, ngã ư bỉ tiền, hiện Độc giác thân nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ giải thoát.

Translation: If there are learners who cultivate the still, wondrously luminous perfection and manifest wondrous perfection, I appear before them in the body of a Solitary Enlightened One, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to attain liberation.

Commentary: If there are learners who cultivate the still, wondrously luminous perfection. "Learners" refers to those who have not yet reached the fourth fruition of Arhatship. They are those who have attained wondrous perfection — their wisdom is most supremely subtle and they have entered perfect penetration. "I appear before them in the body of a Solitary Enlightened One and speak Dharma for them, enabling them to attain liberation. For this class of beings, I shall manifest as a Solitarily Enlightened One" — meaning one of the Two Vehicles who certified to the Way when no Buddha was manifest in the world. They cultivate the dharma of the Twelvefold Conditioned Origination and become enlightened.

In spring they contemplate the white peach blossoms blooming,
In autumn they contemplate the yellow leaves falling.

They awaken to the natural principle of arising and ceasing operating through the myriad phenomena of the world. That is how the Solitarily Enlightened Ones became enlightened. Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva will appear as a Solitarily Enlightened One to speak Dharma for them, enabling them to become enlightened.

Sutra text: 若諸有學斷十二緣。緣斷勝性,勝妙現圓。我於彼前。現緣覺身。而為說法令其解脫。

Transliteration: Nhược chư hữu học đoạn thập nhị duyên. Duyên đoạn thắng tánh, thắng diệu hiện viên. Ngã ư bỉ tiền, hiện Duyên giác thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ giải thoát.

Translation: If there are learners who sever the twelve conditioned links, and through the severing of the links give rise to a superior nature, with wondrous perfection manifest, I appear before them in the body of a Condition-Enlightened One, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to attain liberation.

Commentary: If there are learners who sever the twelve conditioned links, and through the severing of the links give rise to a superior nature. Again, "learners" refers to first-stage Arhats.

The twelve conditioned links have been explained in the earlier text. Those points are most important.
1. Ignorance conditions activity;
2. Activity conditions consciousness;
3. Consciousness conditions name-and-form;
4. Name-and-form conditions the six sense entrances;
5. The six sense entrances condition contact;
6. Contact conditions feeling;
7. Feeling conditions craving;
8. Craving conditions grasping;
9. Grasping conditions becoming;
10. Becoming conditions birth;
11. Birth conditions old age and death.
