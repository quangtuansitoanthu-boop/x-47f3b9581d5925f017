Commentary: Ananda, do not continue to be so intent on study that you neglect cultivating samadhi power. Hearing does not arise of itself. In itself it utterly does not come from nature.
Because of sound it has a name. Because there are sound-objects, there is hearing. We call the recognition of sound-objects "hearing sound." Turn the hearing back, apart from sound. What name for that which is apart? We say there is hearing because there is sound — but when there is no sound, does hearing remain? Hearing remains. For the hearing-nature is not an object of birth and death. Therefore, turn the hearing back. From now on, do not chase after the six roots and six objects. Bring the six roots and six objects back to the self-nature.
Return the hearing to hear the self-nature,
And one becomes the Unsurpassed Way.

What is your self-nature? When you listen, you will recognize it. Now, before you recognize it, you will not understand until I tell you. For instance, when I drink this cup of tea, I myself know whether the tea is cold or hot, but you still know nothing. If you wish to know, you must drink the tea yourself. If you wish to recognize your own self-nature, first of all, you must turn the hearing back. If you do not, how can you hope to recognize your self-nature? You ask:
"When turning the hearing back, how does one hear?"

Use your ears, but do not listen to outer sounds. Do not try to find out what is happening on the street, or who is saying what. Turn the hearing inward and listen to your own inside. In this way your self-nature will manifest. That is how you practice. Do you understand now?

When turning the hearing back, one is at ease with outer sounds. "What name for that which is apart?" If you yourself can be entirely apart from outer sounds — hearing yet not hearing — is this not truly the hearing-nature? It is, and it is not. Not, yet it is. Hearing yet not hearing. That means being at ease with sounds. One hears, but not as one heard before. Though one knows ordinary hearing can hear far or near, it still has a limit. If you truly attain the effort of turning the hearing inward to recognize your own nature, then you can hear throughout the entire Dharma Realm. But you may also choose to hear nothing of the entire Dharma Realm's sounds. You must be in control. Like the telegraph. If I wish to send, I can send to people everywhere at any time. If I do not wish to send a telegram, I shall not do it. If I wish to hear any sounds in the Dharma Realm, I can switch it on. Try it and see. What is such ease with sounds called? It fundamentally has no name. It is what transcends the names of hearing and the true hearing-nature.

Sutra text: 一根既返源 六根成解脫

Transliteration: Nhất căn ký phản nguyên Lục căn thành giải thoát

Translation: Once one root has returned to the source, The six roots are liberated.

Commentary: As soon as names are no more, one may say that the mind root has been opened.
Once one root has returned to the source,
The six roots are liberated.
