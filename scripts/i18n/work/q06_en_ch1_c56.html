They begin saying around that they have certified to the first Arhat fruition. But then that level is not noble enough, so they say, "Oh! I have just certified to the second Arhat fruition." And a second later, they proclaim they have certified to the fourth fruition. Yet the fourth fruition is still only Arhatship, not the highest fruition, so they are not satisfied. They proclaim they have reached the pratyekabuddha vehicle, or the Bodhisattva stages of the Ten Grounds or before the Ten Grounds. They begin telling people they are pratyekabuddhas, or they proclaim they have reached a stage of the Bodhisattva practices, or even the Ten Grounds. Why do such people proclaim themselves Arhats, pratyekabuddhas, and Bodhisattvas? In the end they are cheating people, and they speak great false speech to make people believe in them. If no one believed them, they would have no offerings. As soon as people believe, wealth flows in. So their intention is seeking others' reverence and repentance, greedy for their offerings.

They do not fear falling into the tongue-pulling hells. If one lies, after death, one at once falls into this hell, where iron hooks hook into the tongue and pull it out, then a sword cuts off the tongue. That is the retribution for lying. Yet still people dare do it. We dare not even look upon this realm. Take muteness as an example. Why muteness? Because they undergo retribution for excessive lying (exaggeration). They are made human, but cannot speak — "See how much you can lie now." That is the message. Why can they not speak? Because their tongues have been cut. Though they have tongues, their nature has been lost, so those tongues have no true nature. Why are people blind? Because they look down on others. They always regard themselves as better than everyone else. They regard themselves as cleverer and quicker in every way, so in this life they cannot see people. Now they must ask themselves whether they are truly better than everyone else? The deaf incur retribution for eavesdropping on discussions. They often put ears to keyholes to hear what is being discussed. Today's spies with countless means of overhearing others, stealing private discussions, may undergo the same retribution and become deaf sometime in the future.

However, once you know the principle, you will abandon lying, and you will not become mute. If you do not eavesdrop on others' discussions, you will not become deaf. Muteness, hunchback, and blindness are all retributions for slandering the Triple Jewel.

Sutra text: 是一顛迦銷滅佛種。如人以刀斷多羅木。佛記是人永殞善根,無復知見,沈三苦海,不成三昧。

Transliteration: Thị nhất-điên-ca tiêu diệt Phật chủng. Như nhơn dĩ đao đoạn đa-la mộc. Phật ký thị nhơn vĩnh vẫn thiện căn, vô phục tri kiến. Trầm tam khổ hải bất thành tam muội.

Translation: Such atyantikas destroy the Buddha-seed. Like a person cutting a tala tree with a knife. The Buddha predicts such people forever lose good roots, have no more knowing and seeing, sink into the three seas of suffering, and never perfect samadhi.
