TITLE: 22. THE SCHOOLTEACHER
TOMTAT: A 3-year-old suffered mild cerebral palsy — in a past life a professor who scorned the Buddha Dharma and blocked others from studying Buddhism. His parents loved pork, so the child was born to them. Meeting Master Miaofa, they learned how to repent and become vegetarian to transform it.
-----
{align:center|**THE SCHOOLTEACHER**}

{align:justify|In Shanxi Province a young couple bore a son, now 3, still unable to walk, speech unclear. Examined, the doctors called it mild cerebral palsy. The couple, ten thousand parts worried, asked me to inquire of the Master on their behalf.}

{align:justify|The Master said that in a past life this child was a talented professor, a career teacher, widely read in the classics, a man of great fame and standing. Then, many fond of studying the Buddha Dharma came seeking his teaching, asking of matters concerning birth and death and transmigration through the six realms.}

{align:justify|The schoolmaster scorned them, telling all:}

{align:justify|"All Buddhism preaches is falsehood, unreal! Has anyone ever seen transmigration through the six realms?"}

{align:justify|He even quoted Confucius: "Not yet understanding life — how understand death!" and stubbornly declared his own view perfectly correct!}

{align:justify|With such reckless words he caused many to lose the blessed affinity of entering the Buddhist path.}

{align:justify|Because of that arrogant pride, trusting half-baked knowledge, the professor spoke wildly, using his own mistaken judgments to block seekers of the Buddha Dharma — so after death he was born a pig for many lives.}

{align:justify|In this present life, because the couple dearly loves pork, holding pork the tastiest meat — when that pig was slaughtered, they bought it home and stewed it to eat. Now affinities ripened: the son they bore is precisely that schoolmaster of former lives, reaping evil retribution. And that is also the retribution of a pork-loving couple.}

{align:justify|To change karmic retribution, they must repent the sin of meat-eating. Moreover they must turn wholly vegetarian. They must also repent on behalf of their son's ancient evil karma, reciting the Kshitigarbha Sutra dedicated to all whom the schoolmaster stuffed with wrong thoughts, who missed their affinity to study the Buddha Dharma — and for the pigs they ate, reciting the Kshitigarbha Sutra for their deliverance.}

{align:justify|The Master affirmed:}

{align:justify|"If only the couple persevere to the end, reciting sutras and eating vegetarian as I say, joined with therapeutic methods and bodily training, the child shall gradually turn for the better. His brain shall recover to normal; in future he shall surely care for his own livelihood."}

{align:justify|I relayed all the Master's teaching to the couple. Hearing it, the child's father exclaimed in terror:}

{align:justify|"The Master speaks most truly! The boy's grandparents work at a meat company, and even set up a private slaughterhouse. So the family buys fresh fine meat most cheap. His mother dearly loves pig's head."}

{align:justify|Little did they think retribution would strike the whole house together: truly self-made, self-reaped. Had they known clearly, they would never have acted so.}

{align:justify|The child's mother shed tears, voicing repentance:}

{align:justify|"We shall certainly obey the Master's teaching — eat vegetarian, recite sutras, strive to heal the boy."}

{align:justify|May all persevere with firm resolve — affliction shall turn to awakening.}

{align:justify|COMMENTARY:}

{align:justify|The story THE SCHOOLTEACHER teaches of "scorning the Buddha Dharma — retribution in birth as a pig, one's pork-eating descendants." A talented professor, arrogant and proud, scorned the Buddha Dharma as "falsehood" and blocked others from studying Buddhism — the cause sown. Its fruit: after death birth as a pig for many lives, and in this life rebirth as the son of a pork-loving couple, stricken with cerebral palsy. The story stresses: "To raise without teaching is the parents' fault" — the parents loved pork, buying cheap meat from the grandparents' slaughterhouse, so the child bears the retribution. The transformation: repent meat-eating, eat wholly vegetarian, recite the Kshitigarbha Sutra dedicated to those blocked from the Buddha Dharma and to the pigs eaten. It warns all who despise the Buddha Dharma or block others' practice: "The Buddha Dharma is not for debating right and wrong, but for saving beings. Whoever blocks others from studying Buddhism sows the seed of downfall for self and descendants."}
