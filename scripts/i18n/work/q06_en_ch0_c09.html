Commentary: If there are living beings — these beings are not Hearers, not Condition-Enlightened Ones, not Bodhisattvas; they are ordinary beings of the six paths. They are beings who wish their minds to attain clear awakening, who wish to attain enlightenment — true understanding. They are those who do not offend against worldly sense-objects of desire. They stay far from greed and desire in the world of ordinary passions, because they wish their bodies to be pure. I appear before them in the body of a Brahma King, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to attain liberation. I shall manifest as the Great Brahma Heaven King and speak Dharma for them, enabling them to attain liberation.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生欲為天主統領諸天。我於彼前現帝釋身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh dục vi thiên chủ thống lĩnh chư thiên. Ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Đế thích thân. Nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who wish to be lords of the heavens, ruling over all the heavens, I appear before them in the body of Shakra, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who wish to be lords of the heavens — that is, the Christian God. They wish to rule over all the heavens. They wish to govern the heavens. I appear before them in the body of Shakra (Śakra), speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish. I manifest the body of Shakra for this kind of being. Shakra is the lord of the heavens — God.

Did you know that the Holy Mother of Catholicism is precisely Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva? Catholics believe in the Holy Mother because they wish to be reborn in the heavens. Thus Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva manifests as a goddess to teach them, enabling them to be born in the heavens. Of course, from the heavens they will fall again, but gradually they will progress. Why does Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva manifest and speak Dharma to help people be born in the heavens? His ultimate purpose is to lead beings to believe in the Buddhas. But since at the time their wish is to be born in the heavens, he teaches them how to be born in the heavens. When they fall back from the heavens, eventually they will believe in the Buddhas. Ordinary people see the time this process takes as very long, but in the Buddha's eye it is but an instant, but the blink of an eye.

This method is like parents who want their child to master an excellent trade, while the child does not want to learn that trade. The parents must indulge the child's wish to study whatever field it likes, but after a few initial failures, in the end it must change direction and study the trade the parents recommended. Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's method of teaching beings is to respond to whatever wishes beings desire. But his ultimate purpose is always to bring beings to the perfection of Buddhahood.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生欲身自在遊行十方。我於彼前現自在天身。而為說法令其成就

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh dục thân tự tại, du hành thập phương, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Tự tại thiên thân. Nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who wish for a body at ease, roaming through the ten directions, I appear before them in the body of an Easeful Heaven god, speak Dharma for them, and enable them to accomplish their wish.
