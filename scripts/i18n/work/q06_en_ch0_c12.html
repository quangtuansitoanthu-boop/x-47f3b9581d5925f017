Translation: If there are living beings who delight in discussing fine words and living a pure life, I appear before them in the body of a lay follower, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who delight in discussing fine words. They may delight in poetry, or in the fine works of famous writers. They may memorize many works of this kind by heart. They delight in living a pure life. If asked to do something they consider unworthy of such pursuits, they will have nothing to do with it. I appear before them in the body of a lay follower, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish. I shall manifest the body of a lay follower and speak Dharma for them.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生愛治國土,剖斷邦邑。我於彼前現宰官身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh ái trị quốc độ, phẫu đoán bang ấp. Ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Tể quan thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who delight in governing the country, judging and deciding matters of the states and cities, I appear before them in the body of a minister, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who delight in governing the country, judging and deciding matters of the states and cities. They settle the great affairs of the nation. I appear before them in the body of a minister, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish. I shall manifest as a prime minister or high official to speak Dharma for them.

Sutra text: 若諸眾生愛諸數術攝衛自居。我於彼前現婆羅門身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược chư chúng sanh ái chư số thuật, nhiếp vệ tự cư, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện Bà-la-môn thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.

Translation: If there are living beings who delight in the arts of reckoning, gathering their minds and guarding themselves, I appear before them in the body of a Brahman, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish.

Commentary: If there are living beings who delight in the arts of reckoning. They delight in studying reckoning arts — that is, astronomy and geography, yin-yang and the five phases. "Arts" means medicine, divination, astrology, water mantras and written charms for nursing body and mind and protecting life. "Reckoning" refers to using numbers and calculations to predict the future. "Arts" also refers to demonic magic and other crafts. It further means the mantras of non-Buddhist paths. The Brahma-heaven mantra of the Kapila outsiders used by Maudgalyayana's mother is an example of this kind. These people delight in gathering their minds and guarding themselves. They understand that if they learn a mantra or a teaching, that mantra and teaching can protect them.

I appear before them in the body of a Brahman, speak Dharma for them, and help them accomplish their wish. Brahmanism is a religion of India. That name means "originating from purity" and represents the cultivation of pure conduct. These people have a great many magical arts. They can recite mantras and have many magical crafts. And so Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, ever according with all beings, also manifests as a Brahman to speak Dharma, so that such people may obtain what they wish.

Sutra text: 若有男子好學出家持諸戒律。我於彼前現比丘身。而為說法令其成就。

Transliteration: Nhược hữu nam tử hiếu học xuất gia, trì chư giới luật, ngã ư bỉ tiền hiện tỷ-khưu thân, nhi vị thuyết pháp linh kỳ thành tựu.
