If a man can forget his girlfriend and a woman can forget her boyfriend — if you can smash this realm to pieces — then your effort has some substance. You are more or less attached to your siblings, to your sons and daughters, and to all your relatives. To study the Buddhadharma, you must set down everything. While studying the Buddhadharma, you should pay attention to nothing else. You should be as one beginning anew; forget all past affairs. In that way, the waters of the Buddhadharma can permeate the bodhi mind. If you cannot set down everything and cannot renounce anything, then the waters of the Buddhadharma cannot nourish the bodhi mind. If you can constantly recite the name and revere Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, then your lustful mind will vanish.

The most important matter in cultivation is utterly severing thoughts of lust. If you cannot do this, you cannot leave the Triple Realm. You cannot resolve to become enlightened yet remain unable to leave behind this world's experiences. If you cannot detach from worldly romances, you cannot become a Buddha. You cannot hope for both. Confucius also said:

One cannot have both fish and bear's paw at once.

Though people like eating fish and bear's paw, they cannot eat both at the same time. In the same way, you cannot enjoy worldly pleasures and transcendental blessings at the same time. You wish to become a Buddha, yet you cannot leave the five worldly desires of wealth, beauty, fame, food, and sleep. Then there is no way to succeed.

Sutra text: 十者純音無塵,根境圓融,無對所對,能令一切忿恨眾生離諸嗔恚。

Transliteration: Thập giả thuần âm vô trần, căn cảnh viên dung, vô đối sở đối, năng linh nhất thiết phẫn hận chúng sanh ly chư sân khuể.

Translation: Tenth, I retain only the pure single perfect sound, with no object of sound-dust; root and realm perfectly interfused, with no opposing subject and object, I enable all resentful and hateful beings to leave behind anger and hatred.

Commentary: The tenth fearlessness power is: I retain only the pure single perfect sound, with no object of sound-dust; root and realm perfectly interfused.

When one has turned the hearing back to hear one's own nature, sound becomes pure — which only means there is no sound at all within it. Sound is void and still, and sound-dust is extinct. At that point only the interfusion of the six roots and six sense-objects remains. In this world, no matter how evil anything is, it can become good if you know how to handle it. And everything good can become evil if you do not know how to handle it. Earlier in the sutra text the Buddhas of the ten directions instructed Ananda that the six sense-root thieves are precisely the cause of error, and that these same six roots are what can enable us to perfect Buddhahood.
