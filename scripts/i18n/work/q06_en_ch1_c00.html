Sutra text: 爾時世尊於師子座,從其五體同放寶光,遠灌十方微塵如來,及法王子諸菩薩頂.

Transliteration: Nhĩ thời Thế tôn ư sư tử toà, tòng kỳ ngũ thể đồng phóng bảo quang, viễn quán thập phương vi trần Như Lai, cập Pháp vương tử chư Bồ-tát đỉnh.

Translation: At that time the World Honored One, upon the lion's throne, from his fivefold body simultaneously emitted precious light, pouring from afar upon the crowns of the Tathagatas as numerous as dust motes throughout the ten directions, and upon the crowns of the Dharma-Prince Bodhisattvas.

Commentary: At that time the World Honored One, upon the lion's throne, from his fivefold body simultaneously emitted precious light. Shakyamuni Buddha, seated upon the lion's throne, emitted light from his two hands, his two feet, and the crown of his head all at once. Pouring from afar upon the crowns of the Tathagatas as numerous as dust motes and upon the crowns of the Dharma-Prince Bodhisattvas.

Shakyamuni Buddha touching the crowns of the Tathagatas symbolizes that his teaching is the highest, is the crown. The teaching of the Buddhas is likewise. The Tathagatas throughout the ten directions all proclaim this same supreme teaching, just as Shakyamuni Buddha is proclaiming it.

The teachings of the Tathagatas are all identical,
mutually containing one another.

Sutra text: 彼諸如來亦於五體同放寶光。從微塵方來灌佛頂。并灌會中諸大菩薩及阿羅漢。

Transliteration: Bỉ chư Như Lai diệc ư ngũ thể đồng phóng bảo quang, tòng vi trần phương lai quán Phật đỉnh. Tinh quán hội trung chư đại Bồ-tát cập A-la-hán.

Translation: Those Tathagatas, too, from their fivefold bodies simultaneously emitted precious light, coming from lands as numerous as dust motes to pour upon the Buddha's crown, and to pour upon the great Bodhisattvas and Arhats in the assembly.

Commentary: Those Tathagatas, as numerous as dust motes throughout the ten directions, from their fivefold bodies also simultaneously emitted precious light, from worlds as numerous as dust motes. The precious light likewise issued at once from their two hands, two feet, and the crowns of their heads, just like the rays of light Shakyamuni Buddha had emitted. The light came and poured upon the Buddha's crown and upon the crowns of the Bodhisattvas and Arhats in the assembly.

Sutra text: 林木池沼皆演法音。交光相羅如寶絲網。是諸大眾得未曾有。一切普獲金剛三昧。

Transliteration: Lâm mộc trì chiểu giai diễn pháp âm. Giao quang tương la như bảo ty võng. Thị chư đại chúng đắc vị tằng hữu. Nhất thiết phổ hoạch kim cang tam-muội.

Translation: Groves, trees, ponds, and marshes all proclaimed the sounds of Dharma. The interweaving lights formed like a jeweled gauze net. The great assembly obtained what they had never had; all universally attained the Vajra samadhi.
