Sutra text: 聲無既無滅 聲有亦非生 　生滅二圓離 是則常真實。

Transliteration: Thinh vô ký vô diệt Thinh hữu diệc phi sanh Sanh diệt nhị viên ly Thị tắc thường chơn thật.

Translation: When soundless, the hearing-nature is not extinguished; When sounding, the hearing-nature does not arise. Both arising and ceasing fully transcended — This is eternal truth.

Commentary: When soundless, the hearing-nature is not extinguished.
The absence of sound does not mean the hearing-nature has been lost. When sounding, the hearing-nature does not arise. When hearing sound, nothing changes in the hearing-nature.
Both arising and ceasing fully transcended —
This is eternal truth.
The hearing-nature is eternally self-sufficient within itself and apart from both ideas of arising and ceasing. Arising and ceasing have become entirely interfused. The hearing-nature is the ever-abiding nature, unchanging, not an object of arising and ceasing.

Sutra text: 縱令在夢想 不為不思無 覺觀出思惟 身心不能及。

Transliteration: Túng linh tại mộng tưởng Bất vị bất tư vô Giác quán xuất tư duy Thân tâm bất năng cập

Translation: Even amid dreams and thoughts, It is not that, unthought of, it is absent. Awakened regard transcends thought; Body and mind cannot reach it.

Commentary: Whether there is sound or no sound, whether sound has just arisen or has entirely vanished, the hearing-nature never vanishes. People can recognize this in the ordinary waking state. But it is ever present even amid dreams and thoughts.
Someone fast asleep still hears the pounding of the washing mallets and the rice-hulling pestles like bells and drums playing together. This proves that the hearing-nature is present even in dreams.
It is not that, unthought of, it is absent.
Unlike the mind, which does not exist when not thinking.
Awakened regard transcends thought. The knowing of that hearing-nature transcends thinking.
Body and mind cannot reach it.
Body and mind can never compare with the hearing-nature of the ear root.

Sutra text: 今此娑婆國 聲論得宣明 眾生迷本聞 循聲故流轉 阿難縱強記 不免落邪思 豈非隨所淪 旋流獲無妄。

Transliteration: Kim thử ta-bà quốc Thanh luận đắc tuyên minh Chúng sanh mê bổn văn Tuần thinh cố lưu chuyển A-nan túng cường ký Bất miễn lạc tà tư Khởi phi tuỳ sở luân Triền lưu hoạch vô vọng.

Translation: Now in this Saha land, The doctrine of sound is proclaimed; Beings have lost their original hearing, Following sounds, thus they turn. Ananda, though of strong memory, Could not avoid falling into deviant thought. Is it not through what engulfs one That, turning the flow, one attains freedom from falseness?

Commentary: Now in this Saha land,
The doctrine of sound is proclaimed.
Beings have lost their original hearing.
