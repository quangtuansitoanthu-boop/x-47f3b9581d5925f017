With arising and ceasing already quenched, still extinction manifested.
The emptier and the emptied both became still and extinct, yet the idea of "extinction" remained — and where extinction remains, arising remains — so one must continue advancing in cultivation until the characteristics of both arising and ceasing are entirely eliminated; only then can the sixth knot, that of the idea of "extinction," be untied. At the same time, the eighth consciousness, the alaya consciousness of the consciousness skandha, is destroyed. The two words "arising and ceasing" refer precisely to the knots: when movement ends, stillness arises; when the roots cease, awareness arises; when awareness ceases, emptiness arises; when emptiness ceases, extinction arises. These six knots are precisely the dharma of arising and ceasing. The characteristic of extinction is most difficult to untie; the characteristic of extinction must be entirely eliminated before one can reach the state of neither arising nor ceasing, before one can see one's own original face. Now the six knots have been untied, the five skandhas have been completely destroyed, falseness is entirely exhausted and all truth is revealed — that is, with arising and ceasing already quenched, still extinction manifests, and ultimate perfect penetration is certified.

Sutra text: 忽然超越世出世間,十方圓明,獲二殊勝。

Transliteration: Hốt nhiên siêu việt thế xuất thế gian, thập phương viên minh, hoạch nhị thù thắng.

Translation: Suddenly I transcended the mundane and the transcendental worlds; throughout the ten directions a perfect, luminous oneness manifested, and I obtained two supreme states.

Commentary: When the state of still extinction had manifested, suddenly I transcended the mundane and the transcendental worlds. This refers to the realm of sentient beings and the material world. Throughout the ten directions a perfect, luminous oneness manifested. Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva merged as one with the worlds of the ten directions, without obstruction. And I obtained two supreme states.

Sutra text: 一者上合十方諸佛本妙覺心。與佛如來同一慈力。

Transliteration: Nhất giả thượng hợp thập phương chư Phật bổn diệu giác tâm. Dữ Phật Như Lai đồng nhất từ lực.

Translation: First, above, I united with the fundamentally wonderful enlightened mind of all Buddhas of the ten directions. I attained the same compassionate power as the Buddhas, the Tathagatas.

Commentary: Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva's compassionate mind is entirely the same as the compassionate minds of all Buddhas.

Sutra text: 二者下合十方一切六道眾生。與諸眾生同一悲仰

Transliteration: Nhị giả hạ hợp thập phương nhất thiết lục đạo chúng sanh. Dữ chư chúng sanh đồng nhất bi ngưỡng.

Translation: Second, below, I united with all living beings of the six paths throughout the ten directions. Together with living beings I share the same ngưỡng of compassion.

Commentary: Second, below, I united with all living beings of the six paths throughout the ten directions.

What are the living beings of the six paths throughout the ten directions? To take the simple aspect of human beings — eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, and mind — these are the beings of the six paths. They are the turning in birth and death through the six paths, just as form, sound, scent, taste, touch, and mental objects are also a turning through the six paths. Those are the six paths of living beings within one's own nature. There is a mutual relationship between the inner six paths and the outer six paths. The outer six paths are the realms of gods, asuras, humans, animals, hungry ghosts, and hells. The asura realm includes all beings fond of contention.
