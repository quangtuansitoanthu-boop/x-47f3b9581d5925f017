<p><strong>This is called <em>the samadhi-state settling smoothly into the mind, lacking the wisdom to restrain oneself</em>, hence deluded, indulging in the desires.</strong></p>
<p>In samadhi there is a state of ease and contentment entering the mind, but lacking the wisdom to restrain one's emotions and cravings &#8211; ending in abandon to lustful desires.</p>
<p><strong>Recognized, it is without fault</strong>.</p>
<p>This is only a temporary, incidental state. The cultivator should recognize this state as a demonic state, and <strong>not sage realization</strong>. If you understand, very good &#8211; what is fearful is when you do not recognize the demonic state you are sunk in. You will fall into an enchanting maze with no way out.</p>
<p><strong><em>Sutra text: Nhược tác thánh giải, tắc hữu dục ma nhập kì tâm phủ. Nhất hướng thuyết dục, vi bồ-đề đạo. Hóa chư bạch y, bình đẳng hành dục, kì hành dâm giả, danh trì pháp tử. Thần quỷ lực cố, ư mạt thế trung, nhiếp kì phàm ngu, kì số chí bách, như thị nãi chí, nhất bách nhị bách, hoặc ngũ lục bách, đa mãn thiên vạn. Ma tâm sanh yểm, li kì thân thể. Oai đức kí vô, hãm ư vương nạn. Nghi ngộ chúng sanh, nhập vô gián ngục. Thất ư chánh thọ, đương tòng luân trụy.</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: But if interpreted as sagehood, a demon of lust enters the very heart: ever proclaiming lust the Bodhi Way, teaching lay followers to practice lust equally, calling its lust-practitioners Dharma-heirs. Through ghosts' and spirits' power, in the Dharma-ending age it gathers ordinary fools &#8211; a hundred, even one or two hundred, or five or six hundred, many up to thousands upon myriads. When the demon mind grows weary, it leaves the body; prestige and virtue gone, one falls into legal peril. Misleading beings with doubts and errors, one enters the uninterrupted hell. Losing right samadhi, one is sure to fall.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>But if you claim to be a sage</strong> &#8211; <strong>then a demon of lust enters the heart.</strong> If the cultivator takes this for sage-fruition realization, through mistaken views a demon of lust and craving enters the mind, possessing the body. <strong>Ever proclaiming lust the Bodhi Way</strong> &#8211; it becomes an all-out advocate of lust, calling it the Bodhi Way.</p>
<p>It will say: &#8220;Apart from lust there is nothing.&#8221;</p>
<p>Just as in America there is a sect advocating polygamy, holding &#8220;the more wives the better.&#8221; This sect is exactly like that kind of demon &#8211; encouraging and enticing lustful craving by saying: &#8220;Very good to have many wives &#8211; nothing grave in it. That is the Bodhi Way &#8211; the more wives the better.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong> </strong>It <strong>teaches lay followers to practice lust equally, calling its lust-practitioners <em>Dharma-heirs</em>.</strong> It teaches disciples to practice lust equally, saying: &#8220;Make no distinctions between this person and that &#8211; enjoy pleasure together.&#8221; Regardless of one's own wife or another's, they simply act on fancy. In America there is a bizarre wife-swapping fad: a place where people go to exchange wives with one another &#8211; just like the deeds of the demon discussed in the sutra.</p>
<p><strong>Calling its lust-practitioners <em>Dharma-heirs</em></strong>: those joining in lust with it are called keepers of the Dharma-seed, continuers of the Dharma transmitted by the demon.</p>
<p><strong>Through demonic power, in the Dharma-ending age, it draws and deludes disciples from among ordinary fools &#8211; a hundred, even five or six hundred, up to thousands upon myriads.</strong> At last <strong>when the demon king grows weary</strong> &#8211; what do you think happens? <strong>It abandons the person</strong>. What happens when the demon leaves? The person no longer has spiritual powers.</p>
<p><strong>When the demon's alluring power is gone, one falls into legal peril</strong>.</p>
<p>Its alluring power came from spiritual powers &#8211; the demon king had spiritual powers. But now the demon king has left, so the person has no more spiritual powers. Do you know what happens? He violates the nation's laws: before, while he still had spiritual powers, the demon king could help him evade national law and keep the authorities from learning of the matter. But with the demon king gone, this ordinary fellow is back to normal &#8211; a lawbreaker. People recognize him as a master of heterodox side-doors, habitually cheating and deceiving students with bizarre mantras and spells. Violating the law, he is arrested and jailed.</p>
<p><strong>For misleading beings with doubts and errors</strong>, never meeting the true Way, <strong>one falls into the uninterrupted hell</strong> &#8211; surely falling into the Avichi hell. Why? <strong>For losing right samadhi, one is sure to fall.</strong></p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong><em>THE WORLD-HONORED ONE'S CONCLUDING EXHORTATION ON THE FEELING SKANDHA</em></strong></p>
<p><strong><em><u>Sutra text</u></em></strong><strong><em>:  A-nan, như thị thập chủng thiền-na hiện cảnh, giai thị thọ ấm , dụng tâm giao hỗ, cố hiện tư sự. Chúng sanh ngoan mê, bất tự thốn lượng, phùng thử nhân duyên, mê bất tự thức, vị ngôn đăng thánh, đại vọng ngữ thành đọa vô gián ngục.</em></strong></p>
<p><strong><u>Translation</u></strong><strong>: Ananda, these ten states appearing in dhyana all come from the feeling skandha and the mind's applied effort interweaving, hence these occurrences. Obstinate and deluded beings, not assessing themselves, encountering such conditions, deluded and unaware, claim to have ascended to sagehood &#8211; committing the great falsehood, they fall into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong><u>Commentary</u></strong>: <strong>Ananda, these ten states appearing in dhyana all come from the feeling skandha and the mind's applied effort interweaving, hence these occurrences.</strong></p>
<p>Ananda! Such states manifesting while cultivating dhyana all lie within the scope of the feeling skandha interweaving with vigorous effort in practice. These events result from various transformations occurring while the cultivator applies vigorous effort.</p>
<p><strong>Obstinate beings, deluded and confused, do not assess themselves.</strong></p>
<p>Beings, deluded, do not turn back to contemplate within themselves.</p>
<p><strong>Encountering such conditions, deluded and unaware.</strong></p>
<p>Beings do not recognize who they are.</p>
<p><strong>Claiming</strong> to have <strong>realized the sage rank</strong>.</p>
<p>Then they claim to have attained what they never attained and realized what they never realized &#8211; proclaiming to have attained the Way and realized the sage rank, when they never attained such things at all.</p>
<p><strong>Thus</strong> incurring <strong>the offense of the great falsehood.</strong> Surely <strong>falling into the uninterrupted hell.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Sutra text: Nhữ đẳng diệc đương, tương Như Lai ngữ, ư ngã diệt hậu, truyền thị mạt pháp, biến linh chúng sanh khai ngộ tư nghĩa. Vô linh thiên ma đắc kì phương tiện. Bảo trì phúc hộ, thành vô thượng đạo.   </em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: After my extinction, you should transmit the Tathagata's words, proclaiming them in the Dharma-ending age, so that all beings awaken to these meanings. Do not let the heavenly demons gain their advantage. Protect and shelter beings, accomplishing the unsurpassed Way.</strong></p>
