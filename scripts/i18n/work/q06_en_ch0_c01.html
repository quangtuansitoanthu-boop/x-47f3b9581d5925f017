That is a mistake! Not only will you be unable to carry your karma with you, you will be unable to be reborn there at all, because you will be obstructed by your own karma. We who have faith in the Buddha must be careful not to create further karmic obstructions once we have learned Buddha-recitation. This chapter on Mahasthamaprapta's perfect penetration through Buddha-recitation is extremely important. Everyone should understand what the Buddha-recitation method essentially is.

Why should we recite the Buddha's name? Because we have a great affinity with Amitabha Buddha.

Amitabha Buddha became a Buddha ten kalpas ago. Before that, his dharma name was the Bhikshu Dharma Treasury. At that time, he made forty-eight vows. In his thirteenth and fourteenth vows, he vowed: "If any living being throughout the ten directions recites my name and yet does not become a Buddha, I vow not to attain right enlightenment." In other words, if any living being recites his name without becoming a Buddha, he vows not to become a Buddha himself. And through the power of Amitabha Buddha's vows, everyone who recites his name will be reborn in the Land of Ultimate Bliss.

The Pure Land dharma-door is one of the five schools of Chinese Buddhism.
1. The Chan (dhyāna) school.
2. The Teaching school.
3. The Vinaya school.
4. The Esoteric school.
5. The Pure Land school.

The Pure Land school will be the last school to survive. In this world, in the Dharma-Ending Age, the Shurangama Sutra will be the first to disappear. After that, the other sutras will vanish one after another, and only the Amitabha Sutra will remain. While the Amitabha Sutra still remains in the world, it will take very many people across to liberation. After a thousand years or more, the Amitabha Sutra will also disappear completely.

The "Dharma-Ending Age" simply means that the right Dharma vanishes entirely. Once the Amitabha Sutra has disappeared, only the phrase "Namo Amitabha Buddha" will be left. This extraordinary phrase will also take very many people across to liberation; yet after another thousand years, it too will disappear. All that remains will be the name "Amitabha Buddha," which will also stay in the world for a hundred more years before vanishing. By then, no more Buddhadharma will circulate in the world. While we are still before the Dharma-Ending Age, we should cultivate and preserve the ways of the Right Dharma age. That is called "requesting the Buddha to turn the Dharma wheel."

In the Dharma-Ending Age, we should not fear any hardship. I do not mind the difficulty of expounding the Dharma for you, and you should not fear the hardship of coming to hear the sutra. Rouse your spirits! Do not say you are tired and must go rest. Forget yourselves for the sake of the Dharma. Consider how Shakyamuni Buddha lived in the Snow Mountains for six years seeking the Way. We do not go to live six years in the mountains, but the least we can do is to study Buddhism. Regard the Buddhadharma as the food you need to eat. "If I do not go hear the sutra lecture, it is as if I will have nothing to eat for the next few days."

You should think in this way. "I must go hear the Dharma. I must certainly find a way to truly understand the Way." Where can you gain a genuine understanding of the Buddhadharma? You must often listen to sutra lectures. Without hearing sutra lectures, you cannot open wisdom. This is especially true of the Shurangama Sutra, for it is precisely the sutra that opens wisdom for living beings.
