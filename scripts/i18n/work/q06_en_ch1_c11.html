Sutra text: 神通本宿因 何關法分別 念緣非離物 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Thần thông bổn túc nhơn Hà quan pháp phân biệt Niệm duyên phi ly vật Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Spiritual powers come from past-life causes; What have they to do with discriminating dharmas? Thought-moments never leave things — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Maudgalyayana was foremost in spiritual powers, which he obtained through consciousness.
Spiritual powers come from past-life causes.
Having spiritual powers comes from causes accumulated in past lives. That has nothing to do with discriminating consciousness. That is an entirely different matter from discriminating consciousness.
Thought-moments never leave things.
Yet spiritual powers still require the process of thought-moments. Thus spiritual powers are not separate from the physical category, so in themselves they are not complete. How can one attain perfect penetration? So cultivating to obtain spiritual powers is also not the best way.

Sutra text: 若以地性觀 堅礙非通達 　有為非聖性 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Nhược dĩ địa tánh quán Kiên ngại phi thông đạt Hữu vi phi thánh tánh Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: If one contemplates the nature of earth, Its hardness obstructs, not penetrating. The conditioned is not the sagely nature — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: If one contemplates the nature of earth.
Its hardness obstructs, not penetrating.
The Bodhisattva Upholding Earth used the method of contemplating the nature of the earth element. Earth has the qualities of hardness and obstruction. It is not interfused. That is a conditioned dharma, not a dharma-door for entering the sagely flow.
The conditioned is not the sagely nature —
How can one attain perfect penetration?
It cannot be regarded as the best method for reaching perfect penetration.

Sutra text: 若以水性觀 想念非真實 如如非覺觀 云何獲圓通

Transliteration: Nhược dĩ thuỷ tánh quán Tưởng niệm phi chơn thật Như như phi giác quán Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: If one contemplates the nature of water, Contemplative thought is not true; Suchness is not perceiving regard — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Some cultivators wish to cultivate by contemplating the water element and entering the Water-Contemplation samadhi as Moonlight Youth cultivated.
If one contemplates the nature of water,
Contemplative thought is not true,
For when the cultivator enters samadhi by this method, the process of thought-moments, the state of subtle deliberation, is still involved at first. But since the realm of suchness is not perceiving regard, the method of contemplating the water element is not a method of contemplating the original nature.
How can one attain perfect penetration?
Hence the method of contemplating the Water element is not selected as the perfect-penetration cultivation.

Sutra text: 若以火性觀 厭有非真離 　非初心方便 云何獲圓通。

Transliteration: Nhược dĩ hoả tánh quán Yếm hữu phi chơn ly Phi sơ tâm phương tiện Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Contemplating the fire element to dispel the fire of lust Comes from weariness with suffering, not true departure. It is not an expedient for beginners — How can one attain perfect penetration?
