<p>Now I shall explain this more deeply, and speak very truthfully: if I do not speak truthfully, you will never know how <em>outflows</em> truly concern you. To escape all <em>outflows</em> means keeping your essence intact; to lose your essence means there are outflows.</p>
<p>Now I shall reveal the secret of heaven and earth: if you preserve your essence, there will be no outflows. Moreover, not only preventing the loss of your own nature, you must also be able to cut off lustful thoughts even in the subtlest mental arising &#8211; then you have truly escaped all outflows. Now do you understand? Why are heavenly demons not free of all outflows? Because they still have thoughts of lust; ghosts and spirits also still have such lustful thoughts.</p>
<p>For they are still <strong>clinging to this worldly toil</strong>.</p>
<p>Worldly toil is simply a form of lust &#8211; that is what they crave.</p>
<p><strong>How could they let you destroy their dwelling</strong>?</p>
<p>Since they still crave lust, they do not want to see you cut yourself off from it; they want you to indulge in lust too. They will say:</p>
<p>&#8220;We two are close friends &#8211; I shall never cut myself off from lust. So you should not reject it either.&#8221;</p>
<p>That is why demons and ghosts come: none of them wants you to transcend this worldly toil.</p>
<p>Hence <strong>ghosts, spirits, heavenly demons, goblins, and sprites will come to sabotage you when you cultivate samadhi</strong>.</p>
<p>When I mentioned the word sprites before, its meaning was not clear to you; now I shall explain. Do you know that the Chinese character <em>yao</em> 妖 (sprite) is written with the <em>woman</em> 女 radical beside <em>yao</em> 夭 (dying young)? It means dying before age thirty. Once you understand the meaning of <em>yao</em>, no lengthy explanation is needed: in short, whoever dies young becomes a sprite. (Dying young from indulgence in pleasure.)</p>
<p>When you cultivate samadhi (concentration), they all come in a crowd to harass you. Their purpose is what they usually call <em>eating the flesh of Tripitaka Tang</em> &#8211; Tripitaka Tang being Great Master Xuanzang: a great many sprites wanted to eat his flesh &#8211; that is, they wanted to destroy his samadhi power. If in cultivation you attain samadhi power, demons and ghosts all want to devour your flesh. In fact they want more than just to eat your flesh: I shall state it for you with utter truthfulness, holding nothing back. What really happens when your essence, energy, and spirit are full? Because you do not dwell on lust, these demons and ghosts all want to seize your treasury by force &#8211; that is why they come to harass you. If you have a girlfriend, or you ladies have a boyfriend, then those persons will likewise rob you of your treasury. Think what else could happen?</p>
<p>You will say:</p>
<p>&#8220;The Buddhadharma teaches me giving, so I give my whole treasury away to others.&#8221;</p>
<p>Excellent! In the future you will take rebirth as a hungry ghost. When that happens, the one who robbed you of your treasury will not say:</p>
<p>&#8211;&#8220;Here, I return a little of the treasure to you, so you may escape this hungry-ghost realm.&#8221;</p>
<p>Then no one at all will help you. Think it over well.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhiên bỉ chư ma tuy hữu đại nộ, bỉ trần lao nội. Nhữ diệu giác trung, như phong xuy quang, như đao đoạn thủy, liễu bất tương xúc. Nhữ như phất thang, bỉ như kiên băng, noãn khí tiệm lân, bất nhật tiêu vẫn. Đồ thị thần lực, đãn vi kì khách.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Although those demons have great power, they still abide within worldly toil; while you abide within wondrous enlightenment, demons cannot touch you &#8211; like wind blowing light that does not waver, or a knife cutting water that is not parted. You are like boiling water while the demon king is like solid ice: near the warmth, the ice gradually melts away. They rely on demonic power, but they are only guests!</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary:</strong> When you attain a little samadhi power in your effort, the demon king grows afraid and comes to destroy it &#8211; it does not want you to attain samadhi power.</p>
<p><strong>Those demons</strong> come to harass you, and <strong>although they have great power, they still abide within worldly toil</strong> &#8211; driven by defiled emotional consciousness.</p>
<p><strong>While you abide within wondrous enlightenment</strong>, demons cannot affect you at all &#8211; <strong>like wind blowing light that does not waver, or a knife cutting water that is not parted</strong>.</p>
<p>They can do nothing to you &#8211; just as wind cannot make light waver, and just as a knife, however many times it slashes the water, leaves the water unharmed.</p>
<p>You are like boiling water: the samadhi power arising from your cultivation effort is <strong>likened to boiling water</strong>, while <strong>the demon king is like solid ice</strong> &#8211; the demon king likened to solid ice in winter. <strong>Near the warmth, the ice melts away</strong>: hard as ice, yet the heat of boiling water gradually melts it. <strong>They rely on demonic power, but they are only guests</strong>.</p>
<p>They rely only on their own demonic power, so they can never be called hosts &#8211; they are only &#8220;guests&#8221; (who cannot stay long) and cannot succeed in their efforts to harass you.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Thành tựu phá loạn, do nhữ tâm trung ngũ ấm chủ nhân. Chủ nhân nhược mê khách đắc kì tiện</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Their success in causing havoc depends on the master of the five skandhas within your mind. If the master is deluded, the guest gets its chance.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary:</strong> <strong>Their success in causing havoc depends on the master of the five skandhas within your mind</strong>: fundamentally they cannot cause havoc. However, if the master is deluded &#8211; if your mind, the master of the five skandhas, is deluded &#8211; then the guest gets its chance to cause havoc.</p>
<p>The guest will get its chance to cause havoc; but if you, the master, are not deluded, it can do nothing to harm you. Who is the master? Your own nature: if your own nature is deluded, demons and ghosts have a way to harm you; but if your own nature is not deluded, they are helpless and can do nothing to you.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Đương xứ thiền-na, giác ngộ vô hoặc, tắc bỉ ma sự vô nại nhữ hà? Ấm tiêu nhập minh, tắc bỉ quần tà hàm thụ u khí. Minh năng phá ám, cận tự tiêu vẫn. Như hà cảm lưu nhiễu loạn thiền định?</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Abiding in dhyana, awakened and undeluded, what can those demonic deeds do to you? When the skandhas vanish and you enter brightness, the flock of evils all draw upon dark energy; since brightness can destroy darkness, they melt away of themselves on approaching you. How would they dare linger to disturb your chan samadhi?</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>Abiding in dhyana</strong>, you purify your mind, attain right samadhi, and are <strong>awakened and undeluded</strong> &#8211; demonic deeds can do nothing to you; demons and ghosts cannot disturb you in their way. <strong>When the skandhas vanish, you can enter the bright inherent nature</strong>: demons and ghosts belong to darkness (yin) &#8211; darkness can be dispelled as ice melts in boiling water. Your wisdom is very bright, so you completely merge with the light.</p>
