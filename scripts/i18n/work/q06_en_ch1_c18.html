Commentary: When Manjushri Bodhisattva reached this point, he called to Ananda. Manjushri Bodhisattva is a Dharma Prince of most venerable age, while Ananda is still very young. Here, Manjushri Bodhisattva speaks on behalf of Shakyamuni Buddha proclaiming the teaching, so he draws Ananda's attention. Ananda! Listen attentively!
Pay attention! Listen to every word; slight none of it. Do not sit there dozing while I speak this verse! You should know that this verse I am speaking is not from my own idea. The verse is proclaimed through the awesome power the Buddha transmitted to me. The Buddha appointed me to speak and expound this content. He told me to select the dharma-door for your cultivation. You should not think it is I, Manjushri, speaking this to you; it is precisely the Buddha speaking to you. Do you understand now?
Proclaiming the Vajra King's samadhi. Relying on the Buddha, I shall proclaim the dharma-door of the Vajra King.
Illusory, inconceivable,
The true samadhi that is mother of Buddhas.

This is a cultivation that needs no cultivation. Cultivating yet not cultivating. Not cultivating yet cultivating. It is rather like a game, so at the same time it is real. The subtlety lies there. You cannot imagine how sublime this dharma-door is. You cannot imagine it or express that sublimity in words. That samadhi is the mother of Buddhas. All Buddhas are born from this samadhi. So pay attention. Do you wish to become a Buddha? If so, attend to what I shall say. And then you must apply effort. Do not merely waste effort on outer skills. You must attain the effort of inner regard.

Sutra text: 汝聞微塵佛 一切祕密門 欲漏不先除 　畜聞成過誤。

Transliteration: Nhữ văn vi trần Phật Nhất thiết bí mật môn Dục lậu bất tiên trừ Súc văn thành quá ngộ

Translation: You have heard the Buddhas as numerous as dust motes And all their secret doors; If desire-outflows are not first removed, Hoarded hearing becomes error.

Commentary: Manjushri Bodhisattva continues:
You have heard the Buddhas as numerous as dust motes
And all their secret doors.

Ananda, you have heard countless Buddhas speak of secret dharma-doors. "Secret" means you say something for one person to hear that a second person does not know. Or you speak about the second person what the first person does not know. Each person does not know what was said to the other. Why does the Buddha have secret dharma-doors? Because beings' natures differ. Each being likes different things. Moreover, each person cultivated different dharma-doors in past lives. Therefore the Buddha regards potentials; he examines which dharma-door you cultivated in a past life, and on that basis teaches you how you should cultivate now.
Regarding potentials, bestowing teaching.
Teaching Dharma to each person individually.

He does not teach you a dharma-door unsuited to you.
If desire-outflows are not first removed,
Hoarded hearing becomes error.
