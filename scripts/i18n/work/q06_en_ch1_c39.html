Commentary: Though one has much wisdom and samadhi manifests, if killing is not cut off, one will surely fall among spirits. "Wisdom" here refers to worldly cleverness and skill in debate, not transcendental wisdom. It is a kind of ordinary cleverness giving people a certain ability to argue. And though you cultivate to the point of effort in dhyana — you have some responses — still, if you do not rid yourself of killing karma, you will fall into the realm of spirits. That is, you may become a god-spirit ruling the heavens. The highest grade becomes powerful ghosts. That is, mighty soldiers of the heavens. The middle grade becomes flying yakshas, ghost commanders, and the like. Flying yakshas are ghosts that fly through the sky. Or one becomes a commander of ghosts. The lowest grade becomes earth-traveling rakshasas.

Sutra text: 彼諸鬼神亦有徒眾。各各自謂成無上道。

Transliteration: Bỉ chư quỷ thần diệc hữu đồ chúng. Các các tự vị thành vô thượng đạo.

Translation: Those ghosts and spirits also have retinues. Each claims to have perfected the unsurpassed Way.

Commentary: The commanding gods, the powerful ghosts of the heavens, the yaksha ghosts and rakshasa ghosts of the human realm, the ghosts of the hell realm also have retinues. There are some ten thousand different kinds of ghosts. Kuan Ti Kung of China is an example of a god of wealth. But after he took refuge with the Buddha, he was called the Bodhisattva Ch'ieh Lan, a Dharma-protecting spirit. In the Buddha's Dharma assembly, he only stands; he is not permitted to sit. However, the ghosts spoken of here all proclaim themselves to have perfected the unsurpassed Way.

Sutra text: 我滅度後末法之中,多此神鬼熾盛世間。自言食肉得菩提路。

Transliteration: Ngã diệt độ hậu mạt pháp chi trung, đa thử thần quỷ xí thạnh thế gian. Tự ngôn thực nhục đắc bồ đề lộ.

Translation: After my extinction, in the Dharma-Ending Age, many such ghosts and spirits will blaze across the world, claiming that eating meat leads to the bodhi way.

Commentary: After my extinction, in the Dharma-Ending Age, many such ghosts and spirits will blaze across the world. That is precisely the Buddha speaking of the present age — the age you and I are living in. There are countless ghosts and spirits in this Dharma-Ending Age, all because in past lives they did not abandon killing karma. They cultivated, but could not stop killing, so they fell into the realm of spirits. In the Dharma-Ending Age, these kinds will proclaim that eating meat leads to the bodhi way.

They say, "Eating meat and becoming a Buddha are the same. I need not abandon killing or eat vegetarian, yet I am enlightened and have certified to the bodhi way — that is, I am a Buddha." This is like someone proclaiming himself a Buddha yet eating meat, drinking wine, smoking, keeping a group of young disciples and teaching them to smoke marijuana and take drugs. Has anyone ever heard of an enlightened person acting so? When the Buddha became enlightened, he did not use such drugs. Now you use drugs to poison your nervous system, overturn your balanced inner energy, and bring yourself to the brink of death, yet insist you are enlightened. I ask you, is that upside-down or not?
