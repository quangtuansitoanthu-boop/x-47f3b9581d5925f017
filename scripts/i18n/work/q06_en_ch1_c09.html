Commentary: The body aware of touch is the same as its object of touch.
The method of using the body root (for cultivation) is also like touch-objects. Neither is pervasive, neither complete.
Their knowing-nature is not yet fully complete.
Limited, not darkly merged —
How can one attain perfect penetration?
It is measured by limits and quantity, and that is an unseen relationship with touch-objects; therefore it is not chosen as the best perfect-penetration cultivation.

Sutra text: 知根雜亂思 湛了終無見 想念不可脫 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Tri căn tạp loạn tư Trạm liễu chung vô kiến Tưởng niệm bất khả thóat Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: The knowing root is mixed with scattered thought; In still clarity there is ultimately no seeing. If discursive thought cannot be cast off — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: The knowing root is mixed with scattered thought. What the mind knows is dharma-objects — a mixture of scattered thoughts.
In still clarity there is ultimately no seeing.
If discursive thought cannot be cast off —
How can one attain perfect penetration?
It never reaches what is called still extinction, nor ever reaches the ultimate, because people can never transcend while thought-moments — false thoughts and waves of consciousness — still exist. How could these factors lead to perfect penetration?

Sutra text: 識見雜三和 詰本稱非相 自體先無定 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Thức kiến tạp tam hoà Cật bổn xưng phi tướng Tự thể tiên vô định Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Conscious seeing is mixed of the threefold union; Probed to its root, it is called markless. Its own substance fundamentally lacks fixity — How can one attain perfect penetration?

Commentary: Conscious seeing arises through root and object.
This verse passage concerns the consciousnesses, which are the union of the six inner roots and the six outer objects. The six roots have knowing, while the six objects do not. Thus the six consciousnesses are half-knowing and half-unknowing. These three components combine to make consciousness. Probed to its root, it is called markless. Consciousness has no self-marks.
Its own substance fundamentally lacks fixity.
How can one attain perfect penetration?
For the consciousnesses themselves are in a state of constant flux. How could one use them to cultivate perfect penetration? Hence eye-consciousness is also not selected.

Sutra text: 心聞洞十方 生于大因力 初心不能入 云何獲圓通 。

Transliteration: Tâm văn đỗng thập phương Sanh vu đại nhân lực Sơ tâm bất năng nhập Vân hà hoạch viên thông?

Translation: Mind-hearing penetrates the ten directions, Born of the power of a great cause; Beginners' minds cannot enter it — How can one attain perfect penetration?
