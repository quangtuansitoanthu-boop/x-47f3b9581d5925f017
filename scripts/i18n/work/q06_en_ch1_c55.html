Translation: Ananda, though beings of the six paths in such worlds have no killing, stealing, or lust in body and mind, their three practices perfected, if they engage in great false speech, then samadhi cannot be pure; they become demons of love and views, losing the Tathagata's seed.

Commentary: Ananda, though beings of the six paths in such worlds have no killing, stealing, or lust in body and mind. Their bodies do not commit killing, stealing, lust. Their minds also have no thoughts of killing, stealing, lust. Their three practices perfected, if they engage in great false speech, then samadhi cannot be pure.

This means it is their habit, their habitual tendencies; always speaking great false speech. Thus they are not pure. They become demons of love and views, losing the Tathagata's seed. They become demons of love, or demons of deviant views. Why do they lose the Tathagata's seed? Because they speak too falsely.

Sutra text: 所謂未得謂得,未證言證。或求世間尊勝第一,謂前人言。我今已得須陀洹果。斯陀含果阿那含果。阿羅漢道辟支佛乘。十地地前諸位菩薩。求彼禮懺貪其供養。

Transliteration: Sở vị vị đắc vị đắc, vị chứng ngôn chứng. Hoặc cầu thế gian tôn thắng đệ nhất, vị tiền nhơn ngôn: Ngã kim dĩ đắc Tu-đà-hoàn quả, Tư-đà-hàm quả, A-na-hàm quả, A-la-hán đạo, Bích-chi Phật thừa, Thập địa địa tiền chư vị bồ-tát. Cầu bỉ lễ sám, tham kỳ cung dưỡng.

Translation: That is: what is unattained, called attained; uncertified, claimed certified. Or seeking the world's highest veneration, saying before others: "I have now attained the srota-apanna fruition, the sakridagamin fruition, the anagamin fruition, the Arhat Way, the pratyekabuddha vehicle, the ten grounds or pre-ten-grounds Bodhisattva stages." Seeking others' reverence and repentance, greedy for their offerings.

Commentary: How do they speak? Set aside ordinary lies. What is unattained, called attained; uncertified, claimed certified. They have not certified to the Way. Fundamentally, they understand nothing of cultivating the Way. They do not know how to recite the Buddha's name. They do not know how to keep precepts; they do not know how to sit in meditation.

They act as if they understood, but they understand nothing. They hear others explain a few principles, and at once interrupt: "I understand. I have known that long ago." Or they say: "Hey! I have certified to the Way. I am enlightened. I am a Buddha." Uncertified, claimed certified.

They have not attained even the first Arhat fruition; at most they should understand those fruitions, yet they say, "Do you know who I am? I am an Arhat." Or say, "I am a Buddha." Or say, "I am a Bodhisattva." Why do they say such things? Or seeking the world's highest veneration.

That is like recently someone said to one of my disciples: "Which school do you follow? We should rank ourselves; I shall be the leader. I am the founder of American Buddhism. I am the first patriarch of American Buddhism." That is "seeking the world's highest veneration." They say before others: I have now attained the srota-apanna fruition, the sakridagamin fruition, the anagamin fruition, the Arhat Way.
