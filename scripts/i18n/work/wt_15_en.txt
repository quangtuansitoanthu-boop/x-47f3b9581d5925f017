TITLE: 16. THE FETUS WHO LOVED HEARING THE KSHITIGARBHA SUTRA
TOMTAT: Mrs. Tran's daughter, pregnant, suffered a fetus that kicked painfully. Advised to recite the Kshitigarbha Sutra, she delivered smoothly; the baby grew healthy and showed "understanding" when its mother ate vegetarian.
-----
{align:center|**THE FETUS WHO LOVED HEARING THE KSHITIGARBHA SUTRA**}

{align:justify|Mrs. Tran is my neighbor. Just now she confided: "My daughter is more than seven months pregnant; lately the fetus keeps punching and kicking in her belly, causing its mother boundless pain."}

{align:justify|I told her that by Buddhist teaching, the child in the womb is a debt-collecting affinity come to torment and claim its debt — or born of the karma of killing and meat-eating. Best that from today the expectant mother stop eating meat (that is, stop meat foods and eat wholly vegetarian). Tell your grandchild to recite one copy of the Kshitigarbha Sutra for the fetus and try. With reverent, one-pointed recitation, the effect will be good.}

{align:justify|I added:}

{align:justify|"If the expectant mother perseveres in reciting seven copies of the Kshitigarbha Sutra dedicated to the child, the enmity between mother and child shall dissolve. And if reciting the Kshitigarbha Sutra brings no effect, I shall let your daughter eat meat again..."}

{align:justify|Then I gave her a copy of the Kshitigarbha Sutra to take home for her daughter to recite.}

{align:justify|The next day Mrs. Tran thanked me joyfully. She said:}

{align:justify|"Last night I went home and told my grandchild what you said; hearing it, she at once bathed, began reciting the Kshitigarbha Sutra — and finished to find the fetus lying still, striking no more. At last last night my daughter slept in peace. A miracle never granted these past months."}

{align:justify|Her child also abandoned the habit of meat-eating. Mrs. Tran praised again:}

{align:justify|"The Buddha Dharma is truly miraculous! Had my daughter been told a little sooner, had she known to recite sutras in ordinary days, how would she have suffered so?"}

{align:justify|Before long, Mrs. Tran's daughter bore a lovely boy. The delivery was almost without pain. Through the whole month of confinement, each day she recited one copy of the Kshitigarbha Sutra. Before, the new mother had loved meat — yet strangely, now the mere mention of meat disgusts her. Mrs. Tran asked me why.}

{align:justify|I said that finding meat's smell repulsive comes through the merit of recitation purifying body and mind — letting one's inherent Buddha nature reveal itself, no longer wishing to receive the bloody foods of beings. Your daughter need only eat plenty of vegetarian greens and fruits; her body will surely be better than before.}

{align:justify|COMMENTARY:}

{align:justify|The story THE FETUS WHO LOVED HEARING THE KSHITIGARBHA SUTRA continues the spirit of story 12, affirming the merit of the Kshitigarbha Sutra for the unborn. The fetus kicked painfully as a "debt-collecting affinity," but when the mother recited the Kshitigarbha Sutra in dedication, the baby kicked less, delivery went smoothly, and after birth it showed "understanding" when the mother ate vegetarian (never crying at vegetarian milk). When the mother ate meat, the baby cried and refused the breast. This proves the fetus possesses spiritual awareness — receiving merit and reacting to killing karma. The story urges expectant mothers and families: "For a healthy, easy child, early become vegetarian and recite the Kshitigarbha Sutra dedicated to your child." It is the best way to dissolve past-life enmity and create blessing for the child from pregnancy itself.}
