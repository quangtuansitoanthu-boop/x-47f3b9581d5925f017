<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE REALM OF FORM</strong></p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE FIRST DHYANA</strong></p>
<p><strong>A-nan! Thế gian nhất thiết, sở tu tâm nhân. Bất giả Thiền-na, vô hữu trí tuệ.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda! All people in the world who cultivate the mind cannot have wisdom without the aid of Dhyana.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: Ananda, now we shall speak of the Realm of Form, the heavens of the Four Dhyanas. All cultivators in the world cannot have wisdom without the aid of Dhyana. They are not yet accustomed to cultivating meditative concentration &#8211; they cannot yet restrain all their false thoughts, and therefore they have no wisdom yet.</p>


<ol>
<li><strong>Đãn năng chấp thân, bất hành dâm dục. Nhược hành nhược tọa, tưởng niệm câu vô. Ái nhiễm bất sanh, vô lưu dục giới, thị nhân ưng niệm, thân vi phạm lữ. Như thị nhất loại, danh Phạm Chúng Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>If they can control the body and refrain from sexual desire, whether walking or sitting, with no thought or remembrance; with no love or defilement arising, and no lingering in the Desire Realm, they become companions of Brahma. Such a class is called the Brahma Multitudes Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: They are able to control their own body and mind, restraining the body from sexual desire. &#8220;Whether walking or sitting, with no thought or remembrance.&#8221; Whether walking, standing, reclining, sitting, or even in sleep &#8211; at all times and in all places, &#8220;no love or defilement arises.&#8221; Even upon meeting something or someone beautiful, they do not allow thoughts of love and lust to arise, and thus they &#8220;have no lingering in the Desire Realm.&#8221;</p>
<p>Such people can take on the bodily form of Brahma at will. They become companions of the Brahma gods, free of all lustful ambitions. Such a class is called the Brahma Multitudes Heaven. They become pure beings dwelling in this heaven &#8211; one among the great multitude of gods in the heavens.</p>


<ol start="2">
<li><strong>Dục tập ký trừ, ly dục tâm hiện. Ư chư luật nghi, ái lạc tùy thuận. Thị nhân ưng thời, năng hạnh phạm đức. Như thị nhất loại, danh phạm phụ Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>With the outflows of desire already removed, the mind of detachment from desire appears; delighting in abiding by all the rules of deportment, in pure conduct and virtue, such a class is called the Brahma Ministers Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;With the outflows of desire already removed, the mind of detachment from desire appears; delighting in abiding by all the rules of deportment.&#8221; Human nature is fond of food and sex; these habits are inborn in people. But by this stage, such beings have left behind their habits of sexual lust. When they no longer have lust or craving love, wisdom begins to arise. They observe the precepts and keep the rules of deportment. They cherish the precepts and uphold them in cultivation. Such people can practice pure conduct and virtue; &#8220;such a class is called the Brahma Ministers Heaven.&#8221; At all times and in all situations, they cultivate according to the right path. Their conduct is moral and pure. &#8220;Brahmaministers&#8221; signifies purity. Such people are reborn in the ministers' heaven, where, holding the office of Dharma masters, they assist the Great Brahma Kings.</p>


<ol start="3">
<li><strong>Thân tâm diệu viên, uy nghi bất khuyết. Thanh tịnh cấm giới, gia dĩ minh ngộ. Thị nhân ưng thời, năng thống phạm chúng, vi Đại Phạm Vương. Như thị nhất loại, danh đại phạm thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>With body and mind wondrously perfect, majestic deportment complete, pure moral prohibitions, and added understanding and awakening, they are able to rule over the Brahma multitudes as Great Brahma Kings; such a class is called the Great Brahma Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: Those who have &#8220;body and mind wondrously perfect, majestic deportment complete, pure moral prohibitions, and added understanding and awakening.&#8221; These are beings with no more lustful thoughts, complete in the three thousand modes of awesome deportment and the eighty thousand subtle practices. They perfect the practices of the precepts, upholding purely the moral rules taught by the Buddha. Not only do they uphold the precepts completely, they also have a penetrating understanding of them. They have realized awakening through the body, through material things, and through the Dharma realm. They &#8220;are able to rule over the Brahma multitudes as Great Brahma Kings; such a class is called the Great Brahma Heaven.&#8221; When the lives of such people end, they are born in the heavens, where they can rule over countless multitudes in the Brahma heavens.</p>
<p><strong>A-nan! Thử tam thắng lưu. Nhất thiết khổ não, sở bất năng bức. Tuy phi chánh tu, chân tam-ma-địa. Thanh tịnh tâm trung, chư lậu bất động, danh vi sơ Thiền.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Ananda! These three superior levels cannot be oppressed by any suffering or affliction. Though they have not truly cultivated the genuine samadhi, in their pure minds the outflows do not stir; this is called the First Dhyana.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Ananda! These three superior levels cannot be oppressed by any suffering or affliction.&#8221; These three levels are the Brahma Multitudes Heaven, the Brahma Ministers Heaven, and the Great Brahma Heaven &#8211; the heavens of the First Dhyana &#8211; in which suffering and affliction do not trouble the cultivator.&#8221;</p>
<p>Although they &#8220;have not truly cultivated the genuine samadhi&#8221; &#8211; although they have not deeply penetrated the Buddhadharma nor truly cultivated meditative concentration &#8211; yet &#8220;in their pure minds the outflows do not stir.&#8221; They constantly uphold the precepts, and through upholding the precepts their minds become so pure that they are not moved by the enticements of outer things. They commit no faults. They do not pursue the endless desires of the material world, such as food, clothing, and all objects of craving. All of these are enticements. Those who dwell in these heavens have no weaknesses, afflictions, habits, or bad tendencies. They are &#8220;called the First Dhyana.&#8221; When cultivators reach this stage, the pulse stops beating upon attaining the First Dhyana. If you wish to know whether you have attained it, check your pulse. That is one sign of attaining this stage. It is no ordinary attainment.</p>


<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE SECOND DHYANA</strong></p>

<ol>
<li><strong>A-nan! kỳ thứ phạm thiên. Thống nhiếp phạm nhân, viên mãn phạm hạnh. Trừng tâm bất động, tịch trạm sanh quang. Như thị nhất loại, danh thiểu quang Thiên.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Ananda! The Brahma gods who rule over the Brahma multitudes, perfect in Brahma conduct, with minds settled and unmoving, give rise to light out of stillness and tranquillity; such a class is called the Lesser Light Heaven.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: Those who stand above the realms of the Brahma Multitudes Heaven, the Brahma Ministers Heaven, and the Great Brahma Heaven are able to rule over and gather in the beings of those three heavens, because they have already perfected Brahma conduct.</p>
<p>&#8220;With minds settled and unmoving, giving rise to light out of stillness and tranquillity.&#8221; Their minds are very calm and still, unwavering. When the mind is pure, still, and tranquil, a halo of light gradually shines forth. Such cultivators dwell in the Lesser Light Heaven.</p>
