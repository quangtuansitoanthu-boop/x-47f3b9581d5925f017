<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: <strong>Again that good man &#8211; the feeling skandha empty and wondrous, untroubled by deviant concerns, the perfect samadhi disclosed.</strong> In the empty, penetrating, wondrous state after the feeling skandha has dissolved.</p>
<p>Now the feeling skandha has become empty, penetrating, and wondrous; the cultivator cultivating right samadhi no longer has deviant concerns, dwelling in the bright, perfect samadhi. <strong>Within samadhi</strong> &#8211; perfect and wondrous &#8211; a sudden shift occurs. What? <strong>The mind fond of long life</strong>: suddenly the cultivator thinks: &#8220;How marvelous to live forever without dying!&#8221;</p>
<p>Hence <strong>bitterly studying the subtle, longing for immortality.</strong> Hence the cultivator bitterly, diligently studies the subtlest secret and wondrous methods, longing for immortality &#8211; wishing to stay forever young, never aging.</p>
<p><strong>Discarding and renouncing sectional birth-and-death of the body</strong>.</p>
<p>Each character of &#8220;<em>sectional birth-and-death</em>&#8221; concerns the birth-and-death of each individual: each person has a portion, and each person a section. What does <em>portion</em> mean? You have a body, I have a body; you are a person, I am a person &#8211; you have your portion and I have my own portion. What is <em>section</em>? The lifespan &#8211; the span of time from living to dying. <em>Section</em> also means the body's stature, from head to foot.</p>
<p><strong>Abruptly hoping for the subtle transforming birth-and-death.</strong></p>
<p>Ordinary people must undergo sectional birth-and-death; those who have realized the four Arhat fruitions no longer pass through this state &#8211; but must still pass through &#8220;<em>transformational birth-and-death</em>&#8221; &#8211; thought after thought shifting: undergoing birth-and-death in every thought &#8211; changing in every thought, hence transformational: that is <em>transformational birth-and-death</em>. It concerns the mind-thoughts' unceasing continuity: thoughts continuous &#8211; this thought just arising, that thought at once perishing &#8211; like an endless stream. When the cultivator is no longer subject to <em>sectional birth-and-death</em>, suddenly he longs to attain <em>transformational birth-and-death</em>.</p>
<p>To <strong>ever abide long within those subtle marks</strong> &#8211; a very subtle, wondrous form: abiding in it forever means attaining eternal life.</p>

<p><strong>Sutra text: Nhĩ thời thiên ma, hậu đắc kì tiện. Phi tinh phụ nhân khẩu thuyết kinh  pháp.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Then the heavenly demon gets its chance, sending its essence to possess another person, using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma.</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Commentary</em></strong>: The moment the cultivator cherishes the fantasy craving eternal life.</p>
<p><strong>Then the heavenly demon gets its convenient chance, sending its essence flying to possess another person.</strong> The heavenly demon at once sends some of its followers to possess someone else, <strong>using that person's mouth to expound sutras and Dharma</strong> for the good man cultivating right samadhi to hear.</p>
<p><strong>Sutra text: Kì nhân cánh bất giác tri ma trước, diệc ngôn tự đắc vô thượng niết-bàn. Lai bỉ cầu sanh thiện nam tử xứ, phu tòa thuyết pháp. Hảo ngôn tha phương, vãng hoàn vô trệ, hoặc kinh vạn lí, thuấn tức tái lai. Giai ư bỉ phương, thủ đắc kì vật. Hoặc ư nhất xứ, tại nhất trạch trung, xúc bộ chi gian, linh kì tòng đông, nghệ chí tây bích, thị nhân cấp hành, luy niên bất đáo. Nhân thử tâm tín, nghi Phật hiện tiền. </strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: That person, even less aware of demonic possession, likewise claims to have attained unsurpassed nirvana. Coming to the place of the good man seeking long life, it spreads its seat and preaches the Dharma &#8211; fond of boasting it can go anywhere and return unhindered, or traverse myriad miles, returning in an instant &#8211; all fetching objects from those places. Or in one place, within one house, within a few steps, making one go from east to west wall &#8211; that person walking swiftly, years never arriving. Hence the mind believes, suspecting a Buddha manifest.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: Since <strong>that person's</strong> mind is wholly controlled by the heavenly demon, he himself <strong>truly does not know he is demon-possessed, yet proclaims to have attained the wondrous fruition of unsurpassed nirvana. Coming to the good man seeking long life, spreading its seat, preaching the Dharma.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Fond of boasting it can go anywhere and return unhindered.</strong> What does the demon love to say? It says: &#8220;In a very brief moment I can go somewhere thousands upon myriads of miles away and return at once &#8211; I can go to Japan without a plane and buy goods back. If you do not believe, I shall demonstrate.&#8221;</p>
<p>It constantly displays spiritual powers for people to see &#8211; loves people seeing the inconceivable miracles it performs, saying: &#8220;I can come and go at will in the blink of an eye.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>It can roam a thousand miles and return in the blink of an eye</strong>, saying: &#8220;I can go and return within your single breath.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong>It can fetch back objects from the places it has been.</strong></p>
<p>Not only that &#8211; it can fetch back objects from the places it has been: buying goods back, asking: &#8220;Do you not believe me? Look at these goods &#8211; I bought them from company&#8230; in Japan &#8211; special sample goods, sold only to regular customers&#8230;&#8221; Then it produces for everyone a cassette player or radio made in Japan &#8211; no customs duty or import tax &#8211; truly manufactured by a Japanese company.</p>
<p><strong>Or it can tell someone to walk from the east to the west side of a room within a distance of but a few steps</strong> (perhaps seven or eight paces) &#8211; <strong>yet though that person walk a whole year, never arriving</strong>: it cannot stretch the floor's space to a year's maximum. Seeing it perform so, <strong>hence everyone believes the possessed person's words, mistaking it for the Buddha</strong>.</p>
<p>They think: &#8220;Oh, a Buddha has come to lecture the Dharma for us.&#8221;</p>
<p><strong><em>Sutra text: Khẩu trung thường thuyết, thập phương chúng sanh,  giai thị ngô tử. Ngã sanh chư Phật, ngã xuất thế giới, ngã thị nguyên Phật, xuất sanh tự nhiên, bất nhân tu đắc.</em></strong></p>
<p><strong>Translation: Its mouth constantly says: beings of the ten directions are all my children. I gave birth to the Buddhas; I brought forth the world. I am the original Buddha, born spontaneously of nature, not through cultivation.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: <strong>It constantly says</strong>: do you know? <strong>Beings of the ten directions are all my children; I gave birth to the Buddhas</strong> &#8211; shamelessly boasting it gave birth to the Buddhas.</p>
<p><strong>I brought forth the world; I am the first Buddha</strong> &#8211; I am the earliest Buddha; no Buddha before me. I brought forth this world spontaneously, not through cultivation. <strong>I made this world spontaneously of nature</strong>, <strong>not through cultivation</strong> &#8211; and I was already a Buddha coming into this world: &#8220;I needed no cultivation to become a Buddha.&#8221;</p>
