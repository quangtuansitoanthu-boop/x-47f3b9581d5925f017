TITLE: 17. ILLNESS ENTERS THROUGH THE MOUTH
TOMTAT: Mrs. Ly's mother-in-law suffered aching feet when walking, from her love of stewed pig's trotters. Meeting Master Miaofa, she learned that "as you sow, so shall you reap," repented, became vegetarian, and healed.
-----
{align:center|**ILLNESS ENTERS THROUGH THE MOUTH**}

{align:justify|If you would be free of illness}

{align:justify|The heart must be pure}

{align:justify|All beings have feelings}

{align:justify|Cherish life — protect the living}

{align:justify|Mrs. Ly came from Taiwan to consult Master Miaofa:}

{align:justify|"My mother-in-law has no illness in her body — only, whenever she steps out and has not walked a hundred meters, both her ankles ache piercingly, and she must sit and rest a while before walking on. So she has visited hospitals all over Taiwan and America for diagnosis, yet no illness is found. We beg the Master's teaching."}

{align:justify|"Is your mother most fond of stewed pig's trotters?"}

{align:justify|"Yes, Master. How do you know? My mother demands pig's trotters every day — without them she cannot sleep at night," Miss Ly said trembling, eyes brimming with tears, her face full of emotion.}

{align:justify|"Tell her on your return: henceforth she may eat no more pig's trotters. If she can repent, become vegetarian, and recite the Buddha's name, gradually the illness will heal."}

{align:justify|The young woman asked:}

{align:justify|"Why may she not eat pig's trotters?"}

{align:justify|The Master explained:}

{align:justify|"As the cause, so the fruit! Who loves eating fish eyes often falls ill in the eyes; who loves chewing chicken necks will have trouble in the neck bones. Who craves pig's stomach will suffer stomach pain... Do not foolishly trust the theory that 'eating an animal's organ nourishes that organ in yourself.' Believing such foods nourish this place or that is utterly and greatly mistaken! Besides cruelly harming others to benefit yourself, in the end the result is self-harm. Retribution will surely come — the debt must be repaid, that is all."}

{align:justify|COMMENTARY:}

{align:justify|The story ILLNESS ENTERS THROUGH THE MOUTH is a brief but profound lesson in "eating an organ — suffering retribution in the corresponding organ." Mrs. Ly's mother-in-law loved stewed pig's trotters and suffered aching ankle bones when walking. Master Miaofa states plainly: "As the cause, so the fruit." The belief that "eating an organ nourishes that organ" is mistaken — it is eating the "debt of hatred" of animals. After repenting and ending pig's trotters (eating vegetarian), the illness will heal. The story warns: "Illness enters through the mouth." Whoever loves pig's trotters, chicken necks, pig's stomach, fish eyes... should awaken soon lest retribution fall painfully upon their own bodies.}
