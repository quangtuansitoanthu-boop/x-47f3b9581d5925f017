<p>As for cultivation free from friction, these gods all contemplate cause and effect: they have extinguished lust and advanced to that realm, yet have not escaped the form-body. The Realm of Form includes the Non-Returning Heavens; however, since sages dwell in these realms, they truly belong to a separate rank. In the sutra, the text compares the sages to those living in remote places unknown to people. This is called the Realm of Form.</p>

<p style="text-align:center"><strong>THE FORMLESS REALM</strong></p>

<p style="text-align:center"><strong>* * *</strong></p>
<p><strong>Phục thứ A-nan, tùng thị hữu đính, sắc biên tế trung. Kỳ gian phục hữu, nhị chủng kì lộ. Nhược ư xả tâm, phát minh trí tuệ. Tuệ quang viên thông, tiện xuất trần giới. Thành A-la-hán, nhập Bồ-tát thừa.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Further, Ananda! From the summit of the Realm of Form, there again diverge two paths: if, within the renunciant mind, one develops wisdom, with bright wisdom perfectly interfused, one then transcends the dusty world, becomes an Arhat, and enters the Bodhisattva Vehicle; such a class is called the Great Arhats Who Have Turned the Mind.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;Further, Ananda! From the summit of the Realm of Form, there again diverge two paths.&#8221; At this point there is another fork in the road. &#8220;Within the renunciant mind&#8221; &#8211; once they practice renouncing the characteristics of form, they can open wisdom. &#8220;Developing wisdom, with bright wisdom perfectly interfused,&#8221; they are no longer obstructed. They transcend the dusty world, leaving behind the three realms (the realms of humans, asuras, and gods) by taking this path, and accomplish Arhatship. They attain the fruition of Arhatship and enter the Great Vehicle path of the Bodhisattva. Such cultivators are called the Great Arhats Who Have Turned the Mind &#8211; meaning they have turned from the small to the great, from the Small Vehicle toward the Great Vehicle.</p>


<ol>
<li><strong>Như thị nhất loại, danh vi hồi tâm, đại A-la-hán. Nhược tại xả tâm, xả yếm thành tựu. Giác thân vi ngại, tiêu ngại nhập không. Như thị nhất loại, danh vi không xứ.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>If, within the renunciant mind, renunciation and detachment are accomplished, perceiving the body as an obstruction and dissolving the obstruction to enter emptiness, such a class is called the Station of Emptiness.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: We have finished discussing the Four Dhyanas and now begin explaining the Four Stations of Emptiness. &#8220;If, within the renunciant mind, renunciation and detachment are accomplished, perceiving the body as an obstruction.&#8221; These gods have renounced bliss and rejected suffering; they know the body is an obstruction to cultivation. They &#8220;dissolve the obstruction to enter emptiness; such a class is called the Station of Emptiness.&#8221; They wish to be hindered by nothing, and so they turn the light around to illumine within, seeing the body as illusory as empty space. In this way they sweep away all obstructions. Such beings take the other path and enter the Station of Emptiness.</p>


<ol start="2">
<li><strong>Chư ngại ký tiêu, vô ngại vô diệt. Kỳ trung duy lưu, A-lại-da thức. Toàn ư mạt na, bán phần vi tế. Như thị nhất loại, danh vi thức xứ.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>With obstructions already dissolved, neither obstructed nor extinguished, within there remains only the alaya consciousness and half a subtle part of the manas consciousness; such a class is called the Station of Consciousness.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: &#8220;With obstructions already dissolved, neither obstructed nor extinguished.&#8221; At the summit of the Fourth Dhyana, the gods who wish to advance upward feel the body to be an obstruction, so they dissolve the obstructions and enter the nature of emptiness. Now that they have advanced to the Formless Realm, there are no further obstructions of material form; with no obstructing things, there is nothing to extinguish.</p>
<p>&#8220;Within there remains only the alaya consciousness and half a subtle part of the manas consciousness.&#8221; At this point there is no body &#8211; only consciousness remains, namely the alaya consciousness, the eighth consciousness, called the storehouse consciousness. &#8220;Storehouse&#8221; is truly the treasury of the Buddha-nature; but at this point consciousness has not yet fully returned to the Tathagata's inherent nature, and so it is still called consciousness. Every act, every word, everything you do and communicate in daily life is recorded in this eighth consciousness. For these cultivators, there remain the alaya consciousness and half a subtle part of the manas consciousness &#8211; manas being the seventh consciousness, called the transmitting consciousness, in which transformation takes place.</p>
<p>Strictly speaking, ignorance arises within the eighth consciousness, but here it is very close to becoming the great perfect mirror wisdom, free and undefiled. Only when the messages stored in the eighth consciousness run through the seventh does it become defiled. Now, however, even the manas consciousness functions at only half capacity, so the defilement remains extremely subtle. &#8220;Such beings are among those of the Station of Consciousness; they are reborn in the Station of Consciousness.&#8221;</p>


<ol start="3">
<li><strong>Không sắc ký vong, thức tâm đô diệt. Thập phương tịch nhiên, huýnh vô du vãng. Như thị nhất loại, danh vô sở hữu xứ.</strong></li>

<ol>
<p><strong>Both form and emptiness forgotten, mind and consciousness both extinguished; the ten directions still and silent, with no coming or going anywhere; such a class is called the Station of Nothing Whatsoever.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Commentary</strong>: When they reach this stage, everything is empty. It is truly a case of:<br />
The house destroyed,<br />
The people gone,<br />
Nothing left to say.</p>
<p>Everything has vanished. &#8220;Both form and emptiness forgotten, mind and consciousness both extinguished.&#8221; The house destroyed, the people gone. At the previous stage there still remained half of the manas consciousness, but at this stage the seventh consciousness is gone. It is hard to describe in words: if you no longer use consciousness, how can you speak? Then there is nothing more to say.<br />
&#8220;The ten directions still and silent, with no coming or going anywhere.&#8221; All worlds throughout the ten directions of the Dharma realm have vanished; a silent sky fills all. There is nowhere to go, nor any place to come from &#8211; no coming and no going.</p>
<p>&#8220;Such a class is called the Station of Nothing Whatsoever.&#8221; Although there is nothing, still the nature of these beings remains; their nature is the same as the nature of emptiness. Thus the beings of the Station of Nothing Whatsoever still have a lifespan &#8211; how long? Six thousand great eons. The lifespan of the gods and their stature grow enormously between the Realm of Form and the Formless Realm. I have not mentioned their height &#8211; they are truly gigantic. I shall wait until the later section to impress you with some huge numbers. If you wish to know all the figures in between, you may look them up.</p>
<p>This is the second-highest heaven, and the gods' lifespan is sixty thousand great eons. We call it a lifespan, but in fact these gods long abide in samadhi. At the end of that time, their samadhi power is destroyed, and then they are once again reborn in another realm &#8211; it is not certain into which realm they will be reborn. These are the beings dwelling in the Station of Nothing Whatsoever.</p>

<li><strong>Thức tánh bất động, dĩ diệt cùng nghiên. Ư vô tận trung, phát tuyên tận tánh. Như tồn bất tồn, nhược tận phi tận. Như thị nhất loại, danh vi phi tưởng, phi phi tưởng xử.</strong></li>
