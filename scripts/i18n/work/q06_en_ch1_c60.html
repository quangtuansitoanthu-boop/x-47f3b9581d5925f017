When the disciple reached the address written on the envelope for Miss Qing Liang, she had become a prostitute. The disciple was utterly amazed, "Why would my teacher write to a prostitute?" He wondered, "Is she my teacher's lover, and is the teacher asking me to be the go-between?" But he delivered the letter, saying, "My teacher, Du Shun, sent a letter to you." Miss Qing Liang received the letter, read it, sat down, and said, "Good! He is going. I shall go too."

Then she died right on her seat. She had entered nirvana. The disciple found the whole affair too strange, and he picked up the letter to read. Only then did he learn Miss Qing Liang was in fact Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, for the letter said, "Guanshiyin, I have finished my work here and shall depart. You should go with me." The disciple sighed with regret, "Had I known she was Avalokiteshvara Bodhisattva, I would have knelt before her; I would not have risen until she entered nirvana, so I could have gained enlightened wisdom. How fine that would be. But now I have missed the chance."

That truly matches the meaning of the saying: Before him, yet not recognizing him as Guanshiyin. He took the other letter and headed for Old Mother Pig's dwelling.

But when he reached the address in the letter, no one knew of such a person. But as he passed a pigsty, an old sow asked him, "What business have you seeking Old Mother Pig?" The disciple was amazed, not knowing what monster he was meeting. He hurriedly answered, "My teacher told me to deliver a letter to Old Mother Pig." The old sow said, "Oh! Good. I am Old Mother Pig. You may give me the letter."

The old sow received the letter and looked. How hard to understand how she could know what the letter said. Yet, having looked, she sat down and said, "Oh! His work is done. I shall go too." And she died. When the disciple read the letter, he learned she was precisely a transformation of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva. "Can Samantabhadra Bodhisattva truly be that pig?" He still harbored doubts. He paid no attention to whatever work his teacher had finished as the letter said. He climbed Wutai Mountain. There, he met a most aged old monk, who asked him, "What have you come here for?" "I have come to bow to the Great Wisdom Manjushri Bodhisattva, hoping for enlightened wisdom."

"You fellow!" the old monk said, "You climb here to bow to Manjushri Bodhisattva, but bowing to your teacher is a thousand times better." The disciple asked, "Why?"

"Your teacher, Elder Master Du Shun, is precisely Amitabha Buddha reborn. He manifests in the human realm to teach beings. You have been his disciple more than ten years. How did you not recognize it?" "Oh! My teacher is Amitabha Buddha!" the disciple said, "He looks nothing like a Buddha!" When he looked again, the old monk had vanished.

Then he found a short letter saying: "Manjushri Bodhisattva tells you to return at once to your teacher, Elder Master Du Shun, who is precisely Amitabha Buddha." Finally, the disciple believed it. He had met Manjushri Bodhisattva in the flesh, and he told him to return to his teacher.

He hurried back; Elder Master Du Shun had passed away the day before. Once again, he missed the chance. He had been Amitabha Buddha's disciple for ten years without recognizing it. He abandoned what was at hand to seek what was too far away, only to find he should return to his teacher. Now whom else is there to meet?

Sutra text: 終不自言我真菩薩真阿羅漢。泄 佛密因輕言未學。
