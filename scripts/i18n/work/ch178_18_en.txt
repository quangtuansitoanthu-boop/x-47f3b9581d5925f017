TITLE: 01. FIRST, PRAY FOR MY MOTHER'S KARMIC CREDITORS. (HAVING LEARNED BUDDHISM FOR OVER 20 YEARS, I KNOW THAT ILLNESSES NO TREATMENT CAN CURE ALL BELONG TO KARMIC OBSTRUCTION—CAUSED WHEN KARMIC CREDITORS COME TO COLLECT THEIR DEBTS AND HARASS US... SO IT IS ESSENTIAL TO DEDICATE MERIT TO THESE KARMIC CREDITORS UNSEEN BY ORDINARY EYES). THE DEDICATION RUNS ROUGHLY AS FOLLOWS:
TOMTAT: "I dedicate the merit of reciting this 'Diamond Sutra' to all of my mother's karmic creditors. I beg you creditors to torment my mother no longer. I know you suffer greatly, and my mother suffers too. But harassing her like...

{align:justify|"I dedicate the merit of reciting this 'Diamond Sutra' to all of my mother's karmic creditors. I beg you creditors to torment my mother no longer. I know you suffer greatly, and my mother suffers too. But harassing her like this only harms both sides—both lose. Now I recite the Diamond Sutra and dedicate its merit to you, praying that your karmic obstructions dissolve, that you may escape suffering and gain happiness, be reborn in a wholesome realm. I hope you will resolve to take refuge in the Three Jewels, believe in and vow to recite the Buddha's name, and be reborn in the Western Pure Land of Ultimate Bliss."}
